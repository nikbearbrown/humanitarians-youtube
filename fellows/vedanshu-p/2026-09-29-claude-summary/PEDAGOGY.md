## The ONE idea

> Each of these three techniques does exactly what it promises and then stops.
> None of them makes an answer true. What they buy is a system where being wrong
> is traceable.

## Why these three chapters summarise together

They are not three unrelated tips. Chapter 7 separates the pipeline into stages
so a fault can be localised. Chapter 8 works inside one of those stages.
Chapter 9 works inside another. Read in order they look like a sequence of
upgrades — which is exactly the misconception this reel corrects, because in
each case the honest finding was a **limit**:

- the pipeline localises a fault and stops short of repairing it;
- prompt assembly improves the odds and stops short of compelling the model;
- re-ranking orders a shortlist and stops short of retrieving.

Three techniques, three ceilings, each set by something upstream. That is a
genuine through-line rather than a stitched-together recap.
