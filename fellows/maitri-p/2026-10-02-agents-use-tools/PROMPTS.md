# PROMPTS — agents-use-tools

Beat-prefixed prompts for open slots.

**There are no open slots.** Every beat is filled by a registered, deterministic
Remotion composition rendered from the beat sheet; nothing in this reel requires
a generated image, a stock still, or human-supplied media.

The two prompts that appear ON SCREEN are film content, not generation prompts:

- **B00** — the cold-open ask typed into the composer:
  `What is the largest file in this repository?`
- **B08** — the HANDOFF prompt, read aloud verbatim and discussed:
  `Before you call any tool, tell me which one, why, and what you expect to get back. Then call it and tell me whether the result matched.`

Neither is sent to any API. Both are rendered as text inside the Claude skin.
