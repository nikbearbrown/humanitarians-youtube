## This week's contribution
Explainer on how GPS finds your position: satellites broadcast time and position, the phone only listens, delay becomes distance, circles narrow to one point (plus a fourth satellite for the clock), 1 µs ≈ 300 m, and why buildings and tunnels reduce accuracy.
