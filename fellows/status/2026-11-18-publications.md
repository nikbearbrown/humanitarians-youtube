## Common false positives excluded

- **A cited paper is not a fellow-authored paper.** For example, Neelabh B.'s [README](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/humanitarians-youtube/blob/91b92d8028f12ac05360c136aab4648ed728ada0/fellows/neelabh-b/README.md) explicitly describes research explainers whose source material is published research. Tanmay K.'s self-correction explainer distinguishes an ICLR paper and a preprint by other authors; that does not transfer publication credit.
- **“Publication decision pending” usually refers to a video.** It is not evidence that a scientific manuscript was submitted, accepted or published.
- **A Substack article is public writing, not automatically a peer-reviewed paper.** Agrima P. and other communications contributors should receive credit for articles and outreach, separately from scholarly publication.
- **A planned methods paper is not a paper result.** Satwik S.'s plan explicitly lists publication as future work.
- **Fictional examples are not fellow achievements.** `example-f` and `maya-r` are excluded from research-achievement counts.

## Audit scope and caveat

Searched the checkout's Markdown, JSON and text for publication, acceptance, authorship, paper and preprint claims; read the strongest candidate project and renewal records and their linked in-tree evidence. Searched the web by exact manuscript titles and the explicit IJCAI credential. The searches did not establish a publication for the current manuscripts. Not every person's historical bibliography, every linked private document, every external repository or every media file was audited. This is an evidence-status audit, not a bibliometric census of all fellows' lifetime publications.

The most useful monthly improvement is a small publication register with title, contributor folder IDs, project attribution, status (draft/submitted/accepted/preprint/published), venue, DOI or public paper URL, date, and independently checked evidence. Do not fill missing fields by inference. Treat “paper-ready” as an assessment requiring methods, data, evaluation and manuscript review; none of the above is certified paper-ready by this pass.
