# Frictional log — collaborative video plan

## 2026-10-18 — planning with someone else in the room

> Written 2026-10-17, within the week it describes, from the Week 20 report.
> The reasoning below is reconstructed; the plan itself was not captured here.

- **Evidence:** the [channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MedhavyAI) as it now stands
- **Preceding thread:** [video suggestions](../2026-10-08-youtube-video-suggestions/) (Week 19), [channel reorganization](../2026-09-24-channel-reorganization/) (Weeks 17–18)
- **This week's other thread:** [first branded videos](../2026-10-15-first-branded-videos/)
- **Hours:** Week 20 total 22.0 — 9.0 on this thread — [HOURS.md](../HOURS.md)

**What I was working on.** Turning last week's solo suggestions into an actual
plan for the channel's videos, with a teammate.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** I expected collaboration to be mostly
additive — my suggestions plus theirs, merged. I expected the hard part to be
scope.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **Working with someone forced the decision I had been avoiding.** For three
  weeks my own entries kept circling the same blocker: I could not say who the
  channel is for, so I could not choose content types. Alone, I could leave that
  unresolved indefinitely and still feel productive. With a second person you
  cannot — you have to say out loud what you think the channel is, because
  otherwise you are not planning the same thing. That pressure is what moved it.
- **A plan is a different object than a list of ideas.** Last week's suggestions
  were a set of candidates; each stood on its own. A plan has to say how they
  relate, what comes first, and what the shape is. Several suggestions that
  looked fine individually did not survive being placed in an order.
- **I shared it.** This is the part that breaks the recent pattern. Weeks 17
  through 19 all ended with work held — the plan unwritten, the suggestions
  unsent. This one went to the team and the professor. Partly because a shared
  artifact has a second author who also expects it to go somewhere.
- **I still did not commit it here.** Fourth week running. The honest reading is
  that sharing it felt like it discharged the obligation to record it, and it
  does not: a plan sent to two people is not a plan in the repository.

**What Claude contributed, and what I accepted, changed or rejected.**

Varun's contribution is recorded in [README.md](README.md): the intro and outro
are mine alone, and the two new YouTube videos were made jointly with him.

On Claude's side, nothing specific was logged for this thread at the time.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand that some decisions do not get made alone — not because they are
too hard, but because solitude lets you defer them without cost. Three weeks of
my own logs named the same blocker and did not clear it; one week with a
collaborator did. That is a fact about working alone, not about the problem.

I do not know whether the plan is any good — it is shared, not reviewed, and no
response has come back. I also do not know how the credit splits, which matters
because this is the first piece of work in this folder that is not mine alone,
and I would rather say so precisely than vaguely.

## 2026-10-17 — there is no plan document, and saying so closes it

> Written 2026-10-17. Appended; the entries above stand as written.

Asked directly what the plan was, the answer is that it was never a document.
The week's work was: delete the unwanted videos from the channel, keep the ones
that belonged, and make the ones that were missing. That is a plan in the sense
of a decision acted on, not in the sense of a file.

My earlier entries read as though a `PLAN.md` was coming. It was not — the work
had a different shape, not a missing artifact. The evidence is the
[channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MedhavyAI) as it now stands, plus the
[two new videos](../2026-10-15-first-branded-videos/).

What is genuinely lost: the *before*. Which videos were removed, and the
reasoning for removing those and not others, exists nowhere. The result is
inspectable; the judgement is not. For curation work that is the thing worth
capturing, and I did not capture it either time — Week 17 or now.

**Credit split, confirmed 2026-10-17.** The intro and outro are mine alone. The
two new YouTube videos were made jointly with Varun. The collaboration was on
the content, not on the branding — which is the opposite of how I had it
implicitly framed above, where "the plan" carried the joint work. The joint work
was the making.
