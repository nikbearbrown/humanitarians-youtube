The plan was not written up as a document. It was carried out directly, in two

So "a plan for the videos" is accurate as a description of the week's thinking,
but the output was a changed channel and two new videos, not a planning
document. The evidence is therefore the
[channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MedhavyAI) in its current state.

What a reader still cannot see is the *before* — which videos were removed, and
why those and not others. The reasoning is gone; only the result is inspectable.
