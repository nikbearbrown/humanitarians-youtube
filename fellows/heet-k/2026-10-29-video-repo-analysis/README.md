## What this is

A survey of the 200+ video repository to work out **what kind of video the
Medhavy channel should be adding**.

Up to this point the channel-direction question had been answered by
instinct and by what the pipeline happened to make easy. Weeks 17–19 circled it
without closing it; Week 20 answered it implicitly by shipping two mechanics
Shorts; Week 21 scaled that to twenty. This week went the other way — looking at
what already exists across the repository, at volume, and deriving the answer
from the corpus instead of from the pipeline.

## Why look at the repository rather than the channel

The channel shows what Medhavy has published. The repository shows what the
wider body of work *covers* — which subjects are already well served, which
formats recur, and where the gaps are. Those gaps are the argument for what to
make next, and they are not visible from the channel alone.

## Result

The analysis settled on **Quantum Computing & Information** as the topic to
build against, which became the
[next week's videos and skill](../2026-11-05-topic-videos-and-skill/). That is the test of
whether the week was useful: not whether the survey was thorough, but whether
anything got made because of it. Something did.
