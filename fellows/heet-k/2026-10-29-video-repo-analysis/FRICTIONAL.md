# Frictional log — analysing the 200+ video repository

## 2026-11-01 — reading the corpus instead of arguing about the plan

> Written 2026-11-14, after the week it describes. Reconstructed, not captured
> as it happened.

- **Outcome:** [the videos and skill it produced](../2026-11-05-topic-videos-and-skill/), the following week
- **Preceding attempts:** [channel direction, Weeks 17–18](../2026-09-24-channel-reorganization/) · [suggestions, Week 19](../2026-10-08-youtube-video-suggestions/) · [plan with Varun, Week 20](../2026-10-15-collaborative-video-plan/)
- **Hours:** Week 22 total 21.0 — 18.0 on the analysis — [HOURS.md](../HOURS.md)

**What I was working on.** Going through the 200+ video repository to work out
what sort of video the Medhavy channel should be adding.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** I expected this to be a longer version of
the planning weeks — look at things, form an opinion, write it down. What
actually happened is that the question changed shape. "What should the channel
hold?" is unanswerable in the abstract; "what does this corpus already cover,
and where are the holes?" is a question with an answer in it.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **Volume is its own problem.** 200+ videos is past the point where you can
  hold the set in your head and notice patterns. I had to decide what I was
  looking *for* before looking, or the survey would just be watching things.
- **Coverage and quality are different axes and I kept conflating them.** A
  subject being well covered is not the same as being well covered *in the
  format Medhavy makes*. Some topics appear often but not as short explainers,
  which makes them gaps rather than duplicates.
- **The honest check on this week is downstream.** Four previous weeks produced
  channel-direction thinking that never turned into anything. The difference
  this time is not that the analysis was better — I cannot prove that — but that
  videos got built from it the following week. That is the only evidence that
  separates this from the earlier attempts.
- **I did not write the analysis up.** Same pattern as the planning weeks. The
  mitigation this time is that it produced an artifact one step downstream, so
  the thinking is at least inferable from the output.

**What Claude contributed, and what I accepted, changed or rejected.**

The read of the corpus and the conclusions drawn from it are my own. What the
analysis pointed at — Quantum Computing & Information — is a judgement about
gaps in coverage, not something a tool handed me.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand why four weeks of planning produced nothing and one week of reading
produced a topic. Planning asked me to invent an answer; the corpus already
contained one. Working from evidence beat working from intention, and that is
the same lesson as Week 19 — concrete beats abstract — arriving a second time
from a different direction.

I do not know whether the gaps I found are the gaps that matter. A hole in the
coverage is only worth filling if someone wants the thing, and nothing in this
week's work touches demand.
