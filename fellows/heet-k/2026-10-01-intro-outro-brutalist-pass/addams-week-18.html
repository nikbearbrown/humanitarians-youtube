The output and the machine are two different claims

When you build the tool that makes the thing, the honest accounting gets a little more interesting. There are two separate things I could claim credit for this week: the videos, and the skill that produces them. They’re not the same, and conflating them would be a subtle overclaim in either direction.

The videos this week are a refinement pass — created and updated, self-reviewed, not sent. That’s their real status, and it’s modest. But the skill is the more durable thing: a reusable capability that turns the Brutalist tool plus a CLAUDE.md into finished intro/outro videos. When I say “I created the intro and outro,” the honest expansion is “I ran them through a skill I built” — which is a bigger statement about capability and a smaller statement about this week’s specific videos. Both are true at once, and keeping them distinct is the accurate way to log it.
