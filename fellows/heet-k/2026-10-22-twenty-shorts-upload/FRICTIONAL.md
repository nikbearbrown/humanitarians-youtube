# Frictional log — twenty Shorts

## 2026-10-25 — twenty in a week

> Written 2026-10-26, the day after the week closed. The production detail below
> is reconstructed rather than captured as it happened.

- **Published:** <https://www.youtube.com/@MedhavyAI/shorts>
- **With:** [Varun N.](../../varun-n/)
- **Previous batch:** [two Shorts](../2026-10-15-first-branded-videos/), 12–18 Oct
- **Hours:** Week 21 total 22.0 — [HOURS.md](../HOURS.md)

**What I was working on.** Creating and uploading twenty YouTube Shorts with
Varun.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** After Week 20's two videos I expected the
next batch to be maybe four or five — a step up, not a step change. Twenty was
not the plan at the start of the week; it became possible once the pipeline
stopped needing attention.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **The bottleneck moved.** For two months the hard part was the branding: the
  mark, the palette, the tracking, the formats, getting it confirmed. Once that
  was fixed and regenerating through `logo-motion`, the cost per video dropped
  far enough that the limit became how fast we could decide what to make and get
  it uploaded. That is a different kind of week and it took a few videos to
  notice the constraint had shifted.
- **Upload is not a free step.** Twenty uploads means twenty titles, twenty
  descriptions, twenty thumbnails and twenty sets of metadata. That work scales
  linearly where the production did not, and by the end it was the larger share
  of the effort. I had been thinking of publishing as what happens after the
  work; at this volume it is the work.
- **Splitting with Varun actually mattered.** Two people on twenty videos is not
  twice as fast on each one; it is the difference between a batch finishing and a
  batch stalling. We divided creation and upload rather than both doing both.
- **I logged the batch, not the videos.** They are all in the
  [Shorts feed](https://www.youtube.com/@MedhavyAI/shorts), which is the honest
  record at this volume — but it means the unit of accounting changed this week
  without my deciding it. At two videos I named each one; at twenty the feed
  does the naming.

**What Claude contributed, and what I accepted, changed or rejected.**

Claude did the video creation through the skills — that is the part that made
twenty in a week possible at all. What Varun and I did was decide what to make,
direct the output, and handle the publishing: titles, descriptions, thumbnails
and metadata, twenty times over.

Stated plainly, the split is that the tool produced the videos and we produced
the judgement and the upload. Neither half gets to the channel without the other,
but they are different contributions and worth naming separately.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand what the two months of branding work were actually buying. It did
not feel like throughput at the time — it felt like one sting being revised over
and over. The payoff is that a week like this one is possible at all, and the
jump from two to twenty is the measurement of it.

I also understand that the recording problem gets worse with volume, not better.
Every previous week I could reconstruct afterwards because there were one or two
things in it. Twenty is past that point.

What I do not know is whether any of them land. Twenty published videos is a
throughput result, not an audience one, and nothing here says anyone watched.
