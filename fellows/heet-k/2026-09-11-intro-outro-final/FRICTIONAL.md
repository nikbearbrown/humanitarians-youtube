# Frictional log — intro and outro, confirmed final

## 2026-09-13 — the branding arc closes

> report and the Drive folder; not a contemporaneous entry.

- **Hours:** Week 15 total 21.0 — 8.0 on this thread — [HOURS.md](../HOURS.md)

**What I was working on.** Getting the outros to the state the intros reached two
weeks earlier, and making the intro/outro set read as one identity rather than two
pieces that happen to share a palette.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** I expected the outro review to come back
with revisions, because the previous week's set had gone out without a clear
favourite. I expected another round trip.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **It didn't resist, and that was its own problem.** Both were finalized on
  review. After five or six weeks of first-pass, interim, sent, revised, and
  approved-then-not-quite, a clean confirmation felt like it must be missing
  something. The temptation was to keep polishing past the point where anyone had
  asked me to. I stopped.
- **The real judgement call was what "final" meant.** The design was confirmed.
  The deliverable formats — 16:9, 9:16, 4K — did not exist yet. I could have
  written this week as "outro delivered." I wrote it as the design confirmed, and
  kept the format delivery as its own week. Those are different pieces of work and
  collapsing them would have made the following week look like padding.
- **Two threads closed in the same week** — this one and the explainer. Writing
  them up, the pull was to let one confirmation vouch for the other. They were
  reviewed separately and are recorded separately.

**What Claude contributed, and what I accepted, changed or rejected.**

Claude proposed the intro and outro designs. I did not take them as given — I
sent back changes and iterated, and what shipped is the result of those rounds
rather than a first suggestion accepted. The shape of the collaboration was
consistent throughout: Claude generated candidates, I directed the revisions,
and the professor's confirmation is what closed it.

What that means for credit: the design ideas started on Claude's side, the
direction and the judgement calls were mine, and neither half produced the final
on its own.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand the difference between a confirmed design and a delivered asset, and
that reporting them as one event would have overstated this week and hollowed out
the next.

I do not know *why* this set was confirmed when the previous one wasn't — I have
the outcome, not the reasoning. That means I cannot generalise anything from it to
the next brand job, which is the part that actually would have been worth learning.
