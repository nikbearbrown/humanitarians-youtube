## Why the verdict is a refusal

Most weekly work reports show something that got finished. This one shows a
field that did not change, which is a harder beat to carry — so the structure
puts the refusal at the end of a rubric the viewer has already been given, and
B09 scores it line by line rather than asserting it.

The risk in this reel is self-congratulation: "look how rigorous I was". B08 is
the counterweight and it is placed *after* the verdict on purpose, so the
episode's last substantive beat is the one where the previous episode was wrong.
