## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the week's ask

    claude "diff the record my gate command writes against the ones written
            by hand"

**B03** - the ask, expanded

    claude "make each gate record name and hash the files its own test
            actually points at"

**B10** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this audit record: list the artifacts it names, and tell me
            which fields are defaults rather than observations"

The B10 prompt asks for two things that are each checkable without knowing the
system: what the record names, and which of its fields nobody actually supplied.
The narration adds a third by hand - look at the timestamp and ask whose day it
is - because that one is a single glance and it is the error this episode ends
on.
