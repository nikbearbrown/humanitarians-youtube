## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the topic question

    claude "why pair process supervision with multi-hop? what does outcome
            grading miss?"

**B09** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this agent result: define one step, say who labelled it
            correct, and whether that label was knowable before the episode
            ended"

The B09 prompt is three asks, and the third is the one that does the work.
A step label that could only be assigned after seeing how the episode turned out
is an outcome label wearing a different name, and that question surfaces it
without needing to understand the system. The GOOD/BAD discriminator makes the
same test concrete: a step definition you could apply yourself, versus the
phrase "process reward model" with no step ever defined.
