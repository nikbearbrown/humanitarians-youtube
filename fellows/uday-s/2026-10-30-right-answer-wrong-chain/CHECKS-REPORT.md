## How the commissioned topic was handled

"Process-Supervised & Multi-Hop AI Agents" names a thing that does not exist as
a single object. Rather than invent one, the reel asks what the two halves have
to do with each other and answers it with a measurement: on the benchmarks built
to test multi-step reasoning, a model can score **68.8 answer F1 while bypassing
the reasoning steps.** That is the whole reason the two belong in a sentence.

The payoff is a rubric a viewer can apply to any agent-evaluation claim, which
is more durable than the numbers.

## The risk, and what answers it

Two opposite risks.

- **Overselling process supervision.** B06 exists to prevent that, and it is
  placed immediately after the headline result rather than at the end.
- **Dismissing it.** B03 and B04 establish a real, measured problem first, and
  B08's excitement line is specific about what was actually achieved.
