## Laws observed

- ILLUSTRATE LAW - Claude UI only in B00, B09, B10. Every evidence beat is a
  data animation with no product chrome.
- AUDIO-FIRST - durations are measured MP3 lengths, never estimates.
- 4K - Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- PROPORTIONAL BARS - B03's three bars are proportional to their figures
  (68.8/63.4/37.8 at a constant scale), and B04's two series share one scale so
  the flat line is visibly flat against the descending one.

## The direction label is not optional

B03 puts "lower is harder to cheat" on screen under the bars. A high DiRe score
reads like a good result and means the opposite - a model scoring well while
bypassing the reasoning. Leaving the direction to narration would let the
longest bar be misread as the best benchmark.

## The one strip that marks its own limit

B07's citation strip reads: "the contrast is sourced; the inference about agent
labelling is reasoning." That beat argues from two verified facts to a
conclusion nobody measured, and the strip says so rather than leaving it to the
paperwork.

## Side-by-side holds

B01 (two categories), B04 (two series) and B07 (two senses of step) are the
comparison beats. B01 holds 1.4s and 1.8s before the joining panel; B04 holds
1.8s after each series so both are up together before the verdict line; B07
holds 1.8s and 2.0s between its two panels.
