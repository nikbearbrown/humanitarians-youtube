## Laws observed

- ILLUSTRATE LAW - Claude UI only in B00, B03, B04, B10, B11. Every evidence
  beat is a data animation with no product chrome.
- ACTUAL-CODE LAW - B04 is the commit's own command and error text.
- AUDIO-FIRST - every duration above is a measured MP3 length.
- 4K - Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- B05 shows REAL interpreter output, not a mock-up of one. The two panels carry
  what a standalone python3 process actually printed.

## Why B05 stacks rather than sits side by side

The two cases are a before/after in severity, not a left/right comparison, and
stacking keeps the group taller than it is wide - which is the composition rule
these reels converged on after several beats underfilled when laid out wide and
short.

## Side-by-side holds

B05 (two cases), B07 (two populations) and B08 (rule versus its consequence) are
the comparison beats. B05 holds 1.8s after the first case before the second is
drawn; B07 holds 1.8s and 2.0s between its two cards; B08 holds 2.4s and 2.6s,
the longest in the reel, because the viewer is being asked to connect it to an
episode from five weeks ago.
