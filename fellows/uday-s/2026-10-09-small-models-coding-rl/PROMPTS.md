# PROMPTS - One Model, Three Numbers

No image or video generation was used. Manim + Remotion only, so there are no
model prompts to record. GATE F requires this file; its honest content is that
nothing was generated.

## Stills

None. No archival fetch, no `pantry/`, no Ken Burns beats. Every frame is drawn.

## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the topic question

    claude "how much of a small coding model's score is the model, and how much
            is the agent around it?"

**B09** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this coding benchmark score: name the scaffold, the context
            window, whether it is pass@1 or best-of-N, and who selected the
            winning trajectory"

The B09 prompt is deliberately four separate asks rather than one judgment,
because each is independently checkable against a model card and none requires
trusting the answer. The last clause - who selected the winning trajectory - is
the one that separates a 59 from a 71, and it is the one most model cards leave
to a footnote.
