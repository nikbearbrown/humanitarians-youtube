## Laws observed

- **ILLUSTRATE LAW** — Claude UI only in B00, B09, B10. Every evidence beat is a
  data animation with no product chrome.
- **AUDIO-FIRST** — durations are measured MP3 lengths, never estimates.
- **4K** — Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- **NEVER THROUGH TEXT** — B07 negates two claims and the cross sits beside each
  headline, never across it.
- **NO INVENTED PROPORTIONS** — B06's two bars encode only the ratio the paper
  states ("about half as many"); the Harvard list's absolute size is not drawn
  as a figure because it is not quoted.

## The one word set in capitals on screen

B03 renders **COUNTS** in capitals inside its caption. That is deliberate: the
widely circulated version of this result says "of the words", which is weaker
and wrong, and the capital is the cheapest way to make a viewer who has heard
the other version notice the difference.

## Side-by-side holds

B03 (figure vs what it measures), B05 (two kinds of misclassification) and B06
(two list sizes) are the comparison beats. B03 holds 1.4s after the headline
card and 1.6s before the clarifying panel; B05 holds 0.8s after each meaning
pair and 1.6s after the industry row; B06 holds 1.4s between the two bars.
