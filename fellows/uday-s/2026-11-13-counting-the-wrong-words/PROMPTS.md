# PROMPTS — Counting The Wrong Words

No image or video generation was used. Manim + Remotion only, so there are no
model prompts to record. GATE F requires this file; its honest content is that
nothing was generated.

## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** — the topic question

    claude "how do I do market sentiment analysis properly - what is the step
            people get wrong?"

**B09** — the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "rank the terms in my sentiment lexicon by share of total score,
            and show me the top 20 with example sentences"

The B09 prompt is deliberately a *ranking*, not an audit. Asking "is my lexicon
good?" invites an opinion; asking which terms carry the most weight produces an
artifact the viewer can read themselves — and the finding announces itself,
because if the top twenty are ordinary nouns from the domain then the score is
measuring the domain. The example sentences are what turn a suspicious word into
a confirmed one.

## What this reel does not do

It gives no trading instruction, names no instrument, and makes no prediction
claim. Both prompts are about inspecting a measurement, not about acting on one.
