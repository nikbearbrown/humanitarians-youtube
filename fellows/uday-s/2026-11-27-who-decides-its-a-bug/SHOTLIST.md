## Motion

Only the three Remotion beats are `fade` — 3 of 11 (27%), under the pantry cap.
Every Manim beat is `build`, `reveal` or `resolve`.

## Bars

B06's set draws its **empty scale track first**, so all three bars are read
against a common reference rather than against whichever is longest. Lengths are
proportional to the stated percentages and encode nothing else; each bar carries
its own number beside it.

The 100% reference is 75% — the largest value in the set — so the bars show the
shape of the funnel, not a share of some unstated whole. FACTCHECK.md records
that the paper itself is inconsistent about whether these are percentages of
test *cases* or of test *classes*, and B07 states that on screen.

## Composition

Every Manim group is sized to fit inside the 4.2-unit body band that
`fit_src()` reserves, so it scales up rather than down, and every beat lands its
citation strip by ~45% of runtime so the midpoint QC frame reaches the bottom of
the safe area.
