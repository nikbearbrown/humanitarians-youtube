## Laws observed

- **ILLUSTRATE LAW** - Claude UI only in B00, B09, B10. Every evidence beat is a
  data animation with no product chrome.
- **AUDIO-FIRST** - durations are measured MP3 lengths, never estimates.
- **4K** - Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- **No invented figures** - the one beat without numbers says so on screen, and
  no fold-gain figure appears anywhere.

## The one diagram that works by omission

B06's middle row draws a waveguide arriving at a detector and **draws nothing
after it**. The absence is the content. A struck-through onward path was the
obvious alternative and was rejected: the converged rule is never to draw a line
through a label, and an absent path states the physics more plainly than a
crossed one.

## Side-by-side holds

B03 (single pass vs resonator) and B05 (two baselines) are the comparison beats.
B03 holds 1.8s after the resonator is drawn before the explanatory line appears,
then 2.4s after the citation. B05 holds 1.5s after each result chip lands, so
both rows are on screen together before the qualifying line.
