## How the commissioning topic was handled

The brief named **energy-recycling photonic hardware** as though it were a
field. It is not — it is a label three different research efforts attract, at
three layers, measured against three different baselines. Rather than pick the
most impressive and imply it represents the rest, the reel makes the conflation
the subject and gives the viewer a way to sort any future claim.

The payoff is practical: after B02 a viewer can read a photonics press release
and say which of the three it is, which is more durable than remembering one
device's numbers.

## The risk, and what answers it

A reel that spends its falsifiability beat saying "this is not what you think"
can read as debunking real work. Three things hold it straight:

- **B03, B04 and B05 are unreservedly positive and come first.** A ~10x power
  saving on a real platform with noise held near the quantum limit is a genuine
  result, and it gets three beats before any boundary is drawn.
- **B06 names the tuning-power literature as "real work, different claim"**
  rather than dismissing it.
- **B07 refuses to inflate the category** in either direction — the other layers
  are named as real, and as unverified here.
