# PROMPTS - Three Things Called Recycling

No image or video generation was used. Manim + Remotion only, so there are no
model prompts to record. GATE F requires this file; its honest content is that
nothing was generated.

## Stills

None. No archival fetch, no `pantry/`, no Ken Burns beats. Every frame is drawn.
The racetrack resonator in B03 and the detector in B06 are drawn primitives, not
photographs or renders of real devices - they are schematics and are not
presented as either.

## Higgsfield

Not used, not installed, not offered. Fellow Tier build, entirely free: Kokoro
for narration, Manim and Remotion for picture.

## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the topic question

    claude "what does energy recycling actually mean in photonic hardware -
            and what gets recycled?"

**B09** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this photonics result: what is physically reused, does it
            happen before detection, and what baseline is the saving measured
            against?"

The B09 prompt asks three things that are each answerable from an abstract and
none of which require trusting a summary. "What is physically reused" is the
load-bearing clause - it is the question that separates a pump-light result from
a heat-recovery result, which is the conflation the whole reel exists to undo.
