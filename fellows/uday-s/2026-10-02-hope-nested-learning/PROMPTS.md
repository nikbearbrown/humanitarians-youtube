## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the topic question

    claude "is Google's HOPE a new way of training models, and does it solve
            forgetting?"

**B09** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this result: which model families are in the baseline table,
            which strong one is missing, and what does the limitations section
            concede?"

The B09 prompt has to work on a paper nobody has read yet, so it asks for three
things that are all checkable without domain knowledge: what is in the table,
what is absent from it, and what the authors themselves conceded. "Which strong
one is missing" is the load-bearing clause - it is the question that would have
caught this paper's own headline framing.
