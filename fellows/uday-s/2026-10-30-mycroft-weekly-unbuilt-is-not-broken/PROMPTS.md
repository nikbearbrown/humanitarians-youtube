## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the week's ask

    claude "725 findings. how many of those are actually wrong, rather than
            just unbuilt?"

**B03** - the ask, expanded

    claude "give findings a severity - what contradicts a claim, versus what
            is just unbuilt"

**B10** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "split these findings: which contradict a stated claim, and which
            just describe work that is not done"

The B10 prompt is one sorting operation, and it works on any tool's output
without knowing the tool. The GOOD/BAD discriminator carries the real test:
naming the claim each defect contradicts is only possible if a claim exists, so
a finding nobody can attach to a claim is a description rather than a problem.
