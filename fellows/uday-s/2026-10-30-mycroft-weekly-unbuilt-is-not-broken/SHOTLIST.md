## The composition rule this reel was built against

Three of the last four reels lost a beat to the same cause: a group that is WIDE
and SHORT is width-limited, so fit() cannot scale it up and the frame
underfills. Every beat here is composed taller than it is wide where the content
allows - B01 stacks a card over a two-panel row, B05 stacks two full-width rows,
B08 stacks three elements - and the two-column beats (B07, B09) pair panels of
comparable height.
