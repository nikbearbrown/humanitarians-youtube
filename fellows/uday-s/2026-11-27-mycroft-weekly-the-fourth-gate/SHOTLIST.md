## Composition, per the rule this series converged on

GATE V scores `cover` as the ink **bounding box** over the safe area. Two
consequences drive every frame here:

1. **The fitted body must fill its band.** `fit_src()` centres the group in a
   4.2-unit band; a group much wider than ~2.9:1 comes back height-starved and
   leaves dead bands. B05's three panels therefore use lines of **≤20
   characters**, which keeps the row near 11.5 units wide instead of 15.
2. **The citation strip must land before the midpoint sample**, because it is
   the lowest element on the frame. Every Manim beat here brings `src` in by
   ~45% of its runtime.
