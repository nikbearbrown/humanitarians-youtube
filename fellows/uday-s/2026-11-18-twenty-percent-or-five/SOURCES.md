# SOURCES — Twenty Percent, Or Five

This reel has **one** primary source. Every number on screen was read out of the
paper itself, from a locally saved PDF extracted with `pdftotext -layout` — not
from a summary, a press release, a blog post or a book about it.

## Primary

| # | Source | Access | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Macnamara, B. N., Hambrick, D. Z., & Oswald, F. L. (2014). "Deliberate Practice and Performance in Music, Games, Sports, Education, and Professions: A Meta-Analysis." *Psychological Science*, 25(8). | local PDF, `pdftotext -layout` | every figure in B03–B08 |

Specific passages used:

- **Scope** — 111 independent samples, 157 effect sizes, 11,135 participants → B03
- **Domain results** — music r = .46 (21%); games .51 (26%); sports .42 (18%);
  education .21 (4%); professions .05, *p* = .62 (<1%) → B03
- **Practice-measurement moderator** — Q(2) = 16.19, *p* < .001; retrospective
  interview r = .45 (20%); retrospective questionnaire r = .34 (12%); log method
  r = .22 (5%) → **B04**
- **The paper's own judgement** on which method it trusts: the log method
  "presumably yields more valid estimates than retrospective methods do" → B04
- **Performance-measurement moderator** — Q(3) = 14.41, *p* = .002; group
  membership r = .51 (26%); laboratory tasks r = .37 (14%); expert ratings
  r = .30 (9%); standardized objective scoring r = .28 (8%) → **B05**
- **The conclusion**, quoted verbatim with attribution: deliberate practice is
  important, but not as important as has been argued → B06
- **Open data** — the meta-analytic data set is posted publicly → B08

## Deliberately NOT used

- Any secondary retelling of this result. The popular version of it is
  "practice explains 21%, so talent is the other 79%" — which the paper does not
  say, and which B07 exists to refute on screen.
- Any AI music product, by name or otherwise. No product was tested, and no
  claim is made about any specific app's accuracy, pedagogy or results. B01 and
  B06 describe the *category* — an app that logs practice — because that is what
  maps onto a measured variable in the source.
- Any figure about learning outcomes from AI tutoring. The reel does not claim
  AI practice tools work better or worse than a teacher; it has no source for
  that and does not raise it.

## A note on what this reel is not

It is not a review, a recommendation, or a claim that practice apps do not help.
The source's own conclusion is that deliberate practice *is* important. The reel
is about an instrument-fidelity result that happens to apply directly to how
these tools measure.
