## Motion

Every Manim beat is `build`, `reveal` or `resolve`. Only the three Remotion
beats are labelled `fade` — 3 of 11 (27%), under the pantry cap.

## Composition rule applied throughout

Every Manim group is sized to fit inside the 4.2-unit body band that `fit_src()`
reserves once the citation strip is accounted for, so the group scales **up**
rather than down. This is the fix for the underfill defect: a group that is
taller than the band gets scaled to ~46% and reads small even when the gate's
bounding-box metric passes it.

## Bars

Each set draws its **empty scale track first**, so every bar is read against a
common reference that is visible before any bar appears, rather than left to be
inferred from the longest one.

Bar lengths are proportional to the stated percentages and encode nothing else.
Every bar carries its own **r** in the value text beside it, so the bar is never
the only evidence on screen. The track's full width is the 100% reference:
26% in B03 and B05, 20% in B04.
