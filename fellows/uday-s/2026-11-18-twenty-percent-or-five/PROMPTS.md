# PROMPTS — Twenty Percent, Or Five

The two composer beats, verbatim as they appear on screen.

## B00 — cold open

```
claude "how much of musical skill does practice time actually explain?"
```

Answered:
- in music: 21% of the variance
- but only 5% when practice was logged
- and the logs are the accurate method

## B09 — your turn (the scaffolded task)

```
claude "my practice app shows me this metric — what is it a percentage of, and against what was it validated?"
```

With the discriminator on screen:
- **GOOD:** validated against a measured score
- **BAD:** validated against who uses the app
- ask what the number is a percentage of

## Why the task is shaped this way

Both halves are answerable by the viewer in a few minutes with their own tool,
and both map onto a question from the framework: the denominator question is
B02's third card, and "validated against a score or against a category" is B05's
moderator in miniature — group membership gave 26%, an actual score gave 8%.

A viewer who finds their app's headline number is validated against *category
membership* has reproduced the paper's own weakest-instrument condition on their
own dashboard. That is the point of the exercise.
