## Laws observed

- **ILLUSTRATE LAW** — Claude UI only in B00, B09, B10. Every evidence beat is a
  data animation with no product chrome.
- **AUDIO-FIRST** — durations are measured MP3 lengths, never estimates.
- **4K** — Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- **NEVER THROUGH TEXT** — B01 and B06 negate phrases, and in both the cross sits
  BESIDE the phrase. Nothing is struck through.
- **NON-OPERATIONAL** — no frame contains code, a layer index, a hyperparameter,
  a model name to edit, or a repository.

## The scope panel comes before the numbers

B07 is the only beat whose source could be over-read, so its order is fixed:
the panel saying what the experiment was NOT lands first and holds 2.4s, and
only then do the two stat cards appear. Reversing that order would let the
numbers set an impression the scope then has to walk back.

## Side-by-side holds

B01 (wrong vs corrected), B03 (erase vs add) and B07 (fires vs clean) are the
comparison beats. B01 holds 1.4s after each corrected phrase; B03 holds 1.2s
after each symmetry row so both are up together; B07 holds 0.6s between the two
cards and 1.8s on the punch line.
