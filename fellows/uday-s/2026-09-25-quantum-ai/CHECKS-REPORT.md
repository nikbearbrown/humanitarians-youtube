## How the commissioning brief was handled

The brief's central claim — qubits finding patterns in large classical data
sets faster than ordinary computers — is the least supported claim in the
field, and repeating it would have made a confident, wrong video. Two of its
claims were rewritten, one reversed in direction, and one kept.

**The correction became the structure rather than a footnote.** The brief's
drug-discovery and materials-science promise survives and is given its
principled reason (B06); the forecasting promise does not, and the reel says
which question it fails. Full claim-by-claim table in FACTCHECK.md.
