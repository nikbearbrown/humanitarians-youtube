nothing was generated.

## The on-screen prompts that ARE in the reel

Written, not generated, and shown to the viewer as content inside the composer:

**B00** - the week's ask

    claude "how widespread are the recipe/script contract mismatches? give me
            a number"

**B03** - the ask, expanded

    claude "compare what each recipe documents against what its scripts
            implement - without executing them"

**B10** - the scaffolded task handed to the viewer

    claude "for this percentage: name the denominator, and tell me how many of
            those items could actually have produced the finding"

The B10 prompt is the episode compressed into one ask. "How many could actually
have produced the finding" is the load-bearing clause: it separates an item that
passed from an item that was never measured, which is the exact error that made
a universal defect look like a 4% one.
