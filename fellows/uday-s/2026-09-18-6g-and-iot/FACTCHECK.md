## The claim I deliberately weakened

An early draft of B07 said 5G's massive-IoT promise "never materialised". That
framing appears in commentary, not in a measurement, and it is a verdict on a
previous generation rather than evidence about this one. The beat now makes the
narrower claim the forecast actually supports: **in 2030, four in ten cellular
IoT connections are still projected to run on technology introduced 2015–2017.**
That is checkable, and it carries the same argumentative weight without
asserting anything about why.

## Why no speed figure appears

IMT-2030 defines peak-data-rate and latency targets, and they are the most
quotable numbers in the whole framework. They are deliberately absent. The
reel's argument is that those are not what binds an IoT deployment; putting
them on screen would both undercut the thesis and date the video the moment the
targets are revised.

## Claims deliberately NOT made

- **Nothing about spectrum bands.** Allocation is unsettled; stating it would
  present a moving target as fixed.
- **No vendor, chipset, operator or product is named.**
- **No network-energy figure.** The cost is described qualitatively because no
  authoritative single number exists for it yet.
- **No claim that 6G will succeed or fail.** The verdict is scoped strictly to
  what a person deploying IoT should do now.
