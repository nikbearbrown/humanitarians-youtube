## How the pros/cons brief was handled

The ask was "pros and cons". Two lists would have been the obvious shape and
the weakest one — nothing to stop it becoming a feature inventory. Instead the
framework comes first and both the pros (B04, B05) and the cons (B06) are
scored on the *same three axes*, so the verdict in B08 is a comparison rather
than a tally. The falsifiability beat then tests the framework itself.
