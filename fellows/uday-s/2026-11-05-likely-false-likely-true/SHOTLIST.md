## Laws observed

- **ILLUSTRATE LAW** — Claude UI only in B00, B09, B10. Every evidence beat is a
  data animation with no product chrome.
- **AUDIO-FIRST** — durations are measured MP3 lengths, never estimates.
- **4K** — Manim at 2160, Remotion at scale 2; nothing upscaled.
- **NEVER THROUGH TEXT** — B06 negates an equality, and the cross sits on the
  equals sign BETWEEN the two cards, not across either label.
- **PROPORTIONAL BARS** — B03's discovery-rate bars and B05's three bounds are
  each drawn to a constant scale within their beat.

## The direction label is not optional

B05 puts "lower means more of them are real" on screen. A false discovery rate
is the one percentage in this reel that reads backwards at a glance, and the
beat's whole point is that 8.5% is the reassuring number.

## No figure appears without its definition

B05's three bounds measure three different things, and each bar carries what it
bounds rather than sitting under one shared label. That is the episode's own
argument applied to its own graphics.

## Side-by-side holds

B01 (two titles), B04 (two thresholds) and B06 (two concepts) are the comparison
beats. B01 holds 2.0s between the titles; B04 holds 1.2s and 1.8s; B06 draws the
crossed equals between the cards and then holds 2.4s before the explanation.
