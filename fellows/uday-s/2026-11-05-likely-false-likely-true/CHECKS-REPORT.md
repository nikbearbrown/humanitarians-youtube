## Scope decision, recorded before anything was built

**Not investment advice.** This is a methodology explainer about the statistics
of published return predictors. No tradable instrument is named, no strategy is
described or recommended, and no performance figure appears anywhere. The
verdict beat's citation strip says "not investment advice" on screen.

That constraint also shaped what counts as evidence here: the reel reports
figures about *papers*, never about markets.

## The risk, and what answers it

Two risks pull opposite ways on a topic like this.

- **Cynicism.** A reel that stops after B04 would leave the viewer believing
  published finance research is worthless. B05 and B06 exist precisely to stop
  that, and they are not a footnote — they are a third of the running time.
- **Credulity.** Equally, ending on the rebuttal would imply the multiple-testing
  problem was imaginary. B07 is the counterweight: the filter both sides agree
  on is real, and the number that would settle it is the one nobody reports.

The verdict therefore scores the rubric rather than declaring a winner.
