# Sources and claim boundaries

## G — Shortcut learning in deep neural networks

Robert Geirhos et al.; 2020. [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-020-00257-z).

Supports: Shortcuts can succeed on standard tests and fail under changed conditions; generalization and evaluation.

## P — Clever Hans (The Horse of Mr. von Osten)

Oskar Pfungst; English translation by Carl L. Rahn; 1911. [Primary source](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/33936/33936-h/33936-h.htm).

Supports: Historical investigation: tapping responses depended on unintended human movement cues; original German work 1907.

## R — "Why Should I Trust You?": Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier

Marco Tulio Ribeiro, Sameer Singh and Carlos Guestrin; 2016. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04938).

Supports: Context for wolf/husky snow shortcut. New animation is explicitly illustrative, not a reproduction of measured predictions.

## L — Unmasking Clever Hans predictors and assessing what machines really learn

Sebastian Lapuschkin et al.; 2019. [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-08987-4).

Supports: Models can exploit artifacts/spurious features; evaluation and interpretation beyond accuracy.
