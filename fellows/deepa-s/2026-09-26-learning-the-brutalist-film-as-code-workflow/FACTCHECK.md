# Fact-check notes

## Supported by local Brutalist materials

- Brutalist is presented as a local toolkit/source tree with builders and setup checks, rather than a conventional app installer. The local `setup` script is documented as a dependency readiness doctor.
- The beat sheet is central: it links narration, beats, visuals, and production; narration/audio timings act as the clock and beats are individual production slots. A successful render is not itself human approval.
- Landscape and portrait are separate compositions; the fellow workflow explicitly calls for a separately finished native portrait sheet.
- AI Explainer defines a workflow for structuring an educational technical explanation; its visual direction calls for explanatory visuals and purposeful interface use, not repetitive UI filler.
- CLI Explainer specifies a narrative sequence while tool/interface selection is a distinct skin choice. The narration compresses that workflow into the user-requested sequence.
- Deep Explainer is intended for multi-act topics with several linked mechanisms; length alone is not the criterion.
- Duration Planner treats duration as content-derived, and the audio-first pipeline measures actual narration duration.
- Evidence-led visuals, automated technical checks, and human editorial review are distinct parts of the production approach.
