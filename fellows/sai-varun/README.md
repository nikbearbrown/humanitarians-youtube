# The Uncertain Eye
### A Science-and-AI Documentary Series on catching glaucoma with machine learning

Building an AI to detect glaucoma from eye scans — the real research journey, from
an ambitious two-tier design, through a hard lesson in data rigor, to an honest rebuild.

**Creator:** Sai Varun Garimella Narasimha — Humanitarians AI · Northeastern University

**Watch all episodes here:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1TzKe2x5TIthRWy5crOgbTJjbfhUQ7xa0?usp=drive_link

---

## Episodes

1. **The Silent Thief** — Why a 2,400-year-old disease still causes irreversible blindness, and why catching it is a detection problem.
2. **Turning a Nerve into Data** — From the 1850 ophthalmoscope to OCT, and the Harvard-GDP dataset our work is built on.
3. **Learning from Scraps** — Training a classifier with only 350 labels using semi-supervised learning, and how AUROC actually measures it.
4. **The Uncertain Eye** — Turning the model's own doubt into a triage signal.
5. **A Council of Specialists** — A multi-agent, vision-language reasoning system for the hard cases.
6. **Too Good to Be True** — When peer feedback challenged the results and made the science stronger.
7. **The Trap in the Data** — Discovering data leakage, and keeping the hard problem honest.
8. **The Honest Machine** — The rigorous rebuild, and where the research goes next.

---

## About the project
An investigation into automated glaucoma screening from OCT retinal-nerve-fiber-layer
scans, using the Harvard Glaucoma Detection and Progression (Harvard-GDP) dataset.
Ongoing research at Humanitarians AI and Northeastern University.
