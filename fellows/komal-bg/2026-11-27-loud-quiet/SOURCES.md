## Rails spoken on film

1. **Karen Millen via Robb Report, 20 Apr 2023.**
   https://robbreport.com/lifestyle/news/quiet-luxury-stealth-wealth-google-1234833256/
   Monthly Google searches after *Succession* S4: “quiet luxury” 6,100;
   “stealth wealth” 2,200; “old-money style” 25,000 (up from 3,400 a year
   earlier). Quiet luxury +373% and stealth wealth +334% **in the past
   month**; old-money style +568% **over the past year**. Do not mix those
   windows on one percent chart. The film charts monthly volumes and speaks
   the 373% with its month window.

2. **Bain & Company / Fondazione Altagamma, 18 Nov 2026.**
   https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2026/global-luxury-holds-steady-at-1.5-trillion-as-experiences-and-new-consumer-priorities-reshape-growth-bain-company

   315 million in 2026. Global luxury spending ~€1.5 trillion in 2026
