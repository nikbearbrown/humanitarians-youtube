## What this video is about

Quiet luxury hid the logo. The comments did not. Searches spiked in 2023;
they are not sales. Bain’s personal-luxury shopper count shrank from 330
million (2025) to about 315 million (2026) while the talk stayed. AI
sentiment lets a brand read reputation at a scale a person cannot. **A
positive comment is not proof the brand is still quiet.**

Body plates are drawn charts, not photographs.
