Corrections applied under DOUBLE-CHECK LAW are logged at the bottom.

## Primary

1. **Directive (EU) 2024/825** — Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition
   (EmpCo), amending Directives 2005/29/EC and 2011/83/EU.
   - Official Journal / EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202400825
   - Adopted 28 February 2024; published ~6 March 2024.
   - Transposition deadline: **27 March 2026**.
   - Application / enforcement: **27 September 2026**.

2. **European Commission FAQs** — Empowering consumers for the green transition
   (practical guidance on environmental claims / labels).
   - https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/3c257883-bb2a-4dd9-a6dc-501d587bb34f_en

## DOUBLE-CHECK corrections applied in narration

| Temptation | Correction |
|---|---|
| Call the live rule “the Green Claims Directive” | Distinguish: **EmpCo 2024/825 is adopted**; Green Claims proposal COM(2023)166 **withdrawn June 2025**. |
| Invent a luxury brand fine or named scandal as EmpCo precedent | None used — EmpCo enforcement starts Sept 2026; older greenwashing cases under prior law are out of scope for this reel. |
| Quote “4% of turnover” as an EU-wide EmpCo fine ceiling | Softened / omitted — penalties are **member-state discretion**; 4% appears in secondary summaries, not asserted as a uniform EU maximum on screen. |
| Claim the withdrawn Green Claims Directive still applies | Explicitly denied in B01 / verdict. |
| Invent product-level luxury claim quotes as real brand copy | Examples on screen are **illustrative category language** (“eco leather”, “climate positive collection”), not attributed to a named maison. |
