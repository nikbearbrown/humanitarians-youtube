## B02 · ROLES

A virtual influencer is different: an ongoing persona with a feed and a calendar. Human talent is a third machine: a person who can refuse, age, and walk away. Those are not synonyms.

## B05 · LUXURY
Luxury sold craft, scarcity, and a human witness — someone who stood next to the object. Virtual influencers already walked in. Lil Miquela, a CGI character launched in twenty sixteen, took over Prada's Instagram at Milan Fashion Week, and appeared with Bella Hadid for Calvin Klein. The shift: desire used to need a person you might meet. Now it can be aimed at a file the brand owns.

## B06 · VERDICT
Let's recap with Claude. An AI model is inventory. A virtual influencer is a persona on a timer. Luxury is the industry that told you the human was the point. If the face is owned, the object might still be crafted — but the proof of presence is gone. Ask who can refuse the picture. If nobody can, you are not looking at a person. You are looking at control.
