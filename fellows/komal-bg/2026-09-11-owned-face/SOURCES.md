## Campaign AI models (generated catalog bodies)

- **Levi Strauss & Co. / Lalaland.ai test (announced March 2023).** Levi’s said it would test AI-generated models from Amsterdam studio Lalaland.ai to supplement — not replace — human models on e-commerce, showing more body types per product. After backlash (jobs; “diversity via AI”), Levi’s clarified the pilot was **not** its DEI strategy and that live shoots would continue.
  - The Verge, 27 Mar 2023, “Levi’s will test AI-generated clothing models to ‘increase diversity’”
  - WWD / Sourcing Journal, 22 Mar 2023
  - Associated Press / TechXplore, Apr 2024 follow-up on the clarification

On-screen rebuild is a **mannequin + LOOK tiles**, captioned “Redrawn (simplified) from the Levi’s / Lalaland.ai catalog test, announced 2023.” No generated faces of people. No invented body-count metrics.
