SurveyMind

Last time, we decided to build our own fake survey data from scratch, since
only the research paper was on GitHub and not the actual data it describes.
Over the last two weeks, I tried one more time to follow the paper's method,
but kept running into problems. Since then, I've been reading up on how to
build this myself instead.

Four papers looked at before starting:
- "DeepPersona" tests fake survey-takers against the same real personality
  dataset we're using, with similar math to check how close they are.
- "Delving Into the Psychology of Machines" talks about earlier work where
  researchers used ChatGPT to create thousands of fake personality profiles
  and checked if they held up statistically.
- "Evaluating LLMs for Synthetic Personas Generation" has an AI first answer
  a personality quiz, then write a short bio based on the answers.
- "Big5PersonalityEssays" a fake dataset made by ChatGPT, checked to make
  sure the personality traits were balanced and realistic.

Current experimentation: split real data in two — use 80% to build the
fake-data generator, and keep the other 20% completely separate to test it
afterward. That way, we're checking if it actually works on new data, not
just copying the answer key. This plan has been sent to Gru for review.
