## Three objects (definitional, B04)

- **Score:** HypeAuditor Audience Quality Score. A 1–100 composite.
- **Share:** Modash credibility / fake-follower estimate. How much of the
  room looks real (e.g. 0.85 ≈ 85%).
- **Flag:** CreatorIQ Creator Integrity Quotient. Patterns to review, not a
  guilty stamp.

## Rails

- **HypeAuditor Audience Quality Score (AQS).** Proprietary 1–100 benchmark.
  Help Center (Tanya, 21 Jun 2022): AQS is the result of 15 metrics in 4
  categories — (1) Engagement Rate, (2) Quality Audience (% real people),
  (3) Followers and Followings growth patterns, (4) Comments Authenticity
  (% of recent comments not from engagement pods). **Do not pick on AQS
  alone.** Vendor framing; spoken as their help file, not as our census.
  https://help.hypeauditor.com/en/articles/2221731-what-is-aqs-audience-quality-score-and-how-is-it-calculated
- **Modash — Fake followers 101 (Donna).** Fake-follower / credibility
  scoring is **Instagram-only**. Uses a network-graph model (empty pictures,
  odd follow ratios, account age, posts, bios, growth). Private follower
  lists cannot be scored (the field goes blank). Perfect 100% real is
  unusual; their guide aims under ~25% fake and treats over ~50% as avoid.
  Thresholds **not spoken as our rule**. 0.85 on film is a teaching number.
  https://help.modash.io/en/articles/5759268-fake-followers-101
- **CreatorIQ — Creator Integrity Quotient.** Flags suspicious patterns in
  follower growth, audience location, and engagement versus benchmarks. The
  vendor’s own paper / blog: the report does **not** definitively identify
  fraud; a person still reviews. More flags, more likely — spoken as their
  caveat, not as a detection rate.
  https://www.creatoriq.com/blog/spotting-follower-fraud
  (Integrity Quotient whitepaper / product pages as supporting; do not
  treat vendor compare-tables vs HypeAuditor or Modash as independent.)
