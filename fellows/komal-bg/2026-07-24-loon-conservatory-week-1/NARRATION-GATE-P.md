## B01 · FOUNDATION
The foundation is unglamorous: find enough bird images, legally. Komal is pulling from Unsplash, Pexels, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. All three are free to use. None of them are free in the same way — and a model trained on the wrong image is a problem you inherit much later.

## B02 · LEDGER
Every download gets a row in a spreadsheet: the link, the photographer, the source, and whether the image is free or licensed. That ledger is doing more work than it looks like. It is the difference between a dataset you can publish and one you have to throw away.

## B03 · RESEARCH
Before the team built anything, Komal wrote the research: the species, the habitat, the threats, the conservation status and the legal protections. That brief is not background reading. It is where the annotation fields come from. And the hoped-for beneficiary is a real place — the National Loon Center Foundation in Minnesota.
