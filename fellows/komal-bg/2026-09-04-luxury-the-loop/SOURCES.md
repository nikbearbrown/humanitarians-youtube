# SOURCES — The Loop. (`claude-liam-luxury-the-loop`)

Primary reporting and law used to author the beat sheet. DOUBLE-CHECK corrections at the bottom.

## Primary

1. **Bain & Company + eBay press release** (25 June 2025, Milan)
   *Digital product passports could double fashion products' lifetime value – with consumers reaping the rewards*
   https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/20252/digital-product-passports-could-double-fashion-products-lifetime-value--with-consumers-reaping-the-rewards/
   - DPP as a commercial opportunity, not only compliance
   - Potential doubling of lifetime product value; up to 65% of gains to consumers
   - Illustrative: a £500 item could generate an additional £500 in resale and services with a DPP
   - ~90% of brands surveyed currently view DPPs primarily as a regulatory burden
   - ESPR: nearly every physical product sold in the EU will require a DPP by 2030
   - **Correction applied:** press copy says textiles DPPs become mandatory from 2026. Commission timeline below supersedes that date.

2. **Bain & Company insight** — *Digital Product Passports Introduce New Sources of Value*
   https://www.bain.com/insights/digital-product-passports-introduce-new-sources-of-value/
   - DPP as a digital record of origin, materials, impact, and end-of-life
   - Staged EU milestones (registry / category acts / 2030 coverage) — used as context, not spoken as a 2026 textile mandate

3. **European Commission — Textile Apparel and the Digital Product Passport**
   https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/textile-apparel_en
   - Textiles are a priority product group under the ESPR Working Plan 2025–2030
   - Textile-specific DPP requirements come in a future delegated act
   - Indicative timeline: **Q4 2027** planned adoption of the ESPR Delegated Act for textiles
   - DPP accessible online and via a data carrier (e.g. QR) on the product
   - Information may include identification, fibre composition, repair/maintenance, reuse/resale/recycling, origin

4. **Vestiaire Collective — Trusted Pre-Loved Luxury Authentication Standards**
   https://www.vestiairecollective.com/journal/trust-expert-authentication/
   - 150+ experts in quality control and digital/physical authentication
   - Hubs: US, UK, France, Hong Kong, Singapore
   - Machine-learning algorithms, authentication technology, and AI systems in the verification stack
   - In 2024, roughly **10%** of listed items were rejected during digital verification, including poor image quality and item condition
