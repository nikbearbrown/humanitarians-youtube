## B02 · LINEAR
The old luxury chain is a straight line: design, cut, sell, forget. Circularity bends that line into a loop. Houses love the loop on a slide. They hate the operational question: who proves this jacket is real, and who captures the second sale?

## B03 · IDENTITY
Here's the bottleneck. A used bag without a receipt is a rumor. Counterfeits poison the resale price of the real thing. Human authenticators cannot inspect every listing. So the loop stalls at trust. Until the object can identify itself, circular is a donation bin with better lighting.
