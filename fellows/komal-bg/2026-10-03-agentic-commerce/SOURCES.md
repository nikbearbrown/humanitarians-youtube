## Three machines (definitional, B02)

- **Chatbot:** answers. Human still completes checkout.
- **Recommender / shopping assistant:** ranks. Human still clicks buy.
- **Agent:** searches, compares, and can complete a purchase under permission
  and a scoped payment token. If it cannot complete (or at least initiate)
  checkout, the film treats it as still a chatbot.

OpenAI's Operator / ChatGPT agent is trained to ask before consequential
actions such as purchases (OpenAI, “Introducing ChatGPT agent”). That hedge
is why the film says **can** complete, not “always buys unsupervised.”

## Rails (B03 / B05)

- **Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP).** OpenAI + Stripe, announced 29 Sep 2025
  with Instant Checkout in ChatGPT (US users, Etsy first). Open standard so
  an agent and a merchant can complete a purchase; merchant remains merchant
  of record. Instant Checkout later shifted toward merchant-owned checkout
  UX; the protocol is the claim, not a permanent in-chat button.
  https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/
  https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout
- **Visa Intelligent Commerce.** Visa Payments Forum, Paris, 2 July 2026:
  live agentic commerce transactions in Europe; AI agents purchasing at
  independent merchant sites on behalf of cardholders, within user controls.
  Participating merchants named by Visa include lastminute.com, Frasers,
  Cleverbridge, BrickDepot — **not** claimed as luxury houses.
  https://www.visa.co.uk/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.3457328.html
- **Mastercard Agent Pay** (2025–2026): registered agents, agentic tokens,
  intent/consent. Adjacent; not the on-film worked example.
  https://www.mastercard.com/europe/en/business/artificial-intelligence/mastercard-agent-pay.html
- **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP).** Shopify + Google, NRF 2026: agents
  discover, cart, checkout. Adjacent; not named on film to keep one rails story.
  https://shopify.engineering/UCP
