## What this video is about

Luxury used to sell to a person at the door. **Agentic commerce** lets an
AI agent search, compare, and complete a purchase on a client's behalf.
A chatbot answers; a recommender ranks; an agent can check out. Distinct
from sibling reels: *The Persona File.* (the house, speaking) and
*The Ruler.* (exclusivity as rank). Worked example: **Visa Intelligent
Commerce** live agent purchases in Europe, July 2026 — plus the
**Agentic Commerce Protocol** (OpenAI / Stripe).
