## B02 · THE PILE
Why this is live now. A single study can run hundreds of cameras for months. That is millions of images. Most of them are empty — wind, leaves, a temperature swing. A trained reviewer can clear a few hundred frames an hour. A million-image pile is weeks before any science starts.

## B03 · THE QUEUE
What it changed for operations. The first job is not naming the animal. It is skipping the blanks. Filter the empties, and the review queue shrinks by about an order of magnitude. Less clicking. More conservation.

## B04 · NAMES
Start with names. Detect: is there an animal, a person, a vehicle. Classify: which species. Identify: which individual — that is Wildbook, a different machine, already on this channel. Those are not synonyms.

## B05 · EXAMPLE
Worked example. MegaDetector. Open-source, from Microsoft's AI for Good Lab. It boxes animals, people, and vehicles in camera-trap images. It does not name the species. You set a threshold, skip the blanks, and a person still reviews. Always test it on your own data.
