# PROMPTS — The Empty Frame.

Handoff prompt (B07, typed on screen and read aloud):

> You have a folder from a field you actually know — camera-trap frames, drone stills, or a conservatory backlog. Explain (1) whether the system in front of you is detecting, classifying, or identifying, (2) what share of the pile is empty and who still reviews, and (3) what a missed animal would cost in that field.

B02 / B03 stills are generated illustrations of an empty trap frame and a
review queue. B05 is a public-domain USFWS trail-camera photograph with a
teaching overlay (detect, not species). Portrait 9:16: `*916` Remotion twins
plus `pantry/B0X-916.png`.
