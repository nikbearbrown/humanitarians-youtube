## What this video is about

**Brand marketing is not buying a follower count. It is buying a room.** AI
scores whether that crowd is real — growth patterns, engagement quality,
demographics — before a brand pays an influencer or ambassador. Worked
example: **HypeAuditor Audience Quality Score**, 1 to 100. Their own help
file says: do not pick on the score alone. A hundred is not a contract.
Distinct from *The Owned Face.* (the spokesperson, not the crowd).

Body stills: a hockey-stick growth printout, a comments thread (emoji pile vs
one real sentence), a 1–100 audit page stamped **not a contract**. Claude UI
only at bookends.
