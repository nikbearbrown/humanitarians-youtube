## Three checks (definitional, B04)

- **Growth:** follower / following graphs. Organic rooms look like weather.
  Bought rooms look like a pour — hockey-stick spikes. HypeAuditor flags
  abnormal activity on those graphs.
- **Engagement:** not a like count. Comment authenticity, pods, emoji piles,
  tag-to-win inflation. Real comments argue and ask.
- **Demographics:** estimated age / gender / location / language / interests
  from public profile and interaction data. Sits **beside** AQS for brand fit.
  A real room may still be the wrong room.

## Rails

- **HypeAuditor Audience Quality Score (AQS).** Proprietary 1–100 benchmark.
  Help Center (Tanya, 21 Jun 2022): AQS is the result of 15 metrics in 4
  categories — (1) Engagement Rate, (2) Quality Audience (% real people),
  (3) Followers and Followings growth patterns, (4) Comments Authenticity
  (% of recent comments not from engagement pods). **Do not pick on AQS
  alone.** AQS 100 likely high-quality; AQS 20 low / little sales impact —
  vendor framing; spoken as their help file, not as our census.
  https://help.hypeauditor.com/en/articles/2221731-what-is-aqs-audience-quality-score-and-how-is-it-calculated
- **Four components (blog).** Engagement rate; quality audience (real /
  influencers / mass followers / suspicious); follower & following growth;
  engagement authenticity (pods, tag-to-win). Like-to-comment ratio and
  likes spread as supporting tells — not spoken as formulas.
  https://blog.hypeauditor.com/what-is-audience-quality-score-on-hypeauditor/
- **Demographics (AI features blog).** ML / computer vision on bios, names,
  posts to estimate gender, location, age; language detection; interests from
  interactions. Report layer, not a fourth AQS bucket in the help file.
  https://blog.hypeauditor.com/uncovering-the-ai-capabilities-of-hypeauditor/
- **Suspicious accounts.** Bots and people using purchase/automation
  services. Decision-tree ML; vendor error-rate claims **not spoken**.
  https://help.hypeauditor.com/en/articles/2221742-what-are-suspicious-accounts
- **TikTok AQS** uses a different mix (30-day growth, engagement, comment
  rates). Not this week’s worked example. Do not collapse IG and TikTok
  formulas on film.
