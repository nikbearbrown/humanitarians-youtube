# SOURCES — Quiet Tech. (`claude-liam-luxury-quiet-tech`)

Primary reporting used to author the beat sheet. DOUBLE-CHECK corrections at the bottom.

## Primary

1. **Google — “How LVMH is using Google Cloud for its ‘quiet tech’”** (9 June 2025)
   - https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/lvmh/
   - Franck Le Moal (CIO, LVMH): one-to-one interaction; data+AI “seamless and invisible”; more dedicated attention without being intrusive.
   - Tiffany & Co. advisors: AI-powered chat on client lists — tastes, suitable items the client might not have known about.
   - Also names Louis Vuitton and Sephora as early advisor work.
   - Scale: **more than 40,000 users per month**, **more than 1.5 million monthly queries** on internal gen-AI systems.

2. **Google Cloud Transform — Le Moal interview** (9 June 2025)
   - https://cloud.google.com/transform/lvmh-data-ai-platform-interview-franck-le-moal-luxury-gen-ai-louis-vuitton-sephora-dom-perignon
   - 75 maisons; empower the **client advisor**, not replace them.
   - Data platform core 2021–2022; Vertex AI custom agents.
   - Maison firewalls on customer data; shared benchmarks on finance/HR/supply chain.
   - High-end jewelry allocation: few pieces in the world → choose stores (e.g. Zurich) then notify the right client.
   - Same 40k / 1.5M figures.

3. **Retail Technology — “NRF 2026: LVMH puts quiet tech to work”** (15 January 2026)
   - https://www.retailtechnology.co.uk/news/8604/nrf-2026:-lvmh-puts-quiet-tech-to-work/
   - Gonzague de Pirey: “everywhere but visible nowhere”; gimmicks fail to scale; desire as the key for success.
   - Soumia Hadjali (Louis Vuitton): “AI never replaces the creativity, and it amplifies it”; augmentation not automation; continuous, context-rich client story.
