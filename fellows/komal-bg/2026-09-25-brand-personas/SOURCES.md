## Three machines (definitional, B02)

- **Brand personality** is the adjective page (tone, traits). Mark & Pearson’s
  twelve-seat wheel is a later marketing map of Jung — already covered in
  *Archetype Engine.* / *The Ruler.* This cut does not re-teach the wheel.
- **Campaign face** is a generated look for a still. Distinct from a persona.
  Already covered in *The Owned Face.* (Levi’s / Lalaland.ai; Lil Miquela).
  This cut names the split; it does not rerun those examples.
- **AI brand persona** is the operational character: a job, a never-say list,
  proof it always cites, and the ability to answer in the house’s voice.

## Why now (interpretation, labeled as such)

- Volume: brands now speak on more channels than a copy desk can staff.
- Consistency: generic LLM defaults collapse toward the same “helpful assistant”
  voice unless a persona file is encoded (voice brief + examples + bans).
- Test-before-ship: run a draft through the persona and flag costume copy
  before it publishes.
- Not a claim that unsupervised AI should post live.

## Worked example — L'Oréal Beauty Genius (CES 2024)

- L'Oréal CEO Nicolas Hieronimus demonstrated **Beauty Genius** in the company’s
  CES 2024 keynote (Las Vegas, January 2024): a branded virtual beauty advisor
  that diagnoses, recommends products, and supports virtual try-on.
  Source: L'Oréal, “L’Oréal at CES 2024”
  https://www.loreal.com/en/news/research-innovation/ces/
- Trade coverage: MM+M, “L’Oréal showcases AI-powered advisor Beauty Genius
  and more at CES” — on-stage demo (jet-lag / skin photo / product recs);
  trained on products and in-store advisor conversations.
  https://www.mmm-online.com/home/channel/loreal-ai-powered-advisor-beauty-genius-ces/
- Consumer product page (L'Oréal Paris): conversational tool combining gen AI,
  AR, and house expertise; personalized diagnostics and recommendations from
  the L'Oréal Paris catalog.
  https://www.lorealparisusa.com/beauty-genius-ai-beauty-virtual-assistant
- Diginomica CES 2024 write-up quotes Hieronimus: not “a chatbot” in the
  generic sense — a personalized AI advisor. Film uses “not a generic chatbot”
  as Teardown, not a legal distinction.
  https://diginomica.com/ces-2024-gen-ai-adds-loreals-digital-transformation-in-beauty-tech-revolution
