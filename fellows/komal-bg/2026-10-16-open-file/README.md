## What this video is about

**Knowing where it was made is not a louder logo.** It is a file you can
check. A logo is a public signal of rank. Authenticity is a certificate.
Provenance is an audit trail. Those are not the same machine. Rails:
**Aura** (LVMH, Prada, Cartier, 20 April 2021) and the **EU Digital Product
Passport** (batteries mandatory 18 February 2027; textiles delegated act
planned Q4 2027 — not live on fashion yet). Distinct from sibling reels:
*Sustainable, Prove It.* (EmpCo claim-law) and *Quiet Tech.* (in-store AI).
Worked example: **Prada Re-Nylon for SEA BEYOND** — NFC tap opens a
brand-controlled digital product passport. Not the EU textile act.
