Internal research for the cut. **Not spoken or shown** unless the beat claims it.

## Three machines (definitional, B02)

- **Logo / brand prominence:** a public mark of rank that other people can
  see. Academic rail (not named on film): Han, Nunes & Drèze (2010),
  “Signaling Status with Luxury Goods: The Role of Brand Prominence,”
  *Journal of Marketing* 74(4). Loud vs quiet marks; status signaling.
  https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkg.74.4.015
- **Authenticity:** is this the real object. A certificate tied to a unique
  identifier (NFC, serial). Answers counterfeit, not “who sewed it.”
- **Provenance:** where, how, and who made it. A file (passport, disclosure,
  supplier list). If other people cannot see it, it cannot replace a logo as
  a *status symbol* — it can replace it as a *trust instrument*.

## Rails (B03 / B04)

- **Aura Blockchain Consortium.** Founded 20 April 2021 by LVMH, Prada Group,
  and Cartier (Richemont). Non-profit; open to luxury brands. Product history,
  proof of authenticity, claimed lifecycle, in a secure digital format.
  Microsoft / ConsenSys at founding. Later site copy also lists OTB among
  founding groups — film speaks the 2021 three.
  https://www.pradagroup.com/en/news-media/press-releases-documents/2021/21-04-20-aura-blockchain-consortium.html
  https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/cartier-partners-with-lvmh-and-prada-group-to-form-the-aura-blockchain-consortium/
  https://auraconsortium.com/
- **EU Digital Product Passport.** Horizontal system under Ecodesign for
  Sustainable Products Regulation (EU) 2024/1781. First mandatory group:
  certain batteries, **18 February 2027**, under Batteries Regulation
  (EU) 2023/1542 Art. 77 (QR → unique identifier) — not an ESPR textile act.
  https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en
  https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/batteries_en
- **Textile / apparel DPP.** Priority under ESPR Working Plan 2025–2030.
  Commission page: **Q4 2027 planned adoption** of the delegated act;
  application date not set. Film says “not mandatory on fashion yet.”
  https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/textile-apparel_en
- **Fashion Transparency Index 2023** (Fashion Revolution). 250 largest
  brands; overall average **26%**. Gucci **80%** (second overall; first luxury
  in the top tier). 2024 published a climate-only special edition (*What
  Fuels Fashion?*) — do not mix those scores into this cut.
  https://www.fashionrevolution.org/fashion-transparency-index-2023/

## Worked example (B05)

- **Prada Re-Nylon for SEA BEYOND.** Aura member news, 14 May 2026: each
  piece has an embedded NFC tag; Aura DPP with origin, components, water,
  energy, carbon. Claiming the Certificate of Ownership through the DPP
  unlocks complimentary repair (terms vary by market — **not spoken**).
  https://auraconsortium.com/insights/prada-re-nylon-sea-beyond
- **Prada authenticity FAQ.** From March 2026, **a selection** of Re-Nylon
  for Sea Beyond products can request a Digital Certificate of Ownership by
  tapping the NFC in the internal label. Transferability of the certificate
  **differs by market page** — not spoken.
  https://www.prada.com/ww/en/info/faqs/authenticity.html

## What this reel does not claim

- That transparency *has* replaced the logo as the status symbol (the
  question; answered as Teardown: public mark vs private file).
- That a maison DPP is compliance with the future EU textile act.
- That Aura publishes factory lists (Fashion Revolution still scores
  public disclosure separately).
- EmpCo / Green Claims enforcement (already *Sustainable, Prove It.*).
- Named repair-year terms, transferability, or passport issuance counts.
- Louis Vuitton FTI sub-scores from secondary news (not used).
