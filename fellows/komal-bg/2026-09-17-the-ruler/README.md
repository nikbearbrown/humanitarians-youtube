## What this video is about

The **Ruler** archetype wants control against chaos. Luxury brands and luxury
personas sit there when the offer is rank, closed access, and a standard that
behaves like law — not merely a high price. Worked example: **American Express**.
Not every luxury house: Lover seduces, Magician transforms.
