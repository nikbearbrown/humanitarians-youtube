# Medhavy walkthroughs

Film-as-code records for the Medhavy Hub walkthrough series: one reel per textbook,
from the student's side (sign in at hub.medhavy.com → open the book → the book's own
chapter sidebar, search, "Ask this textbook" AI tutor with sources and memory, night
mode), plus one admin-panel reel. Built with the `medhavy-walkthrough` skill from
[brutalist.art](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/brutalist.art); a copy of the skill as
used is in `skill-medhavy-walkthrough/` so each reel is reproducible from here.

| Folder | Film | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-18-cancer-textbook-walkthrough/` | Cancer Biology and Therapeutics: A Textbook That Answers Back | 3:50 |
| `2026-09-17-hub-admin-walkthrough/` | Medhavy Hub: One Sign-In, Every Textbook (admin panel) | 3:01 |

What is here: `beat_sheet.json`, `coverage.json` (the evidence contract), `CAPTURE.md`
(tenant, privacy, method), `capture/` (the raw 4K browser recordings, action logs,
redaction log, per-tab screenshots), `RIFF.md`, `FACTCHECK.md`, `SHOTLIST.md`,
`PROMPTS.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `BUILD-PROMPT.md`, `TYPECHECK.md`, `_qc/REPORT.md`.

Not here (per the repo rule): narration mp3/wav, cut clips, review cuts, final masters.
The raw captures are kept as source evidence at Bear's request; every email, person name
and invite code in them was masked in the page before recording.

Privacy: production tenant, redacted-live. No credentials were typed by the agent; sign-in
pages are shown signed out. The signed-in session was Bear's own admin account.

## Frictional log

Every work subfolder here carries its own `FRICTIONAL.md` — a dated record of the process
behind that specific piece of work, kept beside the evidence it describes: what was tried
and expected, where it resisted and what was done next, what Claude or another person
contributed and what was accepted, changed or rejected, and what is now understood or
still open. Append as you go; never rewrite an earlier entry. It is not graded and not a
performance review. See <https://www.humanitarians.ai/fellows> for what an entry contains.
