# Cancer Biology and Therapeutics: A Textbook That Answers Back

Here is the correction. This is not a chatbot bolted onto a PDF. Medhavy knows which book you are in, which chapter you are on, what you have already asked, and which course handed you the book. Every answer is pulled from that context, and it lists the pages it used. Let us walk in the way a student would.

What I saw work: sign-in through the hub, one tab per book, chapters with figures and sources, search, and a tutor that answers from the chapter, shows its top sources, and remembers the thread. What to watch: in one answer the listed sources wandered into angiogenesis sections, so read the sources, not just the answer. Not shown: the student dashboard counts, because I am on an admin account, and the instructor tools.
