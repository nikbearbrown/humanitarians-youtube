# Project: Quantifying Uncertainty and Confidence in Environmental ESG Analysis Using AI

## Paper Review

| Week | Dates | Paper reviewed | Hours | Main work completed | Friction / challenges |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 13–18 | The Ambiguities of Uncertainty — Bevan (2022) | 11 | Explored environmental uncertainty frameworks; reviewed 156 frameworks; compared definitions and terminology; developed initial uncertainty mind map; summarized findings and implications for AI. | Found that uncertainty terminology overlaps across disciplines. Struggled to create a clean classification and realized uncertainty cannot necessarily be represented by one confidence score. |
| 2 | Oct 19–25 | Identifying Uncertainty in Environmental Risk Assessments (2014) | 11 | Reviewed the environmental uncertainty typology; studied data, language, system, extrapolation, variability, model, and decision uncertainty; mapped categories to environmental reporting examples. | Several uncertainty categories can occur simultaneously. Found it difficult to classify real-world environmental limitations into one category and began considering multidimensional uncertainty. |
| 3 | Oct 26–Nov 01 | Where Do Uncertainties Reside Within Environmental Risk Assessments? — Skinner et al. (2017) | 11 | Examined UnISERA; studied uncertainty identification, assessment, and prioritization; explored how limitations affect risk conclusions; developed examples using missing measurements and assumptions. | Realized identifying uncertainty is not enough. Struggled with how to determine whether an uncertainty materially affects the final conclusion and how AI could prioritize limitations. |
| 4 | Nov 02–08 | This Is What We Don't Know — Sahlin et al. (2021) | 11 | Studied epistemic uncertainty and Bayesian networks; explored uncertainty arising from data, assumptions, models, and expert knowledge; connected evidence reliability to AI conclusions. | Bayesian concepts were challenging. Had difficulty separating confidence in an outcome from the quality and completeness of the underlying evidence. |
| 5 | Nov 09–15 | Towards a Comprehensive Uncertainty Assessment in Environmental Research and Decision Support — Reichert (2020) | 11 | Reviewed uncertainty propagation, model structures, environmental decision support, and the influence of uncertainty throughout assessment processes; developed environmental examples. | Found it challenging to understand how uncertainty accumulates across stages. Began questioning whether AI should communicate uncertainty only in its final answer or throughout the analytical process. |
| 6 | Nov 16–22 | A Survey of Uncertainty in Deep Neural Networks — Abdar et al. (2021) | 12 | Reviewed AI uncertainty quantification; explored Bayesian approaches, ensemble methods, epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty, and AI confidence; compared AI concepts with environmental uncertainty literature. | Technical AI terminology required additional study. Realized AI-generated confidence may not reflect the reliability of environmental evidence and that uncertainty estimates require appropriate evaluation. |
| **Total** | | **Six papers reviewed** | **67** | Research progression from uncertainty fundamentals to AI uncertainty quantification. | |
