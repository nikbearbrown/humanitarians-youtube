## Decisions and corrections logged during authoring

1. **Voice: `af_bella` (Bella), not `am_onyx`.** Bella is this fellow's existing
   series voice (`banking-domain-sql-running-total-bug`,
   `banking-domain-star-method-behavioral`). Onyx would arm IN-FOR-BEAR LAW and
   rewrite B00 to "Liam, in for Bear" — wrong for a HAI fellow report.
2. **`CLAUDE-BRAND.md` vs `ai-explainer/SKILL.md` conflict — followed the SKILL.**
   `CLAUDE-BRAND.md`'s channel table lists `claude-hai → am_onyx`;
   `skills/make/ai-explainer/SKILL.md` (the brutalist edition, whose header states
   that inherited full-fat text does not apply) lists `claude-hai → af_bella`. The
   SKILL governs, and it agrees with `fellows/README.md`.
3. **`playlist: "Fellows Research"`, not `"HAI"`.** `"HAI"` appears in zero beat
   sheets in either repo. `Fellows Research` is used by 12 fellow beat sheets and is
   named in `ai-explainer/SKILL.md` as the HAI how-to series (vs `Profiles`).
4. **Greeting `"Hi, Supriya"`.** The HAI word budget permits only the shortest cue
   forms (Hi · Ola · Hej · Ciao). "Hi" was chosen so the greeting and the mandatory
   verbatim intro line agree. The Wagwan mod-10 rule does not apply — Wagwan is
   Bear's alone.
5. **The equation tangent was considered and rejected.** `EQUATIONS.md` governs
   beats where an equation appears. Selectivity *could* have been formalised as
   `rows_read = |T| × σ`, but a tangent is a 30–45 s beat group and this is a
   one-insight reel whose insight is not mathematical — it is about what an index
   can and cannot be applied to. Numbers are presented as measured counts, so
   `EQUATIONS.md`'s typesetting rule (real math, not mono; mono reserved for data
   numerals) is satisfied trivially: there is no equation to typeset.
6. **B01's isotype is scaled 1 dot = 1,000 rows, declared on screen.** The
   component renders one dot per unit, so a literal 400,000 is not renderable.
   REBUILD LAW honesty is met by declaring the scale in `slideMeta` and keeping the
   real figures in the voice and in the counter's percentages. Logged as
   `FACTCHECK.md` row 6.
7. **Timings demoted to supporting evidence.** An early draft led with "4× faster".
   Corrected: milliseconds are machine-specific, so the reel's load-bearing claim
   is **rows read** (a structural property of the plan). The timings survive only as
   two small `notes` entries on B06 and in `demo/RESULTS.md`.
8. **B03 shows the plan UNEDITED**, tree characters and all, rather than a
   prettified version — so the viewer recognises the output in their own terminal.
9. **The `CAST` probe deliberately uses `branch_id`, not `account_id`** (added
   2026-09-16 when the fixture was extended). Indexing `account_id` — the obvious
   column for a `CAST(account_id AS TEXT)` example — lets SQLite satisfy pair 1's
   `GROUP BY account_id` by walking that index instead of building a temp B-tree,
   which rewrites pair 1's plan A to `SCAN postings USING INDEX
   idx_postings_account_id`. Still a full 400,000-row scan, but it prints "USING
   INDEX" on the SCAN side and would make B03's on-screen evidence contradict the
   narration. `branch_id` is a derived dimension key pair 1 never touches: same
   canonical mistake, no collateral damage. Logged in `demo/RESULTS.md`.
10. **`account_ref` and `branch_id` are DERIVED from `account_id`, never randomly
   generated**, so they consume no PRNG draws and the `posted_at` stream is
   bit-identical to the original fixture. Verified: pair 1's two captures are
   byte-for-byte unchanged after the extension, so the narration's 11,369 and
   4,459 still hold and GATE P did not need reopening.
11. **Timings re-measured after the extension** (9 runs, medians 60.18 ms /
   14.64 ms, up from 58.5 / 14.2 — the fixture gained two columns and two
   indexes, so the scan touches more pages). B06's on-screen notes changed from
   "59 ms"/"14 ms" to "~60 ms"/"~15 ms" so run-to-run noise cannot falsify the
   frame. No spoken figure changed.
12. **`date_trunc` dropped from B07's spoken list and artifact card**
   (2026-09-16, `FACTCHECK.md` L2 option 2). `strftime`, `CAST` and `UPPER` are
   all executed evidence on the demo fixture; `date_trunc` is a PostgreSQL
   function with no SQLite equivalent and could never be, so leaving it in the
   voice would have put one unmeasured assertion inside a list of measured ones —
   the exact thing DOUBLE-CHECK LAW is for. Rehomed to the video description,
   labelled as documented rather than demonstrated. B07 went 70 → 69 words. The
   edit was free because GATE P was still unsigned; `NARRATION-GATE-P.md` is now
   **rev 2** and carries the before/after.
13. **The L5 collation caveat went in the description, not the narration.** The
   `UPPER` rewrite is only equivalent under a case-sensitive collation with
   normalised data. Two sentences under the takeaway paragraph in `*-youtube.md`
   cost no re-gate and no re-render; a narration mention would have cost both,
   for a footnote to a lesson demonstrated on a date column. Residual risk (a
   viewer who never reads the description) is stated in `FACTCHECK.md` L5 rather
   than hidden.
14. **`--dangerously-skip-permissions` was NOT recommended** in `BUILD-PROMPT.md`,
   unlike the `claude-debunked` exemplar. That reel ran from `books/` inside the
   toolkit; this one writes into a separate book repo, so the seatbelt argument
   ("regenerable outputs, git-tracked") does not transfer cleanly. Supriya's call.
