## OPEN

**B00.** Hi, I am Supriya and this video is about the data analyst interview, end to end. Don't prep by collecting questions. Prep by stage: there are six, and each tests something different.

## ACT I — The Six Stages

**B01.** Act one. The six stages.

**B02.** A typical loop, in order: recruiter screen, hiring-manager chat, technical screen, statistics, case, and behavioral.

**B03.** Usually three to five rounds over two to six weeks. Startups compress it; larger companies stretch it.

**B04.** So learn what each stage scores before you learn anything else.

## ACT II — What's Tested Where

**B05.** Act two. What's tested where.

**B06.** Recruiter: fit and logistics. Hiring manager: relevant experience and motivation.

**B07.** Technical: correct SQL or Python, explained clearly. Statistics: sound reasoning, assumptions stated out loud.

**B08.** Case: framing and a defensible recommendation, over raw correctness. Behavioral: collaboration, ownership, and handling ambiguity.

**B09.** Which means a query that runs but that you can't explain is a weak answer.

## ACT III — What Changed by 2026

**B11.** Act three. What changed by 2026.

**B12.** First: AI-tool fluency is now assessed.

**B13.** They want judgment: knowing when to read the generated query line by line, because it can look right and still be wrong.

**B14.** Second: take-home cases on intentionally messy data, with a time cap and a presentation.

**B15.** Third: communication weighs heavily. Later rounds reward stakeholder translation and picking the question worth answering.

## ACT IV — The Worked Plan

**B17.** Act four. The worked plan.

**B18.** Meet Maya: economics degree, a bootcamp, interviews in three weeks. She can write a JOIN, but has never done a take-home or told a behavioral story that landed.

**B20.** Week one: SQL, the most-tested skill and her slowest, ninety minutes a day, plus statistics.

**B21.** Week two: the case round she's never seen, including one full take-home.

**B22.** Week three: behavioral stories, then two full mock loops, re-drilling her two weakest.

**B23.** Lopsided on purpose: most hours go where she's weakest and the stage counts most.

## CLOSE

**B_VERDICT.** Let's recap. Six stages, each scoring something different. A query you can't explain is a weak answer. 2026 added AI-tool judgment, messy take-homes, and heavier communication weight. So prep lopsided, on purpose.
