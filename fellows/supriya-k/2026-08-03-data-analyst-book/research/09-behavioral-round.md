## Key claims & sources

- **The STAR method is an interviewing technique employers use to evaluate candidates by how they respond to behavioral/situational questions.** STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result.
  Source: Wikipedia, "Situation, task, action, result," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation,_task,_action,_result (accessed 2026-07-15). *Tertiary/secondary reference.*
- **The four components:** Situation (the scenario), Task (the objective), Action (what you did and why), Result (the outcome and what you learned).
  Source: Wikipedia, "Situation, task, action, result" (as above).
- **STAR applies well to questions on conflict management, time management, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills.**
  Source: Wikipedia, "Situation, task, action, result" (as above).
- **Behavioral rounds often decide the offer when technical skill is even** — evaluating collaboration, decisions under ambiguity, handling setbacks, and influence without authority.
  Source: `../blueprint-trends-notes.md` (sourced to DataCamp, KORE1 2026). *Secondary.*

## Interview-craft (editorial)
- Common failure: all Situation, no Result. Build a story bank; map stories to competencies.
- Question archetypes: conflict, failure, ambiguity, influence, prioritization.
