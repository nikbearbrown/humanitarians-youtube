## Key claims & sources

- **The standard process runs ~5 blocks:** recruiter screen → hiring-manager chat → technical screen (SQL/Python) → statistics/experimentation → case/business round → behavioral round.
  Source: `../blueprint-trends-notes.md` (sourced to DataCamp, Exponent, BrainStation, KORE1, Interview Query 2026 guides). *Secondary.*
- **Recruiter screen** is a 15–30 min call: resume, motivation, salary expectations, logistics; no coding.
  Source: as above (Exponent, KORE1).
- **Technical screens grade logic and communication, not just whether the query runs**; delivered live, via shared editor, or as take-homes with messy data.
  Source: as above (DataCamp, Exponent).
- **Timeline:** most processes run 3–5 rounds over 2–6 weeks; startups compress to 2–3; FAANG-adjacent can run longer.
  Source: as above.
- **2026 shifts:** (a) AI-tool fluency is now probed; (b) take-home case studies on intentionally messy data with a presentation are common; (c) communication and business framing are weighted heavily.
  Source: `../blueprint-trends-notes.md` (Exponent case-study guide, DataCamp, KORE1). *Secondary.*
