# Claude, Untangled? — CommBank Case Study Video

A ~3:00 explainer video dramatizing the central finding of the CommBank
case study: two real, independently confirmed CommBank AI systems — a
thinly-sourced payment-disputes tool and a well-documented inbound voice
bot tied to real 2025 layoffs — have circulated in wider coverage as one
autonomous "Dispute Resolution Agent." No primary source supports that
fused version; this video corrects the conflation using the case study's
own citations.

## Files

- **`claude-untangled-commbank.mp4`** — the finished video.
- **`05-commbank-agentic-ai-disputes.md`** — the source
  case study this video dramatizes (Agentic AI Adoption in Financial
  Services series).
- **`beat_sheet.json`** — the beat-by-beat script: narration, visual
  structure, and every Remotion component/prop used to build the video.
- **`PEDAGOGY.md`** — the editorial paper trail: thesis, evidence table
  (every on-screen claim traced to its source), and the sign-off gate the
  narration passed before audio was generated.
- **`QC-REPORT.md`** — the visual/audio quality-control log: defects found
  during production and how each was fixed.
