   Where TMB is very low, the regression is close: it predicts pancreatic
   failure well. Its worst anchor is MMR-proficient colorectal, which it
   over-predicts — a tumor type Yarchoan names as a known outlier. That is
   the point: the taxonomy is needed exactly where the residual is large.

   Sensitivity of the exome conversion (dMMR / pMMR colorectal):
     exome =   25 Mb -> dMMR pred  45.4%   pMMR pred  10.9%
     exome =   30 Mb -> dMMR pred  43.4%   pMMR pred   8.9%

     exome =   40 Mb -> dMMR pred  40.3%   pMMR pred   5.8%
   The dMMR prediction stays within a few points of the observed 40%
   across every plausible exome size, so the conclusion is not an artifact
   of the conversion.

3. VARIANCE — the cost of r = 0.74, as a number
==========================================================================
   r  = 0.74
   r^2 = 0.5476  -> TMB accounts for 55% of the between-cancer-type
         variation in objective response rate.
   1 - r^2 = 0.4524 -> 45% is not accounted for by mutation count.

   Note what the unit is. This is variance across CANCER TYPES, not across
   patients. It is the correlation of 27 group averages. The letter's own
   confidence intervals show the width even at that level:
     basal-cell carcinoma (skin)      40.1%  95% CI 31.2-50.6  (width 19.4 pts)
     sarcomatoid carcinoma (lung)     20.6%  95% CI 16.7-24.5  (width 7.8 pts)

   A 19-point-wide interval on a cancer-type average is the ceiling, not
   the bedside performance. No patient-level claim follows from it.

4. ATTENUATION [ILLUSTRATIVE MODEL] — biology vs sampling noise
==========================================================================
   Model, stated in full so it can be argued with:
     phenotype_i = a*ln(TMB_i) + e_i        e_i ~ TMB-independent biology
     true ORR_i  = linear in phenotype_i     (no extra noise)
     obs ORR_i   = Binomial(n_i, ORR_i)/n_i  (finite trial)
   Under this model with NO sampling noise, corr(lnTMB, ORR)^2 is exactly
   the TMB-explained share, so r=0.74 would mean 45% TMB-independent
   biology. Finite trials attenuate r, so the real share is smaller.

 TMB-independent biology  -> observed r
                     10%          0.874
                     20%          0.831
                     25%          0.813
                     30%          0.786
                     35%          0.763
                     40%          0.743  <-- matches published r
                     45%          0.713
                     55%          0.656

   Closest match to the published r = 0.74: 40% TMB-independent biology
   Naive reading of 1 - r^2:                    45%

   Direction of the correction matters more than the exact figure. Finite
   trials make TMB look WORSE than it is, so part of the gap the source reel
   would attribute to immune phenotype is really just small-n noise. The
   honest claim is weaker than the one the reel could have made — which is
   the whole reason to compute it instead of asserting it.

5. WITHIN-TYPE CHECK — Merkel-cell carcinoma, no TMB value required
==========================================================================
   Nghiem 2016 NEJM (PMID 27093365), first-line pembrolizumab, n=26:
     MCPyV virus-POSITIVE tumours  (low mutational burden, viral antigens)
       response rate 62%  (10/16)
     MCPyV virus-NEGATIVE tumours  (UV-driven, high mutational burden)
       response rate 44%  (4/9)

   The LOWER-burden subgroup responded at the HIGHER rate, inside a single
   tumour type, with treatment and staging held constant. That is a direct
   counterexample to mutation count as the causal variable.

   It is also 16 and 9 patients. Overlapping intervals; not significant.
   Treat it as a mechanism illustration, never as a result. Yarchoan names
   Merkel cell as the canonical above-the-line outlier and attributes it to
   viral antigen presentation; this is that attribution, in one trial.
