## Completed

- **Reproduced and ablated the RamanSPy preprocessing pipeline.** Compared LegacyCNN and Raman1DCNN on the Bacteria-ID data. Found that removing ASPLS baseline correction improved accuracy by 7.61 points. Set up McNemar tests and a noise floor for comparing runs. Evidence: [Week 1 frictional log](2026-09-14-ramanspy-reproducibility/FRICTIONAL.md) and [weekly report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Gpt82OWNMz2f-6N8PGD6tGqbKgsCFutY/view?usp=drive_link).
- **Ran controlled experiments on the corrected pipeline.** These were Experiments 5–9: phase-2 tuning, longer fine-tuning, incremental learning, a seed ensemble and stage-1 augmentation, each evaluated with paired per-sample tests. Corrected earlier claims that the new results falsified, and showed that effects under ~1 point need at least 6 paired seeds. Evidence: [Week 2 frictional log](2026-09-25-experiments-5-9/FRICTIONAL.md) and [experiment record](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wM89cyxttbhjCC3Xpy8iqJOfan4qfU3j/view?usp=drive_link).
- **Designed, built and evaluated a Raman foundation model.** Wrote the design spec and the data plan. Built `corpus_v1`: 242,156 spectra from 25 public sources, with all Bacteria-ID spectra held out. Pretrained model A with masked autoencoding. Compared four adaptation arms, including LoRA, on two tasks against a pre-registered hypothesis. Pretraining helped most under shift over time (+4.1 points) and with scarce labels. Evidence: [Week 3 frictional log](2026-10-01-foundation-spec-plan1/FRICTIONAL.md), [Week 4 frictional log](2026-10-03-foundation-adaptation-results/FRICTIONAL.md) and [results report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xxAKlCVAcHfKf65jREg1RpL_j1e4wj7T/view?usp=drive_link).

Verified the bibliography, now 33 entries. Evidence: [Week 5 frictional log](2026-10-16-paper-prep-base-rerun/FRICTIONAL.md) and [Week 6 frictional log](2026-10-23-paper-section-iii/FRICTIONAL.md).
