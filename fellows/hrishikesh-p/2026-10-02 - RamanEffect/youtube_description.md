# Week 5: Deep Learning for Clinical Bacterial Isolate Identification

This week we do a deep dive into an incredible application of Raman spectroscopy and Deep Learning. Historically, diagnosing bacterial infections requires culturing cells, which takes days. By applying a one-dimensional residual convolutional neural network directly to Raman spectra, researchers achieved culture-free identification of bacterial isolates. 

We explore the architecture, the performance across 30 different clinical isolates, and how the model accurately distinguishes antibiotic-resistant strains like MRSA from MSSA.
