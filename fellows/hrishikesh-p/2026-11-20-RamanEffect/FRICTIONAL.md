# Week 12 Summary: Raman Spectroscopy in Life Sciences

In this technical teardown, we summarize **Teledyne Princeton Instruments' Application Note** (*A Brief Overview of Raman Spectroscopy in Life Sciences*, Rev A0 - 07032020), tracing how inelastic laser scattering turns molecular vibrations into non-destructive, label-free biomedical diagnostics—from single-cell protein folding to clinical *in vivo* oncology.
