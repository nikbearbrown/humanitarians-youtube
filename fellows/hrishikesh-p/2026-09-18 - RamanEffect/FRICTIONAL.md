# Week 3 Summary: 1D Raman Spectra Classification

## Why Simple Models Win

This Week 3 update covers experiments with deep learning architectures for 1D Raman spectra classification across 30 bacterial classes. We tested five different models, including custom Multiscale CNNs, ResNets, and modern 1D Transformers.

The deepest and most complex architecture did not win the benchmark. The simplest and shallowest Legacy CNN dominated the leaderboard, beating the Transformer by more than 20 points. Watch the video to learn why simple architectures and natural bottlenecks can outperform high-capacity models when working with straightforward 1D spatial data.
