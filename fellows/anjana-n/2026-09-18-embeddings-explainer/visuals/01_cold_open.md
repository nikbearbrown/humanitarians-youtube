# B01 — Cold Open

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Word on the left dissolves into a vector on the right.

## Elements

### Word token
- The word "revenue" in clean white sans-serif text, centered left.
- Soft glow behind it.

### Transformation arrow
- Horizontal arrow from the word to the right side of the frame.
- Arrow animates as a flowing line, not static.

### Embedding vector
- A vertical column of floating numbers: 0.23, -0.71, 0.08, 0.44, -0.15, 0.62, ...
- Show 8-10 visible numbers with an ellipsis suggesting more.
- Small label to the side: "768 dimensions."
- Numbers pulse softly with a blue glow.

## Animation sequence
1. "revenue" fades in on the left (1s).
2. Arrow animates across (1s).
3. The word dissolves into particles that reform as the number column on the right (2s).
4. "768 dimensions" label fades in (1s).
5. Numbers pulse gently for the remaining duration.

## Palette
- Word text: #EAEAEA (white)
- Arrow: #4A90D9 (blue)
- Vector numbers: #4A90D9 (blue) with glow
- Dimension label: #95A5A6 (muted grey)
- Background: #1A1A2E
