# B05 — Your Turn

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. The full vector space from B03, enriched and slowly rotating.

## Elements

### Enriched scatter plot
- The 2D scatter from B03 now with more labeled dots filling in the space.
- Additional clusters visible: a neutral cluster in the center (grey dots: "reported," "stated," "noted"), the positive and negative clusters from B03.
- Cluster boundaries glow softly with their respective colors.

### Slow camera rotation
- A subtle parallax shift suggesting 3D depth (dots at slightly different sizes to fake perspective).
- Slow drift to the right over the duration of the beat.

### Title card
- Scatter plot fades.
- Centered text: "What Embeddings Actually Store."
- Subtle glow, hold, fade to black.

## Animation sequence
1. Enriched scatter plot appears with all clusters (2s).
2. Cluster boundaries glow softly (1s).
3. Slow parallax rotation (3s).
4. Fade scatter plot (1s).
5. Title card fades in (1s).
6. Hold, fade to black (2s).
