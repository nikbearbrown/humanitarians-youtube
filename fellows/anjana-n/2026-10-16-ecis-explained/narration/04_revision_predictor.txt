Can the system predict whether a company will revise its guidance next quarter? A feature vector combines hedging index, management confidence, guidance specificity change, CEO-CFO divergence, dodge score, market share language shift, forward-looking density delta, sentiment delta, and sector mood positioning. Six models compete: logistic regression for interpretability, XGBoost and LightGBM for gradient boosting, Random Forest for variance reduction, a small neural network for non-linear interactions, and CatBoost for native categorical handling. All evaluated with time-aware cross-validation, bootstrap confidence intervals, and paired permutation tests.
