Earnings calls have a Q&A section where analysts press management for answers. The system now parses every question-answer pair, classifying questions as forward-looking, backward-looking, clarification, or confrontational. A cross-encoder reranker scores how directly each answer addresses its question — not just semantic similarity, but actual relevance. The dodge score is weighted by question importance: evading a confrontational question about margins scores higher than sidestepping a clarification. Per-speaker and per-call dodge scores are tracked across quarters.
