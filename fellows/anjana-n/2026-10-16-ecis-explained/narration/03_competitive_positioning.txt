How a company talks about competition reveals strategy. Three classifiers work together. A lexicon-based scorer matches phrases like "gaining share" or "defending margins." A zero-shot classifier using BART handles novel phrasing the lexicon misses. And a fine-tuned stance detection model captures subtler positioning. The ensemble scores each call on an offensive-to-defensive spectrum. A tracking module monitors shifts across quarters — when a company flips from offensive to defensive language, the system flags it.
