ECIS extracts financial guidance signals from earnings call transcripts using four readers — keyword, FinBERT, NER, and LLM — triangulated with dynamic weights. A prediction layer forecasts next-quarter direction with pre-registered, graded predictions. Episode 10 added the business analytics layer: sector sentiment aggregation, a mood index, cross-sector heatmaps, management confidence scoring, CEO-CFO tone divergence, signal-to-price correlation ranking, surprise score analytics, and reaction window curves. Now in Episode 11: the system learns to detect when executives dodge questions, track competitive positioning language, and predict guidance revisions.
