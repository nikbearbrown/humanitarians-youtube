# Beat 4 — The Boundary

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

Dark background. Two quote cards appear side by side, separated by a vertical dividing line.

**Left card (0-4s):**
Quote: "We are reaffirming our full-year revenue outlook of twelve billion dollars."
Tag below: "MAINTAINED" in a calm blue, confidence badge: 0.82
This is active reaffirmation — the company explicitly restated their number.

**Right card (4-8s):**
Quote: "We remain focused on executing our strategy."
Tag below: "NONE" in grey, confidence badge: 0.91
This is not guidance — it is a generic statement with no commitment.

**The boundary (8-12s):**
The vertical dividing line between the cards pulses and glows. A label appears on it: "The Boundary." The two quotes look superficially similar — both use comfortable corporate language — but the system draws a precise line between them.

A brief text appears below: "Maintained = actively reaffirmed. None = no guidance at all."

## Technical notes

- The two quotes should feel eerily similar at first glance — that's the point
- The cards should be clean, dark background, monospaced or serif quote text
- The "MAINTAINED" tag in blue, "NONE" tag in grey — simple, not decorative
- The boundary line is the visual hook — it should glow or pulse to draw attention
- This beat is about precision, not drama — keep the visual calm and exact
