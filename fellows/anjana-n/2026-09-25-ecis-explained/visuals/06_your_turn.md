# B06 — Your Turn

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Wide pullback of full ECIS architecture with Ep8 additions glowing.

## Elements

### Full architecture
- Complete pipeline from previous episodes.
- New additions glow gold in sequence:
  - Linguistic gauges (attached to transcript input stage).
  - Market impact charts (attached to scoring/outcomes stage).
  - PostgreSQL stack (replacing old database icon).

### Glow sequence
- Each new addition pulses gold briefly, then settles.

### Title card
- Architecture fades.
- Centered text: "ECIS Episode 8: Reading Between the Lines."
- Fade to dark.

## Animation sequence
1. Full architecture visible (1s).
2. Linguistic gauges glow (0.5s).
3. Market impact charts glow (0.5s).
4. PostgreSQL stack glows (0.5s).
5. All settle (1s).
6. Fade to title card (2s).
7. Hold, fade to black (1s).
