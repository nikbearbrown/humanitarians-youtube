# B03 — Market Impact

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Three mini-charts stacked vertically.

## Elements

### Top chart: Reaction Magnitude
- Two horizontal bars side by side.
- Small bar: +0.5% excess return (light green, thin).
- Large bar: +12% excess return (bright green, wide).
- Label: "magnitude matters."

### Middle chart: Reaction Timing
- A horizontal timeline divided into four segments: "same-day," "1-3 days," "1-2 weeks," "30+ days."
- A glowing marker sits in the "1-3 days" segment, showing when the excess return accumulated.
- Segments before the marker are filled, segments after are empty.

### Bottom chart: Abnormal Volume
- A bar chart showing 10 days of trading volume.
- Most bars are at a consistent level. The earnings call day bar spikes 3x higher.
- A horizontal dashed line marks the 30-day trailing average.
- Label on the spike: "3x average volume."

## Animation sequence
1. Magnitude bars animate, small first then large (2s).
2. "magnitude matters" label fades in (0.5s).
3. Reaction timeline appears, marker glows in its segment (2s).
4. Volume chart bars grow upward, spike bar shoots up last (2s).
5. Dashed average line and "3x average volume" label (1s).
