As the feature set grows, not every feature earns its place. Automated feature selection runs three methods — recursive feature elimination, permutation importance, and LASSO shrinkage — and flags features that rank in the bottom ten percent across all three for removal. Feature interaction discovery identifies synergistic combinations: high dodge score plus rising hedging plus defensive language predicts outcomes better than any feature alone. These interaction features are added to the vector. Embedding drift monitoring tracks whether the Sentence-BERT space is shifting and flags when re-fine-tuning is needed.
