High confidence does not guarantee accuracy. The false positive analysis module examines every prediction where confidence exceeded zero point seven but the direction was wrong. It clusters the errors by root cause — ambiguous language, sarcasm misread, section misattribution, context-dependent phrases, or unusual market conditions. Signals matching known patterns get a reduced confidence modifier. At the same time, multi-quarter signal chaining tracks trends across consecutive calls. Three quarters of rising hedging with declining confidence is no longer three independent readings. It's a sustained deterioration signal.
