## 3. Points of resistance, and next steps taken

### The signal decay chart needed to be corrected
- **The problem:** the script described a half-life as the point where a signal "drops below significance", which is a different idea. A half-life is the point where a value falls to half of where it started. Labelling the chart as written would have shown incorrect information.
- **The fix:**
  - The chart was redrawn to show each signal's value relative to its starting point.
  - Each half-life marker now sits exactly where the value reaches half.
  - The narration did not need to change.
- **Confirmation:** this correction was confirmed before rendering.

### A chart label referred to the wrong measurement
- **The problem:** the drift chart measured one thing but its  line was labelled with the name of a different measure.
- **The fix:** the label was corrected to match what the chart actually shows. The narration was unaffected.



### Colors differed from the series style
- **The problem:** the brief specified its own colors, which differed from the style used across the series.
- **The fix:** the established series colors were applied, following the decision made for Episode 11.

### Several sections ran long
- **The problem:** all six main sections went over the target word count.
- **The decision:** the script was kept as written. Each long section covers more than one idea, and everything the narration lists also appears on screen.

### The ending needed adjusting

- **The fix:** the episode now ends on the title card.
