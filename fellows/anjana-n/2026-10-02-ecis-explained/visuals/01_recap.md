## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout

## Elements

### Pipeline recap (first 15s)
- Transcript document icon enters from left.
- Four reader nodes light up in parallel: keyword (white), FinBERT (blue), NER (green), LLM (purple).
- Arrows converge at the triangulator (central hexagon).

- Label fades in: "proving it works."
