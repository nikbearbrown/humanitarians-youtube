# B05 — Your Turn

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Four use case icon panels in a row, then title card.

## Elements

### Use case panels
Four small rounded-rectangle panels in a horizontal row, evenly spaced:
1. **Semantic search:** A search bar icon with a magnifying glass and the text snippet "meaning, not keywords."
2. **Recommendations:** A 2x2 grid of item thumbnails (abstract squares) with one highlighted. Label: "similar items."
3. **RAG:** A chatbot bubble icon with a small document being retrieved beneath it. Label: "retrieved context."
4. **Duplicate detection:** Two overlapping document icons with a similarity badge between them. Label: "near-duplicates."

### Glow sequence
- Each panel glows gold briefly in order, left to right.

### Title card
- Panels fade.
- Centered text: "What Vector Databases Do That SQL Cannot."
- Fade to black.

## Animation sequence
1. Four panels fade in simultaneously (1s).
2. Panel 1 glows (1s).
3. Panel 2 glows (1s).
4. Panel 3 glows (1s).
5. Panel 4 glows (1s).
6. All panels fade, title card appears (2s).
7. Hold, fade to black (2s).

## Palette
- Panel borders: #2C3E50 (dark slate)
- Panel glow: #F1C40F (gold)
- Icon fills: #4A90D9 (blue)
- Labels: #EAEAEA (white)
- Title text: #EAEAEA
- Background: #1A1A2E
