Until now, every forecast looked one quarter ahead. A Temporal Fusion Transformer changes that. It produces probabilistic fan charts at one, two, five, and ten quarter horizons — wider bands further out, tighter bands close in. Attention mechanisms let it look back selectively, learning which historical quarters matter most for each prediction. And Prophet decomposes each signal into trend, seasonality, and residual — isolating the long-term direction from repeating cycles and random noise. Some companies hedge more before every fiscal year-end. That's seasonality, not deterioration. Now the system knows the difference.
