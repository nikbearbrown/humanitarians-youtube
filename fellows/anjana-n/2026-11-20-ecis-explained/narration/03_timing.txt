Predicting that a company will revise guidance is useful. Predicting when is better. Time-to-revision modeling treats each company as a countdown — ticking forward in quarters until the event happens. A statistical model estimates risk factors for each feature: how much does rising hedging momentum or increasing dodge scores accelerate that countdown? Per-company timing curves show the probability of guidance remaining unchanged over time. When the curve drops steeply, the company enters a high-alert window — revision likely within one to two quarters.
