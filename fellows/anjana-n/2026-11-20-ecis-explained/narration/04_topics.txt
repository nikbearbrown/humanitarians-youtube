Earnings calls aren't just about how management speaks — they're about what management speaks about. BERTopic extracts themes from the full transcript corpus per sector per quarter. It tracks when new topics emerge, when established ones fade, and when a company's topic mix diverges from its peers. If an entire sector is discussing cost optimization and one company is still focused on expansion, that divergence is a signal. Topic shift detection measures quarter-over-quarter changes and flags anomalies — sudden pivots in what management chooses to talk about.
