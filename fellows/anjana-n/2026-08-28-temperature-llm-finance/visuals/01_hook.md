# Beat 1 — The Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~8 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

Three identical quote cards appear in a row on a dark background. Each shows the same sentence:

"We anticipate moderate revenue growth in the back half of the year."

Below each card, an extraction result fades in — but they're all different:
- Card 1: "raised, 0.72" (green)
- Card 2: "maintained, 0.65" (blue)
- Card 3: "raised, 0.81" (green, different confidence)

A question mark pulses above the three cards. Same input, three outputs. What changed?

On "Temperature" — the word appears large below the cards. A circular dial icon fades in beside it, needle pointing at an ambiguous mid-point. The dial is the recurring visual anchor for the rest of the video.

## Technical notes

- The three cards should be identical except for the extraction result — emphasize sameness of input
- The different outputs should feel surprising — same quote, why different answers?
- The temperature dial icon should look like a physical knob or gauge, not a digital slider
- Dark background, clean typography
