# Beat 4 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~7 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The temperature dial returns, centered and large.

It is set to the low end, near zero. Calm, stable. The background is dark and steady.

**The twitch (0-3s):**
On "Creativity is for poets" — the dial needle briefly swings toward 1.0. The background flickers with unstable, warm chaotic colors — reds, oranges — for just a moment. The token distribution from Beat 2 briefly flashes in its T=1.0 flattened state.

**The snap back (3-5s):**
On "Consistency is for predictions" — the dial snaps back to near-zero. The background settles to calm dark. The distribution reappears in its T=0.0 locked state — one dominant bar.

**Title (5-7s):**
The dial and distribution fade. Title text lands:

**Temperature.**
*The dial between prediction and hallucination.*

Hold. Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Technical notes

- The twitch-and-snap is the visual punchline — brief chaos, then control
- The chaotic moment should be genuinely disorienting — just long enough to feel it, not long enough to study it
- The snap back should feel decisive — the model choosing stability
- The closing line "Creativity is for poets. Consistency is for predictions." is the quotable takeaway
- Title text: main line bold, subtitle lighter and italic
