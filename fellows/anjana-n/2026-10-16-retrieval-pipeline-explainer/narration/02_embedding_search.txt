Stage one: the query hits an embedding model. The text is converted to a dense vector — a list of numbers that captures its meaning. This takes five to fifteen milliseconds. Stage two: that vector searches an index. Approximate nearest neighbor algorithms — HNSW, IVF — trade a small amount of recall for speed. From millions of stored vectors, the index returns the top candidates in under fifty milliseconds. These two stages are fast, but they're only the starting point.
