Every stage competes for the same budget. A typical production target is under five hundred milliseconds total retrieval latency. Embedding and search take fifty. Rewriting, when triggered, adds up to two hundred. Reranking costs eighty. Filtering is near-zero. That leaves room for network overhead and the occasional retry. The architecture is a sequence of trade-offs: precision against speed, recall against latency. The pipeline that ships is the one that fits the budget.
