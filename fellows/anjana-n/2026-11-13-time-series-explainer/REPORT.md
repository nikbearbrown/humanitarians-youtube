# Video Report: Time Series — When Order Is the Signal

**Type:** STEM Educational Video

**Week:** Week 20, November 8 – 14, 2026

---

## Summary
A video on what makes time series data different. In a regular table the row order doesn't matter, but in a time series the order is the signal. The video covers trend, seasonality and noise, stationarity, forecasting methods, and the most common evaluation mistake.

## Main Ideas
- **Order is the signal.** Shuffling keeps the same numbers but destroys the pattern.
- **A series has three parts:** trend, seasonality and noise.
- **Stationarity:** many forecasting methods need a stable average and spread over time, and differencing often gets you there.
- **No single method wins everywhere.** ARIMA, Prophet and LSTM trade being easy to explain against needing more data.
- **A random split leaks the future.** It gives excellent scores that mean nothing, so time series need a time-ordered split.
