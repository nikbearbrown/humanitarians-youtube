Forecasting methods range from classical statistics to deep learning. ARIMA models the series as a linear function of its own past values and past errors — small, interpretable, and still surprisingly competitive. Prophet decomposes the series into trend, seasonality, and holidays, making it easy to handle messy real-world data with minimal tuning. And recurrent networks like LSTMs learn patterns directly from sequences — powerful on large datasets but harder to interpret and hungry for data. No single method wins everywhere. The right choice depends on how much data you have, how far ahead you need to forecast, and whether you need to explain the result.
