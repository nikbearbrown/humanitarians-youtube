Every time series is a mix of three things. Trend — the long-term direction, up or down or flat. Seasonality — repeating cycles at fixed intervals, daily, weekly, yearly. And residual — the leftover noise once you strip out trend and seasonality. Decomposition separates these layers so you can model each one. A rising sales curve might look chaotic, but once you pull apart the upward trend, the holiday spikes, and the random day-to-day variation, each component on its own is far easier to understand and forecast.
