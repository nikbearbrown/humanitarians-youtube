Here's where most beginners go wrong. In standard machine learning, you split data randomly — some rows train, some rows test. With time series, a random split is a disaster. Training points from next month sit alongside test points from last month. The model has already seen the future. The fix is simple: split in time. Everything before a cutoff date trains the model. Everything after it tests. No peeking forward. This is the one rule you cannot break — if your evaluation leaks the future, your accuracy numbers mean nothing.
