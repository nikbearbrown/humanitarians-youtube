Most forecasting methods assume the series they're modeling is stationary — its mean, variance, and autocorrelation structure don't change over time. Real-world data almost never starts that way. A stock price trends upward. Energy demand grows year over year. The fix is differencing — subtract each value from the one before it. The result isn't the level anymore, it's the change. And changes often are stationary, even when levels aren't. Make the series stationary first, then fit the model. It sounds like a detour, but it's usually the step that makes everything else work.
