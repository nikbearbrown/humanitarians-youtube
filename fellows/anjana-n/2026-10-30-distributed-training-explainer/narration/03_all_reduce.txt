Averaging gradients across GPUs sounds simple, but the communication pattern matters. All-reduce is the standard approach. In a ring all-reduce, each GPU sends a chunk of its gradients to the next GPU in a ring. After enough rounds, every GPU holds the fully averaged result — without any single GPU acting as a bottleneck. The alternative — sending everything to one central node — creates a bandwidth chokepoint that gets worse with every GPU added. Ring all-reduce scales because each GPU sends and receives the same amount of data regardless of cluster size.
