When the model itself is too large for one GPU, you split the model. Tensor parallelism divides individual layers — a single matrix multiply is split across GPUs, each computing part of the result. Pipeline parallelism splits by layer groups — GPU one runs the first twelve layers, GPU two runs the next twelve. The input passes through GPUs in sequence, like a pipeline. The problem: while GPU two waits for GPU one's output, it sits idle. Micro-batching reduces this bubble by overlapping multiple smaller batches at different stages.
