Production training combines strategies. A large language model might use tensor parallelism within a node — splitting layers across GPUs connected by fast NVLink. Pipeline parallelism spans nodes — different layer groups on different machines. Data parallelism wraps around everything — multiple copies of the full pipeline, each processing different data. The bottleneck is always communication: NVLink handles intra-node traffic at high bandwidth, while inter-node networking is slower and must be used efficiently. The training recipe is an optimization problem over compute, memory, and bandwidth.
