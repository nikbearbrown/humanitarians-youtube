# Beat 6 — Self-Correction

**Visual type:** Remotion  
**Duration:** ~20 seconds

## What the viewer sees

Three feedback loops visualized as circular arrows, appearing one at a time as the narration names each loop.

**Loop 1 — Calibration watchdog (first 6 sec):**
A circular arrow appears on the left third of the screen. Inside the loop: a small calibration curve that shifts — the dots move closer to the diagonal, showing recalibration in action. Label: "Confidence recalibration"

**Loop 2 — Orchestration learning (next 6 sec):**
A second circular arrow appears in the center. Inside the loop: the routing threshold slider from Beat 4 moves — loosening or tightening. Label: "Routing thresholds"

**Loop 3 — Vindication tracker (next 4 sec):**
A third circular arrow appears on the right. Inside the loop: horizontal weight bars for the four readers shift — one grows, another shrinks. Label: "Reader weights"

**Human gate (last 4 sec):**
All three loops are now visible. A horizontal dividing line appears below them. Below the line: a human silhouette icon appears at a gate checkpoint with two buttons labeled "Approve" and "Reject". The "Approve" button pulses once. Label: "Structural changes require human approval"

## Mood

Alive, adaptive. The system is not frozen after deployment — it learns. But the human holds the final key.

## Technical notes

- The three loops should be visually distinct but stylistically consistent
- Circular arrows: use smooth, continuously rotating animation
- The internal visuals (curve shifting, slider moving, bars reweighting) should be subtle, not complex — the loop itself is the message
- Human icon at the gate should feel authoritative, not decorative
- Dark background, accent colors matching the palette from earlier beats
