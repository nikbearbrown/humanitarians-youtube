# Beat 3 — Pre-Registered Predictions

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

**Pre-registration (0-4s):**
A prediction record card appears in gold accent (distinct from the blue extraction cards):

- Ticker: Company A
- Predicted direction: raised
- Confidence: 0.73
- Features: [collapsed row of small icons]
- Model: v1.0
- Timestamp: 2024-10-15

A padlock stamps onto the card with a click. Label: "Logged before the call."

**The wait (4-7s):**
A horizontal timeline appears. The prediction card sits at the left end, locked. A dotted line extends to the right toward a future date marker: "Earnings call: 2024-10-28." The space between is empty, time is passing. A subtle calendar animation shows days ticking by.

**Grading (7-12s):**
The earnings call date arrives. The future date marker activates. A transcript icon appears at that point. The extraction pipeline runs as a quick compact flash (the familiar architecture from Beat 1, just a brief pulse). Output: "Actual direction: raised."

The prediction card (gold) and the actual result card (blue) appear side by side:
- Predicted: raised, 0.73
- Actual: raised

A green checkmark stamps between them.

Label: "Predicted before. Graded after. No edits."

## Technical notes

- The gold accent for predictions is established in Beat 2 and reinforced here
- The padlock is the same visual metaphor as the extraction pre-registration from Episode 1
- The waiting period with the dotted timeline conveys that the prediction is committed before the outcome
- The side-by-side comparison (gold vs blue) is clean and immediate
- The compact pipeline flash should take less than 1 second, it is not the focus here
