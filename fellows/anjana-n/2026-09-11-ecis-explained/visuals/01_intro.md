# Beat 1 — What is ECIS

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~20 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The architecture builds left to right as the narration describes each component:

A transcript document icon enters from the left. It splits into chunks. Chunks flow into four reader nodes arranged vertically: Keyword (blue), FinBERT (teal), NER (orange), LLM (purple). Each lights up as named.

Arrows from all four converge into the Triangulator node. A single signal exits into an append-only log icon (padlock stamps on). A timeline extends right with three checkpoints: 30, 90, 180 days. The signal gets graded at each.

On "That is the foundation" the full architecture settles into a compact overview on the left side of the screen. On "Here is what comes next" a new empty region opens to the right, separated by a subtle dotted border. The extraction pipeline is the past. The right side is the future.

## Technical notes

- Same 20-second intro as Episode 5 for new viewer accessibility
- The left/right spatial split (extraction = left, prediction = right) sets up the visual language for the rest of the episode
- The dotted border between past and future is subtle but important
