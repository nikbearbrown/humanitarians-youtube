The prediction follows the same honesty rules as every extraction. It is logged before the earnings call happens. Ticker, predicted direction, confidence, the full feature vector snapshot, model version, and timestamp. Locked. Append-only. After the call, the transcript is extracted and the actual guidance direction is known. Only then does the scorecard grade the prediction. Did the model get it right? The prediction cannot be revised, softened, or deleted after the fact. The same discipline that governs extraction now governs forecasting.
