Not all analyst questions are equal. The system now profiles individual analysts — tracking their question types, difficulty levels, and how closely their framing aligns with management responses. This matters for dodge scoring. Dodging a hard question from a known tough analyst is less informative than dodging a routine question from a friendly one. The raw dodge score might be identical, but once you normalize for analyst difficulty, the signal diverges. The same evasion, weighted by who asked, tells a different story.
