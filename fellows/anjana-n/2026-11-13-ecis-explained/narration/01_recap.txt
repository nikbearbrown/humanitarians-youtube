ECIS extracts guidance signals from earnings calls using four readers weighted by a five-dimension triangulator. A six-model ensemble predicts guidance revisions, with conformal prediction intervals, Monte Carlo dropout, and ensemble disagreement quantifying uncertainty around every forecast. The system abstains when confidence is low, tracks language shifts propagating across sectors and supply chains, and answers natural language queries through an agentic layer. This week, the system learns to see time — not just single quarters, but patterns unfolding across them.
