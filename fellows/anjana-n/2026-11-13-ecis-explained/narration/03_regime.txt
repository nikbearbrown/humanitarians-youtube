The same hedging increase means something different in a bull market than in a bear market. A Hidden Markov Model now detects three market regimes — bullish, bearish, and transitional — fitted on sector-level signal aggregates using the Baum-Welch algorithm. Each quarter gets a regime label, and that label feeds into every prediction model as a context feature. The models can now adjust their behavior depending on the broader market environment instead of treating all quarters the same.
