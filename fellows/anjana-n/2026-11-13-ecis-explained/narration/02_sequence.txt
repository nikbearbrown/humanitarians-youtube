Tree-based models see each quarter as an isolated snapshot — a flat row of features with no memory of what came before. An LSTM changes that. It unrolls across four to eight quarters per company, passing a hidden state forward through time. A gradual hedging ramp over four quarters is a different signal than a single-quarter spike, and the LSTM learns the difference from sequence structure alone. A lighter GRU variant trains thirty percent faster with comparable accuracy. Both now sit alongside the tree models — sequence-aware predictions complementing snapshot-based ones.
