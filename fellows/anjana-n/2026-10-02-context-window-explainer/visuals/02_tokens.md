# B02 — Tokens, Not Words

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Upper half: tokenization examples. Lower half: math comparison.

## Elements

### Upper half: Tokenization examples

#### Example 1 (left)
- The word "understanding" in large white text.
- A single bracket wraps the entire word.
- Small label below: "1 token."
- Bracket color: #4A90D9 (blue).

### Lower half: The math

#### Counter
- Text: "128,000 tokens x 0.75 = ~96,000 words."
- Numbers animate as if being typed/calculated.
- "x 0.75" in muted grey (#95A5A6), result in gold (#F1C40F).

#### Comparison bars
- Two horizontal bars, left-aligned:
  - Top bar: full width, labeled "128K — what people assume." Color: #95A5A6 (grey).
  - Bottom bar: ~75% width, labeled "96K — what you actually get." Color: #4A90D9 (blue).
- The gap between the bars' right edges is highlighted with a subtle red tint.
