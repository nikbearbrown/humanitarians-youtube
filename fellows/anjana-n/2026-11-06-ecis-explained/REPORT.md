# Video Report: ECIS Episode 14 — Knowing What You Don't Know

**Type:** Weekly Implementation Video

**Week:** Week 19 (Phase 19), November 1 – 7, 2026

---

## Summary
This episode shows ECIS recognizing the limits of what it knows. The system now:
- measures how uncertain each prediction is
- holds back predictions that are too uncertain
- tracks wording that spreads between companies
- answers questions through a conversational agent

## Main Ideas
- **Three measures of uncertainty are better than one.** They are conformal intervals, dropout sampling and agreement between models.
- **Holding back 12% of predictions raises accuracy on the rest by 8 points.**
- **Some companies' wording tends to appear first, and others follow a quarter or two later.**
- **The agent only reports what the system actually found.**
