ECIS extracts guidance signals from earnings call transcripts using four readers — keyword, FinBERT, NER, and LLM — weighted by a five-dimension triangulator. A six-model ensemble predicts guidance revisions. Calibrated confidence scores, contradiction detection, and SHAP explanations tell you not just what the system predicts, but why — and an out-of-distribution detector flags transcripts the models haven't seen before. This week, the system learns something new: when not to answer at all.
