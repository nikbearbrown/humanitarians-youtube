After an LSTM encoder processes the sequence, the TFT applies multi-head attention over the temporal dimension. Each prediction at each horizon can look back across all historical time steps and learn which ones are most relevant. A forecast five quarters out might attend heavily to the same quarter last year while ignoring adjacent months. The key difference from a standard transformer: these attention weights are designed to be interpretable. You can read them directly — which time steps did the model focus on for this specific prediction? It's not a black box. The model shows you where it looked.
