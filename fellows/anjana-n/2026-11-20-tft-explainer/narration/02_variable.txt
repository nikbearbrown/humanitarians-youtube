The first thing the TFT does is decide which inputs matter. A variable selection network sits at the front of the architecture. It takes every input — static, known future, and observed past — and assigns each one a learned importance weight using a softmax over all candidates. High-scoring variables pass through at full strength. Low-scoring ones get suppressed before they even enter the model. And this selection happens per time step, so the same variable can matter a lot at one point in time and barely register at another. Noise gets filtered at the door instead of leaking into the forecast.
