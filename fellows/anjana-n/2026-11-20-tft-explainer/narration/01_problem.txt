Time series forecasting sounds straightforward until you look at the inputs. Some are static — a product category or a region that never changes. Some are known in advance — the day of the week, whether next Monday is a holiday. And some are only observed historically — actual sales, actual temperature, things you can't see into the future. Most models flatten all of these into a single vector and hope for the best. But not all inputs matter equally, and the ones that matter change depending on when you're looking. The Temporal Fusion Transformer was built to handle exactly this problem.
