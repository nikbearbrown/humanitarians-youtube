## Summary
A video on how the Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) forecasts several steps ahead. It covers three design choices:
- the model picks which inputs matter
- it can skip steps that don't help
- it looks back at the past steps it needs

Together, these help it do better than LSTMs over longer horizons.
