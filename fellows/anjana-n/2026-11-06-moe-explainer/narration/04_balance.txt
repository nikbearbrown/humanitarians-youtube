There's a catch. Left to its own devices, the router learns to send almost everything to one or two favorite experts. The rest go unused — routing collapse. The fix is an auxiliary loss that penalizes uneven distribution. It nudges the router toward spreading tokens across all experts roughly equally. Each expert also gets a capacity factor — a maximum number of tokens it can accept per batch. Tokens that exceed the cap get routed to their second choice. Simple guardrails, but without them, the architecture falls apart.
