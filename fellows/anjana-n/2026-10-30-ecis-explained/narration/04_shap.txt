Every prediction now comes with a reason. SHAP values decompose each forecast into individual feature contributions — which features pushed the prediction up, which pulled it down, and by how much. A natural language module translates those contributions into plain English. Instead of a raw feature vector, the system outputs: "Company A flagged because hedging rose forty percent while CEO-CFO divergence hit a four-quarter high and dodge score exceeded the sector average." The explanation is stored alongside the prediction for audit and review.
