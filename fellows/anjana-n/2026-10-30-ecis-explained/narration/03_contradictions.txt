Sometimes a single earnings call sends mixed messages — and the system's signals reflect that. Bullish tone paired with rising hedging. High confidence alongside a high dodge score. Offensive competitive language combined with declining specificity. The contradiction detector scans all signals within each call for these conflicts. Each contradiction gets a severity score weighted by signal strength. And contradictions aren't just noise — they're a signal themselves. A call full of contradictions may indicate genuine uncertainty, a mixed outlook.
