When the system says eighty percent confident, it should be right eighty percent of the time. Platt scaling fits a logistic regression on model outputs versus actual outcomes — independently per signal type — correcting systematic over- or under-confidence. Isotonic regression offers a non-parametric alternative. Both are fitted on held-out data with time-aware splits to prevent lookahead bias. After calibration, the predicted probability curves align with observed accuracy across every decile.
