Models are reliable on inputs that resemble their training data. When an earnings call looks unlike anything the system has seen — a new CEO's communication style, a crisis call, an unusual transcript structure — the predictions carry hidden risk. Mahalanobis distance measures how far each transcript's feature vector falls from the training distribution. An isolation forest provides a second check. Transcripts flagged as out-of-distribution receive an automatic confidence penalty that scales with severity. Marginal outliers get small adjustments. Extreme outliers get large reductions.
