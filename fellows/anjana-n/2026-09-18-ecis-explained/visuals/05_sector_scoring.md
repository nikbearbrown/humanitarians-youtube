# B05 — Sector-Normalized Scoring

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Two evaluation panels side by side, old method fading out, new method brightening.

## Elements

### Left panel: Old method
- Vertical bar chart with two bars.
- Company bar: +5% (blue, #4A90D9).
- Broad market bar: +2% (grey, #7F8C8D). Label: "broad market."
- Green checkmark above. Label: "outperform."

### Right panel: New method
- Vertical bar chart with two bars.
- Same company bar: +5% (blue, #4A90D9).
- Sector bar: +8% (orange, #E67E22). Label: "sector benchmark."
- Red cross above. Label: "underperform."

### Transition
- Left panel dims and fades. Right panel brightens.
- Label below right panel: "sector-normalized excess return."

## Animation sequence
1. Left panel appears: company +5%, broad market +2%, green checkmark, "outperform."
2. Right panel appears alongside: same company +5%, sector +8%, red cross, "underperform."
3. Pause to let the contrast register (2s).
4. Left panel fades to 30% opacity.
5. Right panel brightens, "sector-normalized excess return" label fades in below.

## Palette
- Company bar: #4A90D9 (blue)
- Broad market: #7F8C8D (grey)
- Sector benchmark: #E67E22 (orange)
- Outperform indicator: #27AE60 (green)
- Underperform indicator: #E74C3C (red)
- Background: #1A1A2E
