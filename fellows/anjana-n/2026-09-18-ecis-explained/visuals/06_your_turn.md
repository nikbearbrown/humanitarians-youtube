# B06 — Your Turn

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Wide pullback of the full ECIS architecture with this episode's additions glowing.

## Elements

### Full architecture
- The complete pipeline from B01, but now with new nodes added:
  - Consensus delta node (connected to prediction model).
  - Surprise score badge (attached to signal output).
  - Correlation heatmap thumbnail (miniaturized, to the side).
  - Sector benchmark node (replacing the old broad market node in the scoring section).

### Glow sequence
- Each new addition glows gold briefly in sequence: consensus, surprise, correlation, sector.
- Then all settle to their resting state.

### Title card
- Architecture fades, centered text: "ECIS Episode 7: The Bigger Picture."
- Fade to dark.

## Animation sequence
1. Full architecture visible (1s).
2. Consensus delta node glows (0.5s).
3. Surprise score badge glows (0.5s).
4. Correlation heatmap glows (0.5s).
5. Sector benchmark glows (0.5s).
6. All settle (1s).
7. Fade to title card (2s).
8. Hold, fade to black.

## Palette
- New feature glow: #F1C40F (gold)
- Existing pipeline: standard ECIS colors (blue, purple, teal, green)
- Title text: #EAEAEA
- Background: #1A1A2E
