# B03 — Surprise Score

## Composition type
Remotion

## Layout
Dark stage. Two scenarios side by side on top, scatter plot below.

## Elements

### Left scenario: High surprise
- Company arrow pointing up (raised). Consensus arrow flat (maintained).
- Visible gap between them.
- Badge: "surprise: high" in orange (#E67E22).

### Right scenario: Low surprise
- Both arrows pointing up.
- Minimal gap.
- Badge: "surprise: low" in muted grey (#95A5A6).

### Scatter plot (lower half)
- X-axis: "surprise score." Y-axis: "confidence."
- Dots cluster showing inverse relationship: higher surprise correlates with lower assigned confidence.
- A few high-surprise, low-confidence dots highlighted in orange.

## Animation sequence
1. Left scenario animates: company arrow up, consensus arrow flat, gap highlights, "high" badge appears.
2. Right scenario animates simultaneously: both arrows up, small gap, "low" badge appears.
3. Both scenarios shift upward, scatter plot fades in below.
4. Dots populate the scatter plot, showing the inverse pattern.

## Palette
- High surprise: #E67E22 (orange)
- Low surprise: #95A5A6 (muted grey)
- Company arrows: #4A90D9 (blue)
- Consensus arrows: #7F8C8D (grey)
- Scatter dots: #E67E22 (orange) and #4A90D9 (blue)
- Background: #1A1A2E
