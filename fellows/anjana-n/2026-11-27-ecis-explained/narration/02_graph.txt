A company's signals don't exist in isolation. A Graph Attention Network now models the entire twenty-five-ticker universe as a network — companies as nodes, relationships as edges. Contagion links, supply chain connections, and sector membership all become edges with learnable weights. The model learns which connections carry predictive information and which don't. A company surrounded by neighbors whose language is a different profile than one with identical signals surrounded by stable peers. Same numbers, different context — and the graph captures that context.
