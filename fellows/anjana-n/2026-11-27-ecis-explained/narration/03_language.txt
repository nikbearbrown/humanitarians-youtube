Three upgrades to how the system reads language. First, a language model fine-tuned on the full transcript corpus — it now understands how executives frame guidance, not just general English. Every downstream NLP task gets better embeddings. Second, entity linking — when a CEO mentions a competitor by name, the system connects that mention to the competitor's own node in the company graph. Cross-transcript references become structured data. Third, signal-aware summarization generates a three-to-five-paragraph summary of every call, highlighting the sentences that matter most. No more reading full transcripts for routine monitoring.
