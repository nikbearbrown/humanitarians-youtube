Management makes claims and backs them with evidence. Argument mining now extracts that structure — the claim, the supporting evidence, and the implicit reasoning connecting them. It rates argument strength based on evidence specificity. A claim backed by a concrete number scores higher than one supported by vague qualitative language. And it tracks this over time. When a company keeps making bold claims but the evidence behind them gets thinner each quarter, that pattern of weakening arguments is itself a signal — and the system now detects it.
