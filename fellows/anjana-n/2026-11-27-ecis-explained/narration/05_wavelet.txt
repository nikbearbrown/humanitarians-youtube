Not every signal moves at the same speed. Wavelet decomposition separates each signal into frequency bands. Low-frequency components capture multi-year structural trends — these feed the Temporal Fusion Transformer for long-horizon forecasting. Mid-frequency components capture annual or semi-annual cycles — these feed Prophet for seasonality detection. And high-frequency components capture sharp quarter-to-quarter spikes — unexpected disruptions that don't fit either pattern. Each frequency band goes to the model built to handle it. One signal, three time scales, three different insights.
