Most RAG systems treat every document as a wall of text. They strip out formatting, flatten tables into rows of words, ignore charts entirely, and turn code blocks into paragraphs. The information is still there — technically — but the structure that gave it meaning is gone. A revenue table becomes a jumble of numbers without column headers. A flowchart becomes nothing. A code function loses its indentation and syntax. Text-only chunking destroys exactly the content that's hardest to reconstruct.
