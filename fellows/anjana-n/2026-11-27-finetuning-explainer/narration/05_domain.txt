Here's the practical takeaway. On domain-specific tasks, a smaller model fine-tuned on domain data often outperforms a much larger general-purpose model. A seven-billion-parameter model that's seen thousands of financial transcripts understands corporate language better than a seventy-billion-parameter model that hasn't. Scale helps, but domain knowledge compounds. The model that speaks your language, even if it's smaller, usually gives you better answers than the model that speaks every language equally. Fine-tuning isn't just an optimization. It's how you make the model yours.
