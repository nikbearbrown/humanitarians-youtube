Before any fine-tuning happens, a pre-trained language model already knows a lot. Billions of parameters trained on broad internet text encode grammar in the lower layers, facts and semantics in the middle layers, and higher-level reasoning patterns in the upper layers. It can write, summarize, and answer questions out of the box. But it speaks general English. It doesn't know that "guidance" in a corporate context means a forward-looking earnings estimate, not directions to a destination. The knowledge is broad but not deep in any one domain.
