Task-specific fine-tuning is different. Instead of learning a domain's language broadly, you teach the model to do one thing well. You add a small task head on top — a classifier, a regressor, whatever your task needs — and train on labeled examples. Text paired with the correct output. Gradients flow backward from the task head through the upper layers, adjusting the representations to serve that specific task. Lower layers change less because syntax and grammar are already useful as they are. The model doesn't learn a new language. It learns a new skill.
