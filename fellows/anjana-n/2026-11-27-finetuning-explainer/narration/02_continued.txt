Continued pre-training fixes that. You take the pre-trained model and keep training it — same objective, masked language modeling — but on your domain's text. Financial transcripts, whatever your field produces. Every layer updates. The model's internal associations shift. Words that had generic meanings develop domain-specific weight. The statistical patterns of how experts in your field write — sentence structure, terminology, common phrasings — get absorbed into the model's representations. It still knows general English, but now it speaks your language natively.
