# Beat 5 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~7 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The expanded layer collapses back into its rectangular block with a satisfying click. It rejoins the vertical stack from Beat 1.

**BERT (0-3s):**
On "Stack twelve" the stack shows 12 blocks, all glowing gently in sequence from bottom to top. A label appears beside the stack: "BERT. 12 layers."

**GPT (3-5s):**
On "Stack ninety-six" the stack dramatically extends upward. More blocks appear, the stack grows tall, scrolling beyond the visible frame. Label: "GPT. 96 layers."

But each block is still the same four operations. The camera briefly flashes into one of the new blocks: same attention, same feed-forward, same residual, same norm. Identical.

**Title (5-7s):**
The stack fades. Title text lands:

**What Happens Inside a Transformer Layer**
*Four operations. One building block. Every modern language model.*

Hold. Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Technical notes

- The collapse back into the stack closes the "microscope" metaphor from Beat 1
- The BERT to GPT scale jump should feel dramatic, the stack growing visually tall
- The brief flash inside a GPT block reinforces: same four operations, just more of them
- Title subtitle emphasizes universality: this one building block is everywhere
