# Beat 4 — The AI Edge

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~15 seconds

## What the viewer sees

A central AI node pulses at the center of a dark screen — clean, geometric, glowing. Six data streams radiate outward in a star pattern, each ending at a labeled source node:

| Direction | Label | Visual content |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Top-left | Order Books | Scrolling bid/ask numbers in green/red |
| Top-right | Social Sentiment | Tweet-like text snippets scrolling |
| Right | Whale Wallets | Wallet address fragments + large $ amounts |
| Bottom-right | Liquidation Maps | Heat map cells flashing |
| Bottom-left | News Feeds | Headline text scrolling |
| Left | On-Chain Data | Block hashes and transaction counts |

Each stream activates as the narration names it. Data particles flow inward along the streams toward the central node.

At the center, the AI node processes and outputs decisions: "BUY" / "HOLD" / "EXIT" cycling in rapid succession. A millisecond counter in the bottom-right corner shows: "Reaction time: 0.003s"

On "The edge is not intelligence. The edge is presence." — all six streams pulse simultaneously, the central node glows bright, and the reaction counter freezes at 0.003s.

## Mood

Power. Not brute force — quiet, always-on omnipresence.

## Technical notes

- The central node should feel calm, not aggressive — steady pulse, not frantic
- Data streams: thin lines with animated particles flowing inward
- The content in each stream should look real but scroll too fast to read — it's about volume, not readability
- The 0.003s counter is the key data point — make it prominent
- The final simultaneous pulse is the visual climax of the entire video
- Color palette: dark background, blue/cyan for AI node, warm accents for data streams
