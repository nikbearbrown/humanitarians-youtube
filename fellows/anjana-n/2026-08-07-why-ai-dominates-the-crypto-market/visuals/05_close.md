# Beat 5 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~8 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The globe from Beat 1 returns — same rotation, same cities, same trade lines. But now the three human silhouettes are gone entirely. The AI node sits at the center, calm and bright, with all trade lines flowing through it.

On "That is not the future" — the globe dims slightly.

On "That is Tuesday" — the title text lands hard, center screen:

**AI Doesn't Sleep.**
**Markets Don't Wait.**

The text is large, clean, sans-serif. It holds for two beats in silence. Then fades to the Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Mood

Definitive. Matter-of-fact. Not a warning — just a statement of what's already true.

## Technical notes

- Reuse the exact globe asset from Beat 1 — visual continuity
- The title text is the final image people remember — make it large and clean
- "That is Tuesday" is the punchline — let the TTS pause before it, and let the visual land with weight
- No animation on the title text — it just appears, solid and still
- Fade to outro should be slow and deliberate, not abrupt
