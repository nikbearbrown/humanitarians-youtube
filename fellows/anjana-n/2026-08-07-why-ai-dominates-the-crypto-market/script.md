# AI Doesn't Sleep, Markets Don't Wait — Video Script

**Skill:** ai-explainer
**Target length:** ~60 seconds
**Voice:** am_onyx (Onyx)
**Style:** Vox-style narration, punchy and energetic, accessible to a general audience

---

## Beat 1 — The Hook

**Duration:** ~10 seconds

**Narration:**

Right now, somewhere in the world, a market is moving. A token is crashing in Seoul. A contract is liquidating in London. A whale is buying in New York. And nobody is watching all of it at once. Except the machines.

**Visual direction:**

Dark screen. A rotating globe appears with three cities pulsing — Seoul, London, New York — connected by glowing trade lines. Price tickers animate around the edges, numbers moving fast. On the final line, the human silhouettes at each city dim out and are replaced by a single pulsing AI node at the center of the globe.

---

## Beat 2 — The Shift

**Duration:** ~12 seconds

**Narration:**

For a hundred years, markets had opening bells and closing bells. Traders worked the floor from nine to four, went home, and the world paused. Then crypto arrived, and the clock broke. Bitcoin trades at three AM on a Sunday. Ethereum moves on holidays. There is no closing bell anymore.

**Visual direction:**

A classic stock exchange clock showing 9:30 AM — 4:00 PM, with a green "OPEN" bar between them. The clock animates normally, then cracks down the middle. The crack spreads, the clock face shatters, and behind it is a 24-hour circle with no start or end — just continuous motion. Crypto logos (BTC, ETH) orbit the circle.

---

## Beat 3 — The Human Limit

**Duration:** ~12 seconds

**Narration:**

A human trader needs sleep. Eight hours off the screen every night. Weekends. Vacations. Blinks, bathroom breaks, a moment of distraction. In a market that never pauses, every second you are not watching is a second something can move against you. One flash crash at two AM and your position is gone before your alarm goes off.

**Visual direction:**

Split screen. Left side: a person at a desk, alert, watching screens — a green "ACTIVE" badge above them. A clock fast-forwards through the day. At night, the person walks away — the badge flips to red "OFFLINE." Right side: a continuous price chart that keeps moving 24/7 regardless. A sharp red candle drops at 2:14 AM while the left side shows the person sleeping. A notification pops up on a phone on the nightstand — too late.

---

## Beat 4 — The AI Edge

**Duration:** ~15 seconds

**Narration:**

AI does not sleep. It watches ten thousand pairs across a hundred exchanges simultaneously. It reads the order book, scans social sentiment, tracks whale wallets, monitors liquidation cascades — and reacts in milliseconds. Not because it is smarter than you. Because it is always there. The edge is not intelligence. The edge is presence.

**Visual direction:**

A central AI node pulses at the center of the screen. Data streams radiate outward in six directions, each labeled: Order Books, Social Sentiment, Whale Wallets, Liquidation Maps, News Feeds, On-Chain Data. Each stream animates with live-looking data — numbers, charts, tweet-like text snippets, wallet addresses. The streams converge back to the center where a decision output flashes: "BUY / HOLD / EXIT" cycling rapidly. A millisecond counter in the corner shows reaction time: "0.003s."

---

## Beat 5 — The Close

**Duration:** ~8 seconds

**Narration:**

Markets don't wait for you to wake up. They don't care about your time zone, your schedule, or your sleep. AI doesn't need any of those things. That is not the future. That is Tuesday.

**Visual direction:**

The globe from Beat 1 returns, but now the AI node is at the center with all trade lines flowing through it. The three human silhouettes from the opening are gone. Text lands on screen: "AI Doesn't Sleep. Markets Don't Wait." Hold for two beats. Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

---

## Production Notes

**Total estimated duration:** ~57 seconds of narration + bookends = ~65 seconds total

**Voice:** am_onyx — punchy, authoritative, slightly urgent. This script has shorter sentences and a faster rhythm than a typical explainer.

**Visual mix:** All Remotion — no Manim needed. This is motion graphics, not diagrams. The globe (Beats 1 and 5) is the recurring visual anchor. Keep the palette dark with bright accent lines for data streams.

**Pacing:** Sentences are deliberately short. Let the TTS breathe between them — small pauses add weight. Do not rush Beat 4; it is the payload.
