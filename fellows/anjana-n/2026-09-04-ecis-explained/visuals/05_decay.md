# Beat 5 — Signal Decay

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~8 seconds

## What the viewer sees

**Decaying signal (0-4s):**
A signal card for Company A: "raised, 0.84."

A horizontal timeline extends to the right with three checkpoints:
- 30 days: green checkmark, "Correct"
- 90 days: amber question mark, "Marginal"
- 180 days: red X, "Wrong"

The signal's confidence bar visually fades from green to red along the timeline. Tag: "Decaying. Short-term noise."

**Persistent signal (4-7s):**
A second signal card appears below for Company B: "lowered, 0.79."

Same three checkpoints:
- 30 days: green checkmark
- 90 days: green checkmark
- 180 days: green checkmark

The confidence bar stays solid green throughout. Tag: "Persistent. Genuine read."

**Comparison (7-8s):**
Both signals visible simultaneously. Label below both: "Same confidence. Different shelf life."

## Technical notes

- Use Company A and Company B, no real ticker names
- The fading confidence bar for the decaying signal is the key visual metaphor
- The persistent signal's solid bar provides the contrast
- Both signals have similar starting confidence (0.84 vs 0.79) to emphasize that initial confidence alone doesn't tell you shelf life
- Keep it clean and horizontal, same timeline style as Episode 4's quarterly view
