# Beat 4 — What Came Before

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

**Primary timeline (0-7s):**
A horizontal timeline appears with four quarterly markers, labeled for one ticker: "Company A"

Signals appear at each quarter with a slight stagger:
- Q1: green up arrow, "raised"
- Q2: green up arrow, "raised"
- Q3: green up arrow, "raised"
- Q4: red down arrow, "lowered"

Trend tags animate in between the markers, connecting them:
- Q1→Q2: "consecutive_raise" in a green connecting band
- Q2→Q3: "consecutive_raise" in a green connecting band
- Q3→Q4: "reversal" in a red connecting band that pulses — this is the break in the pattern

The reversal tag glows. It stands out against the three green bands before it.

**Secondary timeline (7-10s):**
A second, smaller timeline appears below for another ticker: "Company B"

Four quarters, all showing: maintained, maintained, maintained, maintained. Four horizontal arrows, all blue.
Tag: "stable_maintained" as a single calm band across all quarters.

The contrast between the two timelines is the point: Company A is volatile with a dramatic reversal, Company B is steady. Both patterns are now tagged and measurable.

**Label (10-12s):**
"Patterns, not snapshots." appears below both timelines.

## Technical notes

- The timelines should be clean and horizontal — left to right, oldest to newest
- Up arrows for raised (green), down arrows for lowered (red), horizontal arrows for maintained (blue)
- The "reversal" tag should be visually distinct — pulsing, red, noticeable
- The Company B timeline is calmer — steady blue, no drama
- Both timelines visible simultaneously for the contrast
- Ticker labels should be prominent above each timeline
