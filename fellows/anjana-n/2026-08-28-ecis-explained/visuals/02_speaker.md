# Beat 2 — Who Said It

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~15 seconds

## What the viewer sees

**The quote (0-2s):**
A quote card appears center screen: "We are raising our full-year revenue outlook."

**CFO version (2-6s):**
Below the quote, a speaker label animates in: "CFO — Sarah Chen" with a small avatar silhouette. A weight badge appears beside it: "1.0" in bright green.

The signal confidence reads 0.85. A clean equation appears below:
0.85 × 1.0 = **0.85**

The output signal glows green — strong, retained.

**Analyst version (6-10s):**
The quote stays identical. But the speaker label swaps with a smooth transition: "Analyst — David Park." The weight badge changes: "0.3" in dim amber.

Same confidence 0.85. The equation updates:
0.85 × 0.3 = **0.26**

The output signal dims — faded, suppressed. Same words, dramatically different outcome.

**Role hierarchy (10-15s):**
The quote card slides left. On the right, a vertical stack of speaker roles appears, each with its weight:
- CFO: 1.0 (bright green bar, full width)
- CEO: 0.8 (green bar, 80% width)
- COO: 0.7 (green bar, 70%)
- IR: 0.6 (amber bar, 60%)
- Analyst: 0.3 (dim amber bar, 30%)
- Operator: 0.0 (greyed out, no bar)

Label at the bottom: "Same words. Different authority."

## Technical notes

- The CFO-to-analyst swap is the visual payoff — same quote, same confidence, different result
- The equation should be clean and readable — this is a multiplication, not a complex formula
- The role hierarchy bars should feel like a simple ranking, not a detailed chart
- Speaker names are fictional — don't use real people
- Green for high weight, amber for medium, grey for zero
