## What it covers

This report covers the delivery of a ~60-second dual-format explainer video (16:9 widescreen and 9:16 portrait) establishing that paired contrastive trajectories prevent unconstrained context pollution and embedding similarity traps where naive cosine retrieval fails to detect logical runtime errors. The technical demonstration outlines prefix alignment to pinpoint exact decision forks, introduces a compact delta schema, and illustrates dual-sided retrieval (“do this” versus “avoid that”) coupled with background dreaming consolidation. Produced collaboratively through human research direction and AI-driven Remotion scene builds voiced by Kokoro am_onyx.
