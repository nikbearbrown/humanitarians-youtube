## De-sensationalised — claims the paper makes that this reel does NOT repeat

- **"Comparable performance to LLM-based methods."** The abstract's phrasing.
  Dropped, because on LiHuaWorld MiniRAG + gpt-4o-mini (54.08) is *below*
  LightRAG + gpt-4o-mini (56.90). The reel makes the narrower, defensible claim:
  MiniRAG beats the lightweight alternatives **on small models**.
- **"State-of-the-art across all evaluation settings"** (§1). Dropped — it does
  not survive Table 1's own gpt-4o-mini row on LiHuaWorld.
- **Storage.** Only the ratio (~25%) is spoken and shown. Figure 3 is a scatter
  plot with no published MB table, so no absolute megabyte figure appears in the
  reel (REBUILD LAW: orderings and anchors only when exact data is unpublished).

## Added because the paper underplays it — DOUBLE-CHECK LAW

**B11 exists for this reason.** MiniRAG's higher accuracy on MultiHop-RAG comes
with a materially higher error rate (28.44% vs 11.78%). Both figures are the
paper's own; the framing is ours.

## Not claimed anywhere in the reel

- That LiHuaWorld is real user data. It is **GPT-4-generated with human
  curation** (Appendix, "Event Generation with Human Oversight"), for stated
  privacy reasons. The reel never calls it real chat logs.
- Any latency, energy, or memory figure. The paper publishes none.
- Anything about models or systems released after 26 Jan 2025.

## Dating risk

Model names (Phi-3.5, Qwen2.5, GLM-Edge, MiniCPM3, gpt-4o-mini) are load-bearing
— they are the experimental conditions, not incidental version numbers — so they
stay. No other version-sensitive claim appears.
