## 2. Work Completed in the Current Period

**Sections revised:** 2.4–2.6, 3.3–3.5, 4.2, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6. Every claim in
these sections was verified against its source, and every section was
rewritten to answer the team's three questions: what has been done, why it
works or fails, and what the evidence supports recommending.

Principal results:

- **Unsupported figures removed.** The draft's claim that reported accuracy
  overestimates deployment performance by 10–30 percentage points had no
  source and was replaced with the accurate statement that this gap has not
  been measured. The claim that no study reaches Tier 1 validation, stated
  without qualification in July, is now scoped to the databases and years
  actually searched.
- **Uncited claims replaced with controlled evidence.** For example, the
  claim in Section 4.4 that multi-task learning "often" improves performance
  was replaced with a leakage-audited comparison showing no significant
  advantage.
- **Reference errors identified and corrected.** These include a reference
  attributed to the wrong authors (U-55, now credited to Georgiev et al.,
  *PNAS*, 2024) and a summary-table citation for a study that did not use
  Raman spectroscopy.
- **Evidence scoped accurately.** In Sections 4.2, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6, the
  strongest available evidence came from conventional Raman spectroscopy or
  from outside spectroscopy. Each revision states that scope beside the
  result, identifying the absence of SERS-specific studies as a research gap.
- **Communication.** Five project update videos documenting each revision,
  and five STEM explainers on SERS topics.
- **Hours.** An estimated 314–324 hours through week 11, as detailed in
  [HOURS.md](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/humanitarians-youtube/blob/main/fellows/kumar-n/HOURS.md).
