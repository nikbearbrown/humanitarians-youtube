The Engine That Says No. — Physics Engine, 6 of 12

The closure check is the moment the physics engine (built for the Medhavy learning platform) decides whether it can answer at all: with what's given, can the equations pin down every unknown? This video covers the three named ways a plan fails, what happens next (marked unverified, logged as a coverage gap), the 60-problem benchmark run with zero wrong answers — because nearly everything was declined — and the time declining was itself a bug. Plus why grade mode refuses to grade against an answer it can't verify. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: trust a tool that's allowed to say "I can't."
