Don't Invent the Mass. — Physics Engine, 4 of 12

The parse step is the one place the physics engine built for the Medhavy learning platform lets an AI model touch the problem. Its job is reading comprehension only: pull out the stated quantities and name what's asked — never solve, rearrange, or pick a value. This video walks through its rules (the engine's own vocabulary, SI units, no constants, "from rest" means zero) and the bug where it invented m = 1.0 for problems with no numbers in them — and how keeping letters as letters let SymPy solve those problems exactly. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: check what an AI read before you check what it got.
