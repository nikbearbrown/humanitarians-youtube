Same Problem, Same Answer. — Physics Engine, 9 of 12

The team ran one physics problem ten times and compared every byte of the output: identical. This video explains why: the solver has no randomness, the only variation comes from which formula gets picked, and the engine decides that before it solves.

The claim: consistency you can explain beats consistency you hope for.
