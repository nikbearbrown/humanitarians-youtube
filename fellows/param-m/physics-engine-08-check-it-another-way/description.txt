Check It Another Way. — Physics Engine, 8 of 12

An answer that looks right isn't checked until something else agrees with it. The physics engine checks every solution three ways: substitute it back, test that it is physically possible, and, on 11 of 31 cards, reach it by an independent route.

The claim: one path to an answer is a guess; a second, independent path is a check.

Paste a problem and an answer into any chatbot and ask:
Here is a problem and an answer: [paste both]. Verify the answer three ways. 1: substitute it back into the original equations and show what is left over. 2: check it is physically possible: signs, sizes, units. 3: reach it by a different method. Say which checks pass and which fail.
