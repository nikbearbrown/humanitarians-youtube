Hints, Not Answers. — Physics Engine, 11 of 12

Every chatbot can give you the answer. The physics engine's tutor mode gives you the next step, and only the next step: a seven-rung hint ladder built from the plan the engine already verified, with no second AI call that could invent a step.

The claim: a good tutor gives you the next step, not the last one.

Paste a problem you are stuck on into any chatbot and ask:
Tutor me on this problem: [paste it]. Do not give the answer. Give one hint at a time, in this order: what is given and asked; the principle; the conditions it needs; the equations; the common mistake; the rearranged formula; the result. Wait for me after each hint.
