## Learning outcome

After watching, a viewer with no physics or coding background can:

1. **Name** the three badges (verified, needs review, unverified) and the "no worse
   than off" floor.
2. **Walk** the seven-rung hint ladder in order on the Atwood problem.
3. **Explain** why the hints come from the plan rather than a second AI call, and
   why symbolic equations made them better.

Measurable: the viewer asks an AI for one hint at a time, in ladder order.

## Audience

Students and general viewers. Defined on first use: *badge* (B01), *floor* (B02),
*principle*, *conditions* (B03), *rearranged formula* (B04), *plan* (B05),
*symbolic* (B06).
