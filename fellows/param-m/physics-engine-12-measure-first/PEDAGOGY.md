## Learning outcome

After watching, a viewer with no physics or coding background can:

1. **Explain** the golden set and the OpenStax cross-check (3.27 vs 3.2667).
2. **Read** the benchmark honestly: 18% scorable; 79% topic coverage is a ceiling.
3. **State** the fine-tuning trigger, why it hasn't fired (coverage, not intelligence),
   and what is still open (baseline never run; nothing deployed).

Measurable: before proposing a bigger model for any problem, the viewer asks what has
been measured and where failures come from.

## Audience

Students and general viewers. Defined on first use: *fine-tuning* (B00), *trigger*,
*plateau* (B01), *golden set* (B02), *benchmark*, *derivation*, *ceiling* (B03),
*bottleneck* (B04), *baseline* (B06).
