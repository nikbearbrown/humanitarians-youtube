What's on a Card. — Physics Engine, 5 of 12

Every equation the physics engine (built for the Medhavy learning platform) uses comes from a formula card written and checked ahead of time. This video opens one real card — spring energy to kinetic energy — field by field: the knowns it needs, what it solves for, its equation, units, when it applies, and the mistake people usually make. Then how the team builds new cards against real benchmark problems, and where the library is still thin. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: a formula is only as good as the conditions that come with it.
