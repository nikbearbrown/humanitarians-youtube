# Pedagogy: Week 29 work video
- Takeaway: for each output of your own pipeline, name who reads it next (script or person), what they do, and what their page must hold; build both pages from one set of facts and check the counts agree.
- Move taught: follow one fact across two renderings; read your own output as its reader.
- Prior knowledge assumed: a pipeline that produces a result file. No finance needed.
- Check for understanding: the viewer writes three lines for one of their own outputs (B11).
