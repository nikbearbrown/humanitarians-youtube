# Script: Two Customers, One Run: Writing a Pipeline's Results for a Program and a Person

Angle D (approved). Structure: one fact followed through two desks (agent log, human report), three chapters by count, reason, call; a turn found by
reading the machine's page as its reader; what keeps the pages in agreement; the viewer's task. Facts verified in FACTCHECK.md (35 of 35).

Visual plan per beat: split screen, left desk (JSON field, monospace) and right desk (report sentence); the same fact lights on both. B08/B09 show the
log line `triggered` flipping and the report line "Stop conditions: 0 of 4 triggered". B11 shows the viewer's prompt as three lines. B12 shows the
recipe status line (DRAFT, three open) and the two PR numbers. Only committed files appear on screen.
