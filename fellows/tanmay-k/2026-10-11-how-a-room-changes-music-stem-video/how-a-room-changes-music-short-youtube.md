He Slowed Down in the Driest Hall Too #Shorts

David Byrne of Talking Heads argues that a room doesn't just hold music, it shapes it. In 2015, two
researchers in Berlin measured how much one cellist changes from room to room: all six Bach cello
suites, seven European concert halls. In the most echoing halls he slowed down. In the driest halls,
he slowed down too. Same answer, opposite questions.

Try it in your room: clap once, then say the sentence at the pace your room seems to want. Did you
speed up, or slow down?
