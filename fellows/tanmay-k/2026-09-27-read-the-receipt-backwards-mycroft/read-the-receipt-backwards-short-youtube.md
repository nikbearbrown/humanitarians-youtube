The receipt said Morgan approved it. Morgan never saw it. #Shorts

Hi, I'm Tanmay Kulkarni, in for Humanitarians AI. An AI agent bought $42.50 of groceries, and the
receipt said Morgan approved it. That receipt came out of an early version of my own build of the
checks Mastercard has described for Agent Pay. So now I read receipts backwards: from the bottom
up, asking which step checked each line before it was written down.

Two lines were being carried along, and both receipts looked perfect: a person the agent didn't
belong to, and a category spelled with a capital H. Both are fixed now, with tests. A seal can show
nobody changed a record, but it can't make the lines true; that comes from the checks before it.
And one line, merchant, stays honestly marked "not checked", because Mastercard hasn't published
how that check works.
