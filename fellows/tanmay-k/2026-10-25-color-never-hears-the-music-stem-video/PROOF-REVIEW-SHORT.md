## Findings from this pass (applied to `SHORT-SCRIPT.md`)

1. **[EDIT, applied] S05's first draft said "happy and strong" / "sad and weak".** The paper's own illustration says Bach's dance conveys "happy, energetic" and Brahms's Adagio "sad, depressive". "Strong" and "weak" were mine. Now "happy and energetic" and "sad"; the dots move to the HAPPY and SAD ends only.
2. **[EDIT, applied] S04 had a four-word fragment ("Feeling.")**, as did S06 ("Now swap them.") and S07 ("One limit:"). All merged into full sentences, since Kokoro mangles short ones.
3. **[EDIT, applied] S07's change cadence** was 2.67 s apart; a fourth change was added so the maximum gap is 2.5 s.
4. **[EDIT, applied] S01 widened from 8 s to 10 s**, since it carries two tunes under 24 words.
5. **Kept:** no essay-derived claim in the Short; the S04 source line says "Correlation, not proof"; S08 says the full film is where the task lives.

## What the render gate must still prove

- Source lines ≥ 34 px, none in the right rail, closing title inside the safe area for about 3 s.
- A change in every 2.5 s window at 540×960; no stretch over 3 s.
- The full gate: 2160×3840, ink zones, type size, figures on screen when spoken, no hard cuts, loop-friendly last frame, 48 kHz audio and loudness, no foreign handle, duration.
- The ear test: after audio exists, listen once for rush. A script cannot prove it does not feel rushed; it can only remove the causes.
