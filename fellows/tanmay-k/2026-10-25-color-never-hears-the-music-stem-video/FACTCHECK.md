## Corrections the paper forced (draft 1 → draft 2)

1. Draft 1's "calm–agitated" scale was not the paper's. The paper's dimensions are happy/sad and strong/weak; angry/calm is *not* a single bipolar dimension in its data, and is its weakest correlation (0.89, the low end of the quoted range).
2. Draft 1 treated fast-minor as a case "the study doesn't hand you". The study crossed tempo and mode fully, so the case is in the data and the figure answers it.
3. Draft 1 called the scales "our simplification". They are the paper's.
