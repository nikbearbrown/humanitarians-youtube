## 5. Frame-difference activity

The literal test (consecutive frames within 0.1% mean absolute difference) is not meaningful here: every element that fades in changes only a small part of a 480x270 frame, so the mean difference stays under 0.1% even while the picture is changing (a first run flagged whole beats as static for that reason). The measure used instead: a frame is *static* if fewer than 60 of its 129,600 pixels (0.046%) differ by more than 10 gray levels from the frame **one second later**; slow cloud drift (about 5 px/s) and fades register as change, a truly still frame does not.
