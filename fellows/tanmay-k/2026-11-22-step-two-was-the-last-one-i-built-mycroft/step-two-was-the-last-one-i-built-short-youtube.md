Your shortcut passed the bad file. #Shorts

My recipe has six steps and I built step two last. While it was missing, the recipe said step three reads the files directly "since step two is still open", and step five reads step three's output "since step four is still open". Those are stand-ins, shortcuts for steps that did not exist yet. So I gave them a wrong file, deliberately, on a scratch copy: two digits switched in one price, still a number, still valid JSON. Step one stopped. Step three, on its own, said pass. Step five priced the portfolio 1,800 higher than the real run. Then the real step two read the same folder and turned the file away: it differs from the hash recorded when the sample was made.
