## What the film teaches (for the record)

- **Subject, plain words (B02):** one example from Sutton & Barto's textbook, a four-square corridor
  where the best move turns out to be a weighted coin.
- **Framework (B02, before any answer):** every rule for this corridor is one chance of pressing
  right. Each answer is placed on that line and scored.

  survives, look for someone who'd exploit a pattern.
