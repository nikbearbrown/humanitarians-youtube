## Findings

1. **[EDIT, applied] Context.** Mycroft is explained, "named shocks" is defined on screen as well as aloud, and the AI list appears as a row before it is used.
2. **[EDIT, applied] Pace.** The packed lines were split; S03 reads a four-row list one row per phrase.
3. **Positive framing:** unchanged from draft 1: nothing negative; "added up" and "matched" are the only verbs about the workflow.
4. **Risk, optional:** ticker symbols on screen (NVDA 40 · AMD 30 · TSM 30). The portfolio is called invented in S01 and S02; the alternative is "chip stock A / B / C".
5. **Not yet measured:** the audio rate and the retimed schedule; the 11-point compiled-file gate.

## What works

- One idea per line, and the opening number is the object that is explained and then returned.
- The arithmetic is on screen term by term while it is spoken, and the list the sum comes from is shown before it is used.
