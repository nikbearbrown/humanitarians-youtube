from-scratch ML notebooks, built with Claude on a free-only local toolkit (Kokoro TTS,

**GitHub:** [@Chinmay1220](https://github.com/Chinmay1220)

## What's in this folder

11 video projects, each in a dated `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug/` subfolder holding its
`beat_sheet.json`, README, gate paperwork, build sources and `FRICTIONAL.md`.

Master MP4s are **not** committed — this repository keeps large distribution media out of
Git. The beat sheet is the source of truth and each reel rebuilds from it.

Two strands run through these. The **teardowns** check a notebook's own claims by
re-running its code — three of them found real defects, and the figures on screen are
recomputed at build time under assertions rather than transcribed. The **kid-friendly
cuts** carry the same findings in plain words and cartoon metaphor, with no technical
content softened into being wrong.

## Frictional log

Every work subfolder here carries its own `FRICTIONAL.md` — a dated record of the process
behind that specific piece of work, kept beside the evidence it describes: what was tried
and expected, where it resisted and what was done next, what Claude or another person
contributed and what was accepted, changed or rejected, and what is now understood or
still open. Append as you go; never rewrite an earlier entry. It is not graded and not a
performance review. See <https://www.humanitarians.ai/fellows> for what an entry contains.
