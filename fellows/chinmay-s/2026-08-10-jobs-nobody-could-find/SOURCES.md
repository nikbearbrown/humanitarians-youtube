# SOURCES — The Jobs Nobody Could Find

## Primary source
- **addams.pdf** — "Chinmay Sawant — Addams Reports", Humanitarians.ai,
  contract renewal May 4 – July 24, 2026 (12 weeks). A weekly OPT engineering
  ship log for the **Jobsekr** job board. Supplied by the user as the build
  source on 2026-07-28. Each week records: Executive Summary, OPT Hours,
  Primary Objective, Work Completed, a Claude Artifact, Substack status,
  Blockers, and Next Steps.

## What was drawn from it
- Weeks 1–2 → Act I (the five-tier visa-classification pipeline).
- Weeks 3–8 → Act II (JobPosting JSON-LD, page metadata, the sitemap tree,
  programmatic landing pages, FAQ rich snippets).
- Weeks 9–11 → Act III (Search Console, the SEO audit, end-to-end
  verification, documentation + handoff).
- Week 12 → the coda (the blocked AI+1 onboarding, hours below 20, reported
  honestly).

## Corrections / editorial choices
- Individual names in the source (PM, "Engineer 3", etc.) are NOT spoken in
  narration — the film is about the systems, not the people.
- The Substack-publication-access blocker (recurring weeks 5–12) is folded into
  the coda's theme (honest reporting of what didn't happen) rather than
  narrated week by week.
