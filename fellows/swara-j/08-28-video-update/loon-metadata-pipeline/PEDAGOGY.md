# GATE P - PEDAGOGY

## Audience
Biologists and conservation researchers who understand field footage but do not need machine-learning jargon to follow the data workflow.

## Learning spine
1. Drone footage can carry time, place, altitude, and technical context.
2. Metadata extraction keeps that context attached to videos and frames.
3. Video, frame, timestamp, location, and annotation form one research chain.
4. PostgreSQL and PostGIS make the resulting observations queryable.

## SHOW check
Every beat has a visible event: metadata layers stack, fields collect, the pipeline flows, or the evidence chain resolves into a research record.
