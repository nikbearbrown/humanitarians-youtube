# PEDAGOGY — "Stem Separation: Estimation, Not Extraction" (v2)

Narration sign-off record. The audience is a smart non-technical viewer —
someone who uses audio tools but has no background in signal processing. The
reel fails if they leave thinking stem separation is a solved or reversible
process.

## The one thing this video has to land

**The model estimates — it does not recover.** Every beat either builds
toward this sentence or unpacks its consequences. The word "estimates"
does the most work: it implies confidence without certainty, which is exactly
the epistemic stance the viewer needs when deciding whether to use a stem.

## Visual grammar (v2)

The three purpose-built scenes (B01, B02, B04) alternate with the two
text-based artifact beats (B03, B05), so no two consecutive beats share the
same visual register. The opener and outro are shared chassis components —
consistent with the first video.

**Why the bleed ghost in B02 (StemSepStemOutput) matters:** Each stem box
already shows a faint ghost of the adjacent stem at low opacity. This is not
a mistake — it seeds the concept before B04 names it. A viewer who noticed
the ghost in B02 gets a payoff in B04; a viewer who missed it still gets the
full explanation.
