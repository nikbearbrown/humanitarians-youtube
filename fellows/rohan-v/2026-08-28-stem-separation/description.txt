Stem Separation: Estimation, Not Extraction — Humanitarians AI

A stem separator doesn't recover your original tracks. It never had them. It
receives one flat mixed file and produces its best guess at what the
contributions probably were. This is the difference between estimation and
extraction — and it changes how you should use the output.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
What the model actually receives (one mixed file; the originals are gone).
Why separation is estimation, not reversal — and why that's structural, not
fixable with a better model. What bleed, leftovers, and metallic smear are and
why they happen. Three things to check before trusting a stem.
