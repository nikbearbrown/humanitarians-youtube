Homes of Hope Songs: Proof of Concept Progress — Humanitarians AI

Lyrical Literacy wants to give every girl in a Homes of Hope home a song in her own language that
builds real English as she sings. This week: a small proof of concept in English and Hindi.

The method is call and response: the lead sings a line in Hindi, the group answers with the same line
in English, so each English phrase arrives with its meaning. Each song comes in two tiers, half and
half, then mostly English, on the same melody. Two themes, four songs, drafted with Claude and
generated in Suno; the second round rebuilt the sound around dhol, tabla and sitar.

The songs sing about dreams and daily routines, never names, places or backstories, and no real
girl's voice or likeness is used. Next: a native Hindi review, then a short proof of concept video
for the pitch. If you speak Hindi fluently, I'd welcome your review.
