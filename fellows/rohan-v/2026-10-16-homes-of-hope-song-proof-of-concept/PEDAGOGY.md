# PEDAGOGY — "Homes of Hope Songs: Proof of Concept Progress"

A progress report under 90 seconds, so each beat carries one thing the viewer can check or use.

| Beat | Job | What the viewer can do after it |
|---|---|---|
| B00 ASK | what the proof of concept is | say what's being built and for whom |
| B01 THE METHOD | call and response, with the songs' own lines | explain how a song teaches the English |
| B02 TWO TIERS | one melody at two levels | see how a girl moves up without a new song |
| B03 THE SOUND | what the revision changed | judge the sound direction |
| B04 SAFEGUARDS | what the lyrics never use | trust the work is safe for girls in care |
| B05 NEXT | picks, review, the pitch video; a call for Hindi reviewers | help, if they speak Hindi |
| B06 OUTRO | sign-off | — |

## Choices worth defending

- **Show the method, not people.** The subject is minors in care, so nothing on screen is a person
  or a likeness; the lyric lines carry the idea.
- **Safeguards get a full beat.** In a 90-second update it would be easy to cut; it is the part a
  partner organisation will check first.
- **A concrete ask.** The Hindi still needs a fluent reviewer, so the video asks for one.
