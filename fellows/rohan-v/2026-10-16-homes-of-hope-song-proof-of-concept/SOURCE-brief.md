## The source

Rohan V.'s project summary (PDF, 5 pages), kept outside the repository because it names team members.
Its content, as used:

- **Goal.** Lyrical Literacy would give every girl in every Homes of Hope home a song in her own
  language that builds real English. Before pitching the full vision (eight language pairings, two
  tiers), a narrow proof of concept: English + Hindi, both tiers, two songs per tier.
- **Method.** Call and response: the lead sings Hindi, the group answers in English, each English
  line cued by its meaning. Tier 1 (Foundation) about 50/50; Tier 2 (Bridge) mostly English with Hindi
  as a thread; same melody and message.
- **Songs.** Two themes: "Sisters Side by Side" (belonging, daily life) and "I Will Be" (dreams,
  courage). Lyrics, Suno styles prompts and meta tags drafted with Claude; versions generated in Suno.
- **Sound revision.** First versions violin-heavy; "I Will Be" reworked around dhol, tabla and sitar,
  108 BPM (Tier 1) and 110 BPM (Tier 2), with a powerful lead and big group answers.
- **Safeguards.** No names, schools, jobs or stories; nothing about anyone's past; no likeness, voice
  or photo of a real girl; home-life lines to staff review; Hindi still needs native validation.
- **Next.** The team picks versions; Hindi pronunciation and meaning review; staff review; the proof of
  concept video for the pitch.

## Who it is for

Humanitarians AI and Lyrical Literacy: the team, reviewers and anyone who could help with the Hindi.
