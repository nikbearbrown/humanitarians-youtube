# PROMPTS — "How Spotify Picks Songs"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a
deterministic Remotion render of recorded data. Two drawings are illustrations and say so: B01's listener rows
and B02's grid corner and taste-number values.

## The library-first gate

| Need | Closest in the library | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| plays, skips, saves; two listeners who share songs | none | miss → **`RecsSignals`** |
| a listener × song grid, mostly empty | `TempoGrid` (one square per groove) | near-miss: its idea, rebuilt as a grid with an ILLUSTRATION label → **`RecsGrid`** |
| every song placed by its taste numbers | none | miss → **`RecsNeighbours`** |
| three recommenders compared, one bar each | `CapNoise` (one bar per noise level) | near-miss: its idea, rebuilt for hit rates → **`RecsAccuracy`** |
| share of listening vs share of picks; a new song with no plays | none | miss → **`RecsPopularity`** |
| before and after exploration | `Stat` pairs in earlier reels | near-miss: reused inside → **`RecsExplore`** |

`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `HaiApplyCard` (`phoneType` and `sparkScale` on the phone) and `HaiTitleOutro` are reused
as-is.

## Shared kit — `scenes/recs/`

- `recsData.ts`, **generated** by `export_recs_data.py`: sizes, the three hit rates, the same-genre shares, the
  example song and listener, popularity bias, both feedback loops, the cold-start rank, and every song's 2-D
  position (`pca`).
- `recsKit.tsx`: `SpotifyTag` (LOGO LAW: Spotify's mark beside its name), `Tiles` (a row of song squares,
  dashed when hidden), `GENRE_COL` (one muted hue per genre); `Chip`, `Stat`, `PhoneBaseline`, `LandBaseline`,
  `Caps` reused.
