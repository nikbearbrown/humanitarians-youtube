## One new component — `HaiDocPages` (+ `HaiDocPages916`)

A fellow's written deliverable shown as itself. Whole pages at video size are unreadable and
fail GATE T's type floor, so the scene shows **crops** of the real PDF (300 dpi), cut tight
enough that their body type stays above the floor, one at a time on a sheet with two blank
sheets behind it for the document's thickness (no thumbnails with unreadable text). Each crop
slides in on its cue word; the section label sits under the sheet; the right column carries
the document's facts as designed type (46 pages, 12 sections, v1.0 Word file) and the credit
line: drafted by Claude, reviewed by Rohan V. Portrait shows each crop 2.2× larger with its left edge
pinned (no pan), so a wide crop stays readable at the phone floor and every line starts in view.

Crops (B01, in order): title, contents, Suno's three-ingredient table (5.4), Midjourney's seven
questions (6.4), who does what (8.1), the first-week checklist (1.2).
