Writing the Lyrical Literacy Fellows Handbook — Humanitarians AI

Until now, getting started on Lyrical Literacy meant asking around: which account, which link,
who approves what. This week all of it went into one document: the Fellows Handbook, version
1.0. Forty-six pages in twelve sections: getting into every tool, full guides to Suno and
Midjourney, the weekly reporting rules, the whole video workflow, and how renewals work,
opening with a first-week checklist.

It was drafted with Claude from the program's own pages and our nine tutorials, then reviewed
and corrected: rebuilt in the website's look, three review comments applied, and two rule
changes folded in during the week. It closes the first item on my renewal plan, a month early.
Next: testing two more AI video editors in November.
