# FEEDBACK — "What MP3 Throws Away"

Reviewer notes. Empty until someone reviews it.

## How to leave feedback

Point at a beat by its ID. Narration durations are the master clock, so a note
that changes narration forces regenerating that beat's audio, re-rendering its
scene to the new duration, and recompiling both cuts.

| Beat | Act | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| B00 | ASK | Cold open — edited, not compressed |
| B01 | BLUF | The 16 kHz cliff, shown |
| B02 | MECHANISM | Masking — why deletion is allowed |
| B03 | COMPARISON | 128 / 320 / lossless |
| B04 | LIMIT | Generation loss |
| B05 | APPLY | When lossy is fine, when it is not |
| B06 | OUTRO | Sign-off |

If a **number** is disputed, check [MEASUREMENTS.txt](./MEASUREMENTS.txt) first
— every figure on screen has a line in it, and the experiment is four commands
long if you want to re-run it.
