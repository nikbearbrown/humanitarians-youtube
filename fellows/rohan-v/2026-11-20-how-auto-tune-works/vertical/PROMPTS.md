## Shared kit — `week-13/_kit/` (`hfkit.py`, `kit3.js`, `add_qc.py`, `vertical_strings.py`)

The week-12 kit, carried over and extended for both of this week's films. `build_hf.py` looks for `_kit/` in this
folder first, then in `week-13/_kit/`, and copies `kit3.js` into `hyperframes/assets/` when it changes. three.js
0.187.0 ships in `hyperframes/assets/` with `three-LICENSE.txt`.

- **New this week in `kit3.js`**: `pianoRoll` (the lanes of C major, the tune's notes as soft boxes, pitch tracks as
  tubes that draw on progressively), `tubeN` / `drawTube`, `slab`, `grid`, `glowBox`, `ribbon`, `canvasTex`,
  `pathTube`. Every frame is still drawn from timeline time alone: no clocks, no `requestAnimationFrame`, seeded
  randomness only.
- **The look, "the vocal booth at night"**: a near-black plum ground, a faint floor grid, drifting dust, a slow
  camera in every beat, glass HUD panels. Source Serif 4 for titles, Inter for the HUD.
- **Colour carries meaning, everywhere (3D and HUD)**: the notes of C major = lilac lanes; as sung = coral; gentle
  correction (60 ms) = gold; retune speed zero = cyan.
- **Data**: `evidence/scene.json` (the detected and corrected pitch tracks, the A4 frame and its line-up score, the
  per-note offsets, the shift results), the beat's measured duration and its word-clock cues. B04's vowel curves
  are `autotune.py`'s own `envelope()` evaluated on the run's held-note files.
- **Pages** (`hfkit.py`): chapter, title, HUD column and spark line in landscape; on the phone, a shorter title
  where needed and one evenly spaced column under the 3D stage, with no closing line.
