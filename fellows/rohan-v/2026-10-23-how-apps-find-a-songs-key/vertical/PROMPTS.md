# PROMPTS — "How Apps Find a Song's Key"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a
deterministic Remotion render of recorded data. The listening clip is the run's own WAV file.

## The library-first gate

| Need searched | Closest candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "piano keyboard notes highlighted scale" | `PitchMelodyFix` (a piano roll) | miss → **`KeyNotes`** |
| "chroma heatmap twelve rows over time" | `HaiProgressOverturned` | miss → **`KeyChroma`** |
| a results grid of 60 songs | `TempoGrid` (last week) | near-miss: its idea, rebuilt for 5 × 12 |
| "bar chart" of 24 correlations | `BarChart` (another channel's palette) | miss → **`KeyProfiles`** |
| a typeset equation | `typeset_math.py` | **hit**, B03's correlation |

`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `HaiApplyCard` (`phoneType` on the phone) and `HaiTitleOutro` are reused as-is.

## Shared kit — `scenes/keys/`

- `keyData.ts`, **generated** by `export_key_data.py`: the C major example's chroma frames, profile and 24
  correlations; the A minor example; the key-change windows; the Krumhansl–Kessler profiles; all 60 results.
- `keyKit.tsx`: `NOTES`, `ProfileBars`; `Chip`, `listening`, `Stat`, `PhoneBaseline`, `Caps` reused.

## Per beat

| Beat | Component | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | `KeyNotes` | One octave of piano: C major's white keys with C ringed as home; then A minor, same keys, ring moved to A; two cards spell both scales. |
| B02 | `KeyChroma` | The C major song's chroma (12 rows × the run's first 40 frames), then its 12-bar note profile with the three tallest in terracotta; the clip plays. |
| B03 | `KeyProfiles` | The song's profile beside the 1982 C major template; then all 24 correlations, C major and A minor marked; the correlation formula typeset. |
| B04 | `KeyGrid` | 5 styles × 12 tonics, one square per song: right / same notes, other home. |
| B05 | `KeyRelative` | The A minor ballad's profile; the near tie (0.891 vs 0.886, on a zoomed axis that is printed); the counts; MIREX partial credit. |
| B06 | `KeyChange` | Three lanes over 20 s: what was played, the one answer, the 8-s windows. |

Every new scene has a native 9:16 layout (`<Name>916`), shorter strings from `vertical_strings.py`.
