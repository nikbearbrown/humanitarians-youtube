# PROMPTS — "How Auto-Captions Find Each Word"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a
deterministic Remotion render of recorded data. The listening clip is the run's own WAV file.

## The library-first gate

| Need | Closest in the library | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| a spectrogram of one sentence | `ShazamPeaks`, `TempoOnsets` (other runs' spectrograms) | near-miss: their idea, rebuilt as Whisper's 80-band input → **`CapSpectro`** |
| true vs recognised word starts on a waveform | none | miss → **`CapTiming`** |
| one mark per word, right / wrong | `TempoGrid` (one square per groove) | near-miss: its idea, rebuilt as 238 dots → **`CapAccuracy`** |
| results across noise levels | `ShazamNoiseSweep` (queries per level, no error rate) | miss → **`CapNoise`** |
| words said vs words written | none | miss → **`CapCatch`** |

`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `HaiApplyCard` (`phoneType` and `sparkScale` on the phone) and `HaiTitleOutro` are reused
as-is.

## Shared kit — `scenes/captions/`

- `capData.ts`, **generated** by `export_cap_data.py`: sentence 1's waveform envelope, true starts and Whisper's
  words and times; the noise curve; the clean totals; the 0 dB and −5 dB results; the voice reel's two clean
  examples. `public/captions/mel.png`: sentence 1 as an 80-band log-Mel image.
- `capKit.tsx`: `WhisperTag` (LOGO LAW: its maker's mark beside the name), `Env` (waveform bars); `Chip`,
  `listening`, `Stat`, `PhoneBaseline`, `Caps` reused.

## Per beat

| Beat | Component | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | `CapSpectro` | Test sentence 1 as the model sees it, 80 bands, low at the bottom; the words it wrote appear one by one; three numbers: 80 bands, 30-second chunks, 680,000 hours. |
| B02 | `CapTiming` | Sentence 1's waveform; true word starts as ink ticks above, Whisper's as terracotta ticks below, each word written at Whisper's start. |
| B03 | `CapAccuracy` | 238 dots, one per word, the 4 written wrong in terracotta; then 234/238, 0.24 s early, nine in ten within 0.3 s. |
| B04 | `CapNoise` | Word error rate, clean to 0 dB, one bar each, high ones in terracotta; then the 0 dB sentence plays, with what was said above what it wrote. |
| B05 | `CapCatch` | At −5 dB: words said vs written, and a stretch of the repeat loop; then clean speech: the respelled name and the price, said vs written. |

Every new scene has a native 9:16 layout (`<Name>916`), shorter strings from `vertical_strings.py`.
