SOUND BECOMES A PICTURE
The sound is turned into a spectrogram, eighty frequency bands, read thirty seconds at a time. The model,
Whisper, looks at the picture and predicts the text one small piece at a time.

WHEN WAS EACH WORD SAID?
It checks where in the sound it was looking while it wrote each word, and lines those moments up in order:
dynamic time warping.

I TESTED THE ONE OUR VIDEOS USE
On 24 sentences built from my narrator's voice, Whisper's base model got 234 of 238 words right, with word starts
about a quarter of a second early in my test. With the speech 10 dB louder than the noise it still got 97% of
the words; with noise as loud as the speech, the error rate went over 70%. Louder still, it wrote over 300 words
for 238, repeating phrases. Even on clean speech it writes what it expects: a respelled name comes back in its
usual spelling, and "dollar, three, fifty" comes back as "$3.50".

WHAT TO DO
Read auto-captions before you publish, check names and numbers first, and record somewhere quiet.
