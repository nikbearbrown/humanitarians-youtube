## How to leave feedback

Point at a beat by its ID. Narration durations are the master clock, so a note
that changes narration forces regenerating that beat's audio, re-rendering its
scene to the new duration, and recompiling both cuts.

| Beat | Act | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| B00 | ASK | You turned it up; they turned it down |
| B01 | BLUF | Same ceiling, very different loudness |
| B02 | MECHANISM | One instant versus the whole song |
| B03 | THE CATCH | Both tracks land on the same level |
| B04 | THE COST | The gap collapses, and gain cannot restore it |
| B05 | APPLY | Four things to do on export |
| B06 | OUTRO | Sign-off |

If a **number** is disputed, check [MEASUREMENTS.txt](./MEASUREMENTS.txt) — every
figure on screen has a line in it, and the experiment is four commands long if
you want to re-run it.

Two narration lines were already corrected during the factcheck; see
[FACTCHECK.md](./FACTCHECK.md) notes 1 and 2 before re-raising the peak
difference or the "nine points" phrasing.
