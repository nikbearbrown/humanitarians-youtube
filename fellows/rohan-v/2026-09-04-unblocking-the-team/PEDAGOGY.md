# PEDAGOGY — "Unblocking the Team."

Narration sign-off record. This reel is the project-progress half of the weekly
submission, not an explainer, so the pedagogy question is different: the reel
fails if a viewer cannot say what changed this week and what is still open.

## The one thing this reel has to land

**Every blocker became a reusable asset.** Not "I was busy" and not "look what
I made" — the through-line is that each obstacle was converted into something
the next person does not have to hit. A workshop became a video. A learning
curve became a series. A maze became documentation.

That framing is what earns a progress update a place on a public channel
instead of in a status email.

## Register — Pragmatist, per `brands/hai.md`

- **Leads with the constraint.** Every body beat names the obstacle before the
  response. ✓
- **States what is not done.** B03 and B04 both carry explicit in-progress
  status, in the graphic as well as the voice. ✓
- **No personality tax, no marketing language.** No "excited to share", no
  "thrilled", no exclamation. ✓
- **No self-congratulation.** The closest the reel comes is "I'd already solved
  it for myself, so I helped them" — which is a reason, not a boast. ✓

## Tone decisions made deliberately

**Nobody is named but the presenter.** The brief describes helping specific
colleagues. Naming them in a public video would put their learning curve on
record without their consent, so the reel says "our marketing team" throughout.

**The team is credited before the blocker is described.** "Our marketing team is
creative — making things was never their problem" comes before the GitHub wall.
Reversing those two clauses would make the beat read as a complaint about
colleagues. It is a complaint about a tool's onboarding.

**No perk framing.** Carried from the standing Suno series rule: this is
internal reporting for a nonprofit, and Pro access is not the story. The word
never appears.

**The stale plan document was not deferred to.** `SERIES-PLAN.md` estimated the
parts at ~3:30 and ~3–4 min. The built masters are 3:57 / 3:09 / 3:29. The reel
reports what was built. Repeating the plan's numbers would have been easier and
wrong.

## Vocabulary discipline

| Term | How it is handled |
|---|---|
| stem / beat sheet / render | avoided entirely — the audience for a progress update is not necessarily the toolkit's audience |
| "rebuilt in code, not screen-recorded" | plain-language stand-in for "programmatic Remotion"; the durability consequence is stated right after, so the phrase earns its place |
| "tribal knowledge" | used once, then immediately defined by example: "you find out by asking someone who already did it" |
| 4K | used as a badge and spoken once as "four K"; not explained, it is a floor the audience already understands |
| GitHub | named as the blocker but never explained — B01's point is that the fellow should not need to understand it, which the week-01 walkthrough already covers |
