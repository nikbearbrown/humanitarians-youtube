# PROMPTS — "Unblocking the Team."

The design intent behind each visual, and the HAI channel standard the reel
holds to. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model — every beat is
a deterministic Remotion render, so these are component briefs rather than
generation prompts.

## The library-first gate

Per toolkit doctrine, `./art scenes` was run for each of the four visual needs
**before** any component was authored. All four came back as genuine misses:

| Need searched | Closest candidate | Why it was not a match |
|---|---|---|
| "blocker obstacle wall blocked from submitting unblock" | `HaiSubmitForkBranch` | Correct subject area but it is a git-graph of fork-vs-branch mechanics; no notion of work being blocked and then released |
| "multi part series lineup three parts runtime badges" | `SunoL3Download` | A download-options panel from inside the Suno series, not a series lineup |
| "account signup steps chain discord adobe tools access onboarding" | `SunoL1DiscordLogin` | Four procedural sign-in steps for Suno specifically, not a multi-tool account chain |
| "roadmap timeline shipped next up whats coming" | `ClaudeRoadmap` | Flagged `[derived text]`; opened it — it is a full reel composition for another project, not a reusable graphic |

Four punts, four components built, all now registered under folder
`hai-weekly-progress` and in `scenes.json`.

## B00 — `ClaudeComposerAsk` (chassis, reused)

The channel's fixed opener. Props frame the week as a question put to Claude,
with the three output lines pre-resolving the reel's structure: a blocked team,
a shipped series, a path being written down. Segment reads "Week of September 4"
so the reel is self-dating.

## B01 — `HaiProgressBlocker` (new)

**Brief:** make the blocker legible without narrating at it. A viewer should be
able to pause anywhere in the first half and see the problem.

Three chips on the left are the work that *already existed* — a 16:9 master, a
9:16 short, docs. That matters: the team's creativity was never the constraint.
A solid warm-ink wall labelled GITHUB sits centre with a padlock and a BLOCKED
badge. The right-hand SUBMITTED zone is dashed and empty.

At 46% of the beat the wall splits vertically and the halves travel off-frame,
a terracotta bridge draws across labelled with the fix, and the chips slide over
one by one, their check icons filling terracotta as they land.

**Reusable as:** any progress beat shaped "the work existed; one step blocked it."

## B02 — `HaiProgressSeriesCards` (new)

**Brief:** report a shipped series with the receipts on screen.

Three cards, one per part, each carrying its number badge, name, core skill, a
deterministic four-harmonic waveform strip (these are music-tool tutorials, so
the waveform is the honest motif), its measured runtime, and a 4K badge. A DONE
chip sits top-right of each.

Beneath them a terracotta total bar counts from 0:00 up to the real 10:35, so
the volume claim is *shown accumulating* rather than asserted. A WHY footnote
carries the durability point: rebuilt in code, so it does not rot on redesign.

The waveform generator is the same four-harmonic function used by the
stem-separation scenes, keeping the channel's waveform language consistent.

**Reusable as:** any progress beat reporting a finished multi-part series.

## B03 — `HaiProgressSignupChain` (new)

**Brief:** show the sequence, then show the artifact being written to cover it.

Four cards — Discord, Suno, Midjourney, Adobe CC — each with a step number, its
own brand-accurate accent dot and base rule, and one line on what it is for.
A connector draws between them left to right.

Once the chain is complete, a documentation sheet rises from beneath and wraps
the entire run, bordered terracotta, its lip carrying the label and an
`IN PROGRESS` stamp. The stamp is in the graphic, not just the narration, so the
status survives being screenshotted out of context.

**Reusable as:** any progress beat shaped "here is a sequence, here is the doc
being written to cover it."

## B04 — `HaiProgressRoadmap` (new)

**Brief:** make the shipped/committed split a claim about time, not a two-column
list.

A spine draws left to right — solid ink to the midpoint, dashed terracotta past
it. A NOW pin drops last with a cream halo. Shipped items sit left as solid
cards with filled ink checks. Committed items sit right as dashed terracotta
cards, each with its own due chip. Nothing on the right can be mistaken for
done, because the visual grammar of the two sides differs at a glance.

**Reusable as:** the closing beat of any weekly-progress reel.

## B05 — `ClaudeTitleOutro` (chassis, reused)

The channel's fixed outro. Title / handle / presenter, unchanged from week-01.

## The portrait siblings

Per THE ONDA CHECK the vertical cut is a native re-render, so each new component
has a `916` sibling registered at 1080×1920. Each re-exports the landscape
schema, so there is no format-specific content in the beat sheet.

**Brief for all four:** reflow the composition's logic for portrait — never
shrink the landscape layout. A row becomes a stack; a horizontal journey rotates
to vertical; a horizontal spine becomes a vertical one. Type is sized off
`height` so it fills the frame, and everything stays inside the active band
(y 230–1440) because the top 12% and bottom 25% belong to platform UI.

| Sibling | The rotation |
|---|---|
| `HaiProgressBlocker916` | The wall becomes a full-width horizontal band between the work above and the landing zone below. It parts sideways; the bridge descends through it, its label masked with a page-coloured chip so the bar reads as a labelled connector rather than a strikethrough |
| `HaiProgressSeriesCards916` | Cards stack; each becomes a horizontal unit — badge, then name/skill/waveform, then runtime and 4K badge right-aligned. The spark line is positioned relative to the footnote, not pinned to a fraction, because the footnote wraps |
| `HaiProgressSignupChain916` | Cards stack and the connector runs vertically through the hue dots, which reads as a numbered sequence more naturally than the horizontal version does |
| `HaiProgressRoadmap916` | The spine turns vertical in the left gutter, NOW pin on the boundary, shipped above and committed below — reading downward is reading forward in time |

**Endcard:** `shorts.py` offers a silent branded endcard, built with
`--no-endcard` here. B05 is already a full branded outro, and skipping the card
keeps the vertical the same length as the landscape master (119.8s), matching
the week-01 deliverables.

## HAI channel standard

Held identical across all reels on this channel:

- **Opener**: `ClaudeComposerAsk`; narration opens "Hi, I'm Rohan, for Humanitarians AI."
- **Outro**: `ClaudeTitleOutro` with `title` / `@HumanitariansAI` / `Rohan Vijaykumar`
- Narration closes "I'm Rohan Vijaykumar, for Humanitarians AI."
- Palette: the `claude` token set, never retinted
- Every body beat ends on a spark line: serif italic, terracotta rule, bottom left
- Eyebrow on every body beat: `HUMANITARIANS AI · <SECTION>`
- No screen recordings anywhere — every interface is drawn in code
