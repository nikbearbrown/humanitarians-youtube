# PROMPTS — "How AI Music Generators Turn Text Into Audio"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a
deterministic Remotion render of recorded data, so these are **component briefs**, not generation
prompts. The listening clips are the run's own WAV files.

## The library-first gate

## Shared kit — `scenes/tokens/`

- `tokData.ts`, **generated** by `export_tok_data.py` from `evidence/codec.json`: the sample run, one
  frame's shape and its 1/2/4/12-codebook reconstructions, example codebook entries, the frame's
  tokens, the quality curve, a token stretch with the model's guesses, the generated tokens, and the
  typeset equations (full and phone-length).
- `tokKit.tsx`: `shapePath` (64-band log-magnitude shape → SVG), `TokenBox`, `Label`; `Stat`,
  `PhoneBaseline`, `Chip`, `listening` reused from the Shazam and pitch kits.

## Per beat

**B01 `TokSampleCount`**: 4 ms of the test piece as 96 stems and dots with their values printed; then
44,100 per second per ear on a CD and 15,876,000 in a three-minute song; "too many to predict".

**B02 `TokCodebook`**: one 10.7 ms frame's shape; the 1,024-entry codebook as a 32×32 grid with six
real entries drawn; the chosen entry lit and its shape laid over the frame; its token, then the next
frames' tokens.

**B03 `TokResidual`**: the same frame decoded from 1, then 2, then 4 codebooks, closing in on the
original; its four tokens; the residual sum and the token-rate arithmetic, typeset.

**B04 `TokCodecQuality`**: treble kept and overall SNR against the number of codebooks, measured on an
unseen piece; 1, 4 and 12 labelled; the point being heard is lit during the four clips.

**B05 `TokNextGuess`**: a real stretch of the unseen piece's tokens with the model's guess under each
(hits lit); 16.5% against 1.2% and 0.1%.

**B06 `TokTextSteer`**: published systems (prompt → text encoder → tilted guesses; MusicGen's verified
settings) beside my toy (the prompt picks the training music; the first generated tokens for each
prompt; 0.26 s longest copied stretch); the prompt being heard is lit.

**Portrait (`*916`)**: native phone layouts on the PHONE type scale, short strings in
`vertical/beat_sheet.json` (`vertical_strings.py`), phone-length equations (units moved into the
captions), and the ghost baseline at 78%.
