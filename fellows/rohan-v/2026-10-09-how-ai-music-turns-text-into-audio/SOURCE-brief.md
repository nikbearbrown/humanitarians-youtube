# SOURCE-brief — "How AI Music Generators Turn Text Into Audio"

## What was asked for

One of two STEM videos for the week of 5–9 Oct (see [PROMPT.md](./PROMPT.md)).

## Who it is for

Lyrical Literacy fellows who make songs in Suno every week and the wider Humanitarians AI audience:
people who type a prompt and get a song, and have never been told what happens in between. No maths
or machine learning assumed.

## What it must leave them with

Published music generators squeeze sound into tokens, predict the next token again and again, and
let the words tilt each prediction. So: the prompt steers, it doesn't write notes; generate several
and choose; name what the text can steer; and listen for treble lost in the squeeze.

## Evidence the reel stands on

`codec.py` and its outputs in `evidence/`; the SoundStream, EnCodec, MusicGen and MusicLM papers;
the CD audio standard. Details in [SOURCES.md](./SOURCES.md).
