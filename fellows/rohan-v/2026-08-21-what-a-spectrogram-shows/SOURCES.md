# SOURCES

- STFT / spectrogram as time-frequency magnitude: Oppenheim & Schafer, Discrete-Time Signal Processing.
- librosa.specshow convention (time × frequency): https://librosa.org/doc/latest/generated/librosa.display.specshow.html
- Phase discarded in magnitude spectrograms; reconstruction needs an estimate: Griffin & Lim, 1984, IEEE TASSP.
- Window length vs time/frequency resolution: standard STFT treatment (same textbooks).
- Harmonic series of voiced speech: Fant, Acoustic Theory of Speech Production (classic); any intro phonetics text.
- Rainbow / jet colormap false contours: Borland & Taylor, "Rainbow Color Map (Still) Considered Harmful," IEEE CG&A, 2007.
