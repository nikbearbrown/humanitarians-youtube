# PROMPTS — "How Pitch Correction Works"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every
beat is a deterministic Remotion render of recorded data, so these are **component
briefs**, not generation prompts. The listening clips are the run's own WAV files.

## The library-first gate

`./art scenes` was run before anything was authored:

## Shared kit — `scenes/pitch/`

- `pitchData.ts`, **generated** by `export_pitch_data.py` from `evidence/pitch_demo.json`:
  times, the sung pitch, each run's target and **re-measured** pitch, the worked frame's
  wave and YIN curve, the typeset equations.
- `pitchKit.tsx`: `curvePath` (Hz → note lanes, breaks at unvoiced frames), `Lanes`
  (semitone bands, naturals labelled, optional highlight and strike), `TargetBlocks`
  (written notes as dashed blocks), `ToneWave` (B01's explanatory tone, the one
  non-measured drawing), `Chip`, `listening` (which clip is playing at a beat fraction).

## Per beat

**B01 `PitchCycles`**: a clean tone whose cycles are bracketed one by one while a
counter runs to 440; a second wave at twice the rate for the octave; a ruler from A to A♯
cut into 100 cents. Shells (the octave frame, the ruler) sit faint from the first frame
so the lower third is never empty at a QC sample.

**B02 `PitchFindPeriod`**: the run's real frame (A4 sung 40 cents flat) in ink with a spark
copy sliding right; beneath, the YIN difference drawn as the copy moves, dipping to 0.01
at 51.27 samples and again at every whole cycle. Live readouts: shift, difference, then
430 Hz = 1 ÷ 2.33 ms. Method chip: "the idea behind the first Auto-Tune (1997)".

**B03 `PitchSnapCents`**: a tuner whose needle swings to −39.5 cents on "How far below"
and is pulled to 0 on "the target is A"; the rule and the worked example as typeset SVG.

**B04 `PitchMelodyFix`**: a piano roll of the whole test melody. Dashed blocks = the notes
as written; ink = the sung pitch as measured; spark = the pitch measured again on the
corrected audio. BEFORE 39.9 / AFTER 2.8. During the two listening clips a playhead runs
along whichever curve is heard, and a "now playing" chip names it.

**B05 `PitchRetuneSpeed`**: the held last note corrected twice, INSTANT (0 ms) and SLOW
(250 ms): sung pitch faint, re-measured result in spark. Callouts: FLAT STEP, the 1998
"Cher effect", VIBRATO KEPT. The panel being heard is lit during the clips.

**B06 `PitchWrongNote`**: the last note on F4 / F♯4 / G4 lanes, with its 75¢ and 25¢
distances; the chromatic result lands on F♯4 (✗, in tune and wrong); the F♯ lane is
struck ("not in C major") and the C-major result lands on G4 (✓).

**Portrait (`*916`)**: each component carries a native phone layout on the PHONE type
scale (title 5.5vh, labels ≥ 3.3vh × 0.8 for lane names), with shortened titles and
strings in `vertical/beat_sheet.json` and content spread across y 21–78%, the spark line
at 80%.
