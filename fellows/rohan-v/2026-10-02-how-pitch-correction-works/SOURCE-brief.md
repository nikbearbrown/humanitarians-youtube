# SOURCE-brief — "How Pitch Correction Works"

## What was asked for

One STEM video on any topic for the week of 28 Sep – 2 Oct, built late on 5 Oct, following
the current Brutalist framework (see [PROMPT.md](./PROMPT.md)). Rohan chose pitch correction
from four options.

## Who it is for

Lyrical Literacy fellows and the wider Humanitarians AI audience: people who record a vocal
over a Suno track, or hear "Auto-Tune" in every other song, and have never been told what the
tool measures or what its two main settings change. No music theory or maths assumed.

## What it must leave them with

Pitch correction measures the pitch, snaps it to the nearest allowed note and pulls it there.
So: set the key first, choose the speed on purpose, and listen, because in tune is not the
same as the right note.

## Evidence the reel stands on

`pitch_demo.py` and its outputs in `evidence/`; Wikipedia's AutoTune article for the 1997
release, autocorrelation and the 1998 "Cher effect"; de Cheveigné & Kawahara (2002) for YIN;
Moulines & Charpentier (1990) for PSOLA. Details in [SOURCES.md](./SOURCES.md).
