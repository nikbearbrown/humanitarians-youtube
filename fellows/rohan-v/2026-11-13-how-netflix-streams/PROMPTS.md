## Shared kit — `_kit/` (the week-12 kit, shipped in this folder)

- **`kit3.js`**: three.js helpers for both week-12 films. Every frame is drawn from timeline time alone (HyperFrames
  seeks each frame), with no clocks and seeded randomness only. Glows, tubes, dust, dotted globes, and `pin`, which
  places an HTML label over a 3D point each frame.
- **`hfkit.py`**: the page around the stage: chapter line, title, the HUD column, LOGO LAW tags, the closing line,
  and the phone layout (one evenly spaced column under the stage, no closing line).

## The look, "the player at night"

- Netflix's own black and red: a black ground with a faint red glow; white for the clean test card; Netflix red
  (#E50914) for the grainy clip and the top quality; Netflix grey (#B3B3B3) for secondary text.
- Source Serif 4 for titles (embedded, OFL), Inter for numbers and labels.
- **Colour carries meaning**: white = the clean card, red = the grainy clip, the top rung and a stall, grey =
  the speed-guessing player and the far server.
- **Data**: `evidence/scene.json` and `results.json` (the twelve encodes and scores, the per-title bitrate, every
  chunk each player chose, the connection, the two download times) and each beat's measured duration and
  word-clock cues from the beat sheet, so each reveal lands on its word.
