# PEDAGOGY — "How Spatial Audio Works"

How the reel is built to teach someone who wears earbuds every day but has never been told how they place a sound.

## The register

*No assumed knowledge* and *the viewer has to learn something*: one listener, the textbook sphere, carried through
every beat; each cue shown on it and measured; then the viewer hears the result for themselves; Apple's claims
kept apart from my model's numbers; and an ending on three things to do.

## The shape

| Beat | Job | What the viewer can do after it |
|---|---|---|
| B00 ASK | the question: beside you, behind you, across the room | expect a small model, built and tested |
| B01 TIMING | the near ear hears it first; about 0.66 ms at the side, zero ahead | explain how a delay tells left from right |
| B02 LEVEL | the head's shadow: 14 dB for high sounds, about 2 for low; Rayleigh, 1907 | say why timing works for low sounds and loudness for high ones |
| B03 FRONT OR BACK | a mirror pair gets the same cues; the outer ear breaks the tie | explain why front and back get confused |
| B04 THE FILTERS | 710 measured directions; one pair places a sound; then listen | say what an HRTF is, and hear one turn around the head |
| B05 HEAD TRACKING | off, the sound turns with you; on, it stays put | say what the motion sensors are for |
| B06 YOUR EARS | ears differ; a camera scan builds a profile | say why a personal profile helps |
| B07 WHAT TO DO | head tracking, the profile, Dolby Atmos | set it up on their own headphones |
| B08 OUTRO | sign-off | — |

## Choices worth defending

- **One listener for every beat.** The same glass sphere, with the right ear always cyan and the left always
  violet, carries timing, shadow, front/back, the filters and head tracking, so each new idea lands on a picture
  the viewer already knows. It is the model that was measured, so the picture and the numbers agree, and no person
  is drawn.
- **Hear it, don't just see it.** B04 ends with eight seconds of the run's own binaural render, so the viewer tests
  the idea on their own ears. The narration says "Put your headphones on", and the description says it too.
- **Apple's claims and mine are kept apart.** "In my model" or "in my head model" introduces every measured number;
  Apple is credited only with what it has said (head tracking on AirPods in 2020; the camera-scan profile). The
  narration never says how Apple renders.
- **The limit is taught, not hidden.** B03 shows that the head alone can't tell front from back, and B06 brings
  it back as the reason for personal profiles.
