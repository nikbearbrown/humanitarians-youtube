## Shared kit — `_kit/kit3.js` and `hfkit.py`

The week's kit ships in this folder as `_kit/` (`hfkit.py`, `kit3.js`, `add_qc.py`, `vertical_strings.py`; a copy
of `week-12/_kit/`, which `build_hf.py` falls back to). three.js 0.187.0 ships in `hyperframes/assets/` with
`three-LICENSE.txt`.

- **The look, "listening room at night"**: a deep indigo ground, a polar floor of rings and spokes (directions
  around you), drifting dust, a slow camera in every beat, glass HUD panels. Source Serif 4 for titles (embedded,
  OFL), Inter for the HUD.
- **The listener**: the textbook spherical head that `spatial.py` measures (`headModel`: a glass sphere with two
  ears and a small nose). Never a person.
- **Colour carries meaning, everywhere (3D and HUD)**: sound = amber light; right ear = cyan; left ear = violet;
  the mirror source behind = pink; tracking off / on = coral / green.
- **Data**: `evidence/scene.json` (the ITD table, both level differences, the front/back numbers, the 40° turn), the
  beat's measured duration and its word-clock cues, so each reveal lands on its word. The filter curves in B03 and
  B04 are `spatial.py`'s own filters, evaluated with the same constants, and B04's chart says so ("MY MODEL'S
  FILTER PAIR").
- **Pages** (`hfkit.py`): chapter, title, HUD column and spark line in landscape; on the phone, a shorter title and
  one evenly spaced column under the 3D stage, with no closing line.
