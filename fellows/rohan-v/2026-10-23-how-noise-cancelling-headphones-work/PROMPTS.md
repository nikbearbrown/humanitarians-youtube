# PROMPTS — "How Noise-Cancelling Headphones Work"

Design intent per visual. Nothing was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a deterministic
Remotion render of recorded simulation data. The listening clip is the run's own WAV file.

## The library-first gate

| Need searched | Closest candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "waveform plus inverted waveform cancel sum" | `MonoCancelPair` (week-04: two channels cancelling) | near-miss: drawn sines, not this run's data → **`AncWaves`** |
| "signal chain diagram microphone chip speaker" | `MonoOneSpeaker` | miss → **`AncSystem`** |
| "bar chart decibels reduction" | `BarChart` (another channel's palette) | miss → **`AncTypes`** |
| "frequency response curve across bands" | `LossyMaskingCurve` (a masking curve) | near-miss → **`AncCurve`** |
| a typeset equation | `typeset_math.py` | **hit**, B03's phase rule and worked cases |

`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `HaiApplyCard` (`phoneType` on the phone) and `HaiTitleOutro` are reused as-is.

## Shared kit — `scenes/anc/`

- `ancData.ts`, **generated** by `export_anc_data.py`: 40 ms of the hum at the ear (off, anti-noise, on), the four
  noise results, the 23 one-third-octave bands, the phase-rule table, the model's parameters, typeset equations.
- `ancKit.tsx`: `wavePath`, `WaveStrip`; `Chip`, `listening`, `Stat`, `PhoneBaseline`, `Caps` reused.

## Per beat

| Beat | Component | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | `AncWaves` | Three strips on one scale: the hum, its upside-down copy, what is left. |
| B02 | `AncSystem` | One ear cup as a diagram (no head or person): outside mic, chip, speaker, inside mic; then the timing race, 83 µs vs 125 µs. |
| B03 | `AncPhase` | A 100 Hz and a 2 kHz tone, each with anti-noise 0.1 ms late (dashed) and the leftover (bold); the phase rule typeset. |
| B04 | `AncTypes` | Four bars of dB change from a zero line; the hum plays, off then on. |
| B05 | `AncCurve` | 23 one-third-octave bands of pink noise, quieter below and louder above; the cups' region shaded. |

Every new scene has a native 9:16 layout (`<Name>916`), shorter strings from `vertical_strings.py`.
