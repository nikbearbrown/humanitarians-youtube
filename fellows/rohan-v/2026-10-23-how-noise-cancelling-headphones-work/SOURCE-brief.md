# SOURCE-brief — "How Noise-Cancelling Headphones Work"

## What was asked for

One of two STEM videos for the week of 19–23 Oct, following the current Brutalist framework (see
[PROMPT.md](./PROMPT.md)). Rohan chose noise-cancelling headphones from four options.

## Who it is for

Lyrical Literacy fellows and the wider Humanitarians AI audience: people who wear noise-cancelling headphones
and have noticed they kill a plane's hum but not a conversation, without knowing why. No physics assumed.

## What it must leave them with

The headphone adds an upside-down copy of the noise, and it has to be on time; being late costs more at high
pitch, so steady, low noise cancels and sudden or high sounds don't. The ear cups do the rest. So: use it for
steady noise, get a good seal, and turn the music down.
