A weekly progress report. The Midjourney training series shipped this week —
six parts, 18:59 of 4K material. But the useful part of the week was not the
videos. It was finding out that the plan, written from web research before I
had access, was wrong in five specific ways.

WHAT CHANGED THIS WEEK
Twenty-nine screenshots of the real interface overturned five assumptions in my
scoping plan. There was no Chat section. The results grid was one row of four,
not a 2x2. What I had scoped as "upscale, vary, download" was a six-row action
grid with a menu hidden by default. The settings were a 2x2 of four cards
holding twelve controls. And video was not a footnote — it is everywhere in the
product. Two whole surfaces, Style Creator and video, were missing from the
plan entirely.

WHAT THE CORRECTION LEFT BEHIND
Twenty-three new reusable components, an 863-line interface spec written while
reading the captures, and zero screen recordings. When Midjourney redesigns,
that means editing a component instead of re-recording six videos.
