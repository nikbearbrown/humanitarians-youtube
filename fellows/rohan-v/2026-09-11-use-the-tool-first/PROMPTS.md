# PROMPTS — "Use the Tool First."

Design intent behind each visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or
video model — every beat is a deterministic Remotion render, so these are
component briefs, not generation prompts.

## The library-first gate

`./art scenes` was run for all three visual needs before anything was written.
Three needs, three *different* outcomes — worth recording, because "genuine
miss" was only true once:

### 1. Six-part series grid → **near-hit, variant built**

`HaiProgressSeriesCards` scored 14.5 and is genuinely the same idea; it was
built for exactly this purpose in week-02. But its card width is
`(CONTENT_W − GAP×(n−1)) / n`, which at n=3 gives 530px and at **n=6 gives
245px** — too narrow for a 27px serif title. Rather than fork it, a 3×2 grid
variant was written and the relationship is documented in the new component's
header so nobody wonders why both exist.

### 2. Plan-vs-reality columns → **hit opened, then rejected on layout**

`MedhavyTwoColumnCard` scored 7.5 and its props are *exactly* right:
`leftHeader / leftItems / rightHeader / rightItems`, paired by index. Despite
the name it uses the CLAUDE token set, so it was a serious candidate.

Opening the file settled it. Three mismatches:

- a **fixed 900px centred card** — the right column would have to hold
  sentence-length corrections in roughly 400px
- the spark line sits **top-centre**; this channel's grammar is bottom-left
- **no eyebrow or title props**, and this channel puts an eyebrow on every
  body beat

Adding those would have changed how the component renders for the medhavy
channel, which is not a cost this reel is entitled to impose. Purpose-built
instead. **This is the doctrine working as intended: the search returned a
lead, reading the file returned the verdict.**

### 3. Shipped/next roadmap → **reused outright**

`HaiProgressRoadmap` and `HaiProgressRoadmap916` already exist from week-02 and
take `shipped[]` / `next[]` arrays. Used unchanged. Zero lines written.

## B01 — `HaiProgressSeriesGrid` (new)

**Brief:** report six finished things without the grid feeling like a
spreadsheet.

Six cards, 3×2. Each carries a terracotta number badge, the part title in
serif, its measured runtime large, a 4K badge and a DONE check. Underneath, a
full-width terracotta bar counts from 0:00 up to the real 18:59 — the volume
claim accumulates on screen rather than being asserted.

The total rounds rather than truncates, fixing the class of bug that put 10:34
against a documented 10:35 in the week-02 MP3 reel.

**Reusable as:** any progress beat reporting four to six finished parts.

## B02 — `HaiProgressOverturned` (new)

**Brief:** make being wrong watchable. A viewer who reads nothing should still
see five things get crossed out.

Assumption on the left in plain serif; correction on the right on a bordered
card. As each row lands, a terracotta rule **draws across the assumption**, the
text fades toward `GHOST`, an arrow appears in the gutter, and the correction
card slides in with a terracotta border. Five times, staggered.

The strike-through is the only destructive motion anywhere in the reel. That is
deliberate — it is the one beat whose subject is error.

**Reusable as:** any beat shaped "here is what I believed, here is what I found."

## B03 — `HaiProgressKitGrid` (new)

**Brief:** "23 components" is a claim; the list is the evidence. Show the list.

A counting total ticks 0 → 23 in a terracotta block, with three supporting
stats beneath it (863 lines of spec, 29 captures read, 0 screen recordings).
The right two-thirds is all 23 component names as monospace chips, staggering
in four frames apart.

Naming them matters. A viewer can pause and check, and a future fellow can
search for `MjL4Panel` and find it. The `0 SCREEN RECORDINGS` stat is the one
that carries the argument — it is why the kit exists at all.

**Reusable as:** any progress beat shaped "the deliverable shipped, and here is
the durable asset it left behind."

## B04 — `HaiProgressRoadmap` (library, reused)

Unchanged from week-02. Solid cards left of the NOW pin are done; dashed cards
right of it carry their own due chips, so nothing on the right can be misread
as finished.

## B05 — `ClaudeTitleOutro` (chassis, reused)

## The portrait siblings

**Brief for all three:** reflow the logic, never shrink the landscape layout.
Type sized off `height`; everything inside the active band (y 230–1440).

| Sibling | The rotation |
|---|---|
| `HaiProgressSeriesGrid916` | 3×2 becomes **2×3** — portrait has the height for three rows, and each card keeps its title unwrapped |
| `HaiProgressOverturned916` | side-by-side pairs **stack**; the two column headers collapse into a single legend line, since a stacked pair no longer needs two headings |
| `HaiProgressKitGrid916` | the left column becomes a **full-width banner**, stats become a row of three, chips wrap beneath at smaller type so 23 names still clear the footnote |
