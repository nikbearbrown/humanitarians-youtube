FOUR WAYS TO LET AN AGENT EDIT
Remotion (what Brutalist is built on): a video written as code. HyperFrames,
from HeyGen: a video written as a web page, built for agents. OpenReel: an
open-source editor that runs in the browser, whose desktop app connects to
Claude. DaVinci Resolve, through community connectors that let an agent drive a
professional editor; full scripting works best with the paid Studio version.

ONE REAL TEST
HyperFrames was given the finished footage and narration from Suno Part 1 and
asked for word-by-word captions and a new intro. It returned a 4:05 cut with 94
caption lines and none overlapping. Where it resisted: the glass style vanished
on cream backgrounds, the captions copied the phonetic spelling from the voice
script, the render needed about 60 GB of scratch space, and it came out at 1080p,
not 4K.

WHAT IT SHOWED
Good at packaging footage that already exists: captions, titles, an intro. It
didn't add teaching visuals and didn't reach our 4K standard. OpenReel and
DaVinci Resolve haven't had a hands-on test yet.
