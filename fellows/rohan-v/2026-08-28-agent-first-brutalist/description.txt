Your Weekly Video, Handled. — the complete Humanitarians AI fellow workflow

Four video files are due every Friday. If you don't write code, that can sound
impossible. It isn't. This is the whole pipeline, end to end: you talk to Claude
in the desktop app, approve what it asks, watch the preview, and upload four
files. Everything between those steps is automated.

WHAT'S DUE EVERY FRIDAY
Two videos — one on a STEM or AI topic you're interested in, one on your project
progress. Each exported twice, 16:9 for YouTube and 9:16 for Shorts. Four files,
all in 4K. You can swap in a topic you're passionate about, as long as at least
one of your four files across any two-week stretch is a real research update.

WHAT'S AUTOMATED
Cloning and installing the toolkit · writing the beat sheet · generating and
measuring the narration · rendering every visual · compiling the 4K master ·
deriving the 9:16 Short · forking or branching, committing, and opening the
pull request.

WHAT STAYS YOURS
Saying what the video is about · watching the preview and approving it ·
uploading the four files to Drive.

Not one of them requires a command line.

Built with Brutalist — Kokoro TTS, Manim and Remotion, all running locally and
