# PROMPTS — "How AI Voices Read a Script"

Design intent per visual. Nothing here was generated by an image or video model: every beat is a
deterministic Remotion render of recorded data. The listening clips are the run's own WAV files.

## The library-first gate

| Need | Closest in the library | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| a three-step pipeline carried by one real example | `HaiExplainerFig1Pipeline` (a five-node map, no data) | miss → **`VoicePipeline`** |
| words beside their phonemes | none draws IPA | miss → **`VoiceG2P`** |
| 16 sentences marked right / wrong | `TempoGrid` (one square per item) | near-miss: its idea, rebuilt as 8 word rows × 2 sentences |
| typed text beside what was read | none | miss → **`VoiceNumbers`** |
| a before / after with a clip for each | `PitchMelodyFix` (written vs measured, on a piano roll) | miss → **`VoiceRespell`** |
| a Kokoro voice card | `KokoroRosterCard` (another channel's palette, a roster) | miss: not needed |

`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `HaiApplyCard` (`phoneType` and `sparkScale` on the phone) and `HaiTitleOutro` are reused
as-is.

## Shared kit — `scenes/voice/`

- `voiceData.ts`, **generated** by `export_voice_data.py`: every heteronym sentence with its IPA and verdict; the
  seven clean-up cases and how each reads back as words; the four example words; the four respellings.
- `voiceKit.tsx`: `Ipa` (phonemes on a soft plate, exactly as the step produced them), `Mark` (right / wrong drawn
  as a shape); `Chip`, `listening`, `Stat`, `PhoneBaseline`, `Caps` reused.

## Per beat

| Beat | Component | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | `VoicePipeline` | "Dr. Smith" carried through the three steps: the text, "doctor smith", the narrator's phonemes for it, a voice; the last card says the voice model never sees the spelling. |
| B02 | `VoiceG2P` | Four everyday words with their phonemes; two plain cards (a dictionary for the words it knows, a guess for the rest); then one spelling, two words. |
| B03 | `VoiceHeteronyms` | Eight words, two sentences each, right / wrong from the run; the totals 10 / 16 and 2 / 8; "Last night I read a book" with /ɹˈiːd/ where /ɹˈɛd/ belongs. |
| B04 | `VoiceNumbers` | Seven typed cases beside what was read; six marked right; the price lights up last, then its clip plays. |
| B05 | `VoiceRespell` | Four of our narrator's own mistakes: the word, its phonemes as read, the respelling, the new phonemes; the name row lights while each clip plays. |

Every new scene has a native 9:16 layout (`<Name>916`), shorter strings from `vertical_strings.py`.
