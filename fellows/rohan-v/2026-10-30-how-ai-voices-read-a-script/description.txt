Why does an AI voice say a name or a price wrong? It never sees your spelling: it turns the words into sounds
first, and that is where it slips. Explained from nothing, using the AI voice that narrates this video.

THREE STEPS
First the text is cleaned up ("Dr." becomes "doctor"). Then every word becomes phonemes, the small sounds speech
is built from. Only then does the voice model make audio, and it only ever gets the sounds.

LETTERS TO SOUNDS
The pronunciation step looks a word up in a dictionary, and guesses from spelling rules when the word isn't there.

I TESTED THE ONE NARRATING THIS
Eight words that are spelled the same but said two ways, like "read": sixteen sentences, ten right, and only two
words right both ways. Numbers and short forms did better, six of seven, but "$3.50" came out as "dollar, three,
fifty". My own name and two instrument names were misread until I respelled them.

WHAT TO DO
Write numbers and prices out as words, respell names until they sound right, and reword a line when a two-meaning
word comes out wrong.
