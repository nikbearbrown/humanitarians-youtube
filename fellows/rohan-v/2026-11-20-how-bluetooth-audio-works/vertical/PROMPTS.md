## Shared kit — `week-13/_kit/` (`hfkit.py`, `kit3.js`, `add_qc.py`, `vertical_strings.py`)

The week-12 kit, carried over and extended for both of this week's films (the new helpers are listed in the
Auto-Tune reel's PROMPTS). `build_hf.py` looks for `_kit/` in this folder first, then in `week-13/_kit/`, and copies
`kit3.js` into `hyperframes/assets/` when it changes. three.js 0.187.0 ships in `hyperframes/assets/` with
`three-LICENSE.txt`.

- **The look, "the radio sky"**: a near-black blue ground, a faint floor grid, drifting dust, a slow camera in every
  beat, glass HUD panels. Source Serif 4 for titles, Inter for the HUD.
- **Colour carries meaning, everywhere (3D and HUD)**: music = violet light; the radio link = Bluetooth blue; Wi-Fi
  = orange; a try that gets through = green; a lost one = red.
- **Data**: `evidence/scene.json` (the codec data rates, the first 160 tries of the listening window with and
  without adaptive hopping, the Wi-Fi channels, both buffers' levels every 10 ms of the window, the gap times), the
  beat's measured duration and its word-clock cues.
- **Pages** (`hfkit.py`): chapter, title, HUD column and spark line in landscape; on the phone, a shorter title
  where needed and one evenly spaced column under the 3D stage, with no closing line.

## Per beat

| Beat | Composition | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | `data` | A wide river of CD-quality data (violet) runs into a narrow Bluetooth link (blue). Before "squeezes" it piles up and spills at the mouth; from "squeezes" an SBC ring lights and only 1 in 4.3 particles carries on. HUD: "1,411 kbit/s · CD-QUALITY STEREO" (landscape: "44,100 samples × 16 bits × 2 channels, every second."), "331 kbit/s · SBC ON MY TEST TRACK · MEASURED", two bars and "UNDER A QUARTER · 1 / 4.3", and a tag with ffmpeg's mark, "ENCODED WITH FFMPEG'S SBC ENCODER". |
| B02 | `hopping` | Bluetooth's 79 channels as a row of light columns (2402–2480 MHz), Wi-Fi over channels 24–46 in orange. A packet hops to a new channel every try; the tries are the simulation's own, in slow motion: lost = red burst, through = green ring. From "Adaptive hopping" the Wi-Fi channels go dark and the hops (the adaptive run's own tries) avoid them. HUD: "79 CHANNELS", "UP TO 1,600 HOPS PER SECOND", "17% OF TRIES FAILED · A BUSY WI-FI NETWORK", "0.9% WITH ADAPTIVE HOPPING", key "MY SIMULATION · 2 MINUTES". |
| B03 | `codecs` | One glass pipe per codec, its flow of light proportional to its data rate; LDAC's 990 is Sony's top setting, drawn dashed and thin as "up to". HUD: bars SBC · EVERY DEVICE 331, AAC · APPLE 272, aptX · ANDROID 353, LDAC · UP TO, SONY 990 (landscape: "SBC, AAC and aptX measured on my test track with ffmpeg's encoders (kbit/s); LDAC from Sony."); tags with Apple's mark, "APPLE DEVICES PREFER AAC", and Android's, "aptX · LDAC ON MANY ANDROID PHONES". |
| B04 | `buffer` | Two reservoirs in the earbuds, 50 ms and 200 ms, filled by arriving packets and drained at the playing rate; their levels are the run's own, over the same 6 s the listening clips come from. During the narration the window plays at half speed; during each clip, in real time, in step with what you hear. When the small one runs dry it flashes red ("GAP"). HUD: "GLITCHES IN 2 MINUTES · BY BUFFER SIZE" (50 ms 30, 100 ms 19, 200 ms 3, 300 ms 0), a "NOW PLAYING" headphone card; landscape: "The reserve is the delay: every millisecond kept back is a millisecond late." |
| B05 | `lipsync` | Two lanes running toward you, PICTURE (violet) and SOUND (blue); each drum hit flashes on the picture lane and pulses late on the sound lane. From "holds the picture" the picture lane is held back by the same amount and the pairs line up. For "A game", a gold button-press marker stays put while its sound lags. An illustration: the delay is drawn at 250 ms, inside the measured range. HUD: "170–370 ms · BEHIND THE PICTURE · TWO PUBLISHED TESTS", then "1 · THE HEADPHONES REPORT THEIR DELAY · DELAY REPORTING · A2DP", "2 · THE PHONE HOLDS THE PICTURE BACK", "✕ · GAMES CAN'T WAIT". |
| B06 | `leaudio` | One source (a phone or TV) broadcasts; rings of light spread across the floor and each pair of earbuds lights as a ring reaches it (an illustration, seeded; no data). HUD cards: "LE AUDIO · Bluetooth audio's next generation · ANNOUNCED 2020 · SPECS DONE 2022", "LC3 · THE NEW CODEC · Beats SBC at half the bit rate · SAYS THE BLUETOOTH SIG", "AURACAST · One phone or TV, any number of earbuds · BROADCAST AUDIO". |

Every HyperFrames beat has its own portrait composition (`B0x-v.html`, 2160×3840) with a shorter title where needed
(for example "Too Much Data", "Holding the Picture").
