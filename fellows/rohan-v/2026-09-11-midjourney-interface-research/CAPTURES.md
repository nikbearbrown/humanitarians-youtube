# CAPTURES — Midjourney

The 29 screenshots I took of the Midjourney interface as reference for the
tutorial series. The scenes were **rebuilt** from them — none is composited into any
video — and [`MIDJOURNEY-UI-SPEC.md`](./MIDJOURNEY-UI-SPEC.md) is the written
distillation that the scenes were actually built against. `FACTCHECK.md` in each part
cites these files by name.

## Why they are not committed

The fellows repository allows no personal data about anyone other than the fellow.
Midjourney's Explore feed shows other people's images, and several captures are of it. So the captures stay in my local workspace, and this list is the
record that they exist.

All were taken on the shared Humanitarians AI account, not a personal one.
