# Midjourney — interface research and series plan

The research behind the Midjourney tutorial series for Lyrical Literacy: how Midjourney
actually looks and behaves, captured screen by screen and written up as a spec that
every scene in the series was rebuilt from, plus the series plan and the build
tooling the series shares. Built 2026-09-06 → 2026-09-09.
