  native portrait layout, so the 9:16 cut is designed for the phone rather than
  cropped from the wide version.
- **All 16 masters passed the toolkit's checks** for layout, safe area, text size and
  contrast, plus a frame-by-frame review. Each master has a SHA-256 hash recorded in
  its folder's README.
- **A no-invented-numbers rule.** No statistics appear in any video. Example
  captions, schedules and charts are labelled illustrative on screen, and every
  claim is listed in that video's `FACTCHECK.md`.
- **Week 4 turned the process into a playbook.** I took one finished video apart
  into a decision log, a template and a six-step checklist, which is the starting
  point for the recruit flow below.

## Plan for the requested period

Four phases, in order, each gating the next:

1. **Learning videos on a fixed template** (1 – 31 Oct). Build the Lyrical Literacy
   learning videos from the Week 4 playbook: one template, one checklist per step,
   16:9 + 9:16 for every video. Log my own time per step on each video, so the
   baseline comes from records rather than memory.
2. **The recruit flow** (1 – 30 Nov). Write the production flow as a guide a new
   recruit can follow without me. It covers which steps AI does (script draft,
   narration, scene generation, checks) and which stay in traditional tools (editing,
   sound, thumbnails, review). It includes starter beat sheets, templates and a
   first-video checklist.
3. **Test it with new recruits** (1 – 31 Dec). Each recruit makes one video from the
   guide while I stay hands-off. I'll record where they got stuck, how long each step
   took, and what had to be fixed. A step that consistently fails gets rewritten, and
   "this step still needs a person" is a valid finding that will be reported as one.
4. **Hand-off** (1 – 31 Jan). Fold the fixes into a second version of the guide and
   templates, and publish the before/after time per step from the logged records.
   Then propose the next topic.

Reporting continues as it has: a dated work folder per video, with its beat sheet,
build log, fact-check and Frictional log; weekly hours from October onward.
