## What this folder is

The **source** of the video, not the video. `beat_sheet.json` is the single source of
truth; media (mp4/mp3/png) is not committed. Rendered masters live in
`~/Downloads/brutalist-reels/youtube/yatra-ads-in-the-picture/`.

## What the episode argues

The assistant people use as a judgment-free helper is making room for advertisers — a new,
clearly labeled visual ad format tested during image generation — the same week it gains
"dots", always-on agents with their own cloud computer and over 4,000 app connections. For
marketers: is the AI conversation the next ad channel?

## The constraint this episode is built around

**Three supplied points, one source (Jumpfly, October 2026), nothing added.** The only
figure is "over 4,000". The word "first" is deliberately absent — earlier 2026 ads in
ChatGPT were reported, so this is a new *format*, not ChatGPT's first ads (see
`FACTCHECK.md`). The B04 screen is a redraw of a description, not OpenAI's UI.
