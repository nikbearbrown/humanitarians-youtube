## Description

```
Every week I take one AI tool from Humanitarians AI, use it properly, and write
up what actually happened. This week the tool was Gordy — and one part of the
work is still sitting in review, so this recap ends mid-pipeline rather than
pretending otherwise.

Gordy is described on its own page as "a two-mode music artist brand strategist
and sonic identity architect" — for independent artists, music teams, and the
Musinique era. That one line is the whole public description, which is a decent
argument for a series that uses tools instead of reviewing them from the
outside.

What this week actually produced: I used Gordy, and I made graphics for the
Humanitarians AI LinkedIn page promoting it. I also wrote two articles about it.
Those are with Nina for review right now — they go up on Substack once
approved. They are not published, and I don't describe them here, because
they're not finalised yet. More coming soon.

Four of five stages closed. The last one isn't mine to close.
```
