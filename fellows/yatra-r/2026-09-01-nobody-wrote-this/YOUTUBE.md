## Description

```
A scan of roughly a million long-form social posts found that LinkedIn is the
most AI-saturated major platform measured — 41% of posts past 250 words are
fully AI-generated, the highest share of any platform in the study.

The stranger finding is the shape of it. Only 4.3% of LinkedIn posts are
AI-assisted or mixed. People are not lightly editing with a machine. They are
handing the whole post over. It is all-or-nothing.

This video walks the numbers, shows where they came from on screen, and stress-
tests them — including the part that cuts against the headline: Substack and
Reddit sit near the bottom of the same scan, which means saturation is a
platform choice, not something AI does on its own.

Every figure in this video is cited on screen. Nothing is estimated. Where a
number was not published, it is not shown — including the human-written share
of LinkedIn posts, which you can work out by subtraction but which no source
actually reported.
```
