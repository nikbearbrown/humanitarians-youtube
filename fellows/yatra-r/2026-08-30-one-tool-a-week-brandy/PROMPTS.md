# PROMPTS — One Tool a Week. (Brandy)

Every prompt shown on screen, verbatim.

## B00 — cold open

**Greeting:** `Hej, Yatra` · **runningText:** `recapping the week…`

The spoken open is "Hi, I'm Yatra" — at the human's request the self-introduction sits at
the very start of the video rather than being deferred to the executive-summary beat.

> I run a weekly series on the Humanitarians AI tools: one tool a week, used on something
> real, then written up. This week was Brandy, our brand-audit tool. Help me recap the week
> — what shipped, what I wrote, and what's coming next.

**Result lines:**

1. `this week's tool: Brandy — brand audit`
2. `shipped: one LinkedIn post, two Substack articles`
3. `next: a proposed fashion sustainability team`

The third line carries the word "proposed" even in the cold open, so the framing is set
before the beat that covers it.

## B08 — the handoff (READ ALOUD VERBATIM)

**Greeting:** `Your turn.` · **runningText:** `paste this into Claude…`

> Audit my own product using my own tool, then tell me honestly what I'd have to admit
> publicly if I published the result.

**Rubric shown beneath:**

1. `grade it: does it name something you'd rather not publish?`
2. `grade it: would you still ship the write-up?`
3. `grade it: is the uncomfortable bit the actual article?`

This generalises the week's own method — running Brandy on Humanitarians AI itself — into a
task any maintainer can run on their own product.
