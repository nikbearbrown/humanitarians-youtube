## What the episode argues

We assumed people trust a human over a machine by default. When an AI tool and a company's
rep disagree, consumers are now equally likely to believe either — the rep is no longer the
tiebreaker. Brands share the last word with the customer's own AI.

## The constraint this episode is built around

**Five figures, one source (Capacity, October 2026), nothing added.** The central finding —
"equally likely to trust either" — was supplied **without a number**, so `CtrScale` has no
numeric prop: the beam settles level and no 50/50 is printed. "Most companies haven't caught
up" is the narrator's read, labelled as interpretation on screen (B06, B09).
