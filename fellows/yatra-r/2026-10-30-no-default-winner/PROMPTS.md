Every prompt shown on screen, verbatim.

**B00 — cold open (`Ciao, Bella`)**
> When an AI tool and a company's human rep give a customer different answers, who does the customer believe? Walk me through what the latest survey found. Cite every figure on screen and add none of your own.

**B04 — ask → B05 result (`The ask,`)**
> Draw the moment a customer hears two different answers — one from the company's rep, one from an AI — as a balance scale. Use only what the survey found: consumers are equally likely to trust either one. No numbers.

> Customers sometimes get a different answer from an AI tool than from our reps, and they're as likely to believe the AI. Find the parts of our policy that are easiest to misread, and draft what a rep should say when a customer says the AI told them otherwise.
