## Refusals enforced by the scene types

- **"Equally likely" carries no figure.** `CtrScale` has no numeric prop; the beam settles
  level and no 50/50 or percentage is printed. A number there would be invented.
- **35% and 49% are one finding and its subgroup**, same source — shown as two bars with one
  citation, not as two studies.
- **No remainders captioned** (e.g. 100 − 44).
- **No invented exchanges.** B03 bubbles and B07 question shapes are placeholder lines,
  captioned as such. B08 shows roles only (customer, their AI agent, a human rep).
- **The person icons are neutral silhouettes** — an earlier draft read as frowning faces,
  which editorialised; fixed before render.
