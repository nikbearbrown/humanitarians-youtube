# PROMPTS — What We Ask For.

Every prompt shown on screen, verbatim.

**B00 — cold open (`Namaste, Yatra`)**
> AI can draft a novel or paint an image in seconds. What is that actually doing to artists' work and pay, and what still needs a human? Use only the findings I give you, and cite each one.

**B07 — ask → B08 result (`The ask,`)**
> Draw the pay gap between AI-proficient creative professionals and peers in the same roles without those skills. Use only the figure I give you.

**B16 — handoff (`Your turn.`)** — read aloud verbatim in narration
> Take one creative project I've finished recently and split it into what a machine could have drafted and what only I brought: interpretation, cultural meaning, strategy, emotional read, and purpose. Then tell me which part people are really paying for.

Rubric shown: does it ask about your project before judging? · does it name what only you
