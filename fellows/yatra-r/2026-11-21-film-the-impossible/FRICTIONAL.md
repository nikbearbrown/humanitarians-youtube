## 2026-11-21

**Tried / expected.** A new 1–3 minute AI explainer (not a variation): AI-generated TikTok trends
as their own genre. Fixed intro, three supplied points from one source, no numbers, examples
described visually, `@Yatra`, native 4K in 16:9 and 9:16.

**Where it resisted, and what was done next.**
- *Showing a lion, a car and wolves without footage or invented detail.* Drew them as flat sketches
  and labelled them "Sketches of the trends, not footage"; no creator is shown or named.
- *No numbers at all.* The scene components were written with no numeric props, so a view count
  could not be added by accident.
- *iCloud.* With the disk nearly full, iCloud offloaded ~13,400 toolkit files (including the Kokoro
  model and the scene registry). Audio failed once and renders stalled for ~20 min. Files were
  downloaded back and 43 orphaned headless-Chrome processes cleared. Moving the toolkit off the
  iCloud Desktop, or keeping disk space free, would prevent this.
- *Layout.* Four preview fixes before the 4K render (clipped label, phone vs closer, small wolves,
  undersized film strip).

Claude wrote the beat sheet and narration, built the Ft* scenes, ran audio, render and compile, did
the frame QC, and diagnosed the iCloud stall.
