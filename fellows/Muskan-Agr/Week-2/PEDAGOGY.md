# PEDAGOGY — humanitarians-ai-week2-typography-hero-concepting

## The one thing a viewer should leave with

*A design opinion becomes actionable the moment it becomes a number.*

Week 1's video said the site's hierarchy was weak and its hero was
media-heavy. Both true, both unarguable-with, and both impossible to schedule
work against. Week 2 is the same claims restated as figures a developer can
close a ticket on: 84 characters per line, 4.12:1, 19 styles across 8 sizes,
2.10× the area. That transition — opinion to specification — is the actual
subject of this video, and it is the transferable skill.

## Structure

The reel runs the five Week 2 threads in the order that builds the argument,
not the order the work happened in:

1. **Scope + method** (B01–B02) — establishes the rule the rest obeys.
2. **Navigation** (B03–B04) — the easiest finding to see, so it teaches the
   viewer how to read the annotations before the harder beats arrive.
3. **Footer** (B05–B07) — counting, at increasing resolution: columns, then a
   column, then a defect inside it.
4. **Typography** (B08–B10) — count the problem, specify the fix, then the one
   measurement (line length) that most people never take.
5. **Accessibility + accent** (B11–B12) — the sharpest finding sits here, at
   the point where the viewer has been trained to trust the numbers.
6. **Hero** (B13–B14) — the payoff, because by now "2.10× the area" reads as
   evidence rather than assertion.

## Deliberate teaching choices

- **The contrast beat never sets type in the failing grey.** The swatches are
  filled rectangles; every label is ink. Demonstrating a contrast failure by
  committing it would be both a QC failure and a bad lesson.
- **Numbers are spoken as words, not digits.** "Four point one two to one"
  survives text-to-speech; "4.12:1" does not.
- **Every screenshot beat shows the whole page, cropped hard but not zoomed
  past legibility.** The source captures are 1503 px wide; a tight column crop
  at 4K is a 34× upscale. Where detail is the point, the detail is typeset
  rather than magnified.
- **B14 shows no proposed layout at all.** The hero direction is stated as a
  principle, not drawn. A speculative wireframe would look like a decision that
  has been made, and no redesign has been settled — the honest move is to name
  the intent and leave the screen to the measurements that back it.

## What this video does not do

It does not show the fix implemented. Week 2 produced a specification; a video
that implied shipped work would be dishonest about where the project stands.
B15 says so plainly.
