# PEDAGOGY — "Before You Rebuild, You Read the Room." (Week 1 of 4)

"whole-sheet teaching-arc checklist" (framework beat / worked example /
falsifiability beat / scaffolded viewer task) doesn't apply wholesale — there's
no viewer exercise to hand off. What DOES apply, and is checked below: does
the viewer walk away with a real, concrete takeaway (review-sheet item #6).

## The takeaway (review-sheet item #6)
A viewer who has never seen this project should be able to state, after
watching: **"The team didn't jump straight to a redesign — they audited the
existing site first, found the real problems were hierarchy and typography
(not content or a lack of features), and that's why the plan changed from a
full rebuild to targeted fixes."** That sentence is assembled entirely from
on-screen claims (B03–B11); nothing requires outside context to land.
