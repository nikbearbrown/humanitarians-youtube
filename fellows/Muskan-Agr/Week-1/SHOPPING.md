# SHOPPING.md — humanitarians-ai-week1-diagnostic-audit

**Nothing to shop. Zero pantry requests.**

Every visual beat in this reel is either (a) one of your six supplied
screenshots, cropped/annotated in `scenes.py`, or (b) a pure typography/
diagram card drawn in Manim (the GOAL card, the pivot cards, the type-scale
ladder, the end card). There are no VOX beats, no "drop a 5–10s gen-AI clip
into pantry/" requests, and no archival-photo HOLDs anywhere in this sheet.

This is a direct consequence of the source material: a UX-audit reel about
a real, existing website is made of screenshots of that website, not
illustrative b-roll. The toolkit's `nopunt` doctrine (never punt an
animatable concept as a placeholder) is satisfied here by going straight to
real assets + real diagrams instead of stand-in imagery.

If a future week's video in this series (Week 2, 3, 4) needs something the
live site can't screenshot — e.g. a "before/after" mockup of the proposed
hero redesign that doesn't exist yet — that's the point where a SHOPPING.md
entry (or a Figma-export pantry drop) would actually apply. Not needed here.
