Before You Rebuild, You Read the Room — Humanitarians AI Restructure, Week 1 of 4

Before touching a single pixel of the Humanitarians AI website, the first job was reading the site the way a stranger reads it. This is Week 1: a heuristic audit against the existing structure, a competitive benchmark against comparable nonprofit and education sites, early typography research, and the redesign concepting that got explored before the scope changed.

What the audit found: the homepage's real problem isn't content, it's hierarchy — six concepts competing for attention before a first-time visitor gets a plain answer to what the organization does. The footer lists eight unlabeled project names with zero context. The typography relies on one bold display weight at nearly every level, so nothing reads as more important than anything else. And the hero section leads with a video embed instead of a message.

That audit is also what changed the plan. The original brief was a full rebuild — but a full rebuild throws out everything already working (the mission clarity, the tone, the black-and-maroon identity) along with everything that isn't. So the direction shifted: fix the structural problems first — navigation, hierarchy, hero, typography — and only revisit a ground-up rebuild if targeted fixes aren't enough.
