## Personal / professional content (self-attested)
The two-environments narrative (a large organization needing traffic/
workflow systems; a volunteer-driven organization needing a creative
department built with nothing in place), the four-step build methodology,
and the "operations support vs. operations design" framing are drawn from
the subject's own professional history, provided by Bhakti Save.

**Anonymization:** at the subject's explicit request, this video uses no
company names and no market-count specifics — "a large organization" and
"a volunteer-driven organization" only. This is a deliberate choice for
this video specifically; it is more anonymized than the companion DAM
video, which does name a market count ("5 international markets"). The
two videos are not held to the same disclosure standard by accident —
this was confirmed directly with the subject.
