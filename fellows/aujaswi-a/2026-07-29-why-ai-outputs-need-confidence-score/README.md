## What This Video Covers

Most AI tools return a single answer with no indication of how sure the system actually is. This video makes the case for why that's a design flaw, not a minor omission.

**Takeaway:** understanding why "the AI said so" isn't a stopping point, and what a better-designed tool would show instead.
