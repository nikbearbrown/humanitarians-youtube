## What This Video Covers

AI research tools are increasingly used to generate market and academic summaries, but most present every finding with equal confidence. This video walks through a simple three-part framework for evaluating any AI-generated research output.

**Takeaway:** a practical checklist viewers can apply the next time an AI tool hands them a confident-sounding answer.
