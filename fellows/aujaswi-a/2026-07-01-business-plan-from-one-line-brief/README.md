## What This Video Covers

Real-world assignments rarely arrive with a full specification. This video tells the honest story of turning an open-ended, one-line request into a complete, structured business plan — the interpretive work of figuring out what's actually being asked for, and how to build something useful even when the scope isn't spelled out in advance.

**Takeaway:** a practical approach to handling ambiguous assignments without stalling or guessing wildly.
