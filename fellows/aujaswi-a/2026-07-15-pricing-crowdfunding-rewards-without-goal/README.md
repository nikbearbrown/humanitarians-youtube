## What This Video Covers

A real strategic problem, told honestly: how do you design a tiered reward structure for a crowdfunding campaign when the overall funding target hasn't been finalized yet? This video walks through the actual tension between generosity and sustainability.

**Takeaway:** a framework for thinking about crowdfunding reward design, applicable well beyond any single campaign.
