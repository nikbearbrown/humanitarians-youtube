Embeddings, One Angle at a Time — Machine Learning · Embeddings

Toy vectors cat (4,1), dog (3,2), car (1,4). Distance says a cat is 8.2 from three cats and 4.2 from a car; cosine gives 1.00 for three cats, 0.94 for dog and 0.47 for car. High cosine means near, not the same, and a zero vector has no cosine.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE
Write a short Python script, no ML libraries, that computes the distance and the cosine between cat = (4, 1) and each of dog = (3, 2), car = (1, 4) and cat times 3 = (12, 3). Before running it, predict which of the three each measure ranks closest to cat.
