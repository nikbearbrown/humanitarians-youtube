Why Attention Divides By The Square Root — Tl;Dr · Machine Learning

The Transformer's scaled dot-product attention divides scores by the square root of the key length. The film shows why: longer vectors give wider scores, wide scores make softmax pick one winner and starve the gradient, a fixed divisor fits one length only, and dividing by the square root keeps the spread constant.

Write a short Python script that draws random queries and keys with variance 4 instead of 1, computes the variance of their dot products at vector length 64, and checks whether dividing by the square root of 64 still gives variance 1. Predict the number before you run it, then say what divisor would fix it.
