THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE
Write a short Python script, numpy only, that applies dropout to the activations 1 through 8: keep each unit with probability 1 minus p, scale the survivors by 1 over 1 minus p, then sum. Run it on 10,000 random masks and print the mean and the standard deviation of the sum, for p = 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9. Predict the means before you run it.
