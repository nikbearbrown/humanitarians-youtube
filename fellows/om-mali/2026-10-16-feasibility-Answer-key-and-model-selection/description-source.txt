Grounding an AI debt tracker in real SEC filings — AI Debt Tracker, week 1

A new Mycroft agent that tracks the bonds and loans financing the AI build-out, graded against each company's next quarterly report. Over 495 dated test cases across 100 companies, filings were read only up to each cutoff, through a point-in-time SEC server that blocks later documents. 87.5% of new bonds were announced in filings before the report showed them, and simply carrying the last quarter forward missed a change 46% of the time. Each quarter is graded at the detail the company's report actually gives. That detail was coarser than assumed: 15 of 25 AI companies list their debt bond by bond. Bonds were matched across reports at 93.4%. On 29 real announcements, a local 4-billion-parameter model extracted 99% of bond details in 6 seconds per filing at no cost, ahead of the larger 8-billion-parameter model at 88%.
