Documenting the System and Making the Demo Re-runnable — Private AI Valuation Agent, week 11

The code was finished. What was missing was the part that lets someone else understand it
without me sitting next to them. So the last week of the build was three things, none of them
code: the documents that explain the system, a literature requirement I had written down and
never met, and a demo that re-runs instead of one that was true on the day I recorded it.

Every count below is measured against the last commit with `git show HEAD`, not remembered.

SIX DOCUMENTS NAMED. THREE OF THEM DID NOT EXIST. Not thin, not stale — absent. The proposal,
the system architecture, and the data architecture. The audit found them; the plan had not
predicted them. 444 lines written from nothing, and 104 added to the three that were already
there. All three were written from the project's own measured figures rather than from the
plan's prose.

A STATED REQUIREMENT, MET ZERO TIMES. The plan says the published documents must cite the
prior-art literature honestly. This data source isn't new — it's commercially exploited and
academically mature, and my own plan says claiming novelty wouldn't survive a literature
review. I grepped for every name: Caplight, Gornall & Strebulaev, Agarwal, Chernenko, Kwon.
Zero.

And the honest version of that is narrower than it first looks. **Two** published documents were
genuinely at zero. The third file in that table is the proposal, which did not exist yet, so its
zero is an absent file rather than a document that failed the requirement. 22 citations are in
now, 13 of them where the requirement actually bit.

ONE OF THEM IS NOT DECORATION. A citation count is not a literature review, and most of those
22 are context. Gornall and Strebulaev established that funds write up every share class to the
latest round price. I reproduced that on this cohort — not cited and assumed, checked against
the panel — and it is the reason dispersion is measured per company with the share class
recorded. It overturned an earlier draft's rule. A citation that only decorates a document can
be added at the end; this one had to be read before the measurement was correct.

FIFTEEN TABLES, THREE LAYERS, THREE RULES. The architecture document's spine is that each layer
is allowed to change in a different way. What the SEC filed is immutable. What a human decided
is append-only. What the code derived is rebuildable. A layer that may be rebuilt can be deleted
and recomputed; an append-only layer cannot, because a human decision is not derivable from
anything. Every one of the 5,806 filed holdings carries exactly one match decision — 45 of them
needed a named human — and the resolved layer holds 5,479 marks, 327 fewer than were filed. The
guards are the difference.

ONE PUBLISHED NUMBER, TRACED TO THE BYTES. 25.0% of consecutive observations unchanged — 1,019
steps of 4,079, over 5,178 published marks — followed down eight links: the published sentence,
the generated figure, the function with its guards, the panel, the match decision, the named
human behind it, the filed position, and the archive file the SEC published. Break any link and
you have an output, not evidence.

A DEMO THAT RE-RUNS. The plan said record a demo. I wrote a script instead: five acts, every
figure queried live, so a transcript that disagrees with the database is a bug rather than an
old file. Zero write statements across the demo and the server it calls. Act II shows a decision
a named human already made and deliberately does not make a new one — a demo that recorded a
judgment would be a demo clearing a gate for a screenshot.

WHAT NONE OF THIS ESTABLISHES. The audit covers the six documents the plan names and no others;
a requirement the plan never wrote down stays invisible to this method. 22 citations is not a
literature review. Zero write statements is a static scan of five files, not a proof. And the
run log that makes the provenance chain datable has two rows in it — a start, not a history.

Where a language model sits in all of this: exactly two places, and neither of them touches a
number. That is stated in the architecture document rather than left to be inferred.

Eleven weeks, and the last one was mostly finding out what I had assumed.
