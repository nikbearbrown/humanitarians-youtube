Every number spoken or shown is a prop injected from `figdata.json` by `build_beat_sheet.py`
under assertions. Nothing is typed into a scene. `figdata.json` transcribes the author's own
saved results as published in `pantry/*.svg` and in `narration_script.md`'s source table and
notes; each bar's geometry in those SVGs was recomputed as a ratio and had to agree with its
