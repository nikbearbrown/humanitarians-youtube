Running full episodes from filings to ledger — AI Debt Tracker, week 5

A LangGraph agent that turns one company-quarter of SEC filings into a ledger of its bonds — timeline, triage, parallel event readers, a code ledger engine and self-checks — run end to end on all 95 development quarters. Against carrying the last report forward, on 45 quarters graded bond by bond, recall rises from 90.9% to 97.8% (+6.9 points, paired) and 30 of 34 newly issued bonds are caught against none; precision falls from 92.8% to 90.2%, and all 26 wrongly added bonds are traced to three causes (existing notes read from a prospectus, launch notices with no coupon yet, the banks' option to buy more). One timing bug found by the first run — bonds sold after the quarter end applied to it — was fixed on development data and both runs are reported. Fresh checks on filings labelled before each run: triage found 15 of 15 debt filings, the event reader read 91% of fields against 74% for regex rules (a direct model call 96%, uncited), and the answer key agreed on 3 of 4 reports. The week was delegated end to end and its gate is recorded as delegated, not reviewed.

Om Mali is a Humanitarians AI fellow. Every number on screen is read from the project's generated reports; the figure build fails if any differs. The labels, the reader truth, the hand-check transcriptions and the diagnosis of the wrong additions are Claude's reading, a model judgment, and are attributed as such.
