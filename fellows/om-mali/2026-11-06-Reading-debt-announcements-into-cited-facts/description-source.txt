Reading debt announcements into cited facts — AI Debt Tracker, week 4

An event reader that turns a debt filing into structured bonds and loans, with every value parsed by code from the words of a cited passage, compared with two baselines on 24 filings whose 66 instruments were labelled before any run (51 of 51 labelled new bonds confirmed in the next quarterly report's XBRL). The reader reads 94% of fields against 84% for regex rules, passing the week's check, but a single model call that types the numbers itself reads 100%: the reader's gain is traceability, not accuracy. Its grounding check refused 24 copied values, 8 of them currency signs the model rewrote (€1,500,000,000 copied as $1,500,000,000). A fresh triage check on 50 newly labelled filings found 8 of 9 debt filings, below the 95% pass line; the miss, a prospectus cover blank down to the year, is logged rather than tuned away. A fresh answer-key hand-check agreed on 1 of 5 reports and exposed four new tagging defects. The week was delegated end to end and its gate is recorded as delegated, not reviewed.
