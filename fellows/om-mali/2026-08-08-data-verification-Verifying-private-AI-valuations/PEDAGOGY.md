## Why the structure changed

The 2026-08-08 cut had six beats: three Remotion title cards and three PNG stills held on
`"motion": "hold"`. Under this toolkit that is a hard failure, not a style choice:

- **THE PPT TEST (SHOW-DON'T-TELL LAW).** "If a beat could be exported as a static slide — a
  headline plus a paragraph while the voice talks — it is not done. Two consecutive slide-beats
  is a slideshow, and a slideshow is a build failure." B02/B03/B04 were three consecutive
  held stills.
- **Missing spine.** ai-explainer requires a cold open on the Claude composer, a Beat-2
  executive summary, a verdict recap, a YOUR TURN handoff, and a title-restate outro. The old
  cut had none of the four bookends.
- **REBUILD LAW.** Figures from a source are rebuilt as native animated graphics; lifted PNGs
  are placeholders, not visuals.

The three source PNGs now live in `pantry/` as **reference for the rebuild only** and are never
slotted as media.

## Palette deviation (logged, deliberate)

The source figures used the Mycroft repo's `brutalist/DESIGN.md` tokens: crimson `#C8102E` for
the data series, ochre `#C8860E` for annotation. The rebuild renders in the **Claude fidelity
skin** — cream `#F2F0E9`, warm ink `#3D3929`, terracotta `#D97757` as the ONE accent — because
`ai-explainer` is a fidelity brand that may not be retinted. Data series and the single focal
moment take terracotta; structure takes ink; de-emphasis takes muted ink. **Palette change
only — no datum, ordering, or label was altered.**
