## Landscape-native decisions

- Reading order is **left → right**: explanation on the left, evidence on the right.
- Anything that is a sequence in time (B06 prediction dates, B04 past → future) runs horizontally.
- Three-way comparisons (B05 targets, B08 and B09 rings) sit side by side at equal width.
- Captions sit in a bottom band; nothing essential shares it.
- No scene was built at 9:16 and cropped, pillar-boxed, or scaled.
