## Portrait-native decisions

- The ML pipeline runs **top-to-bottom** everywhere (tracker, B06 date spine, B15 architecture, B16 stages) — the natural reading direction on a phone.
- Comparisons stack vertically (Customer A rings, B14 cards) instead of side by side.
- The only side-by-side layouts are short: three rings in B09, three count cards in B10, two boundary cards in B15 — each ≥ 270 px wide.
- No scene was designed at 16:9 and cropped.
