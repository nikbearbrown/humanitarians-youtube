# Example syllabus (made up for this demo) — Intro to Data Science

## 1. Office hours
Office hours are Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2 to 4 pm in Room 210. You can also book a slot online.

## 2. Grading
Labs are worth 40 percent of the grade, the midterm 25 percent and the final project 35 percent.

## 3. Late labs
Labs may be submitted up to 48 hours late with a 10 percent penalty. After 48 hours a lab receives no credit.

## 4. Extensions
If you are sick or have an emergency, email the instructor before the due date to ask for an extension.

## 5. Final project
The final project is done in teams of three. Each team presents its work in the last week of class.

## 6. Academic integrity
You may use AI tools to brainstorm, but you must say how you used them. Copying another student's code is not allowed.

## 7. Attendance
Attendance is not graded, but labs are introduced in class and are hard to complete if you miss the session.
