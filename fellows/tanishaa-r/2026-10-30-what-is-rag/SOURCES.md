# SOURCES — What Is RAG?

- Lewis, P. et al. (2020). *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks.* NeurIPS 2020. arXiv:2005.11401. Origin of the term and the retrieve → generate design.
- Karpukhin, V. et al. (2020). *Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering.* EMNLP 2020. arXiv:2004.04906. Embedding-based passage search (B04).
- `demo/syllabus.md`: example syllabus written for this film (fictional, labeled on screen).
- `demo/rag_demo.py` → `demo/retrieval_output.json`: the real TF-IDF search shown in B05 (run 2026-11-05, standard library only, deterministic).

Corrections applied while rewriting (DOUBLE-CHECK LAW):
- "makes the AI smarter" is the misconception the B01 writer corrects. RAG changes the input, not the model.
- "the answer cites its source" is softened to "it can say where each part came from". Citation depends on the prompt and model.
- B04 dot positions are schematic. The film says so on screen rather than implying they were computed.
