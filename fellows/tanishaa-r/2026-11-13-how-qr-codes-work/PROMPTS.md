# PROMPTS — How a QR Code Works

No open slots, so there are no media-generation prompts to hand off.

Prompts on screen:
- **B00 (ask):** How does my phone read a QR code?
- **B10 (your turn):** Act as my tutor. Give me a short message as bits, add one extra parity bit, then flip a bit and let me find the error. Then show me how QR codes go further and fix errors, not just find them.
