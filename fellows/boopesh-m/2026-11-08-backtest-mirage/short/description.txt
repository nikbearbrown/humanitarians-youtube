The Backtest Mirage #Shorts

Let a search try enough trading rules and the best one always looks brilliant, even on pure random noise. In my
small experiment on synthetic noise data (zero real edge), the best of 1 rule scores a Sharpe near 0, the best of
100 about 1.1, the best of 10,000 about 1.6. The 10,000-rule winner (1.69) then scores −0.37 on two years it never
saw; across 200 noise markets the winners average about zero. The fix: count your trials, hold out data you never
touch, and demand more as you test more.

Idea: Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu, "Pseudo-Mathematics and Financial Charlatanism", Notices of the AMS (2014).
