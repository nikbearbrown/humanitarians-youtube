Why not sell 10,000 shares all at once? Because a big sale pushes the price against you. Sell slowly and the price can drift while you wait. In a synthetic market (a numpy simulation, not real data), a tabular Q-learning agent learns how to split the order over 10 time steps, and it lands close to the Almgren–Chriss closed-form schedule.

Results on 20,000 held-out simulated days: all at once 100 bps; equal slices 9.9 bps on average with a 65 bps bad day (95th pct); the agent 13.3 bps on average with a 54 bps bad day.
