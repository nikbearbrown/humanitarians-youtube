No open slots: every beat is generated from code and toolkit components. No pantry media, and no
paid or generated imagery.

## B80 — the viewer's prompt (shown in the composer and read aloud)
> Write numpy code that sells 10,000 shares over 10 steps with price impact. Compare all at once,
> equal slices and a Q-learning agent. Then double the impact: does the agent sell faster or slower
> at the start?

What to expect: in this model, doubling η halves κ̃² = λσ²/η, so κ falls and the schedule gets
**flatter** (slower at the start, closer to equal slices).
