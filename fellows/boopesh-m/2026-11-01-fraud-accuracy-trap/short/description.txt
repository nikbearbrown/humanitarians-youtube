The 99.9% Accuracy Trap #Shorts

Card fraud is rare: about 1 transaction in 580 (492 frauds in 284,807, in the widely used ULB / Worldline
dataset). So a model that always says "legit" is 99.8% accurate and catches zero fraud. In my small
experiment on synthetic data with the same fraud rate, a gradient-boosted model is 99.9% accurate too —
but it catches 127 of 148 frauds. For rare events, check recall, precision, and what each mistake costs.
