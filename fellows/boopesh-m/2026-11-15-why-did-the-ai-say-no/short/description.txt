Why Did the AI Say No? #Shorts

US lending rules (Regulation B) say a denied applicant gets specific reasons; "the model said no" doesn't count. In my
small experiment on synthetic applicants, made-up Applicant A is denied by a simple scorecard (53% risk) and by a
black-box model (34%), against a 30% line I chose. The scorecard's top reasons are exact: debt-to-income, card use, a
missed payment. The black box needs 16,000 model runs (Shapley values) and shares only 2 of 3 reasons. Paying card
balances down about $3,850 flips both models to yes. Not legal advice.
