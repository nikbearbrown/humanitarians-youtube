All beats render from props in beat_sheet.json. No generated images, no paid API, no open slots.

## BHTF — Your Turn prompt

```
Here is a workflow where a manager types in everyone's hours: [describe it]. Redesign it so each person submits their own hours with evidence and the manager approves or returns them. List the rules that stop abuse.
```

Rubric:

- Can anyone approve their own hours?
- What happens to an approved entry?
- Is there a record of every change?
