# Frictional Log — Grounding and Retrieval

**Week of 2026-09-11** · Adwait Changan · Agentic AI playlist (Episode 6)
Topic: retrieval doesn't make an agent truthful — a citation you can check does.

**What I built this week**
- Four answers, each with a citation, run through the automatic check anyone would write.
- Two citations pass the check. One of those two is *still false*.

**The one idea it teaches**
- The source is real and the quote is real, but the claim built on top of it doesn't follow. A check can confirm a quote exists in a named source; it can't confirm the quote actually supports your sentence.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- Resisted the tempting shortcut of "it has a citation, so it's grounded." Showed the exact case where that reasoning breaks.

**What I learned**
- Checkable citations only cover the part a check can reach. The gap between "quote exists" and "quote supports the claim" is where things go wrong.
