# Frictional Log — Mycroft: Disagreement Log

**Week of 2026-10-02** · Adwait Changan · Mycroft track
Topic: a reviewer who disagrees needs somewhere to say so that doesn't overwrite the engine.

**What I built this week**
- Before this, agreement was recordable and disagreement wasn't — so the only way to dissent was to change the number.
- Built a log that keeps both sides verbatim: the engine's value is copied from the run, the reviewer's position is named and dated, and nothing is reconciled.

**The one idea it teaches**
- The log resolves nothing on purpose. Its job is to make a disagreement *visible* to whoever reads the report — not to decide who was right.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- Resisted the instinct to "resolve" the conflict in code. Kept both positions side by side instead.

**What I learned**
- Overwriting a number to dissent destroys the record. A disagreement needs its own place to live.
