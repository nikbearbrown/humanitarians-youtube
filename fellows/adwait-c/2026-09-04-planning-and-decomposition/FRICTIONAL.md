# Frictional Log — Planning and Decomposition

**Week of 2026-09-04** · Adwait Changan · Agentic AI playlist (Episode 5)
Topic: planning only pays off when steps are expensive *and* the world holds still.

**What I built this week**
- An explainer that *prices* a plan instead of assuming planning is good practice.
- A worked example: planning first costs 9 calls vs 16 when nothing changes — it saves 7.
- A truth table showing only 1 of 4 situations actually makes planning worth it.

**The one idea it teaches**
- If something changes at step 1, the plan is thrown away and the saving is gone. The later the change lands, the more of the plan survives.

**What I learned**
