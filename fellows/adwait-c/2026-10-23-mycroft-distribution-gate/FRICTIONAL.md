# Frictional Log — Mycroft: Distribution Gate

**Week of 2026-10-23** · Adwait Changan · Mycroft track
Topic: four checks each produced a careful artifact and then stopped — nothing stood between a finished report and someone acting on it.

**What I built this week**
- A gate that blocks by default and clears only when every condition is demonstrated by an artifact on disk.
- It reports every reason at once, not one per round — a gate that reveals failures one at a time gets routed around.
- Two judgements are stated as judgements: STALE clears but EXPIRED doesn't; a missing disagreement log clears while an open entry blocks.

**The one idea it teaches**
- A check nobody ran is invisible on the finished report. The gate makes the checks count.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- QC caught three things: a beat miscounted the gate as 4 conditions when it has 5 (the 5th clears on nothing) — fixed the count, heading, narration and audio; a named approver was shown without a "fixture" label — labelled it; two cards overlapped — shortened the subtitles.

**What I learned**
- The gate is evidence, not enforcement — it can't actually stop anyone, and saying so is part of being honest about it.
