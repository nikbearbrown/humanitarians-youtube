**Week of 2026-09-11** · Adwait Changan · Mycroft track
Topic: the loop that runs after a reviewer questions an investigation.

**What I built this week**
- Each follow-up is bound by hash to the exact investigation it questions.
- Every specialist result is regenerated from scratch and compared by fingerprint, not trusted.
- Ran three follow-ups and got three different outcomes.

**The one idea it teaches**
- Three follow-ups, three answers, only one "yes": one verified by exact replay, one causal claim rejected as unsupported, one left open because the evidence doesn't exist.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- Even when the replay matched exactly, closure was still blocked. Kept that honest rather than letting a clean replay look like a full answer.

**What I learned**
- "Verified" means the evidence replayed — not that anyone established a cause.
