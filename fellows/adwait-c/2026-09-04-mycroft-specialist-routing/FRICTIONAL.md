# Frictional Log — Mycroft: Specialist Routing

**Week of 2026-09-04** · Adwait Changan · Mycroft track
Topic: a supervisor that ranks evidence gaps by money and routes each to the right specialist.

**What I built this week**
- Turned a single finance agent into a supervised team.
- A lineage specialist checks the whole source chain *before* anything is delegated.
- Outstanding gaps are ranked by cumulative money impact and routed to the specialist scoped to chase them. Every delegation is capped; every result is fingerprinted.

**The one idea it teaches**
- The most important gap always sits at the top of the queue — and no agent can close it, because it needs an owner's causal explanation.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- Kept the "human owns the causal explanation" boundary firm: the system ranks and routes, but stops at the human instead of guessing a cause.

**What I learned**
- Routing by money surfaces the right work, but it also exposes that the highest-value answer is the one a human still has to write.
