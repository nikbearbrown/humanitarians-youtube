# Frictional Log — Mycroft: Materiality Approval

**Week of 2026-09-18** · Adwait Changan · Mycroft track
Topic: the agent can compute a threshold, but it cannot approve one.

**What I built this week**
- Split the two halves: the engine *prepares* an approval request, and a named human *signs* it.
- Ran four attempts and checked each one.

**The one idea it teaches**
- Approval is a named person's, or it doesn't exist. The agent signing is refused. A human signing with a placeholder reason is refused. A named person with a stated basis is recorded. Signing again is refused.

**Where it pushed back (and what I did)**
- QC: the on-screen approver could be misread as a real person. Relabelled the artifact as "test fixtures" so it's unambiguously a fixture.

**What I learned**
- An approval you can quietly replace is not evidence of anything. The threshold is a judgement, and judgement needs a name attached.
