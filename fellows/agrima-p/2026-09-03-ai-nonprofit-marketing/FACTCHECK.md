   Complete 2026 guide*.

   workflow 2026*.
4. **"Personalized emails... reportedly double open and click-through
   rates."** — Planetary Labour (2026), same source; note the article's own
   hedge word "reportedly" is preserved in this reel's narration (B05).
5. **"Average donations of about $161 compared to $115 industry-wide."**
   — Planetary Labour (2026), same source.
6. **The tools named as mostly free/discounted** (Claude, ChatGPT, Canva's
   AI design features, AI-assisted email platforms) — Slam Media Lab (2026),
   same source. Not repeated on-screen in this reel (no beat lists specific
   tool names) to avoid the DOUBLE-CHECK LAW's "anything that could date the
   video" concern — a 2026-dated tool-availability claim ages fastest.
7. **The Loon Project references** (B00B, B02, B09) — the user's own
   first-person account, matching the article's own framing (the article's
   author writes in first person about working on the Loon Project); not an
   external statistic.
