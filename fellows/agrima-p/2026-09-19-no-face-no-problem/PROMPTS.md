# PROMPTS — no-face-no-problem

No pantry/archival assets used — every non-composer beat is a from-scratch
Manim scene in scenes.py; every composer beat's "prompt" IS the on-screen
content (see beat_sheet.json `shot.remotion.props.command`).

## B00 — the cold open ask

```
claude "help me understand why faceless AI accounts are
  suddenly everywhere — and why people trust them"
```

## B08 — the handoff (HANDOFF LAW — read aloud and discussed)

```
claude "help me fact-check the next faceless account I
  watch before I trust what it's telling me"
```

## Generated visuals (per the user's explicit request — no stock footage)

- **B01 stock-footage card**: abstract skyline bars, sine-wave lines, and a
  simple flame silhouette inside a bordered "STOCK FOOTAGE" frame, with a
  bold caption ("5 FACTS ABOUT MONEY NOBODY TELLS YOU") standing in for the
  genre's own aesthetic — deliberately not a composer/host card, per the
  user's note that this would reinforce the article's own point.
- **B02 strategy flow**: Topic -> Script -> AI Voice -> Stock Footage ->
  Post, five cards with connecting arrows, plus a crossed-out camera icon.
- **B03 voice-gap comparison**: two cards contrasting a "GPS voice" (jagged
  waveform, real-cost tag) against a voice that "passes as human"
  (smoother waveform, per-minute cost tag).
- **B04 niche grid**: five cards (Personal Finance, Tech & AI News, True
  Crime, Psychology, Self-Improvement).
- **B05 trust-stat card**: the stat plus an on-screen red-bordered
  skepticism flag — not a plain, unqualified stat card.
- **B06 watch-time meter**: a filling progress bar labeled WATCH TIME with
  completion/saves/shares tags and a crossed-out face icon.

If a real clip would meaningfully improve any of these later (e.g. an
actual faceless-account example, screen-recorded with permission), swap it
in for the corresponding generated card — none is required for this build.
