# SOURCE-brief — "Claude, Gated." (Week 2)

## The original ask

Same 4-week `finance-event-signals` build (see Week 1's `SOURCE-brief.md`
for the full original ask). Week 2 adds the GIGO gate and the LangGraph
enrichment agent on top of Week 1's data spine.

## Why this content for Week 2

Week 2 is where the project's Snickerdoodle discipline actually starts
biting: a machine gate that must reject with a reason, and an agent that
must withhold rather than guess. Both are gates that exist specifically to
prevent fabrication, which is the through-line of the whole project.
