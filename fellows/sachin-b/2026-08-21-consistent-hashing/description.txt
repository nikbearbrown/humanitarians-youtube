Claude, Ringed. — Consistent Hashing, Mechanically — Humanitarians AI

A system-design interview staple, explained as a mechanism, not a buzzword.
Hash your servers and your keys onto the same circle; a key belongs to
whichever server comes next, clockwise. Add or remove a server, and only
the keys near it move.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
Why the hash space is a circle, not a line. How a key finds its owner with
no modulo, just "which point is next." Why adding a node only remaps one
local arc, not the whole ring. Why plain consistent hashing is unfair at
small N, and why virtual nodes are the actual fix used in production.
