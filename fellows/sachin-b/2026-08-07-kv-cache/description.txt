Claude, Cached. — Why LLMs Pause Then Stream — Humanitarians AI

Ever notice a model pauses, then the words just stream out fast? That gap
has a name: the KV-cache. This is the actual systems-level mechanism —
prefill vs. decode — not a UI trick, told through purpose-built diagrams of
attention Keys and Values.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
Why attention needs the Key/Value of every past token, and why those never
need recomputing once made. Why prefill (the whole prompt, one parallel
pass) is the "thinking" pause, and decode (one new token at a time) is the
fast part. Why the cache grows without bound — and why that's the real
reason long conversations get slow and expensive, not "the model thinking
harder."
