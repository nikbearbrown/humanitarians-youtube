Claude, Nearest. — How Search Actually Finds "Similar" — Humanitarians AI

"Similar" isn't a string match — it's a location. This is how meaning
becomes a point in space, and how approximate nearest-neighbor search finds
its neighbors fast at scale without checking every single vector.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
How an embedding model turns text into a vector where distance equals
semantic similarity. Why brute-force search works for a thousand vectors
but not a billion. How graph-based approximate search hops toward the right
neighborhood instead of scanning everything. Why "approximate" is a real
trade-off, not a hidden flaw — faster search costs some recall.
