# PROMPTS — Prompt Engineering for Product Managers

No image, video or audio generation prompts were used. Narration is local Kokoro text-to-speech (voice am_onyx); visuals are deterministic Remotion scenes.

Prompts shown on screen (text only, not run by this build):

1. B00 composer ask: "How do prompts shape an AI product?"
2. B09 viewer handoff prompt: "I am a product manager building an AI assistant that does this: [describe it]. Write a first system prompt with a role, rules, tone and limits. Add three few-shot examples, including one tricky case. Then give me ten test inputs, five normal and five adversarial, and a rubric to score the answers. Finally, tell me which line of the system prompt is most likely to be ignored."
