## What it covers
The fundamental reason why Large Language Models fail at formatting and arithmetic, explaining how token prediction causes downstream application crashes and why deterministic middleware is required.

## Why this topic, this week
There is a persistent misconception that you can "prompt engineer" an LLM into behaving like a rigid database. This video breaks down the concept of token fragmentation to prove why LLM output is fundamentally probabilistic and why attempting to pipe it directly into strict backend infrastructure is an architectural failure.
