The Logout That Didn't — a Medhavi Hub postmortem

A bug report: the Logout button logs nobody out. Every user, every time, and
only in production. The same code, on a developer's laptop, logged out
perfectly — and nothing in the logout code had changed for months.

This is the postmortem. What broke, why it was invisible exactly where you would
look for it, and the two things worth taking away.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
What the button was actually doing — a server call, a sweep through every cookie
it could see, local and session storage, then a redirect. What it never did: ask
the identity provider to end the session. Why that worked for months and then
stopped, when the project moved from the provider's development setup to its
production one. Why a cookie marked HttpOnly on someone else's domain cannot be
deleted by your page, and why that is the flag doing its job rather than a bug.
And what the fix looks like when you write it carefully — a sign-out call raced
against a five-second timer, so a slow third-party service cannot strand a user
on a page that will not let them leave.

BE PRECISE ABOUT WHAT WAS FIXED
Logging out always revoked textbook access correctly — that runs off a separate
server-side check and was never affected. It was the hub's own session that
survived. That half is fixed on main.

TRY IT YOURSELF
The prompt from the end of the video:

"My logout clears cookies and local storage by hand instead of calling my auth
provider's sign-out. Tell me what that breaks once the session cookie is
HttpOnly and on the provider's own domain, and what else in my app might be
assuming state lives somewhere it no longer does."

That last clause is the one to push on. This was never really a bug about
logout — it was a bug about an assumption that quietly stopped being true.
