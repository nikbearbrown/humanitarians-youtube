## The One Idea

Applicant Tracking Systems auto-reject international students before a human sees their name. But the filter is based on a declared status that is often wrong — a company that raised a Series A and filed H-1B petitions last year almost certainly can sponsor. Public SEC Form D filings and USCIS H-1B data let you find which companies can hire you *before* you apply, then contact founders directly.
