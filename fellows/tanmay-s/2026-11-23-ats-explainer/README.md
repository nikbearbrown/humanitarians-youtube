## What this video is about

International students applying to jobs get auto-rejected by ATS systems before a human sees their name. The fix is not a better resume — it is bypassing the ATS entirely by finding funded startups that can sponsor visas via SEC Form D filings, then extracting direct founder contact emails.

**The pipeline:**
1. Pull SEC Form D filings — companies that raised capital are legally required to file
2. Cross-reference H-1B petition data to confirm sponsorship history
3. Scrape founder emails directly from company homepages
4. Send targeted outreach to the founder — no ATS involved

**Live test:** 1,000 funded companies, 25% with a publicly listed founder email — 200–250 direct contacts at scale.
