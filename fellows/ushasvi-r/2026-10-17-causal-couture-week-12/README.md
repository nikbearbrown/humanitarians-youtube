The root beat sheet is the horizontal master. Separate compositions live under `remotion/horizontal/` and `remotion/vertical/`; the vertical beat sheet is included beside its composition.

**Validated implementation result:** 9 tests executed, 9 passed, 0 failures. Both primary review functions imported successfully.
