# Correlation Is Not Causation: Why Business Analytics Can Mislead You

- Presenter: Ushasvi Rachel
- Organization: Humanitarians.ai
- Project/topic: Correlation, causation, confounding, descriptive analytics, causal inference, and forecasting
- Summary: A standalone STEM/data-science explainer showing why engagement and sales moving together does not prove causation, and why stronger methodology is required for causal claims.
- Aspect ratios produced: 16:9 (3840×2160 master), 9:16 vertical, and 9:16 Shorts cut
- Main source files: `beat_sheet.json`; `remotion/scenes/CorrelationCausationExplainer.tsx`; `remotion/tokens/{claude,layout}.ts`

Created with Brutalist using a concise beat-sheet script, Kokoro narration timing, Remotion charts and confounding diagrams, and the local assembly pipeline. Visuals are representative generated graphics, not historical screenshots.
