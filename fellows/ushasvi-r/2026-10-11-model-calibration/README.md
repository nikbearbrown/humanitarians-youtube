# Model Calibration: Can You Trust a Model’s Confidence?

- **Presenter:** Ushasvi Rachel
- **Organization:** Humanitarians.ai
- **Type:** Standalone STEM/AI education
- **Formats:** 16:9 master source and portrait-native 9:16 source

An explainer about whether predicted probabilities align with observed frequencies across groups of comparable predictions, including overconfidence, underconfidence, and reliability diagrams.

The root holds the horizontal master beat sheet. Format-specific compositions and the vertical beat sheet are organized under `remotion/`.

**Educational boundary:** Calibration is a group-level probabilistic property. It is distinct from accuracy, discrimination, causal validity, and guarantees for individual predictions.
