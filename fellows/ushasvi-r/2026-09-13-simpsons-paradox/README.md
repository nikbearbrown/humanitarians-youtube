# Simpson’s Paradox: When the Overall Trend Tells the Wrong Story

- **Presenter:** Ushasvi Rachel
- **Organization:** Humanitarians.ai
- **Type:** Standalone STEM/AI education
- **Formats:** 16:9 master source and portrait-native 9:16 source

An accessible explanation of how an aggregate trend can reverse within important subgroups because the groups are represented in different proportions.

The root beat sheet represents the horizontal master. Horizontal and vertical compositions are separated under `remotion/`, with the vertical beat sheet retained beside its source.

**Educational boundary:** Aggregate and subgroup statistics may both be calculated correctly. Simpson’s Paradox does not automatically establish confounding or causal truth.
