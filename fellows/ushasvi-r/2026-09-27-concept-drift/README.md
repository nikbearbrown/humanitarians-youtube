# Concept Drift: Why Good Models Get Worse Over Time

- **Presenter:** Ushasvi Rachel
- **Organization:** Humanitarians.ai
- **Type:** Standalone STEM/AI education
- **Formats:** 16:9 master source and portrait-native 9:16 source

An explainer about model performance changing when the relationship between inputs and outcomes changes after deployment, with a concise distinction from broader data drift.

The horizontal master beat sheet is at the root. Horizontal and vertical compositions, plus the vertical beat sheet, are organized under `remotion/`.

**Educational boundary:** Warning signs require investigation. Not every performance drop is drift, and not every distribution change requires retraining.
