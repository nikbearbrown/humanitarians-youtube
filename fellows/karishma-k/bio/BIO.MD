# Professional Bio

I am Karishma Suresh Kumar, a software engineer and AI researcher with a
background in computer science, cloud engineering, backend development, and
distributed systems. I began my professional engineering journey working on
cloud automation and infrastructure services at IBM and Kyndryl. That work
strengthened my interest in building reliable systems and understanding how
software behaves at scale.

I continued developing this foundation through graduate study in computer
science at New York University and through software development and AI/ML work
at Locomex. Across these experiences, I have worked with backend services,
REST APIs, cloud platforms, data-processing workflows, and applied machine
learning.

I am currently a Humanitarians AI Fellow contributing to the Madison project.
My research focuses on persona-aware LLM visibility: evaluating not only how
often a brand appears in AI-generated recommendations, but also which kinds of
users receive those recommendations. I am developing the research design for
controlled prompts, representative personas, evaluation metrics, and a
reproducible Python-based evaluation pipeline. The project is presently in its
research and design phase; prototype implementation and experimental data
collection are planned as subsequent steps.

I am interested in responsible AI evaluation and in building software systems
that turn complex technical questions into transparent, useful evidence.
