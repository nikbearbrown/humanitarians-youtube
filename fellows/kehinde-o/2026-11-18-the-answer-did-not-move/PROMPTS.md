# PROMPTS — The Answer Did Not Move

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> My graph compiler rewrites resnet18 from 141 operations to 32 and reports that nothing
> changed. Help me prove that independently instead of taking the report's word for it.

## B07 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Think of a tool you trust to tell you it worked: a spell checker, a backup, an auto-save.
> Find one way to confirm it without asking the tool.
