# Fact-Checking Chapters 10 and 13

**Volunteer:** Kehinde Obidele  
**Week:** 8 (chapters reviewed 1 Oct 2026)  
**Type:** Project update  
**Runtime:** 2m58s  

Chapter 10 of the Medhavy textbook covers apoptosis: 114 sentences, 64 flagged,
10 claims in my scope, 7 need revision and 3 approved. Chapter 13 covers invasion and
metastasis: 274 sentences, 131 flagged, 37 in scope, 22 need revision and 15 approved.

Chapter 10 calls a phase 3 trial of APR-246 with azacitidine significant when it missed
its primary endpoint (33.3 against 22.4 percent, P = 0.13). Chapter 13 lists Sticker's
sarcoma, a cancer of dogs, among human cancers, and says MMP inhibitors are in trials when
ClinicalTrials.gov shows no active oncology trial for any of the 29 agents I searched. It
also carries 2,140 words copied from 16 NCBI Bookshelf chapters, and one error came in
with the copy.

I also checked my own 24 replacement sentences for Chapter 13 and found four problems in
them. Both chapters' corrections are merged.

Narration is an AI voice (Kokoro). Research and script are mine.
