# Pedagogy Review

## Topic
Fact-Checking Chapters 10 and 13: Apoptosis, and Invasion and Metastasis, in the
Medhavy Cancer Textbook

## Learning Objective
The viewer leaves able to check their own corrections the way they checked the
original: does the source actually say what my fix says, and does the fix still
read well aloud. Both chapters showed that a review is not finished when the
textbook is corrected.

## Audience
General audience interested in AI, research integrity and how AI-written text goes
wrong. No cell biology background assumed. Each finding is explained by what the
chapter says against what the record says, not by mechanism detail.

## Key Findings Reported
- Chapter 10: 114 sentences, 64 flagged, 10 in scope. 7 need revision, 3 approved.
  7 + 3 = 10. 8 text edits across 3 files.
- Chapter 13: 274 sentences, 131 flagged, 37 in scope. 22 need revision, 15
  approved. 22 + 15 = 37. 24 text edits across all 11 files.
- The two chapters are reported side by side and never summed.
- APR-246 with azacitidine is described as "significantly" improving complete
  remission. The phase 3 trial missed its primary endpoint: 33.3 against 22.4
  percent, P = 0.13.
- Sticker's sarcoma, listed among human cancers, is a transmissible cancer of dogs.
- "Several MMP inhibitors are in clinical trials": none active among oncology
  trials on ClinicalTrials.gov across 29 agents and drug classes. Control query:
  1,292 active pembrolizumab studies.
- 2,140 copied words in 41 runs from 16 NCBI Bookshelf chapters, across 8 of 11
  files; five files roughly half copied. The VCAM-1 error arrived with the copy.
- One copied sentence is correct in file 5 and wrong in file 9; the audit graded
  the two copies the wrong way round.
- My own fixes: two Chapter 10 replacements read badly aloud; a fourth Chapter 13
  pass found four problems in my 24 replacement sentences.

## Accuracy Gate
Every figure traces to the completed Chapter 10 and 13 review records. See
FACTCHECK.md, which also records one correction to the handoff brief.

## Restraint Gate
- The video does NOT say the APR-246 trial failed, was terminated, or that the drug
  does not work. It missed its primary endpoint with a numerically higher rate.
- The video does NOT say no MMP inhibitor trial exists anywhere. It says none is
  active among oncology trials on ClinicalTrials.gov, the registry searched.
- The video does NOT say the whole of Chapter 13 is plagiarised. Three of its
  eleven files contain no copied runs.
- No clinical advice. No individual is named as responsible for any error.
- The narration is an AI voice. The outro says so on screen.

## Takeaway
Check your last correction the way you checked the original: does the source say
what your fix says, and does it read well aloud.
