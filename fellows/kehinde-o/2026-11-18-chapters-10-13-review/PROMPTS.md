# PROMPTS — Fact-Checking Chapters 10 and 13

The on-screen prompts, as typed into the composer.

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> I finished the human fact-check of chapters 10 and 13 of the Medhavy cancer
> textbook, on apoptosis and on metastasis. Help me report both, separately,
> including what I found when I checked my own corrections.

## B09 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Take the last correction you made to anything and check it the way you checked
> the original. Does your source say what your fix says? Then read it aloud.
