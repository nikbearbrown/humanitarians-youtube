# Pedagogy Review

## Topic
Fact-Checking Chapter 2 of the Medhavy Cancer Textbook

## Learning Objective
Viewer understands the scope and findings of the Chapter 2 fact-check review,
including the pattern of stale statistics in AI-generated medical text: that a
number can be real, correctly transcribed, and still wrong because the source it
came from has aged.

## Audience
General audience interested in AI, healthcare, and textbook quality. No oncology
background assumed.

## Key Findings
- 46 main claims reviewed: 25 TRUE, 18 FALSE, 3 FLAGGED (39% false against 15% in chapter 1)
- 134 AI-only sentences reviewed: 3 additional errors caught
- 8 editorial issues documented
- Two systemic patterns: stale 2021 data (6 claims) and stale 2007 WHO data (3 claims)
- AI-only tab errors: reversed pathway, wrong century, garbled hallmark

## Register check (Plain)
Leads with method, states the decision trigger (check the date on the source),
and states where it fails: 3 claims were returned flagged rather than guessed.

## Honesty

estimated. The video does not name the textbook's authors or quote its pages.
