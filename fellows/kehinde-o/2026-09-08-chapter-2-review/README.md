## Chapter 2 by the numbers

39% of the main-tab claims were false, against 15% in chapter 1.

## The pattern: stale data

Six claims in the History of Cancer Research section all came from one 2021
commemorative article and were never updated for a 2026 textbook.

| The textbook says | The current number |
|---|---|
| 71 NCI-Designated Cancer Centers | 74 |
| About 100 targeted therapies | Over 157 |
| 17 million survivors | 18.6 million |
| Death rate declined 31% | 34% |

Three more claims in Epidemiology came word for word from a 2007 WHO
publication, making them nearly twenty years out of date.

A number can be real, correctly transcribed, and still wrong because its source
has aged. Check the date on the source, not just the source.
