**What I was working on.** The Chapter 2 fact-check: 46 main claims (25 TRUE, 18
FALSE, 3 FLAGGED), 134 AI-only sentences spot-checked, and 8 editorial findings.
Then the video reporting it.

**What I tried, and what I expected.**
- After Chapter 1 I expected the errors to be scattered, one bad citation at a
  time.
- I expected the AI-only tab, the sentences the model said needed no human review,
  to be safe to skim.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**
- **The errors clustered by source, not by topic.** Six false claims all traced
  back to a single 2021 commemorative article, and three more to a 2007 WHO
  publication. The chapter was not wrong in scattered places; it had leaned on two
  stale sources and inherited their numbers. That changes what a fix looks like:
  correcting the sentences individually would have left the pattern in place.
- **The AI-only tab was not safe to skim.** Three of the 134 sentences the model
  had cleared were wrong. Spot-checking that tab is now part of how I work rather
  than an optional extra.
- **My supervisor changed three of my calls.** Prof. Evin reviewed the chapter and
  approved it with three minor changes, which I applied to the TextEdit guide.
  That is the first external check on my judgment in this project and it is worth
  recording that it was not unanimous.
- **The same trigger-word bug from week 1 was still present** in this reel's
  overview beat, so the on-screen correction never played. Found and rebuilt later.
- **The narration voice changed this week** from Kokoro `am_onyx` to `af_kore`,
  and stayed there. Recorded as a deliberate re-voice rather than a silent
  per-episode default.
