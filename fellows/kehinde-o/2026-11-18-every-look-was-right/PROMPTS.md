# PROMPTS — Every Look Was Right

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> My analytics engine runs A/B tests. Help me measure what happens to false alarms when
> an experiment is checked ten times instead of once, including with my own sequential test.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Think of a result you checked more than once before deciding. How many times did you
> look, and did you stop at the first answer you liked?
