## Executive Summary

I am reviewing the 38-chapter Medhavy cancer textbook for factual accuracy. The textbook was generated by AI and contains fabricated citations, wrong statistics, outdated figures, and citation-claim mismatches. I verify every flagged claim against authoritative sources (PubMed, FDA Orange Book, FDA Purple Book, NCI SEER, CDC, WHO), record my decisions in the chapter workbook, and submit text corrections as pull requests to the medhavi-cancer repository. In the current period I completed fact-check reviews for seven chapters (1, 2, 6, 9, 23, 25 and 30) and submitted PR #46 with text corrections for five of them; Chapters 6 and 9 are reviewed and documented but not yet submitted.
