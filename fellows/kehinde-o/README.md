## Research question

The Medhavy cancer textbook was generated by AI. **Which of its claims survive
contact with the primary sources, and what shape do the failures take?**

The answer matters because the errors are not what people expect. Very little is
invented outright. The failures cluster into a small number of recurring shapes —
a real citation with the wrong authors, a real number attached to the wrong
protocol, a drug filed in the wrong class, a sentence that was true when written
and is not true now — and every one of them survives a confident read. That is
what makes them expensive, and it is why the check has to open the source rather
than judge the sentence.

Seven chapters have been audited against PubMed, PMC, the FDA Orange and Purple
Books, NCI SEER, CDC and WHO. Each produces a reviewed workbook, an evidence
document recording the source chain for every claim, and an MDX TextEdit guide
for applying the corrections.
