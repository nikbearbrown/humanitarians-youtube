## Executive Summary

I am reviewing the 38-chapter Medhavy cancer textbook for factual accuracy. The textbook was generated by AI and contains fabricated citations, wrong statistics, outdated figures, and citation-claim mismatches. I verify every flagged claim against authoritative sources (PubMed, FDA Orange Book, FDA Purple Book, NCI SEER, CDC, WHO), record my decisions in the chapter workbook, and submit text corrections as pull requests to the medhavi-cancer repository. In my first five weeks I completed fact-check reviews for five chapters (1, 2, 23, 25, and 30) and submitted PR #46 with text corrections.
