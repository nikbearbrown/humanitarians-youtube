> I finished the human fact-check of chapter 23 of the Medhavy cancer textbook,
> the chemotherapy chapter: 474 sentences, 136 flagged, 86 rows reviewed. Help me
> report it, including the finding that matters most for patient safety and the
> fact that I audited my own review three times.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Take a piece of research I have already checked. For every claim I approved,
> open the cited source and confirm the exact sentence I quoted is on the page,
> not paraphrased from a summary. Do not re-check the claims. Check the checking.
