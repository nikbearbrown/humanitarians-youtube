## What it covers

Censoring, measured on my synthetic cancer cohort where every true survival time is known. True median 51.4 months; dropping censored patients gives 32.8; counting them as deaths 43.4; Kaplan-Meier 51.1.

## Why this topic, this week

Pairs with Chapters 19 and 27: a fact loses its meaning when its context is dropped.

## Limits

- Synthetic patients only.
- Censoring here is independent of outcome by design, which Kaplan-Meier assumes.
