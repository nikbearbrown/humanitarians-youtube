# PROMPTS — The Patients Who Left Early

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> My survival project generates 5,000 synthetic cancer patients and knows each one's
> true survival time. About 29 percent leave before their outcome is seen. Help me
> measure what each way of handling them does to the answer.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Find a number averaged over people: a rating, a completion time, a survival figure.
> Who was left out because they had not finished yet? Did leaving them out make it look
> worse or better?
