# PROMPTS.md — Fact-Checking Chapter 30

No open slots. Every beat is a deterministic Remotion render from the beat sheet, so there
are no generation prompts to fill, no pantry requests and no paid API calls. Fellow tier
throughout: Kokoro narration is local and free.

The two prompts that appear ON SCREEN, as content:

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> I finished the human fact-check of chapter 30 of the Medhavy cancer textbook, the future
> directions chapter. 1,654 sentences, 55 flagged, 35 reviewed. Help me report it,
> including the fourth check, which stopped asking whether my sources were real and started
> asking whether they said what I claimed.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Take a report that cites its sources. For each citation, do not just check the link
> opens. Check that the source actually speaks to the claim it is attached to. A real paper
> on the wrong row is still a broken citation.

Read aloud verbatim in the narration, per HANDOFF LAW.
