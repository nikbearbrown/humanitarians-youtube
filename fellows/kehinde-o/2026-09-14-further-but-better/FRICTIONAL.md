# Frictional Log -- Further But Better

**Date:** September 14, 2026
**Fellow:** Kehinde Obidele
**Work:** Brutalist video (STEM/AI explainer, Week 3)

## What I set out to do

Produce a STEM/AI explainer video for Week 3. The title "Further But Better" reflects the idea that going deeper into fact-checking reveals more errors but also builds a better textbook. The video covers lessons learned after reviewing three chapters.

## What I expected

I expected the Brutalist production workflow to be routine by now. The challenge would be making the content fresh and not repetitive of previous videos.

## Where it resisted

Finding a new angle was the main friction. I had covered "what AI fact-checking is" (Week 1) and "what I found" (Week 2). This video needed to say something different. I landed on the meta-lesson: the further you go into a fact-checking project, the better your pattern recognition gets, so you catch things you would have missed in Week 1.

Keeping the narration under 3 minutes while covering enough depth to be useful was a balancing act. I had to cut several beats that were interesting but would have pushed the video past the target duration.

## What I did next

I structured the video around three lessons that only became clear after reviewing multiple chapters: (1) AI errors cluster in predictable ways, (2) the AI-Only tab is not safe to skip, (3) encoding issues and content issues need separate handling. These are practical takeaways a viewer can apply to their own work.

## What Claude or another person contributed

Claude helped with the initial beat sheet draft and duration planning. I revised the content to focus on insights that are uniquely mine from doing this work hands-on.

## What I accepted, changed, or rejected

I accepted the three-lesson structure as a clear framework. I changed the narration to include more specific examples from Chapter 23 rather than staying abstract. I rejected a suggestion to include a "how-to" segment on using PubMed since that deserves its own dedicated video.

## Result

Week 3 STEM/AI explainer video completed. Both 16:9 and 9:16 versions rendered. Uploaded to Google Drive, source files committed to GitHub.
