# PROMPTS — The Check That Passed

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> My two-tower recommender on 25 million MovieLens ratings passes its gradient check but
> loses to a popularity counter. Help me find what the check could not see.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Think of a check you rely on: a test suite, an audit, a dashboard that is green. Write down
> the one question it answers, and one question it cannot.
