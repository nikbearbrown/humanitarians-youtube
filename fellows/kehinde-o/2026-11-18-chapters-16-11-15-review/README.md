**Volunteer:** Kehinde Obidele

**Type:** Project update  
**Runtime:** 2m39s  

Chapter 16 (tumour immunology): 28 claims in my scope, 12 need revision. In Chapters 11
(metabolism) and 15 (stem cells) I decided every sentence, not only the flagged ones:
13 of 90 claims in Chapter 11 need revision, and 28 of 238 in Chapter 15.

These chapters print whole paragraphs, captions and sentences twice, and none of the
repeats was flagged, because the pipeline removed duplicate sentences before checking
them. Checking every sentence also let me measure the flags: in Chapter 11, 14.4 percent of
flagged claims needed revision and so did 5.6 percent of the cleared ones; in Chapter 15,
11.8 against 1.8. Three flags would have made the book worse if I had followed them.
