# Pedagogy Review

## Topic
Fact-Checking Chapters 31 and 32: Solid Tumors by Organ/System, and Central Nervous
System Tumors, in the Medhavy Cancer Textbook (the two largest chapters in the book)

## Learning Objective
When half of a claim checks out, the other half gets less scrutiny. The viewer leaves
able to check both numbers in a comparison, especially when one looks familiar.

## Audience
General audience. "Control" is explained as the comparison group the first time it is
used in a trial context.

## Key Findings Reported
- Chapter 31: 1,183 sentences, 591 flagged, 159 in scope. 99 need revision, 59 approved,
  1 rejected. 143 edits across 8 files.
- Chapter 32: 1,167 sentences, 336 flagged, 101 in scope. 58 need revision, 40 approved,
  3 pending. 50 edits across 6 files.
- Two trials quoted with the right control and wrong treatment number: intraoperative
  MRI 81 vs 68 (trial: 96 vs 68); PALOMA-2 27.6 vs 14.5 months (trial: 24.8 vs 14.5).
- Eight Chapter 31 claims point the wrong way; the clearest swaps smoking's share of lung
  cancer between men (about 95%) and women (about 90%).
- Two of five listed bladder cancer immunotherapies lost that approval; a late-line ovarian
  option no longer exists (the one rejected row).
- The audit cited the wrong paper once, flagged a correct quotation once, and three figures
  were left pending rather than guessed.

## Accuracy Gate
Every figure traces to the Chapter 31 and 32 review records. See FACTCHECK.md.

## Restraint Gate
- No clinical advice. Approval statements are about current US labels as recorded.
- The two chapters are never summed.
- No individual is named as responsible for any error.
- The narration is an AI voice. The outro says so on screen.

## Takeaway
In any comparison, check both numbers against the source, not only the familiar one.

## VERDICT: PASS
