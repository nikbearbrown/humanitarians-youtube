# Fact-Checking Chapters 31 and 32

**Volunteer:** Kehinde Obidele  
**Week:** 10 (chapters reviewed 30 Oct to 2 Nov 2026)  
**Type:** Project update  
**Runtime:** 2m48s  

The two largest chapters in the book. Chapter 31, on solid tumours by organ: 1,183
sentences, 159 claims in my scope, 99 need revision, 59 approved and 1 rejected. Chapter
32, on brain and spinal tumours: 1,167 sentences, 101 in scope, 58 need revision, 40
approved and 3 left pending.

The pattern across both: when the control number in a comparison is right, the wrong
treatment number gets through. Chapter 31 also has eight claims pointing the wrong way,
and approvals that have since been withdrawn. The one row I rejected describes a treatment
option that no longer exists. Three figures I could not confirm from the abstract are left
pending rather than guessed.

Narration is an AI voice (Kokoro). Research and script are mine.
