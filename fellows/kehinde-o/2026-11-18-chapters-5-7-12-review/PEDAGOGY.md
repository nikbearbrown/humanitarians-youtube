# Pedagogy Review

## Topic
Fact-Checking Chapters 5, 7 and 12: Oncogenes, Epigenetics in Cancer, and Angiogenesis,
in the Medhavy Cancer Textbook

## Learning Objective
A flag can be right for the wrong reason. The viewer leaves able to check the evidence
behind a flag, not only the flag, and to treat a number that appears in different sizes
as a number nobody checked.

## Audience
General audience. Drug and gene names are introduced by what they are before detail.

## Key Findings Reported
- Ch5: 12 claims, 10 revise, 2 approved; 15 edits in 5 of 7 files.
- Ch7: 32 claims, 25 revise, 5 approved, 2 pending; 24 edits in 7 of 9 files.
- Ch12: 33 claims, 24 revise, 9 approved; 19 edits in 5 of 8 files.
- In each chapter the pre-check cited a paper that does not say what it claimed.
- RAS mutation frequency stated four times in three sizes (25, 30, 33 percent); measured
  across databases, about 19 percent (Prior et al. 2020). Three caught by the pre-check;
  the figure caption found on the sweep.
- Chapter 7's list of nine FDA-approved epigenetic drugs: tucidinostat never FDA approved,
  panobinostat and tazemetostat withdrawn; EPZ-6438 is tazemetostat; three approved IDH
  inhibitors missing.
- Chapter 12 credits a meta-analysis with a significant OS benefit (HR 0.84); the paper
  found none (HR 0.89, P = 0.08); 0.84 is a progression figure.
- Ch12: seven of 19 edits came from sweeping beyond the flags; four flags did not hold up.

## Accuracy Gate
Every figure traces to the Chapter 5, 7 and 12 review records. See FACTCHECK.md, which
records a correction made to the Chapter 7 evidence document's summary table.

## Restraint Gate
- Three chapters are reported separately and never summed on screen.
- No clinical advice. Approval status reported as recorded in the review.
- No individual is named as responsible for any error.
- The narration is an AI voice. The outro says so on screen.

## Takeaway
When a statistic appears more than once, check that it is the same number each time and
then check it against one source.

## VERDICT: PASS
