**What I tried, and what I expected.**

I expected to spend my time on the flagged sentences. In all three chapters I ended up spending it on

2026; I confirmed it separately before writing it into the correction. A source behind two bladder
cancer percentages sat behind a bot check I could not get past, so I replaced the figures with what I
could verify from the primary studies. In Chapter 12 the worst errors were not flagged at all; I found
them by reading past the flags. I also found a mistake in my own record afterwards: my Chapter 7
evidence document's summary said 26 and 4 where every row says 25 and 5. It has been corrected.
