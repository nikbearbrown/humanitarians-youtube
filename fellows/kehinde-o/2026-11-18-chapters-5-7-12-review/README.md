Chapter 5 (oncogenes): 12 claims in my scope, 10 need revision and 2 approved. Chapter 7
(epigenetics): 32 in scope, 25 need revision, 5 approved and 2 pending. Chapter 12
(angiogenesis): 33 in scope, 24 need revision and 9 approved.

In all three chapters the automated pre-check was at least once right about a sentence for
the wrong reason, citing a paper that does not say what it claimed. Chapter 5 gives the
RAS mutation rate four times in three sizes; a 2020 cross-database study puts it near 19
percent. Chapter 7 lists nine FDA-approved epigenetic drugs, and three are not approved.
Chapter 12 reports a meta-analysis as finding an overall survival benefit it did not find.
Seven of my nineteen Chapter 12 edits came from reading beyond the flagged claims.
