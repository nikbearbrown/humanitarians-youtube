30 assertions in scope: 20 need revision, 10 approved, plus 28 editorial findings.

The chapter still says "the 100,000 genes" when NCBI records 19,116 protein-coding
genes. It attributes double-strand breaks to ultraviolet light, which produces
pyrimidine dimers, not breaks. It has CDKN1C imprinting backwards.

And one finding corrects me rather than the textbook: I had trilaciclib's approval
recorded wrongly. FDA NDA 214200 approved it in February 2021 for small cell lung
cancer. The chapter was right and I was not, and the video says so.
