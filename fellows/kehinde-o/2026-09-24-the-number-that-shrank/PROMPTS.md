# PROMPTS.md — The Number That Shrank

No open slots. Every beat is a deterministic render: Remotion components from the beat
sheet, plus two structured math rows produced locally by
`runtime/scripts/typeset_math.py`. No generation prompts, no pantry requests, no paid API
calls. Fellow tier throughout.

The two prompts that appear ON SCREEN, as content:

## B00 — the cold-open ask
> I ran four disproportionality methods over a year of FDA adverse event reports:
> 1,214,808 deduplicated cases and 472,082 drug-event pairs scored. The largest crude
> ratios in the output are the ones I trust least. Help me explain why the method has to
> shrink them.

## B08 — the viewer handoff prompt
> Next time you see a dramatic ratio, do not ask how big it is. Ask how many observations
> it rests on, and what the expected count was. A ratio without a denominator you can see
> is not evidence yet.

Read aloud verbatim in the narration, per HANDOFF LAW.
