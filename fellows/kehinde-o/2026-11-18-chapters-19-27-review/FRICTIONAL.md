**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

Two things slowed me down. Two NICE recommendations could not be verified by the pre-check only
because NICE was not on its approved source list, so I checked them against the guideline myself.
And in Chapter 27, six of the errors had already been fixed on the development branch by the time
I reached them. I could not just mark them done; I opened each fix and checked it. For "the first
oncolytic virus", sources disagree on which was truly first, so I limited the claim to the FDA
rather than crown a different winner. I also miscounted in my own comment: I wrote that four words
were dropped from the biopsy sentence, and the phrase is six.
