# Pedagogy Review

## Topic
Fact-Checking Chapters 19 and 27: Diagnosis of Cancer, and Gene Therapy and Oncolytic
Virotherapy, in the Medhavy Cancer Textbook

## Learning Objective
The viewer leaves able to check a claim's context, not only its words: the condition,
population or date that made it true in its source. Chapter 19's errors were true in
their source and false once a qualifier was cut. Chapter 27's were true when written
and false once the field moved.

## Audience
General audience interested in AI, research integrity and medicine. Sensitivity and
specificity are defined in plain words before they are used.

## Key Findings Reported
- Chapter 19: 301 sentences, 49 flagged, 19 in scope. 15 need revision, 4 approved.
  14 text edits plus an encoding repair.
- Chapter 27: 195 sentences, 72 flagged, 17 in scope. 9 need revision, 2 approved,
  6 already resolved on the development branch. 10 text edits plus an encoding repair.
- The two chapters are reported side by side and never summed.
- A biopsy-risk list lost "in the case of hepatocellular carcinoma", six words, and
  now attaches liver complications to any tumour.
- Fine-needle aspiration sensitivity and specificity are stated backwards; one series
  reports specificity 97 percent, sensitivity 50.
- Six Chapter 27 errors were fixed on the development branch before the review: e.g.
  "conditional approval" (no such FDA category), "none beyond phase II" (false).
- Still wrong on arrival: "the first oncolytic virus" (first FDA approval only; China
  2005), GS010 is AAV2 not AAV9, AMT-061 carries factor IX not XI.

## Accuracy Gate
Every figure traces to the Chapter 19 and 27 review records. See FACTCHECK.md, which
also records one correction to the review comment's word count.

## Restraint Gate
- No clinical advice. Sensitivity and specificity figures are attributed to one series.
- The video does not claim another country's virus was the world's first; it says the
  chapter's virus was the first FDA approval and China approved one in 2005.
- "Ten phase three trials" is stated as registered, not as currently running.
- No individual is named as responsible for any error.
- The narration is an AI voice. The outro says so on screen.

## Takeaway
Before repeating a claim, find the condition, population or date around it in the
original and ask whether the claim survives without it.

## VERDICT: PASS
