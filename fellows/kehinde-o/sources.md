## Primary Verification Databases

**PubMed / PMC** -- biomedical literature, clinical studies, review articles. Used for verifying every clinical and biological claim. Access via PMID (`pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/[PMID]`) or PMCID (`pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC[number]/`).

**FDA Orange Book** -- approved small-molecule drug products with therapeutic equivalence evaluations. Used for verifying drug approval dates, active ingredients, and application numbers. Access at `accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/ob/`.

**FDA Purple Book** -- licensed biological products with reference product exclusivity and biosimilarity. Used for verifying biologic approvals (antibodies, vaccines, cell therapies). Access at `purplebooksearch.fda.gov`.

**NCI SEER** -- Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results cancer statistics. Used for verifying incidence rates, survival statistics, and epidemiological claims. Access at `seer.cancer.gov`.

**CDC** -- public health statistics, screening guidelines, prevention data. Access at `cdc.gov`.

**WHO** -- international health statistics, global disease burden, classification systems. Access at `who.int`.

## Common Error Patterns Found

Fabricated citations (real PMCIDs paired with wrong author names), wrong statistics (close but not accurate percentages), outdated figures (superseded by newer estimates), citation-claim mismatch (cited paper is real but does not support the specific claim), and editorial issues (garbled text, formatting problems from AI generation).
