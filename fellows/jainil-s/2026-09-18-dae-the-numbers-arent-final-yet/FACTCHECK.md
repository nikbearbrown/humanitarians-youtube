## Claims deliberately CUT

Per-metric delay tables widely repeated online — "views 2–3 hours, retention
24–48 hours, revenue 24–72 hours" — are **not stated**. They appear only in
SEO/blog sources and could not be traced to any Google-published page.

The one figure YouTube does publish, a ~2-day delay on estimated earnings (S7),
is **also not spoken**, because the reel is about view/engagement metrics rather
than revenue and importing a revenue-specific number would misrepresent it.

No magnitude of revision is ever claimed. The reel argues **direction and
mechanism** only.

## Register check

No creator, channel or company other than YouTube is named, and YouTube is
described using its own documentation. Nothing here alleges wrongdoing — the
reel explicitly frames revision as correct behaviour.
