## Deliberately NOT used

- Blog/SEO pages giving precise per-metric delay tables ("views 2–3 h, retention
  24–48 h, revenue 24–72 h"). These numbers are widely repeated but I could not
  trace them to any Google-published source. **Not stated in the reel.**
- Any specific claim about how much a count typically moves between first
  appearance and settling. Unsourceable; the reel argues the *direction and
  mechanism*, never a magnitude.

## Derived, not cited

The worked example (comparing a day-1 number against a day-7 number and getting
an apparent trend from settling alone) is arithmetic built on S1–S7, presented
as an illustration and labelled as such — not as measured platform data.
