# PROMPTS — Accuracy Is Lying to You

Beat-prefixed prompts for open slots. **There are no open slots** — all nine
beats render from registered Remotion compositions. Recorded for completeness
and for anyone re-cutting this reel.

- **B00** — composer content (on screen, not a generation prompt):
  "My classifier is 95% accurate. Show me the case where that number is hiding
  a model that learned nothing."
- **B07** — the viewer handoff prompt (on screen, meant to be pasted):
  "Here's my class balance and my accuracy. Work out what a model that always
  predicts the majority class would score — and tell me how much of my number
  that explains."

No image generation, no stock, no AI video. Free path throughout.
