## Claims deliberately CUT

The Casscells, Schoenberger & Graboys (1978) study is the canonical citation for
base-rate neglect and its full reference is verified (see `SOURCES.md`). Widely
repeated figures — 60 participants, only 18% correct, modal answer 95% — appear
**only in secondary blog restatements**; the NEJM original is paywalled (403) and
no open scholarly source was found restating them verbatim.

**Those numbers are not spoken and not shown.** The reel makes its argument from
arithmetic instead, which needs no citation and is stronger on screen.
