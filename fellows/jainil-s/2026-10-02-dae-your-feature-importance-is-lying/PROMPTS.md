# PROMPTS — Your Feature Importance Is Lying

Beat-prefixed prompts for open slots. **There are no open slots** — all ten
beats render from registered Remotion compositions.

- **B00** — composer content (on screen):
  "Here's my feature importance ranking. Before I drop the bottom half — is
  anything in my data correlated?"
- **B08** — the viewer handoff prompt (on screen, meant to be pasted):
  "Here are the features I dropped for low importance. Check each one against
  what I kept — were any of them correlated with a survivor?"

No image generation, no stock, no AI video. Free path throughout.

The chart figures are not prompted from a model — they are produced by
`importance_evidence.py`, which runs the experiment locally with a fixed seed.
