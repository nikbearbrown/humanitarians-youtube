
## Week 3 — Dataset Discovery and Feasibility Assessment

### Overview

This week focused on identifying suitable biomedical datasets for analyzing shared immune-related patterns across rheumatoid arthritis (RA), psoriatic arthritis (PsA), and systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE).

### Key Contributions and Outcomes

- **Dataset Discovery:** Explored 641 initial candidate series from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) and identified potential datasets for cross-disease research.
- **Data Feasibility Assessment:** Evaluated patient metadata, disease/control labels, sample availability, and measurement technologies to determine dataset suitability.
- **Cohort Selection:** Shortlisted three primary datasets: GSE93272 (RA), GSE61281 (PsA), and GSE112087 (SLE).
- **Research Findings:** Identified differences in data formats, patient populations, and platform compatibility that would need to be addressed before meaningful model comparisons.
- **Optimus AI-Assisted Research:** Used Optimus AI to support research exploration, review dataset selection considerations, and organize findings related to data quality, cohort compatibility, and cross-disease analysis.

### Challenges

- **Dataset Heterogeneity:** Different measurement technologies and sample characteristics made direct comparisons across diseases difficult.
- **Metadata Limitations:** Incomplete patient information and limited independent cohorts created challenges for reliable cross-disease validation.
- **Data Selection:** Identifying datasets with suitable disease/control labels and consistent sample information required additional screening.
