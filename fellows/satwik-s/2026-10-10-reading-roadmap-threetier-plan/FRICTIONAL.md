## What I set out to do and why

Rebuilt the prerequisites into three tiers on faculty feedback, and attached the question bank.

## What I did (my contribution)

- Rebuilt the prerequisites into three tiers on Dr. Gultepe's feedback: required (gates), recommended (strongly helps), curious (enriching), keeping required strict. Result: 1,283 section-level links (298 required, 575 recommended, 410 curious) and 302 chapter-level links, acyclic with 0 cycles.
- Fixed the gap a binary split could not express: broadly foundational chapters now sit in recommended, so Chapter 2 (Introduction to Cancer) became a prerequisite for 109 topics.
- Mapped the collaborators' question bank onto the sections (1,544 of 1,584 questions, 97.5%, about 8 per section over 193 sections) and rebuilt the demo app on the three tiers and the real questions.
- Wrote the week's research report and directed the Brutalist weekly video end to end: the premise and beat sheet, the narration script, the visual plan, the 4K render review, and the PROOF production-gate check before I accepted it.

## Where it resisted, and what I decided

- A binary required-vs-helpful split had nowhere for 'broadly foundational but not blocking'; I added the middle tier to fix it.
- Keeping a plan bounded (taking the required chain in full but attaching recommended and curious directly rather than expanding them deep) took a deliberate rule, or plans ballooned to dozens of readings.

## What Claude contributed (assistant)

- Ran the question-bank mapping and rebuilt the demo app.
- Rendered the video scenes and the narration audio, and drafted figures, from my beat sheet and visual plan.

Claude acted as an implementation assistant under my direction. The research questions, the framing, the
design decisions, the interpretation of results, the faculty collaboration, and the final acceptance of
every output were mine.

## Accepted / changed / rejected

I accepted the three-tier framework and the bounded-plan rule; I rejected the binary split that buried the foundational chapters.
