## What I set out to do and why

Produced a reviewable question for every step so the gate always has something real to ask.

## What I did (my contribution)

- Required that every roadmap step carry a real, reviewable question so the gate always has something concrete to ask, and set a per-section coverage target.
- Organised the full MCQ bank for faculty verification and connected every question to its section, so a plan chains goal -> prerequisites -> sections -> questions.
- Built the coverage report so any gaps are visible at a glance.
- Wrote the week's research report and directed the Brutalist weekly video end to end: the premise and beat sheet, the narration script, the visual plan, the 4K render review, and the PROOF production-gate check before I accepted it.

## Where it resisted, and what I decided

- One question per section was not enough once I reviewed the draft; I raised the coverage bar.
- Aligning questions to the right sections surfaced title and numbering mismatches I had to reconcile.

## What Claude contributed (assistant)

- Generated and formatted the MCQ bank to my coverage requirement.
- Built the coverage report.
- Rendered the video scenes and the narration audio, and drafted figures, from my beat sheet and visual plan.

Claude acted as an implementation assistant under my direction. The research questions, the framing, the
design decisions, the interpretation of results, the faculty collaboration, and the final acceptance of
every output were mine.

## Accepted / changed / rejected

I accepted the per-section coverage target; I rejected sparse coverage that would leave steps untestable.
