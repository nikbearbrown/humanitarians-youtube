## Teaching goal
A viewer should leave understanding that (1) matching is a *selection* problem, not a retrieval problem, (2) similarity measures topical nearness while shared vocabulary measures task relevance, (3) most of a pure similarity search's top-K shares no project vocabulary (85% / 60%), robust across K, (4) selection produces a role-labelled structure a ranked list cannot, with roles set structurally (not by tuned weights), and (5) the exclusion log — what the matcher rejected and why — is the cleanest proof it selects.
