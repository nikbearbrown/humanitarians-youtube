search on the answer itself — no array required. A DSA pattern that
reinforces exactly what you'd be grinding on LeetCode, explained

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
Why a yes/no question that flips exactly once as a number grows can be
binary-searched directly, in log of the range. How "minimum capacity to
ship packages within D days" reduces to guess-check-narrow. Why this
pattern only works if the feasibility check is truly monotonic — and why
trusting an unverified predicate is the real bug, not the search code
itself.
