Claude, Ingested. — Week 1: The Data Spine — Humanitarians AI

A real SEC 8-K signal pipeline, built end to end with Claude Code: an
ingest-gateway polls two independent EDGAR sources, dedupes through Redis,
and idempotently persists to Postgres. Two real bugs — a gzip
double-decompression and a wrong-CIK archive URL — get found and fixed on
camera, not staged. One honest edge case gets disclosed instead of hidden.

WHAT YOU WILL KNOW AFTER WATCHING
Why a manual Accept-Encoding header silently breaks Go's transparent gzip
decoding. Why an SEC archive URL needs the filing subject's CIK, not the
filing agent's. What "idempotent by construction" actually means, verified
by killing a consumer mid-batch and replaying. Why a wide backfill window
can silently swallow an entire data source.
