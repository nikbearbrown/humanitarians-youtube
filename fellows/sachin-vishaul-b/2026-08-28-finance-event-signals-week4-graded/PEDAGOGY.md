## The one thing this video has to land

**Pre-registration isn't paperwork — it's the thing that catches you when
you're the one who made the mistake.** The BCAB dating error (B08) is the
proof: the author's own prediction was wrong, and the discipline of
writing the prediction down first is what caught it, not a second reviewer
(there wasn't one).

## Why B05-B07 is a "revision" without a bug

Unlike Weeks 1-3, this cycle isn't a bug-and-fix — it's a deliberate
adversarial test the project's own attestation format requires (see
`SOURCES.md`). The REVISION LAW's "check → change → better output" spine
still applies; "better" here means "proven robust," not "proven fixed."
