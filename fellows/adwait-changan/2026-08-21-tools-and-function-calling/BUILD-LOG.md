## Two honesty problems I caught in my own source, before audio

Both were in `tools.py`, and both were fixed in the *code* rather than softened in the
script:

1. **B07's central claim was false.** The narration said the two functions' bodies were
   byte-identical while `vague_read_file` actually delegated (`return read_file(path)`).
   Rather than weaken the line, the file was changed so the bodies genuinely match, and
   `tools.py` now **asserts it on screen** — `function bodies identical: True`. The claim
   went from unverified to being its own control.
2. **The `audit()` heuristic's first question was unfair.** It tested for a return value but
   was labelled "what it does", so it marked `"Reads a file."` as failing a question that
   string arguably answers. Renamed to "what it returns", which is what the check actually
   tests and what that docstring genuinely never says.

Also corrected before audio: **"nine lines" → "fourteen lines"** in B05, after counting the
real source rather than estimating it.

## Verification actually performed

- **The source runs.** `python3 tools.py` executed; all three printed blocks captured into
  `SOURCES.md`. B03 and B07 show that output; B05 shows `to_schema()`.
- **Numbers were computed, not estimated.** 14 lines (`len(...splitlines())`), 3 words and
  13 characters for the vague docstring, 292 for the specific one — all measured, and all
  printed on screen by the program itself.
- **B05 cannot drift from the file.** The `code` prop was generated by
  `inspect.getsource(tools.to_schema)` at authoring time. Worth doing for every future code
  beat — it removes transcription error as a category.
- **The frames were read.** Both defects came from reading PNGs. Note that B08 looked like it
  was missing a chip on the contact sheet and was fine at 95 % — the contact sheet samples
  one frame per beat and will routinely catch staged grids mid-reveal. Second time this false
  alarm has occurred (Ep 1 B08); check the settled frame before believing it.

## Traps carried forward from Episodes 1–2 — all avoided

- **Auto-numbering.** B03 and B07 were authored `numbered: false` from the start, so no
  numbered payload or trace ever rendered.
- **The silent beat-sheet clobber.** The patch waited for `remotion_scenes.py` to print
  `done` — not for the last `media/*.mp4` to appear — and the JSON was read back after
  writing to confirm the patch survived. It did, first time.
- **Underfilling card patterns.** `MedhavyConceptCard` was not used at all; `CwcConceptCard`
  only for the two act cards. Every enumerated beat uses `ClaudeScienceLayerStack` or
  `ClaudeScienceChipGrid`.

Result: cleanest QC pass of the series — zero MAJOR defects, versus 3 in Ep 1 and 3 in Ep 2.
