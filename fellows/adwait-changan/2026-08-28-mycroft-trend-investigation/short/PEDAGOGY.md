# PEDAGOGY — Gate P (Short derivative cut)

9:16 Short derived from *Memory and Context* (an earlier episode), 2026-08-28.

## What this cut is

A **derivative**, not a re-edit. Per SHORTS LAW the Short exists to funnel viewers to the
16:9 long; every kept beat reuses the parent's narration and audio unchanged. The only new
audio in this folder is the rewritten outro, which names what was cut and points at the long.

## What was cut, and why it was chosen by hand

The auto-plan wanted to drop **BVDT and BHTF** — the verdict and the call-to-action. That
would have stripped the Short of its conclusion and its viewer task, failing PROOF's *active
task* criterion and leaving a highlight reel. Rejected.

Dropped instead: **B02, B04, B05, B06, B09** — two act-transition cards, the code beat, and the
closing judgment card.

- The act cards are structural breathing beats; a 3-minute cut does not need them.
- **The code beat (B05) is the real loss**, and it was chosen deliberately: a 10-line code
  block is the least legible thing on a phone, and it is exactly what the long is for.
- **B08 was protected over B02.** Both are strong, but B08 carries the claim boundary — what
  the system refuses to say, and who owns "why". On a finance tool that is the ethical core,
  so a cut that kept the mechanism and dropped the boundary would misrepresent the work.
  The rewritten outro names every cut beat so the Short is honest about what is missing.

Kept: the intro line, both framework beats, the worked example, the falsifiability beat, the
reusable rubric, the verdict, and the viewer task.
