King minus man plus woman? A computer answers queen, and nobody ever taught it what royalty or gender means. This explainer covers Word2Vec.

What's covered:
- To computers, words used to be just ID numbers: cat and kitten as unrelated as cat and carburetor
- J.R. Firth, 1957: you shall know a word by the company it keeps
- In 2013, Tomas Mikolov's team at Google trained a network to predict nearby words, giving 300 numbers per word
- Similar words land close, and relationships become directions
- The catch: queen only wins when the input words are excluded, and a 2016 study found the vectors also learn our biases

Sources: Mikolov et al. (2013), Firth (1957), Bolukbasi et al. (2016) and Nissim, van Noord & van der Goot (2020). The similarities and maps shown come from a labelled toy model.
