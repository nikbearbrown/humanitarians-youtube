An LLM only knows its training data — so how can it answer questions about your own documents? This explainer walks through a RAG pipeline end to end.

What's covered:
- Why retrieving at question time beats retraining on every document update
- Embed: chunk documents into vectors that capture meaning
- Store: a vector database or your database's own vector search
- Retrieve: embed the question and find the closest chunks by cosine similarity (scores computed by a real script)
- Augment and generate: the answer is grounded in the retrieved chunks, and only as good as that retrieval

Source: Lewis et al., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (NeurIPS 2020). Vectors, documents and answers shown are illustrative.
