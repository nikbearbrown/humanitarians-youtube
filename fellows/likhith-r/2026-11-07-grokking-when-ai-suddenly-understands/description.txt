A small AI scores 100% on its practice problems almost right away and 0% on new ones. Trained far longer, it suddenly gets the new ones too. This explainer covers grokking.

What's covered:
- In 2022, OpenAI researchers trained a tiny transformer on clock arithmetic, (a + b) mod 97
- Training accuracy hits 100% fast while test accuracy stays near zero: it memorized
- Many times more steps later, test accuracy shoots toward 100%
- Weight decay favours simple solutions, so the model swaps its lookup table for a rule
- Nanda and colleagues found it adds with waves, sines and cosines, like hands around a clock

Sources: Power et al. (2022), Nanda et al. (2023), Varma et al. (2023) and Heinlein's Stranger in a Strange Land (1961). The curves shown come from our own labelled numpy re-run.
