One prompt to Claude, then a finished multi-shot animated film. This explainer covers Higgsfield AI and how it pairs with Claude Fable.

What's covered:
- Higgsfield gathers dozens of video and image models, with camera moves and consistent characters
- Claude connects through Higgsfield's official connector (sign in, no API key); Claude Code uses a CLI and skills
- Claude directs the script, shots and prompts; Higgsfield's models generate each shot
- Clips are short, so Claude plans a run of shots, checks each one and stitches them
- The catch: paid credits (none on the free plan), characters can drift, and labelling AI shots is on you

Sources: Higgsfield's homepage, MCP page, blog, help centre, changelog and terms, Higgsfield's Voyager demo post, and Anthropic's Claude Fable announcement. The Voyager example is redrawn from Higgsfield's demo; no Higgsfield credits were used to make this video.
