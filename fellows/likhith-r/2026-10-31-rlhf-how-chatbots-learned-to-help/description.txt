Ask a raw language model how to bake bread and it may reply with more questions. This explainer covers RLHF, and how chatbots learned to be helpful.

What's covered:
- Pretraining reads huge amounts of text but can't judge a good answer
- Step one: people write example answers and the model imitates them
- Step two: people rank several answers and a reward model learns their preferences
- Step three: RL nudges the model toward higher scores, with a penalty for drifting
- The catch: it learns to please the reward model, not to be right (sycophancy, reward hacking)

Sources: OpenAI's InstructGPT paper (2022), the GPT-3 paper, the DPO paper, Anthropic's Constitutional AI and sycophancy papers, and Gao et al. on reward-model overoptimization. The reward and RL numbers shown come from a labelled toy run.
