## This week's contribution

- **What I did:** made this week's explainer, [How Do Websites Know I'm Not a Robot?](../2026-11-16-how-websites-know-im-not-a-robot/README.md), from research on websites and in two studies to landscape and vertical masters. I also went deeper into film as code, especially how landscape and vertical videos are built and how the gates treat them differently.
- **What I learned:** the text-size check needs at least 41 px in landscape and 72 px in vertical; a rule of checking every line against a source still leaves things for the fact check to find, but makes them easy to fix; the text-size check can read drawn shapes as letters; and a numbered card should number complete thoughts.
- **Takeaway:** more and more websites check how I behave, and a check is only as good as what it measures. This week, I learned to check my videos the same way: every claim needs a source, and every frame needs a second look.
