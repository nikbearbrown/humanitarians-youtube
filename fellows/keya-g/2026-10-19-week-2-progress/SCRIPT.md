I also learned what each file in a video folder does. The script holds my words. The beat sheet times every moment. The fact check ties each claim to a source. And the build state records whether the video really passed.

## 5. Done is not passed
That taught me something important. A command that says done, or even pass, isn't proof. Once, a pass came from old frames. Now I check build-state.json, which records the real result.
