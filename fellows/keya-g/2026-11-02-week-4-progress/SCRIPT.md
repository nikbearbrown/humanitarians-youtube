## 4. Gates and re-rendering
Every Brutalist video has to pass quality gates before it's final. They test layout, text size, contrast and facts. When a gate fails, you fix the scene and render it again. You never switch the check off.

## 5. File integrity
At the end, each final video gets a checksum, a fingerprint of the file. Anyone can use it to confirm that the video on Drive is the same one that passed the gates.
