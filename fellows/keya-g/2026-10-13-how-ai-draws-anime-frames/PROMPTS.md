# PROMPTS — How AI Draws Anime Frames

**No open generation slots.** Five Remotion compositions and seven Manim scenes. No image model, no paid API.

## B00 — the on-screen typed ask

> How does AI draw the frames between an animator's drawings, and should it replace the animator?

## BHTF — the handoff the viewer can paste

> I'm an anime fan, not an animator. Using a scene from [my favourite anime], explain like I'm 12 how an animator decides which drawings to make and how long to hold each one. Then tell me which parts AI could safely help with, and which should stay hand-drawn.
