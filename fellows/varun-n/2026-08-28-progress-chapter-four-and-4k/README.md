## What This Video Covers

I completed chapter four in long and short form: car turning (section 4.2) and rain and relative motion (section 4.5). In review I caught a gold box covering captions, a ground line crossing the divider, and a mispronounced "centripetal", chosen from eight generated candidates. Native 4K Manim ran out of memory under WSL's ~7.6 GB cap, so I rendered at 1080p and upscaled with ffmpeg Lanczos.

**Takeaway:** When a hardware limit blocks you, working within it (render smaller, upscale) can beat fighting it.
