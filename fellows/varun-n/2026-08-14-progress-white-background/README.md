## What This Video Covers

I revised the chapter three ball-and-feather sample to meet faculty-reviewer feedback on visual style: a white background in place of the Medhavy cream, colored balls, and a recognizable polygon feather. The brand palette's cream background conflicted with the reviewer's preference, so I resolved it with an override scoped to white rather than changing the theme.

**Takeaway:** When reviewer feedback clashes with a design system, scope the exception and keep the system intact.
