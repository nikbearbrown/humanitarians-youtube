# PROMPTS — Background AI Agents: How AI Works While You Don't

**B00 — composer (illustrative).** An agent goal, typed once:

> Fix the failing tests in this repo. Run them, find the cause, make the smallest fix, and let me know when they pass.

**B12 — Your Turn.** Read aloud, then discussed:

> I want to try handing a task to an AI agent. Here's a task I do often: [describe it]. Rewrite it as an agent goal with a clear definition of done, list the smallest set of tools and permissions it needs, and give me a checklist for reviewing its work before anything ships.

What to look for: a definition of done you could test, and permissions smaller than you expected. If Claude can't write a definition of done, the task isn't ready to delegate.
