# PROMPTS — on-screen Claude prompts in "The Last Mile"

These are the exact composer prompts shown in the ASK beats (ASK→RESULT LAW) and the
handoff. ASCII-only (Remotion render constraint on this machine).

## T00 — cold open (the question)
> The AI in my project tools... no — I reviewed 85 fellow videos this week for the
> Mycroft project, and almost all of them passed. So what actually sends a finished
> video back?

## T04 — the ask (run the queue)
> Here's this week's Mycroft queue -- 14 fellow reviews. Take each finished video
> through the last-mile check: clean finish, portrait holds, on GitHub. Flag anything
> that isn't upload-ready for the professors.

## T08 — the handoff (copy/paste this — the scaffolded viewer task)
> Act as my pre-submission reviewer for a fellow explainer video.
> Build me a 2-minute LAST-MILE checklist to run on my OWN export:
> 1. CLEAN FINISH: scan the final 10s for any toolkit footer/watermark baked in.
> 2. PORTRAIT HOLDS: open the 9:16 on a phone; flag overlapping or clipped text.
> 3. ON GITHUB: confirm code + assets are committed and the link works.
> Give me a tickable list + the 3 things that most often get a video sent back.

**Good answer:** a short, tickable checklist + the common failure modes, so you catch
your own last-mile defects in two minutes. **Bad answer:** a vague "looks good" with no
checklist — which is exactly how a stray footer or a broken 9:16 slips through.
