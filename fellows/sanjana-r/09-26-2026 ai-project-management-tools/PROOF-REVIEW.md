## The problem (biggest honest weakness)
The worked example (the stand-up note and its four tickets) is illustrative, self-authored
material, not a real user's board — so the specific tickets are a demonstration of the
*mechanical* case, not a claim about any project. This is correct and clearly framed; the
viewer brings their own board via the T08 scaffold. Not a gate failure — a property of a
concept explainer, stated in the film.
