## What it teaches (SCORE / OWN / WATCH)
The rule: an AI risk register is a strong draft of the *known* risks — you own the rest.
1. **SCORE** — let the AI rate the common risks (probability × impact); fast and consistent.
2. **OWN** — add the project-specific risk the AI can't see (your team, vendor, timeline).
3. **WATCH** — give every real risk a trigger and an owner.
4. **Worked example** — score an AI register, add the high/high risk no template had, assign triggers → an active plan.
5. **The trap** — a calm, all-green grid that *looks* safe but is missing the one high-impact risk. A green grid can just mean the real risk is off the page.
6. **Your turn** — get the scored register AND an honest "blind spots" list (copyable prompt in the video).

This is a concept explainer — the example risks/scores are illustrative, not real project
data (see `FACTCHECK.md`). No people are named.
