# PROMPTS — AI Status Reports

## T04 — ASK (generates the draft, T05)
> Draft a sprint status update from my board for the client. Pull straight from the
> tickets -- what's done, in progress, and blocked. Don't declare on-track or at-risk,
> and don't add anything that isn't on the board. Mark anything you're unsure about.

## T08 — HANDOFF (the viewer's scaffolded task)
> Draft my sprint/project status from the board below.
> [PASTE YOUR BOARD]
> 1. SOURCED: tag every claim with the exact ticket ID or date it came from.
> 2. SEPARATE: list everything you canNOT verify from the board alone
>    (on-track / at-risk judgments, off-board risks, anything stale).
> 3. Do NOT declare on-track or at-risk yourself -- leave that to me.
> Give me (a) the sourced draft and (b) the 'could not verify' list.

**Good vs bad result (read-and-discuss, HANDOFF LAW):** good = a sourced draft plus an
honest "here's what I couldn't verify" list. Bad = a confident paragraph with no
sources — the one never to forward.
