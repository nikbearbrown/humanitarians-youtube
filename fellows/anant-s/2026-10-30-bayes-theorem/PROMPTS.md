# PROMPTS — Bayes' Theorem: The Positive That's Usually Wrong

This film has no generation prompts. Every beat is machine-built: Remotion compositions for the bookends, and Manim scenes in `scenes.py` driven by `demo/evidence.json` for the LEARN section. Nothing goes to an image or video generator, no call is paid, and no beat is a pantry request.

| Beat | Prompt |
|---|---|
| B00–BOUT | none (deterministic render) |

The only "prompt" in the film is the viewer's YOUR TURN prompt (BHTF `props.command`). It is reproduced in full in SCRIPT.md, and its answer key is in `evidence.json` under `second_positive`.
