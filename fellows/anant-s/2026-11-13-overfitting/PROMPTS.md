# PROMPTS — Overfitting, When a Perfect Score Lies

There are no generation prompts. Every beat is machine-built: Remotion compositions, or Manim scenes in `scenes.py` (to be written after audio lock) driven by `demo/evidence.json`. Nothing goes to an image or video generator, there is no paid call, and no beat is a pantry request.

| Beat | Prompt |
|---|---|
| B00–BOUT | none (deterministic render) |

The only "prompts" in the film are on-screen content, and are not generation inputs:
- **B00:** the composer ask.
- **BHTF:** the viewer's paste-ready prompt. Both are in `beat_sheet.json` and SCRIPT.md.
