Softmax and Temperature, Explained

Ask a chat model the same thing twice and you can get two different answers. This episode shows the math underneath: the model scores every next token, softmax turns the scores into odds, temperature reshapes them, and a sampler rolls weighted dice. Every number on screen comes from a small seeded Python script; the logits are constructed and labelled so.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE

Write a short Python script, no ML libraries, that applies softmax with temperature to five logits: mat 3.2, floor 2.4, couch 1.9, bed 1.5, roof 0.3. Print the probabilities at temperatures 0.2, 1 and 2. Then draw 1,000 seeded samples at each temperature and compare the counts with the probabilities. Finally, using only the printed numbers, explain what temperature can change and what it can't.

Softmax: J. S. Bridle (1990). Temperature: G. Hinton, O. Vinyals, J. Dean (2015), "Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network", arXiv:1503.02531.
