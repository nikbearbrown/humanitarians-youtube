How RAG Works

How can a chatbot answer questions about your own documents when it has never read them? This episode carries one question through a small RAG pipeline, step by step: chunk, embed, store, retrieve, prompt, answer. Then an old FAQ page wins the search, the answer goes wrong, and the fix turns out to be in the store, not the model.
