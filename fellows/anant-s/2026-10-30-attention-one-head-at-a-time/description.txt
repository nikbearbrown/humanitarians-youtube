Attention, One Head at a Time

How does a transformer let each word use the words around it? One attention head, one four-word sentence, real numbers: a request meets an offer, the scores become a heat map, and each word comes out as a weighted average of every word's value.

Then the limit: attention alone can't tell who chased whom.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE

Write a short Python script, numpy only, for one attention head on a three-word sentence. Use small integer queries, keys and values that you choose. Before running it, predict which word each word will weight most. Then print the scores, the softmax weights, and the outputs. Finally, reverse the word order, run it again, and explain what changed and what didn't.

Concepts from "Attention Is All You Need" by A. Vaswani, N. Shazeer, N. Parmar, J. Uszkoreit, L. Jones, A. N. Gomez, Ł. Kaiser and I. Polosukhin (NIPS 2017), arXiv:1706.03762.
