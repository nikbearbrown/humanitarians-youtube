Why Neural Networks Need a Bend

Stack as many straight layers as you like and they multiply out to one matrix. This film shows it with real numbers, then shows why one ReLU bend lets a network solve XOR, and what the bend still cannot do on its own.

Every number on screen comes from a short numpy script that was run for this episode.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE

Write a short Python script using only numpy. Train a tiny network on XOR twice: once with no activation, once with a ReLU and two hidden units. Use plain gradient descent from twenty random seeds each, and print how many seeds get all four points right. Then, from the printed numbers alone, explain why the linear version never succeeds and why the ReLU version sometimes fails.

Hand-set XOR weights from I. Goodfellow, Y. Bengio, A. Courville, "Deep Learning" (MIT Press, 2016), §6.1 "Example: Learning XOR".
