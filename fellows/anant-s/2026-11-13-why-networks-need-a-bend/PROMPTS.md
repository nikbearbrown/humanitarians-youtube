# PROMPTS — Why Neural Networks Need a Bend

There are no generation prompts. Every beat is machine-built: Remotion compositions, or Manim scenes in `scenes.py` driven by `demo/evidence.json`. Nothing goes to an image or video generator, there is no paid call, and no beat is a pantry request.

| Beat | Prompt |
|---|---|
| B00–BOUT | none (deterministic render) |

The only prompts in the film are on-screen content: the B00 ask and the BHTF viewer prompt. Both are in `beat_sheet.json` and `SCRIPT.md`, and neither is sent anywhere by the build.
