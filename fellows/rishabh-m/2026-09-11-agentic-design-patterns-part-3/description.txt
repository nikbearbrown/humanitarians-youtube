The Constraints Are The Design — Agentic Design Patterns, Part 3

Part 1 was architecture. Part 2 was state. This is the half senior interviews
actually test: what your system costs, what it refuses to do, and how you prove
it still works next month.

Patterns 14–20 — inter-agent communication, resource-aware optimization, tree of
thoughts, evaluation, guardrails, prioritization, exploration. The first thirteen
patterns added capability. These seven take it away on purpose, and that
subtraction is what turns a demo into production.

Closes with the five-step blueprint for the system design interview itself.

Concepts from "Agentic Design Patterns: A Hands-On Guide to Building
Intelligent Systems" by Antonio Gulli.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE
Run the five-step blueprint on the AI system I am building. One: is the goal
linear or dynamic, and which pattern does that imply. Two: what specialised
workers should replace my do-it-all prompt. Three: state my cost, latency and
accuracy trade-off explicitly — name what I am currently paying and what I am
buying. Four: where do guardrails go on input and recovery on output. Five:
describe my state machine. Then tell me which single step I am weakest on.
