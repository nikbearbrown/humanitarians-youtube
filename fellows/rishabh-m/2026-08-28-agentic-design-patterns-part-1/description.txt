The Prompt Is Not The System — Agentic Design Patterns, Part 1

Your prompt works in the notebook and falls apart in production. That isn't a
prompting problem. It's an architecture problem — and there are six named
patterns that solve it, each one buying you reliability and charging you in
latency or tokens.

Part 1 of 3. We cover the six structural patterns: what each one does, what it
costs, and when a single call is still the better engineering.

Concepts from "Agentic Design Patterns: A Hands-On Guide to Building
Intelligent Systems" by Antonio Gulli.

THE PROMPT FROM THIS EPISODE
Take the last multi-step AI feature I shipped. Map it onto the six agentic
patterns — chaining, routing, parallelization, reflection, tool use, planning.
For each one, tell me what it would buy me and what it would cost in latency and
tokens. Then tell me which single pattern I should add first, and why.

Run it on something you actually built, and look for whether it names a cost you
hadn't priced.
