# Narration Script — "Upload to GitHub. No Code." (v3)

Voiced with Kokoro `af_bella`. Narration is the master clock. Intro & outro use the reference video's Claude scenes.

## LONG (16:9)

1. **Intro** — "Hi, I'm Sanjana. This video is about how to upload your files to GitHub, with no code. No terminal, no git — just your browser. Let's go."
2. **Golden Rule** — "One rule to remember. GitHub is for text and documents: your beat sheet, your script, your readme. Google Drive is for video and big media. If it's text, and under twenty-five megabytes, it goes to GitHub. If it's a video, or a large file, it goes to Drive."
3. **Edge case** — "Not sure? Don't guess — apply the rule. A thirty-megabyte readme? It's text, yes. But it's over twenty-five megabytes, so it goes to Drive, linked from GitHub. Size beats type."
4. **Step 1 — create a branch** — "Step one. First, make your own branch — your private copy of the project to work in. Click the button that says main, type your name, sanjana-rao, and choose Create branch from main."
5. **Step 2 — navigate** — "Step two. Now you're on your own branch. Open the fellows folder — that's where your work belongs."
6. **Step 3 — Add file** — "Step three. Click Add file, at the top right, and choose Upload files. This opens a page with a big dashed box for your files."
7. **Step 4 — Explorer** — "Step four. Open your file explorer — Windows Explorer, or Finder on a Mac — and find your folder. Don't open it. You want to see the folder itself, sitting in its parent directory."
8. **Step 5 — drag folder** — "Step five. Drag the whole folder onto the browser, and drop it into the dashed box. GitHub lists every file inside, and keeps your folder's name on the front of each one. Wait for them all to finish uploading."
9. **Step 6 — commit** — "Step six. Scroll down to Commit changes. Add a short message, like: add sanjana-rao fellow folder. Make sure, commit directly to your branch, is selected. Then click the green Commit changes button."
10. **Step 7 — pull request** — "Because you're on your own branch, your files aren't in main yet. Open a pull request. Set base to main, and compare to your branch. Then click Create pull request. A maintainer reviews it, and merges it in."
11. **Gotchas** — "Two quick gotchas. GitHub caps uploads at twenty-five megabytes per file, and a hundred files at a time — so videos are too big for it. And for drag and drop, use Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari can struggle."
12. **Drive** — "That's where Drive comes in. Upload your final videos — the wide one, and the vertical one — to the shared Drive folder. Then share, and copy the link. Anyone with the link can view."
13. **README** — "Back in GitHub, paste that Drive link into your readme, so the video is easy to find from the repo. Then commit."
14. **Handoff** — "Your turn. Copy this into your own readme. Swap in your name, paste your Drive link, and commit."
15. **End** — "That's it. Pushed? Message Sanjana, with links to both. No first-pass review needed before you push."
16. **Outro** — (serif card) **"Push It. No Code."** · @HumanitariansAI · "GitHub for text · Drive for video · you for the work."
