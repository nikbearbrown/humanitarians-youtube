# REFERENCE ANALYSIS

Two references informed this film. Neither was copied.

## 1. The Mycroft ThesisGuard brief (sibling folder, 16x9 cut)

This film is built to sit beside `mycroft_thesisguard-16x9` as a second entry in
the same series, so it deliberately inherits the **format**, not the content.

Inherited:

- 3840 x 2160, 16:9, 24 fps, exactly 03:00.
- Ten numbered scenes with a persistent `NN / 10` marker.
- A persistent `SOURCE` plate at the bottom-left of every scene.
- Audio-first construction: measured narration durations are the master clock.
- A presenter-labelled review framework (Mycroft uses CLEAR; this film uses
  CLAIM) explicitly marked as the presenter's structure, never as a software
  feature.
- A scaffolded viewer task in the final scene, not a generic prompt.
- The same Claude palette and type stack, so the two films read as a series.
- The same script set: sync, render, check, qc-stills.

Not inherited: narration, subject matter, scene content, framework axes, or any
asset. The two films share a chassis and nothing else.

## 2. The supplied reference video (`Mycroft-thesisguard-brief-4k`)

Used only to understand the **delivery format** the creator works in: 4K 16:9
master, three-minute runtime, editorial cream palette, thin bordered cards, one
idea per scene, progressive reveals, restrained motion, and a source tag that
stays on screen rather than flashing.

Its narration, exact wording, specific layouts, and creative assets were not
reproduced.

## What this film does differently, and why

**Evidence style.** Mycroft can screenshot its own notebook and report, because
it reviews a local project it owns. This film reviews published literature. It
therefore shows **typeset citations** — full author, year, title, journal,
volume, pages — held at the moment of each claim, instead of screenshots it
could not honestly produce. Fabricating a screenshot of a paper would be
precisely the failure the film criticises.

**One data-driven graphic.** Mycroft's visuals are cards and captured
artifacts. This film adds a computed scatter of published measured critical
temperatures, drawn from a cited table in the composition source. It is the one
place where the film shows data rather than asserting it.

**A named falsifiability case.** Mycroft's stress test is its own missing
evidence. This film's stress test is external and adversarial: LK-99, a real
claim that collapsed, scored live against the rubric. That is a harder test,
because the rubric had no say in choosing it.

**A visible open item.** Mycroft's known limitation is a missing plot file.
This film's is a single unverified figure (±9.5 K), flagged in `FACTCHECK.md`
and reflected in the ship verdict rather than quietly shipped.
