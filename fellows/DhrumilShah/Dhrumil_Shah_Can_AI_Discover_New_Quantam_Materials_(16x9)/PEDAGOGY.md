## The falsifiability test the framework has to survive

A framework whose axes map one-per-example is reverse-engineered decoration.
CLAIM is deliberately tested against a single case that hits **several axes at
once, with different verdicts**:

LK-99 **passes** C (an exact, published, synthesizable formula), is **not
applicable** on L (it was never a model claim at all), is **unknown** on A, and
**fails I and M together**. One case producing four different verdicts —
including a not-applicable — is the evidence that the axes are independent
rather than invented to fit the example.
