## Traps specific to this reel

1. **Regenerate the beat sheet, don't edit it.** B04's math rows are base64
   outlined SVG emitted by `build_beats.py` from `typeset_math.py`. Change an
   expression there and re-run `python3 build_beats.py`, then
   `remotion_scenes.py --force --only B04` for both aspects.
2. **No text fallback for B04.** If the typesetter fails, the beat is BLOCKED,
   not downgraded to a text card.
3. **Deck strings are budgeted.** `BinaryBranch` label/detail strings must stay
   ≤ 42 chars — fixed-x SVG text does not reflow and overprints silently while
   Remotion still exits 0. `build_beats.py --check` asserts it.
4. **Do not put the 82% next to the model metrics.** Per-image detection
   confidence and mAP50 are unrelated quantities that happen to look alike here.
5. **AGPL stays out** of narration, on-screen text and the description, per the
   author's instruction. It is logged in `SOURCES.md` only.
6. **A killed render leaves an unretimed mp4.** A stray `_ext_*` in `media/`
   means that beat is double-length and the next run will skip it — delete it.
