## Summary

A how-to on placing a figure in an MDX chapter, using the CTLA-4 and PD-1/PD-L1 pathway figures as the example. 

## The method

1. Read the destination section before placing the figure.
2. Use the reviewer-finalized caption.
3. Check that the image is approved, and that it is the corrected version.
4. Place the figure with a centered image and a justified caption paragraph, in the same format as the chapter's other figures.
5. Leave the caption number as a placeholder until the position is settled.

## The example

The same two checkpoint figures had three different homes: Chapter 25 (the tracking sheet), Chapter 16 (earlier drafts) and Chapter 26 (the latest instructions). The Chapter 25 sections cover target validation and TKI classification, with no checkpoint content.
