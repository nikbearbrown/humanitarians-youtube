# Beat 5 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~7 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The original sentence from Beat 1 returns one final time:

"We expect revenue to remain broadly in line with prior guidance despite near-term headwinds."

The full attention web from Beat 3 is visible — the mesh of connections, thick lines between "revenue" and "headwinds," "despite" connected to everything. The web pulses gently.

On "That is attention" — the web pulses once, brightly.

On "And it is all you need to read an earnings call" — the attention web and sentence dissolve together, simplifying into the title text:

**Attention Is All You Need**
*(To Read an Earnings Call)*

The title holds for two beats. The subtitle is smaller, in parentheses, with a slight playfulness — it is a riff on the original 2017 paper title, and the viewer who knows that smiles.

Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Technical notes

- Reuse exact assets from Beat 3 — same sentence, same web, same line thicknesses
- The dissolution from web to title should feel like the concept crystallizing into a phrase
- Title font: clean sans-serif, bold. Subtitle: lighter weight, smaller, parenthetical
- The parenthetical subtitle is the payoff of the whole title joke — give it a beat of its own
- This is the image people remember — make the title clean and satisfying
