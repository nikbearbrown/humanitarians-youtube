# Beat 1 — The Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~8 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The full sentence appears on a dark background, centered:

"We expect revenue to remain broadly in line with prior guidance despite near-term headwinds."

Each word is a separate block — evenly spaced, monospaced or clean sans-serif font. All words are the same neutral white/grey color. No highlights, no emphasis. The sentence just sits there.

On "which word matters most?" — a brief question mark appears above the sentence, floating.

On "That is attention" — a soft pulse ripples across all the words simultaneously, like a wave passing through. Not highlighting any one word — just signaling that the sentence is about to be read in a new way.

## Technical notes

- This exact sentence will be reused in Beats 2, 3, and 5 — use consistent word block sizing and spacing
- The word blocks should have enough space between them for attention lines to draw in Beat 3
- Dark background, white/light grey text
- The pulse is subtle — a brightness wave, not a color change
- This beat sets the stage — keep it calm and clean
