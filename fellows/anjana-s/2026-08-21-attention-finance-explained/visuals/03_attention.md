**Full web (9-13s):**
All words activate simultaneously. Every word connects to every other word. The full attention web is visible — a dense mesh of lines, some thick, some nearly invisible. The web pulses gently — alive, organic.

## Technical notes

- The attention lines are the visual centerpiece of the entire video — make them feel alive
- Thick lines = high attention weight. Thin/faint lines = low attention weight
- The long-distance connection between "revenue" and "headwinds" is the insight — it should be visually dramatic, spanning the full sentence width
- The full web should feel organic, not chaotic — more like a neural pattern than a spider web
- The transition from Beat 2's wrong answer to Beat 3's correct answer is the payoff — the viewer sees why attention matters
- Same word block layout — exact positioning throughout all beats
