# Beat 6 — Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~5 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

A horizontal arc from left to right:

Left side: the extraction pipeline (compact, familiar architecture from Beat 1). Labeled "The Past."

Center: the decision log (the glowing append-only log). Labeled "The Bridge."

Right side: the prediction model with a forecast card (gold). Labeled "The Future."

An arrow flows left to right through all three, showing the progression: read the past, build the log, predict the future.

On "ECIS, episode six" all three sections pulse simultaneously. Then they fade.

Title text lands: **ECIS — Episode 6**

Same sign-off. Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Technical notes

- The left-to-right past/bridge/future layout mirrors the spatial split established in Beat 1
- The decision log in the center is the connecting element, it feeds both extraction grading and prediction features
- Same montage close style as all previous episodes but adapted to the past/future visual
- Each of the three sections should be compact iconic representations, not full recreations
