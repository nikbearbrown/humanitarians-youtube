# Beat 3 — Scale

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

The full architecture from Beat 2 (four readers with dual LLM, triangulator) shrinks to a compact icon at the center of the screen — recognisable but small.

Around it, ticker symbols fade in one by one in a circular arrangement: NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, META, AMD, INTC, TSM, AVGO, CRM, SNOW, PLTR, and more — twenty to twenty-five total. Each ticker glows briefly as it appears.

Data streams (thin glowing lines) connect each ticker to the central pipeline icon. Animated particles flow along the lines toward the center, conveying volume.

Two counters animate upward in the bottom-left corner:
- "Transcripts: 200+"
- "Chunks processed: 10,000+"

On "What started on two tickers" — the first two tickers (NVDA, MSFT) briefly highlight brighter than the rest. On "now watching a full sector" — all tickers pulse simultaneously.

## Technical notes

- The ticker symbols should be real AI-sector company tickers — use ones Anjana actually has in the system
- Don't overcrowd — twenty to twenty-five tickers arranged in a clean circle or grid
- The central pipeline icon should be recognisable as the architecture from Beats 1-2, just miniaturized
- Counter numbers are approximate — confirm actual counts before audio
- The simultaneous pulse at the end is the visual payoff — all tickers alive at once
