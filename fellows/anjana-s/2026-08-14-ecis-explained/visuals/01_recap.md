# Beat 1 — Recap

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~5 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

The four-reader architecture diagram from Episode 1 appears instantly — fully formed, no build animation. Keyword, FinBERT, NER, LLM nodes feeding into the Triangulator. It should look exactly like the Episode 1 diagram so returning viewers recognise it immediately.

Hold for two seconds. Then the camera pulls back (zoom-out transition), revealing this diagram is now a smaller piece inside a larger frame. The edges of the larger system are just barely visible — teasing what's coming in Beat 2.

## Technical notes

- Reuse the exact node shapes, colors, and layout from Episode 1's architecture beat
- The zoom-out should feel like lifting the camera, not shrinking the diagram
- The larger system frame at the edges should be blurred or dimmed — just enough to create anticipation
- Fast beat — no lingering
