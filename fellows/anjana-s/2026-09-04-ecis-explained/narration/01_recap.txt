ECIS is a system that reads earnings call transcripts and extracts financial guidance signals automatically. It runs four independent readers on every chunk of every transcript: keyword matching, FinBERT sentiment analysis, named entity recognition, and a large language model. A triangulator fuses their outputs. Every signal is pre-registered before anyone knows the outcome, then graded against the market at thirty, ninety, and one hundred eighty days.
