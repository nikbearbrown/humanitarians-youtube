# Beat 1 — The Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~10 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

Dark screen. A stylized globe fades in, slowly rotating. Three cities pulse with bright dots as they are named:

1. **Seoul** — a red pulse with a small price chart dropping (crash)
2. **London** — an amber pulse with a liquidation bar draining
3. **New York** — a green pulse with a volume spike (whale buy)

Thin glowing trade lines connect all three cities, crisscrossing the globe. Data flows along the lines as animated particles.

On "Except the machines" — the three human silhouette icons at each city dim to 20% opacity. A central AI node fades in at the globe's center, bright and pulsing, with all trade lines now routing through it.

## Mood

Urgent, global, alive. The world is moving and you're already behind.

## Technical notes

- Globe can be simplified (circle with continent outlines, not 3D)
- City dots should pulse in sync with narration mentions
- Trade lines: thin, glowing, with moving particle dots along them
- Dark background (#0a0a0f), bright accents for the city pulses
- The human-to-AI swap is the visual payoff — make it clean
