# Beat 2 — The Shift

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

A classic stock exchange wall clock, analog, white face, black hands. Below it, a green bar labeled "MARKET OPEN" spans from 9:30 to 4:00, with greyed-out zones on either side labeled "CLOSED."

The clock ticks normally for the first few seconds as the narration describes the old world.

On "Then crypto arrived" — a crack appears at 12 o'clock on the clock face. It spreads. The glass shatters outward in slow motion.

Behind the broken clock: a 24-hour continuous ring with no start or end point — a seamless glowing circle. The green "OPEN" bar stretches to fill the entire ring. Small crypto icons (BTC, ETH) orbit the ring like satellites.

A label appears below: "24/7/365. No closing bell."

## Mood

The old world breaking open. Satisfying destruction of a familiar symbol.

## Technical notes

- The clock should look traditional — wall clock, serif numerals, brass rim
- The shatter effect should feel physical, not digital glitch
- The 24-hour ring is the opposite aesthetic: modern, glowing, borderless
- BTC/ETH icons are small and minimal — just the symbols, not full logos
- Transition to Beat 3 via fade
