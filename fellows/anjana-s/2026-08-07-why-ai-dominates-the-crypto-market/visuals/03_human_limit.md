# Beat 3 — The Human Limit

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

Split screen, divided vertically.

**Left side — The Human:**
A simplified person silhouette at a desk with multiple monitors. Above them, a badge: green "ACTIVE." A clock fast-forwards through the day. At ~6 seconds, the person pushes back from the desk and walks off-screen. The badge flips to red "OFFLINE." The monitors dim. The desk goes dark.

**Right side — The Market:**
A continuous price chart that never stops. It keeps drawing candles — green, red, green, red — regardless of what happens on the left side. The chart doesn't care. At 2:14 AM (timestamp visible in the corner), a massive red candle drops — a flash crash. The chart plummets.

**The payoff:**
On the left side, a phone lights up on the nightstand. A push notification: "POSITION LIQUIDATED." The person is still asleep. Too late.

## Mood

Dread. The market moved while you weren't looking. It's personal.

## Technical notes

- The split screen divider should feel like a physical wall between two worlds
- Left side is warm/dim (bedroom lighting), right side is cold/bright (data)
- The flash crash candle should be visually dramatic — long red bar, sharp drop
- The phone notification is the emotional beat — make it readable but brief
- Clock timestamps should be visible: day → evening → 2:14 AM
