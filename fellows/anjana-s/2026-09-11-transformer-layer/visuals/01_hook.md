# Beat 1 — The Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~7 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

A vertical stack of identical rectangular blocks, each labeled Layer 1 through Layer 12. All grey, uniform, stacked.

One block in the middle (Layer 6) glows and expands outward, opening up like a cross-section. The other layers dim and compress upward and downward, making room. The expanded block reveals an empty interior with four placeholder slots labeled faintly: "Attention," "Feed-Forward," "Add & Norm," "Add & Norm."

A token enters from the left: the word "revenue" as a small glowing block, taken from the sentence "We expect revenue growth to moderate." The other tokens from the sentence trail behind it as dimmer blocks.

The token pauses at the entrance of the expanded layer, about to enter.

## Technical notes

- The stack-to-expansion is the visual metaphor: zoom into one layer, see the internals
- Keep the stack clean, 12 layers is standard for BERT-scale
- The expanded layer should feel like opening a machine panel
- The "revenue" token should feel like a protagonist entering a process
