# Beat 1 — The Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~8 seconds
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

A minimal chat interface on a dark background. Cursor blinks in the input field.

The question types out character by character: "Did Company A raise revenue guidance last quarter?"

A loading pulse (three dots). Then an answer streams in: "Yes, Company A raised full-year revenue guidance from $12B to $12.5B during Q3 prepared remarks. [1] [2] [3]"

Three citation badges glow at the bottom of the answer.

On "Here is what happened" the chat interface shrinks smoothly to the top-left corner and stays there as a reference point. The main area below opens up, blank, ready for the pipeline to build in.

## Technical notes

- The chat interface should look clean and modern but generic, not branded to any specific product
- The typing animation should feel natural, not instant
- The answer streaming should feel fast but readable
- The citation badges [1] [2] [3] are small, glowing, clickable-looking
- The chat interface persists in the top-left corner through Beats 2-3, returns full-size in Beat 4
