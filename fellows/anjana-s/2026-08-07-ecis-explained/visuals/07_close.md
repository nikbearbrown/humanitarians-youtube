# Beat 7 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion  
**Duration:** ~8 seconds  
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

A rapid montage recapping the system in four flashes, timed to the narration:

1. **"Four readers"** — the four reader icons from Beat 3 (Keyword, FinBERT, NER, LLM) flash in simultaneously, arranged in a row
2. **"One scorecard"** — the calibration curve from Beat 5 flashes in below the readers
3. **"Three feedback loops"** — the three circular loop arrows from Beat 6 flash in
4. **"human who signs every gate"** — the human gate icon from Beat 6 appears center-screen, larger than everything else

Hold for one beat, then all elements fade out together. Cut to the Claude-branded outro bookend with:
- Project title: **ECIS**
- Subtitle: Earnings Call Intelligence Signals
- Optional: GitHub repo link or QR code

## Mood

Definitive. Confident. A clean summary that lands and stops.

## Technical notes

- Each flash should be ~1.5 seconds
- Reuse the exact same visual assets from Beats 3, 5, and 6 — not new drawings
- The human gate icon should be the last and largest element, reinforcing the closing line
- Fade to the outro bookend should be clean, not abrupt
