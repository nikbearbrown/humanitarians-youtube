# Beat 2 — The Problem

**Visual type:** Remotion  
**Duration:** ~18 seconds

## What the viewer sees

A dense wall of earnings call transcript text scrolls vertically at high speed — too fast to read, conveying overwhelming volume. The text should look like real transcript content (use sample text from a public earnings call).

As the narration mentions each failure mode:

1. **"keyword search catches the obvious"** — a phrase like "raising guidance" highlights in green within the scrolling text
2. **"single language model will hallucinate"** — a different phrase gets a red highlight with a strike-through line animating across it, tagged "hallucination"
3. **"does not scale"** — the scroll accelerates dramatically, text blurring

End on a freeze-frame of one ambiguous sentence with a pulsing **?** overlay.

## Mood

Overwhelming at first (the volume problem), then frustrating (the accuracy problem), then impossible (the scale problem). Three escalating visual beats matching the narration.

## Technical notes

- Use real-looking but non-copyrighted transcript text (paraphrase a public filing)
- Scroll speed should feel uncomfortable — that's the point
- Green highlight = found, red strike-through = wrong, blur = unscalable
- Dark background, light text, highlights are the only color
