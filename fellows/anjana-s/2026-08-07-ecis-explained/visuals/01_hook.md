# Beat 1 — Hook

**Visual type:** Remotion  
**Duration:** ~12 seconds  
**Follows:** Claude-branded intro bookend

## What the viewer sees

A real earnings call quote fades in center-screen in a monospaced font, large and readable:

> "We remain cautiously optimistic about our forward outlook."

Below the quote, three labels animate in one at a time with a slight stagger:

- **Raised?** (amber)
- **Lowered?** (red)
- **Maintained?** (grey)

Each pulses once. Then "Raised" locks in — it grows slightly, gets a green highlight, and a confidence badge animates in beside it reading **0.87**.

## Mood

Clean, dark background. The text is the star. Minimal motion — just enough to draw attention to the question and then the answer.

## Technical notes

- Font: monospaced for the quote, sans-serif for the direction labels
- Background: dark (#0c0c0f or similar)
- The confidence badge should look like a data tag, not decorative
- Transition into Beat 2 should be a simple fade or cut
