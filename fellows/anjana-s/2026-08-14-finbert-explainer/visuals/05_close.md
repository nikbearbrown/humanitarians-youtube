# Beat 5 — The Close

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~7 seconds
**Followed by:** Claude-branded outro bookend

## What the viewer sees

Split screen. Two sides, clean and minimal.

**Left side:** "BERT" label with a generic text document icon below it. Neutral grey tone.

**Right side:** "FinBERT" label with a financial document icon below it (chart lines, dollar sign). Warm gold tone.

A transformation arrow connects left to right — same architecture, different training.

On "It stops reading words" — the left side (BERT) fades to black.

On "It starts reading money" — the right side (FinBERT) grows to fill the full screen. The gold tone warms.

Title text lands center screen:
**FinBERT**
"BERT, Fine-Tuned for Finance"

Hold for two beats. Fade to Claude-branded outro bookend.

## Technical notes

- The left-to-right transformation mirrors the conceptual journey of the video
- The fade of BERT and growth of FinBERT should feel like evolution, not replacement
- The closing line "It starts reading money" is the punchline — let it breathe
- Title text: clean sans-serif, gold accent color consistent with Beat 3
