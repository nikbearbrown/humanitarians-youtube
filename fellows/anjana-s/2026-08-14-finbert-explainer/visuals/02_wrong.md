# Beat 2 — Where General NLP Gets It Wrong

**Visual type:** Remotion
**Duration:** ~12 seconds

## What the viewer sees

Three example pairs appear one at a time, each on screen for ~4 seconds. Each pair is a split card — left side is general English interpretation, right side is financial interpretation.

**Pair 1 (0-4s):**
Phrase: "beat estimates by a narrow margin"
- Left (green): General → "Positive! They beat it!"
- Right (amber): Finance → "Cautious. Barely cleared the bar."

**Pair 2 (4-8s):**
Phrase: "headwinds"
- Left (grey): General → "Weather? Wind?"
- Right (red): Finance → "Risk ahead. Challenges incoming."

**Pair 3 (8-12s):**
Phrase: "adjusted results"
- Left (green): General → "Improved! Adjusted for the better."
- Right (amber): Finance → "Non-GAAP. Items were excluded."

Each pair transitions with a clean slide or fade. The gap between the two columns widens slightly with each pair — visually emphasizing that the distance between general and financial meaning is bigger than you think.

## Technical notes

- Left column always green (naive positive reading), right column amber or red (financial reality)
- The phrases should be large and readable — they're the content
- Keep the interpretation text short — one line max
- The widening gap between columns is a subtle animation cue, not dramatic
