# Brutalist Prompts, Playlist Architecture Video

*Video title, Playlist Architecture as Product Strategy, with AI as the Co-Strategist*

## How to Use This File

Each scene below has three things. A **narration script** (what you say on the voiceover), a **Brutalist prompt** (the visual description you feed into Brutalist to generate the scene), and any **on-screen text** that needs to appear.

Two scenes are marked *record separately*. Those are the ones you shoot with your webcam or screen recorder, then cut into the final video alongside the Brutalist-generated segments.

**Styling**, all scenes use Brutalist's default color palette, typography, and layout conventions. Do not override with custom colors or fonts. Consistency across the channel matters more than individual scene aesthetics.

Target length, 3 to 4 minutes for the 16:9 version, 2 to 3 minutes for the 9:16 version. Output resolution, 4K (3840x2160).

## Scene 3, The Reframe (0:20 to 0:50)

**Narration**
"A folder answers where does this video live. A playlist answers what does the viewer want to watch next. Every playlist is really a mini product with its own audience, its own entry point, and its own success metric. If you're organizing playlists by topic tag, you're doing filing. If you're organizing them by viewer intent and journey, you're doing product."

**Brutalist prompt**
Split-screen comparison, left half and right half divided by a thin vertical line.

Left half, header *Folder*. Below the header, a folder icon. Under the folder icon, three video thumbnail rectangles stacked vertically, each labeled with generic topic tags like *AI*, *Healthcare*, *Climate*. Overall feel is static and neutral.

Right half, header *Product*. Below the header, a small journey diagram, three video thumbnail rectangles connected by arrows flowing left to right, each labeled with viewer-intent phrases like *I want to learn*, *I want proof it works*, *I want to try it myself*. Overall feel is active and directional.

Both halves appear at the same time at 0:20. At 0:35, add subtle visual emphasis on the right half to reinforce the argument (Brutalist's own emphasis convention, whatever that is). Total scene length 30 seconds.

## Scene 4, The Three Questions (0:50 to 1:35)

**Narration**
"Every playlist has to answer three questions. One, who is this playlist for? A beginner learning the space, a practitioner going deep, or a decision-maker looking for proof. Two, what does the viewer want after video 1? Reinforcement, contrast, next-level depth, or application. Three, what does the channel want the viewer to do at the end? Subscribe, watch another playlist, or take action offline. If a playlist can't answer all three, it's not a product, it's a pile."

**Brutalist prompt**
Three numbered cards that appear sequentially, one at each 12-second mark, stacking vertically down the screen. Each card has a large numeral (1, 2, 3) on the left and text on the right. Use Brutalist's default card styling.

Card 1 text, *Who is this playlist for?* Sub-text below in smaller size, *Beginner, practitioner, or decision-maker.*

Card 2 text, *What does the viewer want after video 1?* Sub-text, *Reinforcement, contrast, depth, or application.*

Card 3 text, *What does the channel want the viewer to do at the end?* Sub-text, *Subscribe, watch another playlist, or act offline.*

After all three cards are visible (around 1:26), a final line appears below the stack, *If it can't answer all three, it's not a product. It's a pile.* Cards slide in from the right with a smooth easing.

## Scene 7, Method 2, Journey Mapping (2:00 to 2:20)

**Narration**

**Brutalist prompt**
Horizontal timeline across the middle of the screen. Three video thumbnail rectangles arranged left to right, connected by curved arrows. Each thumbnail has a title beneath it.

Thumbnail 1, *What is Generative AI?*
Thumbnail 2, *5 real GenAI use cases*
Thumbnail 3, *Building your first GenAI prototype*

Between each thumbnail, a small speech bubble appears with the viewer's inner thought.

Speech bubble 1 (between 1 and 2), *Okay I get the concept, but does anyone actually use this?*
Speech bubble 2 (between 2 and 3), *This is real. Can I try it myself?*

Speech bubbles animate in at 2:08 and 2:14 respectively. Use Brutalist's default styling for thumbnails, arrows, and speech bubbles. Total scene length 20 seconds.
