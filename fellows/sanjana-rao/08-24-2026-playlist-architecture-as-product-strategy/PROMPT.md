# The Clustering Prompt — @HumanitariansAI

This is the copyable scaffold the video hands the viewer (the "Your Turn" beat).
It's the exact prompt Sanjana uses for the clustering method in the reel.

## Paste this into Claude

```
You are my YouTube playlist co-strategist. Here are my channel's playlists and
their video titles: <paste titles here>.

1. CLUSTER them by VIEWER INTENT, not by topic or project. Use three audiences:
   beginners (learning the space), practitioners (going deep / building), and
   decision-makers (looking for proof and impact).
2. For each cluster, name the ONE job that playlist does for that viewer, and the
   success metric I should watch (e.g. watch-through, subscribes, next-playlist
   clicks).
3. Compare your clusters against my CURRENT playlists and list every DISAGREEMENT.
4. Name 3 playlists I should have but don't, and say who each one is for.
5. For my biggest catch-all playlist, propose two DIFFERENT products I could build
   from the same videos (e.g. "Projects That Shipped" vs "Ideas That Failed").

Return a table, then a one-paragraph reorganization plan.
```

## What a GOOD answer looks like

- Clusters that **cut across** your current project-named playlists (Mycroft,
  NeuroVEP, RAMAN) — proof it grouped by intent, not by the labels you already have.
- Each proposed playlist names a **specific audience + a job + a metric**.
- The "missing playlists" are on-ramps into your big piles (the 141-video `HAI`
  playlist, the 32-video `Fellows`), not more topic folders.

## What a BAD answer looks like

- It just re-labels your existing playlists (topic in, topic out).
- Vague audiences ("people interested in AI") with no job or metric.
- "Missing" playlists that duplicate what you already have.
