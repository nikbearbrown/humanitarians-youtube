# PROMPTS — hai-calling-voice-ai

Beat-prefixed prompts for open slots. **This reel has no open slots** — every beat is generated from a Remotion pattern already in the
toolkit. The entries below record what each beat says and which pattern carries it.

---

**B00 — ASK**

Pattern `ClaudeComposerAsk`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
Narration:

> The voice reading this sentence is not mine. It is a model running on my laptop. Before we go further that is the disclosure, and by the end of this you will know exactly what it took to make it say this.

---

**B01 — BLUF**

Pattern `BrutalistHesitantWriter`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
Narration:

> The short version. You do not send text to a voice model. The text is converted to sounds first, and the model never sees your words. And the voice itself is not a recording of anyone. It is a small block of numbers.

---

**B02 — WHAT**

Pattern `ClaudeTldrWhat`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
Narration:

> Every call runs the same three steps. Your text is turned into phonemes, which are the units of sound. The model turns those phonemes into raw audio samples. And the samples get written to a file. That is the whole pipeline.

---

**B03 — TERMS**

Pattern `ClaudeDefinitions`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
Narration:

> Four terms worth having. A phoneme is one unit of speech sound. A voice here is a block of numbers rather than a recording of a person. The sample rate is how many points of audio are stored for every second. And inference is just running the model from input to output.

**B04 — EVIDENCE**

Pattern `ClaudeCodeBeat`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
Narration:

> Here is that first step on my own machine. I gave it the phrase calling voice A I. What came back is the same phrase written in the international phonetic alphabet, with the stress marks included. That string is what the model actually receives.

**B05 — EVIDENCE**

Pattern `ClaudeCodeBeat`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.

Pattern `ClaudeTldrWhy`. Generated by the toolkit; no open slot and no request card.
