# PROMPTS — hai-agents-on-their-own

**No open slots.** The two prompts below appear inside the film as content.

## B00 — the opening ask

> Everyone says AI agents now act on their own. What does that actually mean in the
> software, and if it really decides its own next step, what stops it?

## B08 — Your Turn

> For the task I am about to run you on: list every action you could take without asking
> me again, and tell me the exact condition that would make you stop and come back.

The spoken handoff asks the viewer to write one sentence — what would make this stop —
before starting an agent again.
