Agents, On Their Own — where the limits have to come from

An agent is not a smarter model. It is the same model put inside a loop, and given the
ability to act in between the turns.

Nothing about that loop knows when to stop. That part is still ours, and it has to be
written down.

What's in here: what actually changes when the pause disappears, the four words that carry
the weight (tool, loop, autonomy, guardrail), and two real limits from my own machine — a
guardrail that tells an agent its own echo is not a person speaking, and a toolkit that
refused my last video six separate times for things a human had already decided mattered.

The honest test for letting one run: the work is checkable, a mistake is cheap to undo, and
a rule can be written down ahead of time. If you cannot say what would make it stop, it is
not ready to run on its own.
