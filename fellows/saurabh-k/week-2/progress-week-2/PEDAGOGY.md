# PEDAGOGY — hai-brutalist-week-2

## The one thing a viewer should leave with

*A default is a decision someone already made for you. Read the setup before blaming the tool.*

## Why this is the subject

Week one found a quality gate that had been silently skipping and concluded the cause was a
missing library. Week two went under the hood and found that conclusion was half wrong: the
library had been installed and the gate still would not run, because the interpreter on the
PATH was too old for the checker's own syntax.

That is a more useful lesson than the original, and it is worth saying out loud that the
previous report was corrected rather than quietly dropped.

## The transferable move

Separate *mechanism* from *judgement*, and mark which is which. The variables and the
version numbers are shown; the advice is spoken. FACTCHECK.md keeps the same split as
VERIFIED-LOCAL versus ARGUMENT. A viewer who starts asking which of the two they are being
handed has taken the method rather than the content.

The second move is correcting yourself in public. The reel's own evidence contradicts its
predecessor, and it says so.

## What the viewer is asked to do

Take any tool used every day and find where it reads its environment. Read the list. For
each entry ask whether that value was chosen, or chosen for you and never noticed.
