First week with brutalist.art, the video toolkit I was assigned. I did not just learn it.
I shipped four finished masters with it, and I found a check that was never running.

What's in this one:
— The week's output, probed rather than estimated. MCP, Explained at 2:41 and Agents, On
  Their Own at 2:14, each delivered in widescreen at 3840x2160 and vertical at 2160x3840.
— Four terms I had to learn properly: beat sheet, lane, gate, master.
— The finding. The typography gate had never actually run on this machine. It was skipping
  in silence because scipy was missing, and the command still exited zero.
— Installing the library was not the end of it. The checker then crashed on Python 3.9.6
  over a 3.10-only type annotation.
— When it finally ran, it failed 8 of 10 vertical beats. The smallest text measured 38px
  against a 72px floor. About half the size it needed to be on a phone.
— What I take from it: the gate was right and my frames were wrong. Vertical is its own
  design and not a crop. And a tool that says nothing is not telling you that it passed.

Your turn: take the build or test command you trust most, the one you assume is passing.
Make it fail on purpose, once, and watch whether it actually tells you.
