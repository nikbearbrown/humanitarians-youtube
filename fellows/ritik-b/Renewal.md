## Degree relevance

My research and videos are about modern AI model architectures: each week I study an open-source model in depth and turn it into a video. This is a natural follow-on to my master's in Artificial Intelligence and directly relevant to my field of study.

## Plan for the requested period

I plan to continue the same research direction: understanding how modern open-source model architectures work, and
why they differ. Each week I will take one model, read its paper or technical report and model card, and
turn what I learn into an explainer and a progress video, checking the lab's claims against the released files.

The focus stays on the design choices that separate today's models — attention variants (sliding-window, sparse and
linear), mixture-of-experts routing, looped and recurrent depth, and how models take in images and audio. Where a model
family releases a new generation, I will compare the two, so the series builds into a picture of how these
architectures are changing over time rather than a set of separate episodes.

Alongside the videos, from 1 December I will present each week's work in slides, as discussed, and keep a Frictional
log and an hours record for every week.
