# PROMPTS — *Aim Where It's Going.*

Ep. 16 · `predictive-adaptive-optics`

## No image-generation prompts were used.

There is no FLUX, no nano-banana, no Midjourney, no stock imagery and no
lifted figure anywhere in this reel. Nothing in it was generated by an image
model, and nothing was screenshotted from a paper.

Every frame is one of exactly three things:

1. **A reel-local Manim scene** (`scenes.py`), drawn from the Claude
   fidelity palette — cream ground, warm ink, one terracotta accent.
2. **A plate** from `assets/gen_ao.py` — a PNG drawn with Pillow from the
   output of the experiment in `assets/ao.py`. Plates carry **imagery only**:
   no text, no tick labels, no legend. Every word on screen is a Manim
   mobject placed outside the plate.
3. **A Remotion scene** from the toolkit's proven core —
   `ClaudeComposerAsk`, `ClaudeVerdictArtifact`, `ClaudeTitleOutro` and their
   `916` counterparts.

This satisfies REBUILD LAW the strict way: the figures are not redrawn
*from* a source, they are **generated by re-running the calculation**.

## The one "prompt" in the reel is editorial

B00 and B12 show text typed into a Claude composer. Those strings are
authored in `beat_sheet.json` and rendered deterministically by Remotion —
they are *content*, not calls to a model. No API request is made at any point
in this build.

B12's handoff prompt, verbatim:

> Here is a control loop with a fixed delay and an error budget. Tell me which
> terms a predictor can actually reduce, which are untouchable no matter how
> good the model is, and what measurement would tell the two apart.

## Seeds

| What | Seed | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `gen_ao.py` top level | **1616** | phase screens for the structure-function and lag cross-checks, and the B03 cut |
| modal telemetry atmospheres | **10 … 21** | 12 independent 8192-frame runs; 8 train, 2 validation, 2 test |
| per-layer screens | `seed*1000 + i` | keeps layers within one atmosphere independent |
| measurement-noise draw | **23** | the same noise realisation across conditions, so comparisons are paired |

Same seed → identical plates and identical numbers, every run.
