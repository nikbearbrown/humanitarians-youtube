# Is Registering a Subsidiary the Same as Staying Compliant?

A company registers a subsidiary and treats the job as finished — the state
issued a certificate, after all. But a certificate is a snapshot, not a
status. States don't ask permission before dropping a delinquent entity out
of good standing. Watch the anchor: a Delaware subsidiary spun up three
years ago for a single product launch, its filing date unwatched ever
since — found lapsed only when a financing round's diligence team pulls a
status certificate. Being current in the home state doesn't mean compliant
everywhere (foreign qualifications are separate deadlines), and a clean
list today doesn't mean clean forever — the next filing date is always on
its way.
