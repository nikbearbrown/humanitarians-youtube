## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not that registration is pointless.** The reel never says skip
  registering — only that registering is the start of a recurring
  obligation, not the end of one.
- **Not a specific Claude product feature.** Because the source sheet's
  actual facts were never written (see QUESTION.md), this script describes
  the generic mechanics of a corporate entity-compliance practice rather
  than citing any particular tool's UI or output format.
- **No accusation that anyone was negligent.** An unwatched filing date is
  presented as an ordinary gap in an informal process, not a failure by any
  named person or team.

## Handoff prompt (BHTF, read aloud)

> "List every legal entity your company has — subsidiaries, foreign
> qualifications, everything. For each one, write down its next filing
> deadline and who owns tracking it."

Why it's worth running: the exercise surfaces the same gap the reel is
built around — any row with a blank owner or a deadline nobody can name is
an entity a one-time registration would never have kept current.
