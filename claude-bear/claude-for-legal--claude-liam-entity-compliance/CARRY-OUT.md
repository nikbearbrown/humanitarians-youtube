# CARRY-OUT — claude-for-legal--claude-liam-entity-compliance

**The line (written first, GATE C):**

> A registered entity isn't compliant forever — it's compliant one deadline
> at a time, for as long as it exists.

**Test:** if someone repeats only this in a meeting next week, is it still
true? Yes — it compresses the one distinction the reel is built to land
(registration is a moment; compliance is a recurring schedule), not the
topic (corporate legal-ops generally).

**The wrong guess it defeats:** that registering the entity — filing the
certificate with the state — finishes the job. A certificate is a snapshot
of one moment, not an ongoing status. States don't ask permission before
dropping a delinquent entity out of good standing; the anchor (a Delaware
subsidiary spun up years ago for a single product launch, its filing date
unwatched since) shows how that gap surfaces — quietly, until someone in
diligence pulls a status certificate and finds it lapsed.
