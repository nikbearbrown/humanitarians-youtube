interview itself. It doesn't; the skill builds a structured plan, competency-based
questions, and a scorecard before a human runs the interview, and never conducts the
interview itself.

> Claude doesn't interview the candidate. It preps the plan — same role in, same
> questions and scorecard out, every time.

describing what a Claude Skill named `interview-prep` does correctly — it compresses the
distinction that matters (prep, not conduct) rather than summarizing the topic, and it is
not tied to a detail this build had to invent.
