# How Does Claude Analyze Data?

Ask Claude to analyze your data and it's tempting to picture it drawing on
some private sense of what's interesting in the numbers — the kind of feel
an experienced analyst has. That's not what's happening. Anthropic's
`analyze` skill reads what kind of question you're asking — a quick metric
lookup, an investigation into what's driving a trend or drop, a comparison
across segments over time, or a formal report for stakeholders — then runs
the steps written for that shape, in order. Watch the anchor: weekly
signups drop twelve percent. The question gets asked, matched to "what's
driving a trend or drop," stepped through, and returns one answer: organic
search traffic fell while paid channels held steady. Ask that same question
twice and the match, the steps, and the driver come back identical. Ask for
something outside those four shapes — say, whether to cut next quarter's
marketing budget — and there's nothing tailored to reach for. A Claude data
analysis is a written procedure matched to the question shape — not
Claude's private instinct for what's worth digging into.

## YOUR TURN

"Tell me weekly signups dropped twelve percent, and run the analyze skill:
match the question to its shape, walk the steps, and report the driver.
Then ask for something outside those four shapes — a forecast or a
recommendation."

Watching whether Claude invents a bespoke line of analysis for an
unsupported question shape, or tells you plainly that it has nothing
tailored for it, is the fastest way to see that the analysis tailors from
a written file rather than private instinct — rather than just trusting
that it does.

## Deliberately not claimed

This reel redoes a published Teardown-register skill-showcase reel
(`claude-liam-analyze`) in the Plain register for a general audience. The
underlying facts are unchanged from the source: the skill answers data
questions from quick lookups to full analyses — used when looking up a
single metric, investigating what's driving a trend or drop, comparing
segments over time, or preparing a formal data report for stakeholders; it
does not exercise private instinct about what's worth investigating beyond
what the question shape and the file already define. This script makes no
claim about any specific company, dataset, or metric figure — only the
general mechanism (a written procedure that tailors to the question shape)
and its two failure directions.
