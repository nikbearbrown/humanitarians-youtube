# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** Drafting an offer here isn't just the congratulatory letter —
it's the full comp package, the letter itself, and negotiation guidance for the
hiring manager, once a candidate's ready to hire.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that "draft an offer" means writing one
polite letter telling a candidate they got the job. The actual scope is wider:
it assembles the total comp package (base, equity, signing bonus), writes the
letter text, and preps negotiation guidance for the hiring manager — three
things, not one, and nothing outside that list.

**Secondhand test:** "It's not just the letter — it's the comp package, the
letter, and negotiation guidance for the hiring manager" survives being
repeated by someone who wasn't fully listening, and stays true. It compresses
the distinction that matters (three linked outputs vs. one letter), not the
topic (hiring in general).
