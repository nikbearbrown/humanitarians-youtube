# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** The Friday brief isn't outside market news — it's your own
numbers: revenue against last week, top sellers, wins and watches, run over
whichever window you pick, seven days or fourteen.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that a "Friday brief" means a roundup of
outside market news delivered on a schedule. The actual value is source-bound and
fixed-shape: it never leaves the business's own sales data, and it only ever
reports on the same four things — revenue vs. the prior week, top sellers, wins,
and watches — never a general news feed.

**Secondhand test:** "It's your own revenue and top sellers, not outside news,
over a week or two you choose" survives being repeated by someone who wasn't
fully listening, and stays true. It compresses the distinction that matters (your
own sales numbers, four fixed fields, vs. an outside news feed), not the topic
(small-business reporting in general).
