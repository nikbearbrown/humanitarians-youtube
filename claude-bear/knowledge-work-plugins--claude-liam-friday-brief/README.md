# The Friday Brief Isn't Outside News

Someone hears "Friday brief" and assumes an outside market-news roundup,
delivered every week. It isn't. It's a scan of the business's own sales
data — revenue against the week before, top sellers, wins, and watches —
over a seven or fourteen day window you pick. The instructions live in one
file Claude reads before it works; ask for anything outside those four
fields and there's no mode that covers it.

## YOUR TURN

"Look at my sales from the past week, compare revenue to the week before,
tell me my top sellers, and call out anything that counts as a win or
something worth watching. If I want a longer view, run it over the last
fourteen days instead of seven."

That's the whole idea: your own sales numbers in, four fixed fields out —
never an outside news feed.

---

## Deliberately not claimed

This reel never claims the skill tracks outside market or industry news,
pulls an RSS feed, or searches the web — it stays bound to the business's
own sales records. It also isn't a claim that this replaces a manager's own
weekly review: the boundary (only what the instruction file specifies gets
covered) is stated as fact, not argued with.
