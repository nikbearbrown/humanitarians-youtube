# Claude, Guideline Generation.

Ask Claude to write your brand voice guideline and it's tempting to picture
it forming its own opinion about what your writing should sound like — the
way an editor with good taste would. That's not what's happening.
Anthropic's `guideline-generation` skill reads the source material you
hand it and pulls out the same kind of patterns every time, run the same
way regardless of whose writing it is. Watch the anchor: ten of a
company's own blog posts, handed over for a brand voice guideline. They
get read, extracted, structured, and returned as one guideline document —
patterns pulled straight from those ten posts. Feed the same ten posts
through twice and the guideline comes back identical. Ask something
outside that — whether the voice in those posts is actually a *good*
one — and there's nothing tailored to reach for; the skill stops exactly
where its extraction steps stop. A Claude brand voice guideline is a
written extraction procedure matched to your source material — not
Claude's private taste in good writing.

## YOUR TURN

"Hand me a handful of your own writing samples, and run the
guideline-generation skill: read them, extract the patterns, and return a
brand voice guideline. Then ask me whether that voice is actually a good
one."

Watching whether Claude invents an opinion about an unsupported judgment
question, or tells you plainly that it has nothing tailored for it, is the
fastest way to see that the guideline comes from a written extraction
procedure rather than private taste — rather than just trusting that it
does.

## Deliberately not claimed

This reel redoes a published Teardown-register skill-showcase reel
(`claude-liam-guideline-generation`) in the Plain register for a general
audience. The underlying facts are unchanged from the source: the skill
generates brand voice guidelines from source materials, reading a
`SKILL.md` before acting and executing its steps in order, returning the
same output for the same input; it does not exercise private taste about
what makes writing good beyond what the source material and the file
already define. This script makes no claim about any specific company,
brand, or the literal contents of the skill's SKILL.md steps — only the
general mechanism (a written procedure that extracts from your source
material) and its two failure directions.
