# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** It drafts the whole hiring packet — job post, interview
guide, offer letter — and it will even open DocuSign and build the envelope.
It will never hit send without you.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that once Claude is far enough into the
job to be driving your browser through DocuSign, it will finish the job and
send the offer itself. It won't — it fills out the envelope, saves it as a
draft, and hands you the review link. The same boundary holds for the Gmail
fallback and for the job post itself: drafted, never sent, never published,
without an explicit yes.

**Secondhand test:** "It builds the whole packet and even sets up DocuSign —
but it never hits send without you" survives being repeated by someone who
wasn't fully listening, and stays true. It compresses the distinction that
matters (drafts everything, sends nothing on its own), not the topic (hiring
in general).
