# Job Post Builder Never Hits Send.

If Claude can open DocuSign and set up the whole offer, will it also hit
send? No. The `job-post-builder` skill drafts the full hiring packet — a
market-informed job post, a stage-by-stage interview guide with a scoring
rubric, and an offer letter template — and it can even drive your browser
through DocuSign to build the signing envelope. But there are three hard
stops wired into it: it never sends the DocuSign envelope, never sends the
Gmail fallback if DocuSign fails, and never publishes the job post anywhere.
Every draft goes right up to the edge, then waits for you to say go.

## Deliberately not claimed

This reel never claims the skill screens or ranks applicants — the source
SKILL.md states that as an explicit non-goal. It's also not a claim that the
offer letter or comp figures are legally final: the source flags that the
letter needs legal review and that compensation ranges need HR sign-off
before anything gets published. The approval gate is the reel's whole point,
not a limitation to argue with.
