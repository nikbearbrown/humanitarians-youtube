# What Is a Gap-Surfacer Actually Looking For?

You'd guess a tool like this reads a contract and flags whatever clauses
are badly written — a quality check. It isn't one. Its whole job is
absence: walk a checklist of things a document is supposed to contain,
and report the items that have no matching text anywhere in it. Watch the
anchor: the assignment-clause line comes back with no match — flagged,
not yet resolved. Turns out the protection was there the whole time, just
filed under "Transfer of Rights" instead of "Assignment." A match doesn't
prove a clause is good enough, and a no-match doesn't prove it's actually
gone — both times, a person still has to open the page and read it.

## YOUR TURN

"I have a document and a short checklist of things it's supposed to
include. Scan the document, flag every checklist item with no matching
text anywhere in it, and for each flag, tell me whether equivalent
coverage might exist under different wording before calling it missing."

Why it's worth running: a flagged item isn't a finished verdict — it's the
list of places a person still needs to actually read.
