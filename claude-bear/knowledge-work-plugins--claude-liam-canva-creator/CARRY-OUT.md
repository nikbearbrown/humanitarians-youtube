# CARRY-OUT — GATE C

Written before any other narration. Every beat exists to make this sentence survivable.

## The line

> **canva-creator fills in a fixed template, the same way every time. Step
> outside that template, and it has nothing to fall back on.**

## The wrong guess it defeats

That a skill named "Canva Creator" means Claude can design anything you ask
for in Canva — a free-form design assistant. It can't. The SKILL.md sets a
narrower job: fill an existing template with your text, colors, and logo.
That's what makes the output repeatable (same input, same output, every
run) — and it's also the whole limit. Ask for a layout the template doesn't
cover, and the skill has nothing to reach for.

## The secondhand test

*If someone repeats only this sentence in a meeting next week, is it still
true?* Yes — it compresses the one distinction that matters (fills a
template vs. designs freely) and states the failure mode in the same
breath, without needing a second sentence.

## What it deliberately does not say

- **Not a verdict on the design.** The source's B03/BVDT framed this as
  "what it gets right" / "what it bites" — Teardown language, including an
  implicit judgment that the constraint is a shortcoming. Plain keeps the
  same underlying fact (repeatable inside the template, nothing outside it)
  but states it as a mechanism boundary, not a critique of the skill file.
- **Not a specific claim about Canva's product surface.** The source's own
  narration and props left the specific constraint as an unresolved
  template placeholder (`>`), and the original `SKILL.md` this reel was
  built from is not present in this checkout to verify against. This reel
  states the constraint generically — "fills a template" — rather than
  inventing a specific Canva field, UI panel, or workflow step. See
  QUESTION.md for the full account of that call.
- **Not that every skill behaves this way.** The claim is scoped to
  canva-creator's own documented shape: a linear, template-bound
  instruction set. Other skills may permit more.
