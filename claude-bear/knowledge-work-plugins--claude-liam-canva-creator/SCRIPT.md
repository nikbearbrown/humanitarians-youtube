## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not a verdict on the design.** The source's B03/BVDT framed the
  template constraint as "what it gets right" / "what it bites" — Teardown
  language. Plain keeps the same underlying fact (repeatable inside the
  template, nothing outside it) but states it as a mechanism boundary.
- **Not a specific claim about Canva's product surface.** The source's own
  `>` placeholders (B00's `output`, B03's `body`, BHTF's `command`) were
  never filled in, and the original `SKILL.md` is not present in this
  checkout to recover them from. This reel states the constraint
  generically — see QUESTION.md for the full account.
- **Not that every source beat gets equal airtime.** The source's B00 was
  already `ClaudeComposerAsk` REMOTION (not AI-video or pantry), so
  NO-GENAI/NO-PANTRY LAW required no substitution beyond the WRITER LAW
  swap mandated by hai-simple itself.
