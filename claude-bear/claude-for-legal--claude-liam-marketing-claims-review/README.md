# Claude, Marketing Claims Review.

Claude picked up a skill called marketing-claims-review — does that mean it
will just tell you your ad is legal? No. It sorts every claim in your copy
into a call: fine, needs proof, needs rewording, or cut. Take one real line
— "Trusted by 10,000 companies" — and watch it work: first classified (a
specific, factual claim), then checked for substantiation (is 10,000 the
actual current count, or an old lifetime-signup total dressed up as trust?).
The output is a call, never a verdict, and before any claim ships as
"ready," a non-lawyer user hits one more gate: has an attorney actually seen
this? A flag doesn't mean a claim is false, and a clean pass doesn't mean
risk-free forever — the product can change after the copy ships, and
nothing rechecks it automatically.
