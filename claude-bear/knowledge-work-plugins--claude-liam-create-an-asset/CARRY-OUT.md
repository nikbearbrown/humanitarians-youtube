# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** Create-an-asset doesn't invent a generic deck from
nothing — it builds one of four sales formats, shaped by your actual deal:
your prospect, your audience, your goal.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that "create an asset" means Claude
freely designs a polished deck or one-pager out of its own imagination,
with no input beyond a one-line request. The actual value is deal-specific:
the skill drafts four distinct formats — landing pages, decks, one-pagers,
workflow demos — each shaped from the prospect, audience, and goal you
describe, never a generic template invented from nothing.

**Secondhand test:** "It doesn't invent a generic asset — it builds one of
four formats out of your actual deal" survives being repeated by someone
who wasn't fully listening, and stays true. It compresses the distinction
that matters (deal-specific output vs. an invented generic template), not
the topic (sales asset creation in general).
