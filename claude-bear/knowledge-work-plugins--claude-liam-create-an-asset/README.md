# Creating An Asset Isn't One Generic Template

Someone assumes you can just tell Claude to design a slide deck and get
something polished back, no questions asked. It doesn't work that way — the
create-an-asset skill generates one of exactly four sales formats: landing
pages, decks, one-pagers, and workflow demos. Each one is shaped by the deal
you describe: your prospect, your audience, your goal. Skip that context and
there's nothing for it to build the asset around. Watch it work through a
concrete pitch — a landscaping business selling to a property manager who
cares about reliability and price — and see how a headline, three bullets,
and a call to action come out fit to that specific deal, not a generic
template with the names swapped in.

## Deliberately not claimed

No specific product name, price, or platform beyond "the create-an-asset
skill" and "your deal" — the mechanism (gather deal context, generate one of
four formats) holds regardless of which CRM or deck tool you use. No claim
that Claude infers your prospect or goal on its own; the video states the
opposite throughout. No verdict on whether four formats is the right
number — that boundary is stated as fact, not graded.
