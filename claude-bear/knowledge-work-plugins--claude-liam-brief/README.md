# The Legal Briefing Isn't a News Digest

Someone hears "legal briefing" and assumes an outside news feed, delivered
every morning. It isn't. It's a scan of what's already sitting in your own
email, calendar, and contracts, run through whichever of three fixed modes
the moment calls for — a daily scan, research on one question, or fast
context when something urgent breaks. The instructions live in one file
Claude reads before it works; ask for anything outside those three modes and
there's no mode that covers it.

## YOUR TURN

"Look through my recent emails, calendar, and any contracts I have open, and
give me a short briefing — what's legal-relevant this week, organized by how
urgent it is. If anything looks like it needs fast attention, put that
first."

That's the whole idea: your own sources in, one of three fixed modes out —
never an outside news feed.

---

## Deliberately not claimed

This reel never claims the skill monitors outside legal news, pulls an RSS
feed, or searches the web — it stays bound to the user's own email,
calendar, contracts, and internal sources. It also isn't a claim that this
replaces a lawyer's judgment: the boundary (only what the instruction file
specifies gets covered) is stated as fact, not argued with.
