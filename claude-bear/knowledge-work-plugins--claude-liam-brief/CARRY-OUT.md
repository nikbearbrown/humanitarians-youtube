# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** A legal briefing here isn't outside news — it's a scan of
what's already in your own email, calendar, and contracts, run through whichever
of three modes the moment calls for.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that a "legal briefing" means a digest of
outside legal news delivered on a schedule. The actual value is source-bound:
it never leaves your own materials, and it only ever runs in one of three fixed
modes — a daily scan, a specific question, or a fast-moving incident — never a
general news feed.

**Secondhand test:** "It scans your own email, calendar, and contracts, in
whichever of three modes fits the moment" survives being repeated by someone who
wasn't fully listening, and stays true. It compresses the distinction that
matters (your own sources, three fixed modes, vs. an outside news feed), not the
topic (legal work in general).
