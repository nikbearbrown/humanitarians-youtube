# CARRY-OUT — behind-the-model--claude-liam-three-level-supervision-classifier

**The line (written first, GATE C):**

> Trusting AI the right amount isn't a fixed habit — it's calibrated to what
> you actually handed it, task by task.

**Test:** if someone repeats only this in a meeting next week, is it still
true? Yes — it compresses the one distinction the reel is built to land
(supervision as something that should scale with the level of the task, not
a flat personal comfort setting), not the topic (AI oversight generally).

**The wrong guess it defeats:** that trusting AI "the right amount" means
picking one comfort level — a quick glance, a nod — and keeping it steady no
matter what you asked for. It doesn't work that way. Renaming a variable and
proposing a system architecture are not the same task: one is a Level One
copy-paste edit, the other hands over real Level Three decision-making. A
fixed comfort level can't tell them apart, and the gap between what you
handed over and how closely you checked it stays invisible until you
actually compare the two.
