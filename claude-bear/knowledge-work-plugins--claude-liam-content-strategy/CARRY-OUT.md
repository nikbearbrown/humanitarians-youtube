# CARRY-OUT.md

**Carry-out line:** Content strategy here isn't Claude writing your posts —
it's Claude reading your sales numbers and ranking what's working. It stops
at the brief; building the posts is a separate step that needs your
approval first.

**Wrong guess it's built to defeat:** that "content strategy" means Claude
hands you finished posts or a content calendar. The actual value is
upstream of that: the skill pulls real sales data (QuickBooks, PayPal, or
Square), ranks products into top performers, slow movers, and trending
items, layers in seasonality, and returns a strategic brief — never a
caption, an image, or a scheduled post. Those only happen later, in a
different skill, after the owner signs off.

**Secondhand test:** "It doesn't write your posts — it ranks what your
sales data says is working, then stops at the brief for you to approve"
survives being repeated by someone who wasn't fully listening, and stays
true. It compresses the distinction that matters (strategic ranking +
approval gate vs. content creation), not the topic (marketing in general).
