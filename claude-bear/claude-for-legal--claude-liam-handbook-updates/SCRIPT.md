## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not that annual review is worthless.** The reel never says drop the
  yearly read-through — only that it can't be the *only* trigger, or the
  handbook runs stale for months at a stretch.
- **Not a specific Claude product feature.** Because the source sheet's
  actual facts were never written (see QUESTION.md), this script describes
  the generic mechanics of a law-triggered handbook-update practice rather
  than citing any particular tool's UI or output format.
- **No accusation that anyone was negligent.** A handbook that lagged a law
  change is presented as an ordinary gap in a calendar-based process, not a
  failure by any named person or team.

## Handoff prompt (BHTF, read aloud)

> "Pick one policy in your employee handbook — leave, overtime, remote
> work, anything. Write down the date it was last actually changed, and
> find out whether any law touching that topic has changed since."

Why it's worth running: most people can answer the first half and not the
second — and the second half is the exact gap a calendar-only review never
catches.
