## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not that form letters should be ignored.** The reel never says skip a
  reply — only that the response gets sequenced by what's actually claimed,
  not by how alarming the letter sounds.
- **Not a specific Claude product feature.** Because the source sheet's
  actual facts were never written (see QUESTION.md), this script describes
  the generic mechanics of an infringement-triage intake practice rather
  than citing any particular tool's UI or output format.
- **No claim about who is right.** Triage sorts urgency of response; it
  never determines whether an asserted claim actually holds up — that's
  what the attorney's review is for.

## Handoff prompt (BHTF, read aloud)

> "Pull out four things from this letter: who's asserting the claim, what
> specific right they're claiming, what evidence is attached, and what
> deadline is demanded."

Why it's worth running: the exercise surfaces the same gap the reel is
built around — a missing field (no evidence, no deadline, an anonymous
asserter) is what tells you this one can wait, and a full set of four is
what tells you it can't.
