# When Is a Claude Legal Memo Actually Reliable?

If Claude drafts a legal memo that cites real cases and reads confidently, that
draft isn't the answer yet. A memo handoff that actually works has three
parts: the legal question and facts in, a structured analysis out — issue,
rule, application, conclusion — and a required verification step before
anyone relies on it. Watch the anchor: that same memo comes back with one
citation stamped in the margin, "FLAGGED — VERIFY." Only once that citation
is checked against the law as it stands today does the memo move from draft
analysis to something an attorney can actually rely on. No flag doesn't mean
confirmed, and a heavily corrected draft isn't a failure either — both can be
the verification step working exactly as intended.
