> every time.

**Test:** repeated cold, out of context, next week — still true, and still specific to
what a Claude Skill actually is (a fixed instruction file Claude reads and executes in
order), not a generic "AI can write pitches" line. It compresses the distinction that
matters (a Skill is a followed procedure, not independent financial judgment), not the
topic (investment-proposal drafting in general).

investments or writes the pitch on its own. B00 states that framing and corrects it on

file; B03 states the skill's actual, specific job (create a professional investment
proposal covering the firm's approach, proposed allocation, expected outcomes, and fee
structure) and its scope limit (only what the page says, same result every run). The
carry-out is what a viewer should keep: the reliability comes from Claude following
fixed steps, not from Claude making the investment call itself.
