## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not "individual carefulness doesn't matter."** B01 states the opposite —
  the four teammates were each careful, and that's exactly why nobody caught
  it; carefulness is a precondition, not a substitute for a shared rule.
- **Not "every shared resource needs its own committee."** B05 states the
  failure mode on the other side — a private, unshared read isn't a
  team-level risk, and treating it like one wastes the fence on the wrong
  thing.
- **No accusation of anyone acting recklessly.** Adding a connector for your
  own convenience is an ordinary, reasonable-sounding action, and the reel
  treats it as one; the gap is structural, in where the grant lands, not in
  anyone's judgment.
