## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not "closing a matter deletes it."** The naive framing in B00 ("delete a matter once
  it's done") is stated and corrected within the same beat — the skill archives the
  record out of the active portfolio, it never deletes it.
- **Not "the archived record is the final legal word."** B03 and BCRY both stay inside
  what the source's own SKILL.md specifies: capture outcome, final exposure, and
  lessons, then archive. Neither beat claims the archive replaces any further legal
  review of the closed matter — that scope boundary is the whole point of the carry-out.
- **No verdict on the skill's design.** The source's Teardown register judged the skill
  ("what it gets right," "what it bites"); this Plain redo describes the same constraint
  without ruling on whether it was well designed.
- **No invented specifics.** Unlike some siblings in this family, the source narration
  for `matter-close` was fully filled in (no unfilled `>` placeholders) — every fact
  used here (capture outcome/exposure/lessons, archive without deleting, 6k SKILL.md,
  linear step pipeline) is carried directly from the source's own beat text. See
  QUESTION.md for the source-file check.
