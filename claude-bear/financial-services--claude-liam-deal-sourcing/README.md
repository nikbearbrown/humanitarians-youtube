# Claude, Deal Sourcing.

Claude picked up a skill called deal-sourcing — does that mean it got
judgment about which companies are good investments? No. A "skill" is a
folder holding one file, SKILL.md, that Claude reads before it starts and
then follows step by step: search a sector for target companies, check the
CRM for existing relationships, draft a personalized outreach email to the
founder. Delete the folder and Claude loses no investment judgment, because
there was none to begin with — it just stops running that three-step
checklist. That makes a skill a specification, not new judgment: the
payoff is the same three steps, every sector, every time; the limit is
ranking those candidates by how good an investment they are, because
that's not on the page. And watching Claude surface twenty candidates, or
draft a clean outreach email, proves nothing on its own about whether any
of it is actually a good deal.
