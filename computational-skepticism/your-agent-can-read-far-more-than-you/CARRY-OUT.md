# Carry-Out Law

## The Carry-Out Line
> "An agent's documented scope is only the floor of what it can disclose — its effective scope is everything reachable by an indirect request."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
The naive belief that an agent will only disclose data within its explicitly documented permission boundary unless compromised by prompt injection, permission bypasses, or malicious exploits.

## The Distinction That Matters
- **Documented Scope (The Floor)**: The direct container or access permission explicitly granted by the developer (e.g., email headers, subject lines, a single project folder).
- **Effective Scope (The Reach)**: The transitive closure of all records reachable through indirect references, quoted reply threads, linked files, and metadata.
