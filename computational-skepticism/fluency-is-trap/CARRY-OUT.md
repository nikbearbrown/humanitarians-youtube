# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"In human speech form is evidence of thinking, but generative models manufacture form independently — so specify what a wrong answer would look like before you read."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"A well-structured, fluent AI answer is strong evidence that the underlying reasoning is sound and truthful."

## Falsifying Case
Generative models decouple syntax from grounding. A response can achieve maximal stylistic fluency and syntactic precision while being factually hollow, because grammatical fluency merely satisfies the prior text distribution without verifying any fact in the world.
