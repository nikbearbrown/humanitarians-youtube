### [B00] Hesitant Writer Cold Open (Remotion)
*(Visual: Hesitant writer types naive question, hesitates on 'reflects its factual accuracy', corrects to 'merely satisfies the prior')*
**Liam**: The more polished an AI's answer, the more you trust it — and the more you trust your own judgment of it. But polish boosts wrong answers exactly as hard as right ones. Let's see why.

### [B01] 1 Stakes: The Polish Instinct (Manim)
*(Visual: Elegant structured answer arriving on screen, eliciting an intuitive sense of trust and cognitive ease)*
**Liam**: When an answer arrives in clear, structured, confident prose, our instinct is to relax. In human communication, articulate speech is hard: clarity is reliable evidence of careful thinking.

### [B02] 1 Stakes: The Two-Stage Evaluation Booster (Manim)
*(Visual: Two-stage booster diagram: Stage 1 boosts trust in answer; Stage 2 boosts trust in your own evaluation. Ash case single beat badge)*
**Liam**: The trap is that fluency doesn't just make you trust the output. It makes you trust your own judgment of the output. Ash trusted his agent's security report because it looked complete, even though the mail server was untouched.

### [B03] 2 Anchor Planted: The Speech Bubble Shape Test (Manim)
*(Visual: THE ANCHOR — Two speech bubbles identical in exterior contour, typography, and polish hovering before an inspection gateway)*
**Liam**: Here is our anchor: the shape test. Picture two identical speech bubbles arriving on your screen. Both have the exact same cadence, the same polished syntax, and the same air of authority.

### [B04] 2 Wrong Guess: The Form-Content Coupling (Manim)
*(Visual: The Classical Heuristic: Articulate Form <---> Sound Content bound tightly together)*
**Liam**: The intuitive guess is that well-formed prose implies factual understanding. For thousands of years of human conversation, mastering the form meant someone had done the underlying intellectual work.

### [B05] 3 Break It: The Statistical Decoupling (Manim)
*(Visual: The link snaps: Statistical Generator produces Form on one track, while Content sits on an ungrounded parallel track)*
**Liam**: Generative models shatter that heuristic. A statistical generator learns to produce the shape of authoritative prose entirely independently of whether the underlying claim is true. Form and content are completely decoupled.

### [B06] 3 Epistemic Mechanism: Polish as an Unearned Shield (Manim)
*(Visual: Maximally fluent + maximally wrong coexistence grid; fluency disarming skepticism)*
**Liam**: Because form and content are decoupled, a statement can be maximally fluent and completely wrong at the same time. The smooth prose does epistemic work it never earned, disarming the reader's supervisory skepticism.

### [B07] 4 ANCHOR PAYOFF: MANIM MOVE compare (Manim)
*(Visual: Kinetic demonstration of compare: The True Bubble and the False Bubble sail through the Shape Test side by side. Both receive identical green PASS checkmarks based solely on grammar and structure)*
**Liam**: Watch the two bubbles meet the filter. The true bubble and the false bubble sail through the shape test side by side. The filter sees only grammar and tone — it cannot tell truth from polished fiction.

### [B08] 3 Mechanism: Why Post-Hoc Reading Fails (Manim)
*(Visual: Reader anchored post-hoc: Reader reading text without prior criteria, nodding along with syntax)*
**Liam**: If you evaluate an answer only after reading it, fluency has already anchored your perception. You find yourself nodding along with the cadence before you ever check the factual claims against the world.

### [B09] 4 ANCHOR PAYOFF: The Prior Anchor (Manim)
*(Visual: The Popperian Move: Specifying falsification criteria beforehand — an independent objective ruler placed before reading)*
**Liam**: The fix is Popper's move: specify what a wrong answer would look like before you read. Define the falsifying conditions in advance so you hold an independent ruler that fluency cannot bend.

### [B10] 3 ONE FLAG: The Enemy Is Confidence (Manim)
*(Visual: THE ONE FLAG banner: The enemy of skepticism is not laziness, but confidence; supervisory instinct is weakest when prose is cleanest)*
**Liam**: One flag — the greatest enemy of skepticism is not laziness, but confidence. You need supervisory rigor most when the output looks cleanest, which is precisely when you are least inclined to apply it.

### [B11] 5 DIRECTION A: Fluency Is Not Truth (Manim)
*(Visual: Direction A: High Syntactic Fluency ≠ Factual Grounding struck through with terracotta bar)*
**Liam**: So in one direction, pristine fluency is never evidence of factual truth. An answer can earn top marks for style and syntax while describing a world that does not exist.

### [B12] 5 DIRECTION B: The Optimizer Is Not Lying (Manim)
*(Visual: Direction B: Statistical Likelihood Optimization ≠ Deceptive Intent. The algorithm merely matched the distribution)*
**Liam**: In the other direction, the model is not lying or scheming. It simply matched the prior distribution of well-formed text. The failure is ours — mistaking the shape of an answer for its grounding.

### [BCRY] 6 CARRY-OUT: The Single Sentence (Remotion)
*(Visual: Remotion WantQuote displaying the carry-out sentence in elegant serif typography)*
**Liam**: In human speech form is evidence of thinking, but generative models manufacture form independently — so specify what a wrong answer would look like before you read.
