# Question

"In 1936, the largest poll in history — 2.4 million responses — predicted a landslide for the loser, while a poll fifty times smaller called the race right. Why did 2.4 million answers lose to 50,000?"

**Source**: Candidate 07 — *Computational Skepticism for AI* (Chapter 6: Bias — Where It Enters and Who Is Responsible).
**Asker**: A machine learning practitioner or data scientist assuming that massive dataset volume automatically cancels out sampling bias.
