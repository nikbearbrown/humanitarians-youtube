# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"More data only narrows the scatter around the wrong answer — when your sampling frame is biased, volume gives you convergence to an error with absolute confidence."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"Big data eliminates sampling bias, so collecting millions of responses guarantees an accurate estimate regardless of how the sample was drawn."

## Falsifying Case
The 1936 Literary Digest poll gathered 2.4 million returned ballots and predicted Alf Landon would beat FDR with 57% of the vote. FDR won in a 60.8% landslide. The massive sample was drawn from telephone directories, vehicle registries, and subscriber lists during the Great Depression, skewing the estimator. George Gallup sampled roughly 50,000 respondents with quota sampling and called the race correctly.
