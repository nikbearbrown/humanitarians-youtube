# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"When a dataset faithfully records a discriminatory past, the model's accuracy is the harm — it projects what was into what will be, and calls the projection truth."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"If a dataset has zero errors, no annotator mistakes, and high predictive accuracy, the resulting model cannot produce biased decisions."

## Falsifying Case
Ten years of promotion records accurately reflect historical disparities where men were promoted at 3x the rate of qualified women. The model learns P(Y_historical|x) perfectly, so its accuracy is the harm — it faithfully projects that discriminatory past into future decisions, which then generate the next round of biased data.
