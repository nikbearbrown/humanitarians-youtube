# Carry-Out Law

## The Carry-Out Line
> "Position and count replace mental arithmetic with direct perception."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
The belief that reporting uncertainty as a percentage (like "20% risk of failure" or "90% sensitivity with a 9.9% false-positive rate") is transparent and sufficient, and that when lay audiences, doctors, or engineers misunderstand it, the remedy is more statistical formulas.

## The Distinction That Matters
- **Percentages vs Counts**: Percentages detach probability from concrete population totals and force Bayesian mental arithmetic that human cognition notoriously bungles. Natural frequencies ground probability in tangible, countable individuals.
- **Symbolic Arithmetic vs Perceptual Channels**: Abstract numbers and shaded confidence bands rely on low-accuracy perceptual channels (area, color saturation, mental multiplication). Quantile dotplots translate probability directly into spatial position and discrete counts—the top tiers of the Cleveland–McGill perceptual hierarchy.
