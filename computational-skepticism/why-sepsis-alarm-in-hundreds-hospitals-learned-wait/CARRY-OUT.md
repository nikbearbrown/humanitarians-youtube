# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"An early-warning model cannot warn you about an event if its strongest feature is the trace of you already reacting to it."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"A sepsis model with high validation accuracy is independently forecasting patient deterioration before clinicians notice."

## Falsifying Case
The Epic Sepsis Model (Wong et al., 2021) learned to trigger on clinical workflow traces like blood-culture orders. Its alerts arrived after the physician had already formed suspicion and ordered tests, turning an 'early warning' into a circular echo of human judgment that internal metrics scored as high accuracy.
