# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"A dashboard argues by structure before any number is read — the design choices are the argument."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"If two dashboards display the exact same validation CSV, readers will form the same conclusion about whether the system is safe."

## Falsifying Case
A single validation CSV from a recommendation system — showing 94% average accuracy, but a sharp drop across three critical subgroups and severe overconfidence at high probabilities — is handed to two builders. One is told to reassure; one is told to provoke questions. Through five simple layout choices (headline metric, axis truncation, color salience, tab placement, and framing cards), each taking thirty seconds to make, the builders produce two dashboards from identical data: one leaves a stakeholder convinced the model is safe to deploy, and the other leaves them alarmed with urgent questions about failure modes.
