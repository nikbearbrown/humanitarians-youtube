# Carry-Out Law

## The Carry-Out Line
> "You cannot escape the fairness impossibility — every advanced framework simply relocates the values choice to a new object you have to sign."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
The naive belief that by abandoning group-level metrics for individual fairness, causal models, or an inequality index, an engineering team can mathematically escape the fairness impossibility and avoid making subjective values trade-offs.

## The Distinction That Matters
- **Technical Resolution vs Object Relocation**: The fundamental trade-off of algorithmic fairness cannot be engineered away. Individual fairness relocates the values choice into the similarity metric $d$; causal fairness relocates it into the structural causal graph; the inequality index relocates it into the definition of benefit and the sensitivity parameter $\alpha$.
- **Defended Sign-Off**: None of these objects can be derived purely from observational data or statistical optimization. Every escape hatch demands that a human stakeholder sign off on a theory of the world and declare whose interests take priority.
