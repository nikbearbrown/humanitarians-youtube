# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"A faithful explanation describes the model's internal accounting, not the world — so when a model learns a shortcut, an accurate explanation makes a wrong call feel right."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"If an AI tool's explanation is truthful and accurate, following it will make human decisions more reliable."

## Falsifying Case
A deep neural network predicts malignancy at 84% confidence based on texture pattern X and asymmetry Y. The explanation is 100% faithful: the model really used X and Y. But X and Y are learned shortcuts correlated with a benign condition in the deployment population. Reading the coherent explanation causes a radiologist's confidence in ordering a biopsy to surge from 50% to 90%, even though the true biopsy is benign. *(Labeled as a composite clinical illustration per Chapter 5).*
