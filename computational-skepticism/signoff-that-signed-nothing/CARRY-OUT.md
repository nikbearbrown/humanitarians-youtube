# Carry-Out Law

## The Carry-Out Line
> "The load-bearing section of an attestation is what was not tested."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
The belief that an AI-generated sign-off or automated compliance checklist creates genuine accountability, and that if a validation report sounds authoritative, the system has been verified.

## The Distinction That Matters
- **Asserted Validation vs. Declared Limits**: An AI-generated report asserts completeness by listing passed tests, creating the dangerous illusion of a clean bill of health. An honest attestation defines its own boundaries by explicitly documenting what was *not* tested.
- **Decorative Output vs. Real Stakes**: A signature is not formatting—it is an answerable commitment by a named human who has skin in the game. A model doesn't lose its job.
