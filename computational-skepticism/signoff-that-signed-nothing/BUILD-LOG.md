## Six-Move Audit & Beat Breakdown
1. **Open / Hesitant Writer (B00)**: Prompt typing and correction: "How does AI automate validation sign-off?" -> "When an AI drafts a validation sign-off, why is every word empty?".
2. **Hook / Stakes (B01)**: The one-second sign-off. Instant generation of names, approvals, reviews — all functionally hollow.
3. **Shape vs Substance (B02)**: Why syntax isn't accountability. A signature is a commitment by an entity with stakes. A model doesn't lose its job.
4. **The Four-Block Architecture (B03)**: Introducing the attestation model: Tested, NOT Tested, Named Human, Calibrated Verbs.
5. **The Negative Space Principle (B04)**: The load-bearing section: what was NOT tested. The model cannot write this because it doesn't know what it didn't do.
6. **Morph Transition (B05)**: The kinetic move (`morph`). The decorative signature block dissolves and morphs into the structured four-block attestation stack.
7. **Calibrated Verbs vs Empty Safety (B06)**: Replacing decorative marketing claims ("certified safe", "validated") with empirical boundaries ("tested against test suite C, batch 4").
8. **Both Directions (B07)**:
   - Direction A: A flawless validation summary does not prove rigorous testing.
   - Direction B: When a system fails in production, liability does not adhere to the model; answerability belongs to the human who accepted the sign-off.
9. **Carry-Out Quote (BCRY)**: *"The load-bearing section of an attestation is what was not tested."*
10. **The One Flag (B08)**: Epistemic boundary reminder: an attestation doesn't make code bug-free; it makes the engineering limits visible and accountable.
11. **Your Turn / Prompt (BHTF)**: Interactive audit prompt for viewers to replace automated sign-offs in their stack with the four-block attestation architecture.
12. **Outro (BOUT)**: Series closing badge, chapter reference, and @HumanitariansAI channel skin.
