## Learning outcome

After watching, a viewer with no physics or coding background can:

1. **Name** the three verify checks: plug back in (residual), physically possible
   (positivity), independent path.
2. **Give** one example of an independent path (Kirchhoff's current law; capacitor at
   one time constant; escape = √2 × orbital).
3. **Explain** the labels: verified, flagged for review, not run.

Measurable: given an AI's number, the viewer asks for the same answer by a second route.

## Audience

Students and general viewers. Defined on first use: *residual* (B02), *must be
positive* (B03), *independent path*, *Kirchhoff's current law*, *time constant* (B04),
*escape / orbital speed*, *efficiency* (B05), *flagged for review* (B06).
