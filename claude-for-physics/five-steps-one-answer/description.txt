Five Steps, One Answer. — Physics Engine, 3 of 17

Follow one physics problem all the way through the engine built for the Medhavy learning platform. A car starts from rest and accelerates at 2.5 m/s² for 12 s: how fast is it going, and how far did it travel? You'll see each of the five steps do its one job — parse reads the problem, retrieve pulls candidate formulas, plan runs a closure check, solve does the math in SymPy, and verify checks the answer a second way — and why only the first step uses a language model. What comes out is a solution object: a record of every step, not just a number. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: once the problem is read, the same input always takes the same path.
