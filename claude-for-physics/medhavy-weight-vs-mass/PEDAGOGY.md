# PEDAGOGY.md — weight-vs-mass (Chapter 5, Mass and Weight)

## Concept
The same astronaut weighs 180 lb on Earth and 30 lb on the Moon — did she
lose mass? No. Weight (W = mg) is a force that depends on local gravity;
mass is inertia, resistance to being pushed, and it is identical on both
worlds (same push, same slide distance).
