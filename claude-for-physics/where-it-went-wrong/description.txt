Where It Went Wrong. — Physics Engine, 10 of 12

A normal checker says "wrong." The physics engine's grade mode says which mistake produces exactly the student's number, points at the first step that went wrong, and quotes the card's own warning.

The claim: wrong is a verdict; which mistake is feedback.
