# PEDAGOGY.md — inertia-puck (Chapter 5, Newton's First Law)

## Concept
A pushed puck slides and stops — intuition says that's just what moving
things do. Resolution: friction was acting the whole time; remove it (air
hockey) and the puck glides at constant velocity forever. Zero net force
means constant velocity — Newton's first law, inertia.
