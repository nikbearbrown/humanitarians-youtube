# PEDAGOGY.md — horse-cart-paradox (Chapter 5, Newton's Third Law)

## Concept
If the horse pulls the cart forward with 500 N and the cart pulls back on
the horse with 500 N (Newton's third law), the naive sum is zero — so why
does the system move? Resolution: third-law pairs act on *different*
objects and never appear in the same free-body diagram; each object's own
net force (ground push vs. cart pull on the horse; horse pull vs. rolling
resistance on the cart) is what matters, and both nets point forward.
