# PEDAGOGY.md — doppler-asymmetry (Chapter 17, The Doppler Effect)

## Concept
A source approaching at speed u and an observer approaching a stationary
source at the same speed u produce different Doppler shifts, because sound
propagates in a medium. A moving source shortens the wavelength in the air
(f_o = f_s v/(v - v_s)); a moving observer meets unchanged wavefronts at a
higher rate (f_o = f_s (v + v_o)/v).
