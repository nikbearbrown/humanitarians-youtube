Force Is Not Time. — Physics Engine, 7 of 12

An automatic check in the physics engine (built for the Medhavy learning platform) compared the units on every formula card — and found that one letter, T, meant a force on one card and a time on two others. This video covers why units decide whether an answer is right, the engine's two unit checks (every label is a real dimension; each card agrees with the variable dictionary), how that audit caught the T collision, and an honest swap: the project plan called for the pint library, it couldn't be installed, so the team used SymPy's units module and wrote down the limit. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: a tool that says what it didn't do beats one that claims it all.
