A Comma Changed the Answer. — Physics Engine, 2 of 17

In one study, researchers gave an AI model the same addition problems twice. The only change was commas in the numbers, and accuracy jumped from 75.6% to 97.8%. This video explains why: a language model reads numbers in chunks called tokens, not digit by digit, so its arithmetic is a very good guess rather than a calculation. Then it shows what the physics engine built for the Medhavy learning platform does instead: the model reads the problem, and SymPy, a symbolic math library, does every calculation exactly. That matters most for grading and for clear hints. No math or coding background needed.

The claim: let the model read; let a calculator calculate.
