Who Does the Math. — Physics Engine, 1 of 17

Ask a chatbot a physics question and you get a confident number. But who actually computed it? This video walks through the physics engine built for the Medhavy learning platform, where a language model reads the problem and picks the formula, and a symbolic solver (SymPy, with pint for units) does every calculation. You'll see the five-step pipeline, the three routes the engine chooses before it solves anything, what the team has measured, and what they say is still unproven. No physics or coding background needed.

The claim: words to the model, math to the solver — and an engine that says so when it can't solve something.
