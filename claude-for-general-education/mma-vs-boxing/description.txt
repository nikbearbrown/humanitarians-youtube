Cage, Rules, Belts — MMA vs Boxing, and Who the World Champions Actually Are

Who is the world champion of "MMA boxing"? Trick question — they're two different sports. This explainer is for people who follow neither, and it gets you there in three questions: what does the floor look like, what is a fighter allowed to do, and how many belts are on the line.

MMA is fought in an eight-sided cage; boxing in a square ring with ropes. Boxing allows one thing, punches above the belt. MMA allows six, including kicks, takedowns and submissions. And MMA's top tier is a single organization with one champion per division, while boxing has four separate bodies each awarding their own world title — so one weight class can carry four champions at once.

Belt holders change every few months; the figures here were verified on 3 August 2026 and the video says so on screen. The structure is the part that lasts.
