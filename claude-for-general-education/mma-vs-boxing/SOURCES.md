## Corrections made to the July 2026 script

The original `mma-vs-boxing-VOICED.mp4` was transcribed and re-checked rather
than trusted. Four claims did not survive:

1. **"12 divisions, 12 belts."** The 12 divisions are right; "12 belts" is not.
   Women's featherweight has had no champion since Amanda Nunes retired in 2023,
   and heavyweight currently carries two recognised belts at once (undisputed
   plus interim). The reel now says "12 weight divisions, one champion in each"
   and handles the heavyweight exception explicitly.
2. **"One champion per weight class — simple."** Not true right now: Ciryl Gane
   holds an interim heavyweight title alongside Aspinall. Rather than drop the
   contrast, B03 names the wrinkle and reframes the real distinction as *one
   organization versus four* — which is the durable structural fact, and the one
   the reel actually needs.
3. **"Only one boxer in the entire sport holds all four belts."** True for men's
   boxing (Naoya Inoue). Not true across the whole sport once women's boxing is
   included — at least three women's undisputed champions were reported in April
   2026. B06 now says "in men's boxing today," which is defensible.
4. **The WBC holder is secondary-sourced only.** wbcboxing.com returned HTTP 403
   and could not be fetched, so Agit Kabayel rests on S6 rather than the
   federation's own site. Flagged rather than hidden.
